## Source Conventions

- Primary law and official court materials control over commentary.
- Campaign and party material may establish asserted arguments or procedural events, not their legal correctness.
- Preserve adverse authority and admissibility barriers.
- Mark facts that require current verification as `hot`.
- Use `CJEU` in English prose; retain `EuGH` and `EUGH` as aliases.

## Compile Guidance

- Keep substantive EU-law objections separate from Austrian admissibility and procedural routes.
- Keep each Member State implementation as a distinct article unless evidence is too thin, then consolidate in a comparison reference.
- Maintain a dedicated thesis article for the VfGH-to-CJEU route that states factual gates, counterarguments, and unresolved issues.
