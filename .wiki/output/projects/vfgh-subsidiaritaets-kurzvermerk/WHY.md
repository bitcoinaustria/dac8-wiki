# VfGH Subsidiaritäts-Kurzvermerk

This project keeps the source-backed Markdown report and its plain PDF export together. The concrete report is limited to the risk that a private-person Individualantrag may be rejected because another reasonable route exists; it is not a full admissibility or merits opinion.

The reusable repo template is maintained at [`docs/templates/legal-research-kurzvermerk.md`](../../../../docs/templates/legal-research-kurzvermerk.md).
