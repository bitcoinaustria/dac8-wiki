# Wiki Configuration

## Scope

DAC8 legislation and implementation in Austria and the European Union, with a primary procedural thesis: an affected individual seeks Austrian constitutional review and, where EU-law interpretation or validity is necessary, a preliminary reference from the VfGH to the CJEU.

## Conventions

- Prefer official legislation, court decisions, dockets, and institutional guidance.
- Separate enacted law, pending bills, litigation claims, and strategic inference.
- Record jurisdiction, date, identifier, procedural posture, and current status.
- Do not present campaign material as independent proof or the wiki as legal advice.
