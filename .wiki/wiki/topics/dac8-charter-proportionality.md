# DAC8 Charter Proportionality

> The interference is clear. Invalidity is not: the analysis turns on the exact fields, their inference value, pre-report processing, retention, authority access, safeguards, and remedies.

## Exact reporting profile

DAC8 links identification and tax-residence data to annual aggregates organized by crypto asset and transaction category. Depending on the category, the report includes fiat amounts, fair-market values, units, and counts for acquisitions, disposals, reportable retail payments, and transfers. It also includes an aggregate value for transfers to DLT addresses not known to be associated with a qualifying intermediary.

It does **not** report transaction dates, transaction identifiers, counterparties, wallet addresses, or DLT addresses. A blockchain-correlation theory needs separate evidence showing how a provider or authority obtains and links those facts.

## Strongest challenge theory

- coverage is routine and not based on individualized suspicion;
- due diligence and reportability decisions can require processing a broader customer population than the final transmitted set;
- identity, home address, per-asset values/units/counts, and transfer categories may permit a materially richer annual profile than the bank-account facts considered in Austrian DAC2 litigation;
- Austria selected a ten-year retention horizon, increasing exposure and making the national choice separately reviewable;
- cross-border authority access, correction, security, and judicial remedies must operate effectively;
- targeted requests, risk-based selection, shorter retention, or narrower data categories should be tested as less intrusive alternatives.

## Counterauthority that must be answered

- VfGH E 2097/2021 and VwGH Ro 2023/04/0021 upheld DAC2/CRS reporting and rejected no-threshold, all-customer processing, security-risk, and reference arguments.
- CJEU C-623/22 broadly upheld DAC6's precision and proportionality; C-694/20 is limited to lawyer-client confidentiality.
- G.S.B. and Othymia accept tax-information transmission within a lawful, reviewable framework.
- Digital Rights Ireland, Tele2/Watson, La Quadrature, SpaceNet, and Garda concern far more revealing communications traffic/location data used for security or law enforcement.
- Schecke and Luxembourg Business Registers concern public disclosure; DAC8 exchange is not public.

## Better supporting authorities

Valsts ieņēmumu dienests, C-175/20, is useful for purpose specificity, minimization, time limits, and review in a tax-authority setting. Ligue des droits humains and Opinion 1/15 supply rigorous safeguards for systematic profiling and automated analysis. Schrems I and Berlioz reinforce independent review where personal data cross borders or support tax enforcement.

These cases more naturally support narrowing interpretation and implementation safeguards than automatic facial invalidity.

## Evidence still needed

- real CASP notices, self-certification flows, access responses, and retention schedules;
- a field-by-field Austrian-to-EU schema map;
- proof of who can access which data, for how long, and with what logs/oversight;
- a technically supported public-ledger correlation demonstration using the fields actually retained by the provider;
- evidence of physical-security threat pathways beyond general crypto-crime statistics;
- a quantified comparison with narrower tax-information tools.
