# Claimant Selection: Evidence Boundaries and Corrections

The twenty September 10 records preserve useful research leads, but their strongest conclusions are not all supported. The qualifications below govern their reuse. They do not certify current exchange relationships, provider practices or the outcome of any proposed Austrian proceeding. The [review note](../../raw/notes/2026-09-21-claimant-evidence-corrections.md) records what was freshly checked and what remains inherited evidence.

## Sources and controlling qualifications

| Historical source and evidence class | What may be used, and its limit |
|---|---|
| [OECD activation table](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-oecd-carf-activated-exchange-relationships.md): dated retrieval account | A September 10 snapshot without effective dates cannot establish final reportability for the relevant period. The eleven-state list is not a final claimant filter. Verify every tax residence and the effective qualifying relationship under § 14; signature, activation and reportability are distinct. |
| [CARF-MCAA notifications](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-carf-mcaa-section-7-notifications-unpublished.md): instrument extract and negative search | Section 7 distinguishes safeguards specified, if any, from confidentiality confirmations and activation. Failure to locate public notifications does not prove that none were lodged, that no safeguards exist, or that every relationship is ineffective. The historical universal nonpublication claim is unverified. |
| [CARF identity/reporting baseline](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-carf-baseline-identity-versus-transaction.md): institutional explanation and schema account | Universal status screening and reporting about reportable users are different operations. An XML format's lack of a non-reportable-user element does not settle the lawful scope of provider-held records or processing. |
| [Provider practice](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-casp-carf-transparency-and-practice-gap.md): provider statements and negative search | An aggregation explainer or universal TIN collection does not prove the actual dataset generated for a particular non-reportable customer. Missing public notices do not establish that no individual notice was supplied. Article 15 is an evidence route, not the only conceivable route; provider evidence, authority investigation and other lawful access may also matter. |
| [DAC8 Annex VI V](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-dac8-annex-vi-section-v-verbatim.md): statutory extract | V.B concerns records of reporting/due-diligence steps and information relied upon, with a five-to-ten-year range in its stated terms. It does not erase status-screening documentation for persons ultimately not reported. Its mapping to § 39 and § 20(4) needs the precise object and timing, not an assumption that every transaction datum is outside it. |
| [Article 25 DAC](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-dac-article-25-verbatim-and-retention-limit.md): statutory extract with interpretation | Article 25(5) supplies a necessity limit, not proof of actual indefinite storage. Do not describe the directive itself as automatically imposing horizontally enforceable duties on a private provider; distinguish national implementation, conforming interpretation and directly applicable GDPR rules. |
| [ErlRV 306](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-erlrv-306-section-20-no-directive-basis.md): legislative materials | No separate directive citation or developed necessity analysis for § 20(1) supports a question about national discretion and justification. It does not establish invalidity. GDPR Article 6(3) expressly permits a Member-State legal basis subject to its requirements; absence of an EU mandate does not remove that possibility. |
| [§ 20(4) retention gap](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-krypto-mpfg-section-20-abs-4-retention-gap.md): statutory distinction and legal hypothesis | The transmitted-information deletion rule does not automatically govern never-transmitted data. Neither actual indefinite retention nor an Article 5(1)(e) violation follows from that distinction alone. Identify the records, applicable duties, purposes, necessity and actual period. |
| [GMSG template](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-gmsg-section-5-template-and-escalation.md): legislative history | Preserve the distinction between the original permission and the duty from 2019. Future-partner and administrative-simplification rationales are legislative explanations open to proportionality review, not evidence that no explanation exists. The age of an unchallenged clause neither validates nor invalidates it. |
| [German KStTG comparator](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-germany-ksttg-section-14-comparator.md): foreign statute comparison | Different drafting can identify an alternative to investigate; it does not prove identical practical processing, equivalent tax effectiveness or Austrian unlawfulness. Do not infer EU compliance solely from another state's enactment. Preserve the distinct German CRS-side screening rule. |
| [Austrian FATCA/GMSG contrast](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-fatca-consent-contrast-and-gmsg-cooperation-duty.md): treaty and statutory comparison | Consent for the specified Model-2 mechanism, aggregate reporting and the group-request route must remain separate. Consent to account opening is not proof of a freely optional or equally effective CARF substitute. The bank-secrecy history is not a fundamental-rights holding; customer cooperation remains limited to the required information under the applicable norm. |
| [Consultation silence](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-consultation-silence-on-universal-collection.md): bounded document search | A missing express objection in reviewed submissions supports a documented gap, not proof that no assessment occurred anywhere. Absence of a consultation record is not proof of failure to comply with a procedural duty. |
| [Literature gap](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-austrian-literature-gap-on-universal-collection.md): incomplete negative search and academic comparison | Unread paywalled commentary and incomplete Findok coverage preclude "never examined" or "no adverse commentary". A thesis on another statute is commentary, not a judgment; its trigger argument does not establish the outcome under the GMSG or Krypto-MPfG. |
| [BMF specification gap](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-bmf-carf-specification-publication-gap.md): dated negative search | Public non-retrieval does not settle existence behind the provider portal, normative content, accessibility to the addressee or invalidity under Article 18 B-VG. Recheck actual versions and publication before alleging a defect; an unanswered inquiry proves only the absence of a response within the observed period. |
| [EDPB territorial guidance](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-edpb-guidelines-3-2018-territorial-scope.md): institutional interpretation | Article 3(1) can protect persons outside the Union where processing occurs in the context of an EU establishment. Territorial application does not alone decide the competent authority, admissibility or outcome of a specific complaint. |
| [DSB foreign-complainant practice](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-dsb-objektiver-konnex-and-foreign-complainants.md): decisions and aggregate statistics | The objective Austrian nexus matters; the Poland example is not a third-country merits precedent. The record itself classifies the 105 decisions by controllers outside the EEA, not complainant residence. It does not establish 105 successful or substantive complaints by third-country residents, or Austria as their sole forum. |
| [VfSlg 11.479/1987](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-vfslg-11479-1987-foreign-residence-subsidiarity.md): adverse procedural precedent | The available electoral remedy defeated that applicant's foreign-residence argument. Transfer to a GDPR complaint is an analogy requiring the actual alternative route and burden; it does not decide every foreign claimant's Article 140 standing. |
| [VfGH E 2097/2021](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-vfgh-e2097-2021-competence-bar-distinction.md): competence and adverse merits authority | Separate the fully determined §§ 112/113 GMSG from any demonstrated national discretion. Neither an automatic Article 140 route nor stronger privacy inferences follow merely from that distinction. The record's claim that no subsequent VwGH decision was published is superseded by [Ro 2023/04/0021](../../raw/articles/2026-08-27-vwgh-ro-2023-04-0021-dac2.md), already in this repository. |
| [Adequacy scope](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-adequacy-decisions-public-sector-scope.md): decision-specific extracts | Check the current decision, recipient and processing, not country name alone. Japan's cited 2019/419 text is limited to covered business operators; that does not establish an immutable three-country claimant list. Absence of adequacy does not itself prove absence of another Chapter V ground. |
| [Belgian 79/2025 and C-804/25](../../raw/articles/2026-09-10-cjeu-c804-25-fatca-reference.md): administrative ruling and judicial reference | The Belgian ruling imposed a reprimand and compliance orders, expressly no prohibition. Its operative orders use one year from notification. C-804/25 contains questions, not CJEU holdings; Q10 specifically concerns the EU-US DPF and the disputed tax transfers. Application to CARF, and Article 96 across the full instrument chain, remain legal questions. |

## Residual records and adverse authority

For retention, identify the object of each rule: § 20(4) Krypto-MPfG concerns transmitted information; § 39 concerns documentation of reporting and due-diligence steps. Neither can be applied to every provider datum without analysis. The repository's [FM-GwG source](../../raw/articles/2026-08-27-austria-fm-gwg-current-crypto.md) and [[bitcoin-payment-profile-retention|payment-profile analysis]] ([analysis](../theses/bitcoin-payment-profile-retention.md)) identify an independent § 21(1) duty for required identification and transaction records, ten years after the business relationship ends or the occasional transaction. MiCA records can also survive. Those other purposes do not automatically justify additional tax processing; conversely, success against a tax-processing provision does not automatically delete all underlying records.

The [published VwGH decision of 10 December 2025](../../../legal-docs/core/at-vwgh-ro-2023-04-0021-12.pdf) defeats the September 10 record's negative publication claim. Ro 2023/04/0021 rejected the revision as inadmissible and declined a reference, while endorsing the VfGH's Charter assessment. Preserve both the procedural requirement for a concrete decisive question and the adverse substantive reasoning. Distinguishing DAC8 requires evidence about actual fields and additional inferences; annual aggregates are not an individual transaction ledger.

## Claimant facts still required

Establish the controller and Austrian nexus, all tax residences, the applicable period and effective exchange conditions, accepted status determination, actual additional processing, retention grounds and requested relief. Separate a national-processing claim from a claim about a real third-country transfer and from a DAC8-validity challenge. No claimant, present provider practice or final state list is established here.

The September 10 findings about OECD relationships, publication searches, literature and provider practices remain historical and incompletely reproducible; they were not refreshed in this compilation. The foreign judgments and comparisons above are not Austrian holdings. Current procedural deadlines, notification dates and ongoing transfer operations require case-specific verification.
