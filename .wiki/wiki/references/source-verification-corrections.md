The following corrections govern reuse of the listed immutable raw records. Their original summaries are not reliable statements of the corrected points. Preserve the supersession notices when regenerating raw category indexes.

|---|---|
| [DSB urgent-protection threshold, original extraction](../../raw/articles/2026-08-27-dsb-2024-0215259-urgent-prohibition-threshold.md) | The separate "weighty/serious infringement" threshold is incorrect. ## DAC history qualifications verified on 30 September 2026

The [primary-law scope supplement](../../raw/articles/2026-09-30-dac-scope-primary-law-corrections.md) controls reuse of shorthand in the earlier timeline and source summaries:

| Earlier shorthand | Controlling qualification |
|---|---|
| Annual reports or DAC2 checks concern all customers. | Reporting and review populations differ; DAC2 account exceptions include the existing-entity-account threshold. Independent AML/KYC duties do not remove those DAC2 exceptions. |
| DAC3 binding rulings, five years retrospectively. | Qualifying non-binding forms are included; 2012–2013 validity and pre-April 2016 turnover qualifications apply. The original DAC3 raw body already contains the historical exceptions. |
| General first Austrian CRS exchange on 30 September 2018. | AB 749 specifies a deadline: by that date, not proof of the execution day. Its immutable raw digest already states the distinction. |
| DAC6 abolished lawyers' notification duty; otherwise all valid. | C-694/20 concerns privileged lawyers notifying non-client intermediaries; C-623/22 rejects the examined challenges, not every future objection. |
| DAC9 first central filing on 30 June 2026. | Conditional central filing; deadline for calendar-year 2024 first returns, not a universal completed event. |
| Sanctions use without consent establishes unrestricted Austrian access. | The EU Article 215 exception is supported; the full Austrian authorization remains unresolved. Use the [national-text comparison](../../raw/articles/2026-09-30-at-euahg-purpose-implementation.md), not an inferred transposition defect. |
