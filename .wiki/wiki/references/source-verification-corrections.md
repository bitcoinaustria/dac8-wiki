# Source Verification Corrections

The following corrections govern reuse of the listed immutable raw records. Their original summaries are not reliable statements of the corrected points. Preserve the supersession notices when regenerating raw category indexes.

| Historical record | Correction controlling current synthesis |
|---|---|
| [DSB urgent-protection threshold, original extraction](../../raw/articles/2026-08-27-dsb-2024-0215259-urgent-prohibition-threshold.md) | The separate "weighty/serious infringement" threshold is incorrect. Use the [current correction](../../raw/articles/2026-09-04-dsb-urgent-threshold-correction.md): grounded suspicion of unlawful processing plus substantial immediate danger. Section 25(1) permits action; it does not mandate an immediate prohibition. |
| [DSG corrective-order asymmetry](../../raw/articles/2026-09-04-austria-dsg-gdpr-corrective-order-asymmetry.md) | Its general private-controller restoration description is incorrect. Section 24(5) concerns compliance with the enumerated underlying data-subject requests. Use the [current-law correction](../../raw/articles/2026-09-04-austria-dsg-section-24-complaint-relief-correction.md), also supplying the correct RIS URL. |
| [Cong, Tang and Zhang, original extraction](../../raw/papers/2026-09-04-cong-tang-zhang-crypto-tax-transparency.md) | Its on-chain-flow assertion lacks a verified pinpoint and must not be treated as established. The [scope correction](../../raw/papers/2026-09-04-cong-tax-transparency-evidence-correction.md) supports price responses and the combined KYC/reporting association, not isolated KYC causality. |
| [DSG section 21, original extraction](../../raw/articles/2026-09-04-austria-dsg-section-21-consultation-duty.md) | Its official URL omits `Artikel=2`. Use the [correct locator](../../raw/articles/2026-09-04-dsg-21-consultation-source-locator-correction.md). The consultation requirement does not prove that consultation was omitted in a particular process. |
