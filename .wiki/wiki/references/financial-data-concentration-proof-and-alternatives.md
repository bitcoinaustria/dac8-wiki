# Financial data concentration evidence and alternative processing designs

This reference supports an argumentation aid for counsel. It is not an independent technical opinion. The strongest general claim concerns the combined information and exposure of lawful mandatory records, repeat reporting and additional linkage. Each transition to a specific Austrian crypto system still needs evidence.

## Actual fusion is documented but its scope matters

The BMF-funded BiRank study links thirty years of company-register relationships to tax identities and risk indicators. It establishes an implemented research experiment, not current production use, CRS inputs or wealth calculation. The final scoring model's incremental benefit remained future work. BFG RV/1300013/2020 separately establishes actual use of account reports for 2017–2018, followed by a paid tax correction. This is direct utility evidence but only one case.

BFG RV/5100216/2024 upheld an additional electronic data demand in a GMSG compliance audit, including non-reportable accounts. Broader authority acquisition is thus a real route, not necessarily part of routine reporting. The holding is adverse to blanket illegality claims; full delivery and subsequent profiling are not established, and a later VwGH outcome was not verified.

Rechnungshof Bund 2026/5 limits the inference that available data are automatically joined: some platform data were used manually, not in PACC automated selection. The RH recommended more integration. BMF-reported individual CESOP access and announced future extensions are not observed comprehensive fusion. These findings do not concern CRS/crypto features.

## Retention and broad operational availability are different

Coinbase's published policy distinguishes legally retained records from deletable optional marketing/communication records. It is policy evidence, not observed compliance across all CASPs.

Ledger reported in February 2022 that order data older than eighteen months had been moved into a segregated, access-restricted archive despite ten-year accounting retention. The three-month goal was then unfinished. This is a unique first-party implementation report in hardware commerce, not an independent CASP audit.

The FTC Blackbaud complaint alleges that unnecessary retained backups and weak segmentation aggravated a leak involving financial and wealth data. These are consent-resolved allegations. The final order, by contrast, bindingly requires deletion of unnecessary records, documented legal-hold exceptions, retention schedules, MFA and constrained cross-customer access. Neither source measures achieved risk reduction.

**Inference:** mandatory preservation does not logically require unchanged support access, every operational copy or indefinite retention. A segregated archive is a concrete candidate for comparison, not yet proof of equivalent statutory-service performance.

### Retention-effectiveness supplement checked 15 October 2026

The [ShipMonk incident update](../../raw/articles/2026-10-15-scam-trezor-shipmonk-retention.md) reports that historical customer orders survived despite written deletion confirmations. The lesson is to test actual supplier-copy removal and access, not to infer safety from a policy or to conclude that every deletion architecture fails. The [[leaked-data-scams-and-targeted-attacks|expanded exploitation evidence]] ([expanded exploitation evidence](leaked-data-scams-and-targeted-attacks.md)) separately demonstrates why contact/relationship records can remain useful for phishing and repeat fraud without revealing keys or exact net wealth. The older legal, utility and implementation findings retain their original verification scope.

## Linkage metrics must identify their denominator

The [UTXO](bitcoin-utxo-observation-and-attribution.md), [account/contract](account-contract-chain-data-evidence.md) and [off-chain/privacy](offchain-private-ledger-observation-limits.md) supplements now give the protocol-level foundation. A leaked identity anchor or actually held viewing/derivation material can expand historical and later observation without a new provider query. This is distinct from direct spend authority, ownership and complete net wealth. Deleted origin records do not withdraw already stolen anchor copies. Alternative comparisons should inventory these fields and derived information, not just database row counts.

The Ghost Clusters supplement distinguishes service-address recall from label precision. For its three service cases, own calculation FP/(TP+FP) gives false discovery shares of 0.0424%, 0.1853% and 0.1162%; recall is 24.54%, 78.89% and 94.85%. No general FPR is available without genuine-negative counts. These are service labels, not person ownership or total-wealth ground truth.

## Alternative designs have measurable tradeoffs

Hertha uses synthetic payment-network data. At a common 1% alert target, illicit-account recall is 44% for bank models, 39% for a minimal network model and 49% with active collaboration. Network gain is real within that simulation; equal standalone utility is not.

The Visa PET prototype protects additional bank flags, while the hub already holds transactions and identity fields. AUPRC 0.79 centrally, 0.70 in a local federated simulation and 0.49 in the constrained distributed challenge are different execution conditions. No formal differential-privacy guarantee is offered. Repeated queries can leak information. This is design feasibility with limits, not complete profile prevention.

For the proposed external opinion, compare actual tax tasks and necessary records under identical workloads. Measure model utility, false assignments, accessible customer reach, copies, authorised retrieval latency and archive availability. Security improvements and tax equivalence are separate endpoints.

### Additional control and concentration evidence checked 17 October 2026

[EDPB Statement2/2024](../../raw/articles/2026-10-17-scam-edpb-financial-centralisation.md) explicitly identifies greater interference and security risk in a proposed central financial antifraud platform than in distributed PSP processing. This is an institutional proposal-stage position, not a DAC8 holding, a current PSR-status check or proof of equal decentralised utility. It supports demanding a concrete architecture comparison rather than assuming concentration is necessary.

[NIST SP800-88r2](../../raw/articles/2026-10-17-scam-nist-sanitisation-validation.md) separately distinguishes sanitisation verification and validation. Media/key sanitisation does not prove removal of every application export, supplier record or restored backup. The [[financial-data-threat-model-and-control-evaluation|integrated control assessment]] ([integrated control assessment](financial-data-threat-model-and-control-evaluation.md)) turns these distinctions into copy, role and matched-workload tests. These additions do not refresh the earlier Austrian legal/production-use findings.

## Open proof gaps and access

Public sources do not supply Austria's crypto/CRS model-input inventory, copy-specific retention, role/export matrix or time-window utility comparison. An information gap is not a defective-control finding. Own-data requests, requests for existing redacted records and supervisory access have different scopes and exceptions; consult the access reference before making requests.
