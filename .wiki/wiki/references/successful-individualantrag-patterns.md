# Successful Austrian Individualantrag Patterns

> “Successful” must be split three ways: a true direct application, admission past the threshold, and success on the constitutional merits. They are not synonyms.

## Procedure-checked case matrix

| Proceeding | Vehicle | Result | Transferable lesson for DAC8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| G 151/99 ua | true Individualanträge | rigid four-week appeal-reasoning limit annulled; another limb inadmissible | Quantified exceptional facts and unavoidable preparations can establish current effect. |
| G 119–120/2014 | true Individualantrag | adoption restrictions partly annulled | A concrete intended legal act and proof that no usable decision can be obtained defeat “abstract concern.” |
| G 20/2015, G 281/2015 | joined true Individualanträge | insurers admitted and partly successful; association rejected | Use the actually regulated company, not merely an association; provoking a penalty is unnecessary. Substantively, this case relaxed a data-use prohibition and cuts both ways. |
| G 118/131/204/2015 | joined true Individualanträge | e-cigarette monopoly provisions annulled | Revenue, product, preparation, and expected-loss evidence established present effect before commencement. |
| G 4/2020 | true Individualantrag by children/parents | school head-covering rule annulled | Multiple applicants need their own legally protected sphere and life facts. |
| G 139/2019 | true Individualantrag with applicant-specific admissibility | assistance wording partly annulled; one applicant had an existing-court/Parteiantrag route; section 77 perimeter failed | Do not blend claimants; an available proceeding defeats only the affected claimant, and the requested deletion must remove the defect. |
| G 52/2024 | true Individualantrag | social-egg-freezing prohibition annulled | A non-formal norm addressee can be directly affected; medical finding, sworn declarations, doctor, contract, and experts proved reality. |
| G 229–230/2023 ua | joined true Individualanträge | narrow partial annulment; core regime upheld; many limbs rejected | Current authority for provision-by-provision facts, objections, main requests, and eventual requests. |
| G 109/92, G 13/93 | true Individualanträge | media-access restriction annulled; remainder rejected | Current effect can survive the triggering event if an evidenced continuing legal interest remains. Narrow DAC8 analogy. |
| G 128/92 | true Individualantrag | discriminatory contribution rule annulled | A direct ex-lege financial burden is a clean standing pattern. |
| G 170/92 | true Individualantrag | occupational exclusion partly annulled | Direct legal exclusion supports standing; describe the result as partial. |
| G 28/93 | true Individualantrag | statutory employer substitution partly annulled | Ex-lege alteration of private contractual rights can be direct. |
| G 37/02 ua, V 42/02 ua | true Individualanträge | uncompensated surveillance-equipment cost rule annulled; underlying facilities duty upheld | Strongest CASP analogy: immediate technical duty, high cost, no forced violation. It does not prove that the reporting duty itself must fall. |
| G 100/05 ua | true Individualanträge | mandatory mailbox infrastructure rules annulled | Direct penalised capital expenditure and no need to provoke a fine. Merits turned on the weak public-interest justification, not cost alone. |
| G 105/10 | true Individualantrag | automatic exchange-membership exclusion consequence annulled | A severe automatic consequence may be current where later review would be inadequate. |
| G 59/12 and G 62/70/71/12, joined as G 47/2012 ua | true Individualanträge joined with Kärnten's abstract G 47/12 application | retention provisions annulled after CJEU reference | Cite the individual dockets for standing. G 47/12 itself was not an Individualantrag. |

G 45/00 ua, V 31/00 ua is not a model direct statutory application: the statutory review was opened by the VfGH of its own motion after separate regulation challenges. It belongs in a procedural footnote, not a “successful Individualantrag” list.

## Strongest six for the DAC8 file

1. **G 37/02 ua:** direct technical/compliance cost burden.
2. **G 118/2015 ua:** preparations create present effect before formal application.
3. **G 100/05 ua:** mandatory infrastructure expenditure without provoking a sanction.
4. **G 20/2015 ua:** actually regulated businesses admitted; association rejected.
5. **G 52/2024:** concrete indirect addressee with sworn and expert evidence.
6. **G 59/12 and G 62/70/71/12:** direct user effect of automatic provider-side mass retention and a successful CJEU reference.

G 606/2015 ua is also vital but adverse on the merits: the VfGH accepted preparatory tax-compliance standing and upheld the cash-register regime. It prevents the false inference that admissibility, cost, or universality establishes disproportionality.

## What the initiating brief must contain

The current official guidance and form require the essential case in the initiating submission. New constitutional grounds cannot simply be added later or supplied by references to attachments.

### 1. Jurisdiction, applicant, and representation

- invoke Article 140(1)(1)(c) B-VG;
- identify each applicant and mandatory counsel;
- state the applicant's exact capacity: Austria-home CASP, non-reportable user, or reportable user;
- do not use an association unless it has its own immediate legal injury.

### 2. Exact challenged law

- quote the exact words, paragraphs, version, promulgation, and commencement;
- explain the legal remainder after deletion;
- use a main request and carefully nested unconditional eventual requests;
- avoid attacking an entire act where the facts and concerns reach only section 20(1) or a connected minimum perimeter.

### 3. Current and direct effect, provision by provision

For every applicant and every provision, plead:

- the existing provider/customer relationship and statutory status;
- what the law requires now, without an intervening order;
- the exact data, system, contract, expenditure, or legal choice affected;
- when compliance steps began and why waiting would make them unavoidable;
- supporting notices, invoices, contracts, technical records, or sworn statements.

### 4. No other reasonable route

Identify and answer every candidate path: DSB complaint, BVwG appeal, administrative determination, civil proceeding, tax decision, registration or authorisation decision, and existing court case with a Parteiantrag opportunity.

The applicant need not deliberately breach a penal rule. But inconvenience, cost, delay, or predicted failure before the DSB usually does not make the ordinary route unreasonable. For a user, G 178/2024 and G 17/2024 make this the principal admissibility danger.

### 5. Constitutional concerns allocated to provisions

Each ground needs its own chain:

> challenged words → exact processing or burden → protected right → legal standard → why this design fails → narrower effective alternative → remedy requested.

Possible DAC8 grounds include section 1 DSG; Articles 7, 8, and 52(1) Charter where EU law is implemented; Article 8 ECHR; Article 6 StGG for a provider; equality; and legal certainty. They should not be piled together without explaining which facts and provisions engage each right.

### 6. Evidence and counterfactual

For a CASP, separate MiCA/TFR/DAC8-minimum costs from Austria's disputed increment. For a user, prove actual section 20 processing, reportability status, access/deletion responses, and concrete transaction categories. Include a field-by-field and retention map, security architecture, DPIA, contracts, invoices, sworn declarations, and expert evidence where the factual proposition cannot be judicially assumed.

### 7. Relief, fees, and costs

- request the precise annulment or declaration appropriate to the current/versioned law;
- include service and evidence schedules without outsourcing essential pleading to them;
- include the fee proof and an express costs request;
- counsel must use the required electronic filing channel.

The official 2026 guidance states a EUR 340 filing fee from 1 July 2025 and says that about 15 pages will usually suffice, while allowing more for genuinely complex proceedings. Both details must be rechecked at filing.

## DAC8 claimant architecture

The strongest package remains coordinated but separate:

- **CASP Individualantrag:** direct norm addressee, exact incremental section 20(1) build and cost, section 1 DSG and Article 6 StGG, plus a decision-relevant Article 267 interpretation request.
- **User DSB/BVwG route:** actual processing, access/deletion record, Charter and DSG claims, and requests for Article 267 and Article 140 review by the court.

A joint narrative is useful for communications; a blended pleading is usually weaker because standing, alternative remedies, rights, evidence, and perimeter differ.
