# DAC8 Member-State Implementation and Litigation Map

> Implementation is widespread. The litigation search located no verified national judgment or CJEU DAC8 reference as of 27 August 2026; it was not comprehensively repeated for the September 4 implementation additions.

## France

France supplies the only concrete public challenge claim. Bull Bitcoin identifies a Conseil d'Etat annulment petition against Decree 2025-1276 and asserts over-transposition, procedural, and fundamental-rights grounds. Until an official court record or filed brief is obtained, the filing date, grounds, and posture remain party-reported.

An official order now confirms the litigation. On 14 September 2026 the Conseil d'État interim-relief judge (order n° 519158; applicants Paymium, Leonod and Satoshi Portal Inc.) refused to suspend the decree solely for lack of urgency, without examining legality or referring a question to the CJEU. The judge weighed the privacy interference against the public interest in preventing tax fraud and found a "very low" probability of breach; an alleged breach of EU law alone did not establish urgency. The merits request is pending. See the [order digest](../../raw/articles/2026-09-29-refreshat-conseil-etat-519158-dac8-suspension-refused.md).

The French action is useful for issue spotting and as adverse interim-relief authority, not a merits precedent or a substitute for Austrian standing analysis.

## Comparative use

Germany provides a timely statute with a financial-court route for authority measures. The Netherlands, Belgium, and Luxembourg enacted in 2026; delayed transposition may create domestic questions but does not by itself invalidate DAC8.

Every Article 267 path still needs a live national case and a decision-relevant EU-law question. Procedural routes differ by jurisdiction and should not be transplanted to Austria without local authority.

Retention, reportable-user drafting, pre-filing information and correction mechanisms differ in ways that are useful for necessity analysis. Those features and their limits are maintained in the separate [[dac8-member-state-implementation-comparators|DAC8 Member-State Implementation Comparators]] reference rather than treating this status table as proof of a uniformly more privacy-protective national system.
