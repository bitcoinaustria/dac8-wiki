# Member-State Challenge and Communications Playbook

> Reuse the method, not the Austrian conclusion. Every country needs its own text, claimant, remedy, and evidence file.

## Deliverable per country

Create one four-part dossier. Do not start with slogans or copy an Austrian petition.

### 1. EU baseline

Record the mandatory floor before calling anything national gold-plating:

- reporting provider and reportable-user definitions;
- annual identity and transaction-aggregate fields;
- due diligence and self-certification;
- registration, routing, exchange, notice, correction, and security rules;
- Directive storage corridor and permitted uses;
- MiCA authorisation, home-state supervision, passporting, and Article 68(9) records;
- TFR transfer information, self-hosted-address checks, AML/CFT purpose limitation, and retention.

For each proposition mark it as **EU required**, **EU permitted/discretionary**, or **not found in EU text**.

### 2. National delta

Map the enacted text and all operative instruments:

- law, regulations, decrees, circulars, guidance, technical schema, and commencement;
- whether all customers undergo only status identification or full transaction aggregation;
- data for reportable, non-reportable, and excluded users;
- raw records versus annual aggregates;
- retention for transmitted, non-transmitted, due-diligence, backup, and audit material;
- domestic access, onward use, correction, and deletion;
- registration, blocking, suspension, and sanctions;
- duplicate-reporting relief and MiCA home/host allocation;
- legal privilege and protected-category rules;
- explanatory materials, impact assessment, and asserted alternatives.

Then classify every delta:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Transposition | national wording needed to implement an EU command |
| Choice | discretion expressly left by EU law, such as a period within a corridor |
| Addition | obligation not required by the Directive text |
| Practice | provider or authority behaviour not clearly required by enacted law |
| Unknown | text, interpretation, or implementation evidence still missing |

An addition is not automatically unlawful. It becomes a claim only after the national constitution, Charter scope, GDPR legal basis, necessity, safeguards, and remedy are tested.

### 3. Claimant and route

Build a separate row for every claimant type:

| Candidate | Required facts | Possible route | Typical danger |
|---|---|---|---|
| home-state CASP | authorisation, direct statutory duty, implementation work, sanctions | direct norm review where available; otherwise administrative or enforcement route | costs belong mostly to MiCA/TFR/EU minimum |
| foreign-home CASP | passport status, actual national demand, duplication | authority determination and administrative review | national law may already exempt other-state reporting |
| non-reportable user | residence/status, provider, actual processing, access/deletion record | DPA and administrative/judicial appeal | status or processing is only hypothetical |
| reportable user | actual activity and mandatory report | DPA/tax route and EU-validity reference request | strong adverse tax-cooperation authority |
| association | its own statutory injury or procedural representative standing | jurisdiction-specific | public interest alone is not individual standing |
| protected professional/source | actual confidential relationship and revealing fields | DPA, professional, or constitutional route | DAC8 aggregates may not reveal the protected relationship |

For each country answer:

- Is direct constitutional review available to individuals or companies?
- Is it subsidiary to a DPA, tax, civil, licensing, or administrative route?
- Can a claimant obtain a reviewable decision without breaking the law?
- Which court can refer under Article 267?
- What interim relief, deletion, correction, annulment, damages, or declaration can that body actually grant?
- Which court is last instance, and what reasons must it give if it does not refer?

The litigation request must match the remedy. An EU-validity argument does not turn a national court into the body that can invalidate a Directive; Foto-Frost reserves that result to the CJEU.

### 4. Evidence and remedy

Minimum evidence bundle:

- exact legal text and version history;
- data-flow and recipient map;
- field-level EU-versus-national comparison;
- privacy notice, ROPA, DPIA, retention/deletion and backup rules;
- data-subject access, correction, restriction, objection, and deletion responses;
- actual non-reportable and reportable claimant files;
- home-/host-state and duplicate-reporting positions in writing;
- invoices, FTEs, vendors, storage, security, and engineering deltas;
- incident, threat, chilling, exclusion, and de-risking evidence with methodology;
- one tested less intrusive design;
- precise relief and the institution capable of granting it.

## Common EU legal modules

These can be maintained centrally and adapted:

- Charter Articles 7, 8, 47, and 52(1);
- GDPR purpose limitation, minimisation, storage limitation, legal-basis precision, security, DPIA, and rights;
- Article 8 ECHR for financial data;
- Foto-Frost, Consorzio, KUBERA, and CJEU reference recommendations;
- adverse tax authorities: DAC2/CRS national cases, C-623/22, G.S.B., and Othymia;
- strict-proportionality comparators with their limits: Digital Rights Ireland, PNR, communications retention, and public-register cases.

Do not transplant national constitutional rights, standing rules, challenge perimeters, filing deadlines, or remedies.

## AML message guardrail

For every country, timestamp the AML claim. TFR applies now; most AMLR duties apply from 10 July 2027; recast AMLD6 requires national implementation; BARIS interconnection follows by 10 July 2029. Verify the national implementation before describing a future EU rule as a current domestic duty.

The reusable accurate lines are: self-custody is not prohibited; CASP involvement triggers defined traceability and risk controls; and BARIS concerns covered institution-held account relationships, not every self-hosted wallet. Where the campaign alleges a register-like danger, identify the exact fields, access tier, linkages, retention, and remedies.

## Research quality gates

Before promoting a country claim:

1. full current law obtained from an official source;
2. implementation date and version verified;
3. EU baseline and national addition shown side by side;
4. claimant has current facts, not only plans;
5. all ordinary routes audited;
6. strongest adverse decision summarized fairly;
7. factual harm has a source and causal limit;
8. requested remedy removes the alleged defect;
9. local counsel has reviewed standing, deadline, and procedure;
10. public message does not claim more data than the schema contains.

## Communications kit per country

Maintain four separate public layers:

### Explainer

- what is collected, by whom, for which period;
- what is transmitted and what remains at the provider;
- which parts are EU-wide and which are national;
- rights, requests, correction, and complaint routes.

### Case page

- claimant role without unnecessary personal detail;
- exact challenged national addition or EU question;
- current procedural stage and official docket/source;
- relief requested;
- strongest State counterargument and short answer.

### Risk page

- profiling, linkage, breach, physical safety, chilling, de-risking, competition, and accountability;
- each item labelled legal fact, documented concern, supported risk, or policy hypothesis;
- transparent methodology and correction log.

### Reform page

- reportable-user-first aggregation;
- shorter justified retention and clear deletion for never-reported data;
- purpose and recipient limits;
- access logging, correction propagation, independent oversight, and breach safeguards;
- targeted requests where they can achieve the same result;
- cost and competition safeguards for smaller providers.

## Reusable public narrative

> This is not a campaign against paying tax. It asks whether routine crypto reporting is limited to what is necessary, aimed at people who are actually reportable, kept only as long as justified, protected against reuse and breach, and subject to correction and independent review. Where a country goes beyond the EU baseline, it should prove why the additional intrusion is needed.

## Claims register

Every campaign assertion should have:

- exact wording;
- jurisdiction and date;
- evidence level;
- primary source;
- owner and review date;
- litigation-safe version;
- public short version;
- known caveat or rebuttal.

This prevents an advocacy shortcut in one country from becoming an alleged legal fact in another.
