# DAC8 Bulk-Data Case Transfer Matrix

> No authority establishes that general tax reporting is unlawful merely because it is automatic, population-wide or suspicionless. The case turns on the exact data, inference capacity, lifecycle, safeguards, review and narrower alternatives.

## Transfer matrix

| Authority | Holding and adverse point | Narrow principle usable for DAC8 | Evidence required | Remedy direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *L.B. v Hungary* | General tax measures can be justified without proving that no milder rule could work. The violation arose from inadequate legislative examination of added worldwide publication, address data and misuse risk. | Test the added measure, each materially revealing field, audience, misuse risk and legislative examination. | Austrian and EU reasons for the challenged addition; actual field and audience; narrower design. | Remove or condition the added field or processing step, not tax reporting as such. |
| *Digital Rights Ireland* | Invalidated general communications-data retention because of combined coverage, inference, access, duration and security defects; accepted that retained data can contribute to investigations. | A legitimate objective and some utility do not end strict-necessity review when the combined interference is severe. | DAC8-specific inference and access proof; field, recipient, duration and safeguard map. | Field, access or retention limitation; system invalidity only if the defect is inseparable. |
| *Tele2/Watson* | General traffic and location retention was unlawful in the ePrivacy setting; targeted retention and strict access controls remained possible. | Treat collection, retention, access and later use as separate interferences. | Actor-by-actor access, purpose, notice, review and retention evidence. | Stage-specific restriction or safeguard. |
| *La Quadrature* C-511/18 | Differentiated traffic, location, IP and identity data; some general identity retention can be permissible. | Classify DAC8 fields by actual inference capacity instead of treating all data alike. | Reproducible join and inference test by field group. | Narrow the revealing group or prevent the join. |
| *SpaceNet* | Short duration and security did not cure general retention of data that intrinsically revealed precise private-life information. | Duration is one factor; data category and inference capability may dominate. | Demonstration that the challenged aggregate supports precise conclusions in the real system. | Field or linkage limitation, not a duration-only claim. |
| *Ligue des droits humains* | Systematic PNR processing survived through narrow interpretation; initial general retention could stand while later indiscriminate retention could not. False matches did not negate suitability but required review and safeguards. | Universal intake is not automatically invalid. Later storage, automated use, human review and auditability need independent justification. | Error rates, consequence path, human review, criteria, logs and later-retention basis. | Limiting interpretation, review safeguard or later-retention restriction. |
| *Big Brother Watch* | Bulk interception is not prohibited as such; violations followed from identified end-to-end control defects. | Examine the full processing chain and foreign-transfer safeguards. | Selection, access, use, forwarding, storage, deletion and independent-control facts. | Correct identified lifecycle defects. |
| *Centrum for rattvisa* | Most of a detailed bulk regime survived; cumulative destruction, foreign-transfer and non-binding-review defects caused the violation. | Cross-border protection, deletion and binding review are distinct requirements. | Recipient safeguards, onward use, deletion, reasons and binding remedial power. | Recipient, destruction or review correction. |
| *Gaughran* | Indefinite undifferentiated biometric retention failed; current technical search capability and genuine review mattered. Another State's milder system did not by itself decide the case. | Test present linkage technology and actual necessity review, not labels or abstract future risk. | Current provider and authority join capability; review and deletion practice. | Retention review or differentiated duration. |
| *S. and Marper* | Indefinite blanket biometric retention failed; the cited statistics did not establish the marginal need for the challenged breadth. | Ask for the added effectiveness of the challenged field or duration, not proof that the regime has no utility. | Counterfactual performance, false positives, existing-source overlap and lifecycle controls. | Remove the unproved marginal breadth while preserving core reporting. |
| *Ferrieri and Bonassisa* | Tax access can be legitimate and reviewed ex post, but the available review must be sufficiently certain, timely, independent and binding. | A merely possible later tax dispute is not enough; the actual DSB/BVwG route and its powers must be shown. | Present processing, review timing, jurisdiction, binding order and pre-transfer practical effect. | Effective supervisory or judicial review. |
| *Schecke and Eifert* | Named subsidy payments could not be published globally without adequate examination of less intrusive forms and differentiation. | Legislative consideration of a concrete narrower design matters. Public disclosure remains a major distinction. | Materials, alternative design and consequences of the exact disclosure audience. | Partial field or publication limitation. |
| *Schrems I and II* | In the third-country context, protection must work in practice and independent review and binding redress must remain available. | Test implementation and review, not formal safeguards alone. Intra-EU tax exchange is not an adequacy case. | Actual access, transfer, oversight, complaint and remedial practice. | Safeguard, transfer or remedy correction. |

## Pleading sequence

1. Accept tax transparency and equal taxation as legitimate objectives.
2. Identify the exact field, processing stage, controller and requested relief.
3. Prove actual inference and join capability rather than use a surveillance label.
4. Show the missing link between that field or stage and the stated tax task.
5. Establish an equally effective narrower design with a common test set.
6. Address the State's strongest authorities: DAC2 in Austria, C-623/22 on DAC6 and *L.B.* on general tax measures.

## Claims the authorities do not support

- general or suspicionless tax reporting is unlawful as such;
- annual DAC8 aggregates equal communications traffic, location or biometric data without an inference demonstration;
- every field requires its own published statistical study;
- individual suspicion or judicial pre-authorisation is automatically required;
- a shorter foreign retention period alone proves Austria's period disproportionate;
- a referral, remittal or declaratory violation automatically stops provider reporting.
