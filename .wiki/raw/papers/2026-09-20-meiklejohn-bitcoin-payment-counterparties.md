# A Fistful of Bitcoins: Characterizing Payments Among Men with No Names

Meiklejohn et al., IMC 2013, DOI 10.1145/2504730.2504747. Official conference PDF inspected; 13 pages. Historical measurement, not a current population success rate.

Sections 3.1 and 4.1, PDF pp. 4–6, describe controlled service interactions and distinguish custodial address control from underlying customer ownership. Section 5.2, Table 2, PDF p. 11, traces three paths for 100 hops each to labelled exchanges, gambling services and vendors. The starting owner's identity remained unresolved.

This establishes additional counterparty-flow discovery from a transaction anchor. It does not identify every merchant behind a processor, purchase contents, sensitive personal traits or a representative everyday user's complete payment history. Labels and change-output assumptions require independent validation.
