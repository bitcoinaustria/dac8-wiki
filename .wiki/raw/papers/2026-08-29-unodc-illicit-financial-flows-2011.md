# UNODC illicit-financial-flows study 2011

The study estimated total criminal proceeds for 2009 at about 3.6 percent of global GDP and the amount available for laundering at about 2.7 percent. Its key finding stated that much less than one percent, probably about 0.2 percent, of proceeds laundered through the financial system was seized or frozen.

These are model-based historical orders of magnitude, not observed totals. The ratio does not cover all criminal proceeds and does not measure final confiscation; it must not be paraphrased as a current recovery or general AML failure rate.
