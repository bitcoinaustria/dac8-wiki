# Crypto-wallet breach: full-text clarification

Svetlana Abramova and Rainer Böhme, *Anatomy of a High-Profile Data Breach: Dissecting the Aftermath of a Crypto-Wallet Case*, USENIX Security 2023, pp. 715–732. [Publisher record](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity23/presentation/abramova).

## Findings and limits

- **Recruitment, pp. 719–721:** approximately 31,632 invitations; 193 questionnaire visits, 186 consents and 104 retained responses. The authors expressly reject population representativeness.
- **Reports, pp. 722–723, Figures 3–4:** 93 respondents reported scams/phishing, 33 ransom threats and two physical-security threats. Thirty expressed moderate-to-extreme physical-safety concerns. These are not verified completed physical assaults.
- Ten reported financial losses after July 2020; only four themselves considered a breach connection likely or very likely. Timing and respondent attribution do not establish causation.
- **Limitations, pp. 726–727:** nonresponse, coverage, recall, unverifiable responses and other breaches limit inference. Neither the invitation population nor the 104 respondents supplies a representative attack-incidence estimate.

The study supports existence of reported downstream harms after customer-contact disclosure. It does not test tax reporting, annual aggregates or ledger reidentification.
