# Cong, Tang and Zhang: verified evidence scope correction

The publisher abstract and section 7 of *How transparency shapes tax policy effectiveness: Evidence from cryptocurrency markets*, Research Policy 55(1), 2026, support exchange-price responses to expected tax-information transmission. The US estimate is an average 0.34 percent decline following announcements. Cross-sectional tax-price sensitivity is strongest at exchanges combining KYC with transmission to tax authorities. This is not a separately identified causal effect of KYC alone.

The earlier [source record](2026-09-04-cong-tang-zhang-crypto-tax-transparency.md) also described short-window on-chain flows. No pinpoint supporting that assertion was verified in this audit; it must not be reused as established evidence. This is a verification limit, not a finding that the full paper disproves it.
