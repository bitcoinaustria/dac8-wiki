# Government linkage methodology: match, link and agreement

Doidge, Christen and Harron, original government methodology chapter, updated16July2021. Locators §§2.2–2.4,3.1–3.6,4.

A match is the true relationship, a link its assigned classification, and agreement observed similarity. Ranking weights need stronger assumptions to become probabilities. Clerical review can estimate precision more readily than recall; reference standards may be erroneous or unrepresentative. Report unlinked records, subgroup errors and sensitivity. This is methodological guidance, not a new empirical attack rate or Austrian finding.
