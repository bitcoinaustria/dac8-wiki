# Rechnungshof Bund 2023/26: Criminal tax matters in the tax administration

**What it is.** Austrian Court of Audit report, Reihe Bund 2023/26 (III-1022 d.B. XXVII. GP). The audit ran from October 2021 to June 2022 and covered mainly 2017–2021 (TZ 1). The relevant passage is TZ 31, "Risikoorientierte Bearbeitungsreihung" (risk-based prioritisation of cases), pp. 93–95.

**Findings.**

- No analyses of criminal-tax assessments existed. Such analyses "könnten z.B. durch das Predictive Analytics Competence Center (PACC) erfolgen, wurden jedoch bisher nicht angefordert" (could be carried out, for example, by PACC, but had not yet been requested; TZ 31.1).
- The Office for Fraud Prevention (ABB) said "Eine Unterstützung durch eine elektronische Risikoanalyse als Grundlage für eine Auswahl von Strafverfahren bei Abgabenverkürzungen sei nicht notwendig" (electronic risk-analysis support for selecting criminal cases is not necessary). It added that there were no ministry requirements or resources for it.
- The Court records: "Die Finanzverwaltung setzte IT–Analysen bei der Auswahl der zugrunde liegenden Prüfmaßnahmen zur Fallauswahl ein" (the tax administration used IT analyses to select the underlying audit cases). PACC selection relied on finding codes ("Feststellungscodes").
- TZ 31.2 states: "Bei der Auswahl von Prüfmaßnahmen der Abgabenbehörden wurden hochentwickelte IT–Analysen zur Fallauswahl eingesetzt" (highly developed IT analyses were used to select tax-authority audits). The criminal-tax unit, by contrast, had no risk-based processing hints.
- The Court recommended applying PACC methods to criminal proceedings as well. The ministry said it would take up the recommendation, but stressed that only examination of the individual case and investigative steps under §§ 82 ff and 115 ff FinStrG establish the true facts (TZ 31.3).

**Evidence class and transfer.** This is an official audit, and the passage separates Court findings from ministry statements. It independently confirms that automated risk analysis drives the selection of tax audits. It also shows that, as of 2021–2022, criminal-tax prioritisation did not use PACC scoring. This fits the 2541/AB statement that the ABB uses no AI pattern recognition. The Court itself recommends *expanding* automated risk use into criminal proceedings. That is adverse to any claim that oversight bodies push back on scoring. The report does not discuss data sources, GDPR Art. 22 or exchanged data.
