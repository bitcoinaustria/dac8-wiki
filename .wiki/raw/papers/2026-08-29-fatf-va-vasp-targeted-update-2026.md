# FATF virtual-assets targeted update 2026

Among 109 responding jurisdictions, 91 reported Travel Rule legislation. Fifty-five of those 91 reported no findings, directives, enforcement or other supervisory action focused on Travel Rule compliance. Across 149 assessed jurisdictions, only one was rated fully compliant with Recommendation 15.

The survey is based substantially on jurisdiction self-reporting, was not independently verified, uses a question denominator smaller than the entire FATF network, treats nonresponses conservatively and uses some commercial blockchain-analysis data. Legislative adoption, technical compliance and supervisory activity do not demonstrate crime reduction, data quality or practical interoperability.
