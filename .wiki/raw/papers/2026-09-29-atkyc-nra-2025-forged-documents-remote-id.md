# NRA 2025: forged documents and remote identification

Bundesministerium für Finanzen, "Nationale Risikoanalyse 2025", status 14 May 2025, 294 pages. Page numbers are printed "n von 294" numbers. This digest covers only the passages on identity; the existing raw file `2026-08-27-austria-nra-fatf-2026.md` summarises the NRA overall.

**Document offences as predicate offences (p. 18).** In 2020–2023, on average 2 percent of money-laundering investigations involved document-forgery predicate offences. In 2023 the A-FIU identified possible document offences in about 4 percent of STRs. The passage continues:
- "Besonders häufig treten gefälschte Urkunden im Zusammenhang mit der Onlineeröffnung von Bankkonten mittels Video-Ident-Verfahren bzw. der qualifizierten elektronischen Signatur auf." (Forged documents occur particularly often in online bank-account opening via video identification or qualified electronic signature.)
- These procedures offer "eine große Angriffsfläche für KI-Technologien" (a large attack surface for AI technologies), especially where criminals hold stolen ID documents that can be fed to AI to fake video sequences of the holder.
- Mobile-phone contracts can also be concluded this way.
- "Je nach Qualität der eingesetzten Identifizierungsdienstleister ist das Online-Ident-Verfahren leichter oder schwerer zu überlisten." (Depending on the quality of the identification provider used, online identification is easier or harder to defeat.)
- Cases show that accounts opened with AI-generated false identities are used to receive and pass on fraud proceeds.
- The problem is aggravated because with an account once "identifiziert", further accounts at other banks can easily be opened via "SEPA-Verifizierung (1-Cent-Überweisung)" (a 1-cent SEPA verification transfer).

The tax-adviser sector chapter repeats the video-identification point (p. 201).

**Consumer credit (p. 177).** Risk is higher at institutions and intermediaries specialising in fast consumer credit, "insbesondere aufgrund des erhöhten Risikos der Verwendung gefälschter Identitäten" (in particular because of the higher risk of forged identities being used). Risks also arise where obliged entities increasingly rely on remote identification without taking this adequately into account.

**Crypto money mules (p. 63).** Fraudsters "regelmäßig" (regularly) use money mules with crypto exchanges. The mules are recruited through job offers, open accounts at well-known exchanges in their own names, receive funds in their bank accounts, convert them and transfer them onward.

Evidence class: official national risk assessment. It is a government synthesis of police statistics, STR data and authority input, and states risk judgements rather than measured prevalence. It is the strongest Austrian official statement that the QES and reference-payment routes, not only video identification, are being exploited, and that the reference-payment route lets one false identification spread across banks. The 2 percent and 4 percent shares measure predicate-offence frequency in ML cases and STRs, not the share of accounts opened fraudulently. The mule passage shows that a correctly KYC-verified exchange account (the real mule) may be used for third-party funds. For DAC8, this means that correct identity data does not guarantee that the reported user is the economic owner. That is an inference, not an NRA statement.
