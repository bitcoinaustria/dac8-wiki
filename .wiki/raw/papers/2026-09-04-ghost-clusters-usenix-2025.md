# Ghost Clusters

Lubbertsen, van Eeten and van Wegberg, USENIX Security 2025, evaluate one commercial provider against seized ground-truth data for three illicit services. Reported address coverage varies materially between services while false positives are low in those cases. Coverage also changes over time and a second provider produces different coverage.

This supports the existence of effective attribution for some seeded services, not universal identification of individual users. The study's three illicit-service cases, vendor-specific data and changing coverage prevent generalisation to a retail customer's wallet or to every chain.
