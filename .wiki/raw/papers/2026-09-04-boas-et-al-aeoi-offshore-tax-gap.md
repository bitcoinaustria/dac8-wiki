# Boas et al.: Taxing Capital in a Globalized World

Boas, Johannesen, Kreiner, Larsen and Zucman use the universe of foreign-bank information reports sent to Danish authorities, linked to population-wide income, wealth and cross-border transfer data. Their 2024 NBER working paper uses several research designs to separate repatriation, increased self-reporting and audit detection.

The authors estimate that automatic exchange of bank information closed about 70 percent of Denmark's offshore tax gap in the studied setting. The largest response came from repatriation of wealth.

This is strong adverse evidence to a general ineffectiveness claim. It concerns foreign bank-account reporting, Danish enforcement and offshore wealth. It does not test crypto reporting, DAC8's annual asset-and-category tuple, individual field ablation, security externalities or Austrian implementation choices.
