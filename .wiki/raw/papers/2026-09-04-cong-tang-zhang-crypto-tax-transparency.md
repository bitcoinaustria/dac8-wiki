# Cong, Tang and Zhang: Crypto tax transparency and market response

The 2026 *Research Policy* article studies exchange-level price differences and regulatory events involving KYC and third-party tax reporting. It reports an average 0.34 percent Bitcoin price decline after US announcements increasing expected information transmission and stronger sensitivity to statutory tax liabilities on exchanges combining KYC with tax-authority reporting.

Its event analysis also reports large short-window on-chain flow changes around the publication of CARF and selected national reporting events. The authors cannot trace the ultimate destination of the flows and interpret them as reallocation away from affected jurisdictions.

This provides initial crypto-specific evidence that expected reporting can affect behaviour. Price capitalisation and short-window flows are not taxes assessed, long-term compliance, field-level detection or proof of DAC8's effectiveness. The study supports a behavioural channel and a transparency-privacy trade-off, not the necessity of every DAC8 data element.
