# Steal, deal and repeat, IOCTA 2025

Selected digest, published 11 June 2025. Page 14, section 3.1, describes recruiting employees to exfiltrate data or install backdoors, buying current or former employees' credentials, and infiltrating employers with false applicant profiles. Page 11, section 2.2, identifies repeated targeting after private information is exposed.

Page 7 limits the methodology to Europol-visible investigations and expert inputs. This is an institutional threat assessment, not a representative incidence estimate or judicial finding. It does not establish a DAC8 breach or physical attack caused by financial information.
