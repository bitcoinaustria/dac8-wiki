# Investigating Wrench Attacks

Ordekian, Atondo-Siu, Hutchings and Vasek, AFT 2024, analyse reported physical attacks using news, forums and interviews. Documented target-selection channels include personal acquaintance, public prominence or self-exposure, investigation of assumed ownership and visible peer-to-peer transactions.

The study establishes that physical coercion against crypto holders is a real threat class. It did not identify a leaked tax or DAC8 dataset as a target-selection source. Its media-derived sample is subject to under-reporting and selection bias. It is therefore adverse to any claim that a tax-data leak has already been shown to cause such attacks.
