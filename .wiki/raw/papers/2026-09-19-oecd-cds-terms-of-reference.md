# CDS terms of reference

Selected verified digest, not a complete transcription. Publication date is not identified; a footnote's October 2024 reference is not treated as a publication date.

Paragraphs 1 and 4 address receipt conditions and the actual lifecycle and operational processes covered by assessment. Printed pages 4–5, paragraphs 9–14, specify governance, personnel controls, need-to-know and least-privilege access, classification, retention, destruction, monitoring, alerts, logging, incident management and internal/external audit. Paragraph 16 addresses investigation, remediation and partner notification.

These are assessment criteria, not evidence that a particular authority meets or fails them. They do not prescribe a CASP retention period or create an individual right to inspect confidential audits.
