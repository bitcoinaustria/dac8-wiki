# BMF Amt für Betrugsbekämpfung annual reports 2024 and 2025

**Sources.** "Jahresbericht 2024 Amt für Betrugsbekämpfung" (40 pp.; canonical URL above) and "Jahresbericht ABB 2025" (43 pp.): https://www.bmf.gv.at/dam/jcr:bb22ebfe-784d-47f5-9e82-23467916e588/Jahresbericht%20ABB%202025.pdf. Both are linked from the BMF "Berichte & Bilanzen" page and were read on 2026-09-29.

**DAC handling (2024 report, p. 12).** The ABB says the growth of international data exchange, such as platform-operator reports and money-laundering suspicion reports, challenges its capacity. It adds: "Wenngleich die Datenübermittlung der DAC-Meldungen automatisiert erfolgt, liegt die strafrechtliche Würdigung und die Beauftragung bzw. Durchführung von Ermittlungsmaßnahmen im Aufgabenportfolio der Mitarbeiterinnen und Mitarbeiter des ABB." In English: although DAC reports are transmitted automatically, criminal assessment and the ordering or conduct of investigative measures are tasks for ABB staff. The 2025 report (p. 10) summarises the BBKG 2025 as extending reporting duties for crypto service providers, "ergänzt durch einen verstärkten internationalen Datenaustausch" (supplemented by increased international data exchange).

**Statistics (activity tables, 2024 report p. 37; 2025 report p. 36).**

| Measure | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Tax investigation and audit measures, including risk analyses | 430 | 480 |
| House searches | 148 | 128 |
| Telephone surveillance | 9 | 4 |
| Account openings ("Kontoöffnungen") | 238 | 417 |
| Seized data volume (TB) | 159.6 | 92.5 |

**Evidence class and transfer.** These are institutional self-reports. They are adverse to a claim that exchanged DAC data trigger criminal measures automatically, because the ABB describes a human step between receipt and action. They say nothing about PACC scoring on the assessment side, and nothing about CRS or DAC8 feature use. The 2024 account-opening figure (238) differs from 2541/AB (260 for 2024). The counting units are probably different, but this is not verified.
