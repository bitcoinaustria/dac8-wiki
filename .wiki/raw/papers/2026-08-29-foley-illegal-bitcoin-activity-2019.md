# Illegal activity financed through Bitcoin

For Bitcoin activity through April 2017, the study associated approximately 46 percent of transaction count, 23 percent of transaction value and 26 percent of users with illegal activity.

The estimates concern an early Bitcoin-specific ecosystem and extrapolate from known illicit clusters. They are not current all-crypto market shares. Transaction count, transaction value and users are different units and must not be interchanged.
