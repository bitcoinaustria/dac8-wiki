# Cooper: Anonymity and Autonomy, PrivacyCon 2017 Presentation

James C. Cooper, FTC PrivacyCon, 12 January 2017; only this presentation's PDF pp. 2–10 was verified, not other panel papers. Unit is term×week×state Google Trends score, with 13,000–108,000 observations by window. Page 8 reports 5–10% sensitive-search decline in ±1/±3-month windows, no measurable ±6-month impact or state privacy-demand heterogeneity. Page 9 says the 2011 falsification test fails, 2013 passes. Page 10 cautions Trends is not search volume and omits other service margins. Project had no financial support; affiliated centre had donors including Google (p. 2). Final 2023 article DOI 10.1515/rle-2023-0042 was not fully accessible; slide coefficients are not its verified results. Mechanical credibility 1: recognised academic +1; not peer-reviewed evidence.
