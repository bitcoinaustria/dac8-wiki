# Karpa: Digital Surveillance and Self-Censorship in Kazakhstan

David Karpa, Political Behavior, 14 March 2026, DOI 10.1007/s11109-026-10122-1. November 2023 experiment: 5,025 qualifying completers from 28,201 initial participants; social-media recruitment with age/sex weights, not a probability sample. Method/Data and Results/Table 1: surveillance reminder increased sensitive-item nonresponse by 3.3 percentage points; privacy assurance +0.3 points, nonsignificant. Footnote 14/Table A2: protest item chiefly drove the overall result; sanctions item nonsignificant, invasion item only at 10% significance. Endpoint is an actual refused survey answer, not observed public protest or an Austrian effect. Assurance text is not a trial of technical privacy. Mechanical credibility 4: peer-reviewed +2, academic author/institution +1, recent +1.
