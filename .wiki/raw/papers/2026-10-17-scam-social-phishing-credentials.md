# Social Phishing: relationship context and credential authentication

Jagatic, Johnson, Jakobsson and Menczer. Author preprint dated 12 December 2005; final CACM2007, DOI10.1145/1290958.1290968. April2005 student field experiment; preprint pp.2–3, Table1.

349/487 apparent-acquaintance recipients versus15/94 unfamiliar-sender controls provided valid university authentication, approximately72% versus16%. Public relationships supplied the context. The paper does not clearly document randomized allocation.

Ages18–24 and the early university setting limit generalisation. The921-recipient total includes other conditions, including a flawed condition, and is not this comparison's denominator. No financial data leak, real theft or Austrian rate was tested. Mechanical credibility3: peer-reviewed+2, institution+1; foundational age exemption, not current prevalence.
