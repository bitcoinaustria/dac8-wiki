# From Suspicion to Action

Europol reported almost one million suspicious-transaction or similar reports to EU FIUs in 2014, with about ten percent further investigated. More than 65 percent of the reports came from the United Kingdom and the Netherlands.

Reporting thresholds, categories and FIU practices were not harmonised. A report not further investigated is not proven false: it may be duplicate, intelligence-relevant, below a case threshold or unexamined because of resources. The ten-percent figure is not a ten-percent precision rate.
