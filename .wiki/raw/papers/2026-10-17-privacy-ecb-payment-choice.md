# ECB SPACE 2024: Payment Choice, Privacy and Assistance

ECB, December 2024; methodology pp. 7–9, section 3.1/Chart 18, section 3.3.1/Chart 27 and Box 4 pp. 36–37. Weighted adult euro-area findings: 62% considered keeping cash important, 41% selected privacy/anonymity among cash's three main advantages, while 55% preferred cashless payments. Nine percent reported needing assistance with digital payments; reasons were not established. ECB collected 40,981 responses across 18 countries, supplemented by separate German/Dutch surveys; recruitment combined modes and probability/nonprobability components. These are attitudes and reported experiences, not measured safety effects or per-transaction failure rates. Avoid the inconsistent 58%/60% privacy-concern figure. Cash has risks and digital payments have benefits. Mechanical credibility 2: recognised institution +1, recent +1.
