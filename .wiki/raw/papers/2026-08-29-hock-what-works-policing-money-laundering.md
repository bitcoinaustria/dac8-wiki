# What works in policing money laundering?

The review screened a large, heterogeneous literature and found no study that met its minimum standard for a reliable correlation between an AML prevention programme and reduced money-laundering risk.

The result is evidence of an evaluation deficit. It does not establish that AML interventions have no operational or deterrent effect.
