# FTC: Data Brokers, Transparency and Accountability (2014)

FTC report, May 2014; nine selected US brokers, not a representative prevalence estimate. Executive summary pp. ii–v and chapters III, V, VII and VIII distinguish acquired facts, inferred segments and controls. Combined commercial/public inputs supported sensitive classifications; limited access and correction could leave a person unable to explain an adverse transaction outcome. Some segments operated at household or geographic rather than individual level. The report also recognises identity verification, fraud prevention and useful matching benefits. This historical institutional study does not establish present Austrian practice, accuracy of inferred traits or a causal price/loss estimate. Mechanical credibility 1: recognised regulator +1, old non-foundational empirical snapshot −1, no newness point; unique compulsory-document evidence retained at low tier. Selected substantive sections checked, not every appendix.
