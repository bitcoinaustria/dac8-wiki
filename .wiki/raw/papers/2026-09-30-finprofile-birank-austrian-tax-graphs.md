# BiRank and Austrian fiscal risk prediction

Göschlberger and Deliu, SNAMS 2021, DOI 10.1109/SNAMS53716.2021.9732111; author version posted 5 September 2022. PDF pp. 1–3, 5–6, sections IV, VI, IX and X.

The BMF-funded study, with a PACC coauthor, links company-register names and addresses to tax-administration identities. Director relationships from 1991–2021 become graphs combined with audit/statistical risk indicators. Ten-fold cross-validation reports precision 16.38% and recall 83.40% for the top 20,000 selection.

This is implemented historical, cross-source analysis, not proof of CRS/crypto inputs, wealth reconstruction or present production use. Labels partly come from statistical models, not exclusively independently confirmed fraud. The incremental contribution to final multi-model scoring and time-sliced comparisons remain future work. No causal revenue or necessity of thirty years is measured.

Scoring: peer-reviewed +2, recognised institutions +1; older than three years, no recency point. Disclosed BMF funding is relevant context, not independent validation.
