# International standards in practice

The study identifies increased activity immediately below USD 1,000 at British Virgin Islands exchanges following implementation, estimated at 1.1–7.6 percentage points depending on specification.

The design supports threshold adaptation and possible structuring. It does not show avoidance of originator/beneficiary data transfer, criminal purpose, that KYC or the Travel Rule is wholly ineffective, or an aggregate crime effect.
