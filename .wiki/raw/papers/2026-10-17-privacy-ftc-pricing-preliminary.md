# FTC staff: Surveillance Pricing Research Summaries (January 2025)

FTC staff perspective, January 2025, ten pages; pp. 1–2 disclaim completeness, representativeness and findings of illegality. Sections 1–4 describe tools spanning store-level pricing, consumer segmentation and personalised rankings, using transaction, browsing and other inputs. Some respondents disputed the characterisation of their tools. Section 5 reports vendor revenue/margin claims, not independently verified customer harm; definitive market impacts remain open. The examples are hypothetical. Read with the contemporaneous Ferguson/Holyoak dissent, not as unanimous findings or proof everyone pays more. The official feature page still links this preview; this bounded check does not establish the absence of later materials elsewhere. Mechanical credibility 2: recognised institution +1, recent +1; preliminary status lowers conclusion confidence.
