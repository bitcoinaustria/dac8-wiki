# Ganta2008: composition of independently anonymised releases

Ganta, Kasiviswanathan and Smith, KDD2008; author manuscript v2, 31 March 2008. Locators §§2.1,3.1–3.2,3.6; Figures1–2.

Adult30,162 and IPUMS70,187 records; independently anonymised samples overlap by5,000 people. At k1=k2=5, singleton sensitive-value intersections occur for about12% and60%, respectively. Correct group locatability is assumed; a mentioned centroid heuristic located about70%. Model-defined inverse candidate-set confidence is not calibrated identity probability. Larger groups reduce effectiveness; extra-release gains can be limited. No DAC8 fields or changing financial attributes were tested.
