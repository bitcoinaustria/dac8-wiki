# Gmail IMC2020: prior breach exposure and targeting odds

Simoiu, Zand, Thomas and Bursztein, ACM IMC2020, DOI10.1145/3419394.3423617. Locators §§3.2–3.5,5.1–5.2, printed pp.568–572.

April–August 2020: 1.2 billion detected phishing/malware emails. Weekly models compare targeted users with a 10% random baseline sample. Prior breach-corpus inclusion has average adjusted targeting odds ratio5.20.

This is observational receipt/detection, not loss risk or a causal breach coefficient. Weeks are not independent unique-person follow-up. Classifier coverage, online footprint, coarsening and the COVID period limit transfer. Academic/vendor collaboration and proprietary detection are disclosed; no financial wealth is measured. Mechanical credibility 2: peer-reviewed+2, institution+1, vendor-primary framing−1.
