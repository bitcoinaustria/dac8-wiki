# Netflix2008: record linkage, abstention and absent targets

Narayanan and Shmatikov, IEEE S&P2008, DOI10.1109/SP.2008.33. Locators §4,§5 pp.8–13 and Figure5.

100,480,507 ratings from480,189 pseudonymous subscribers. Eight ratings with specified noise/date assumptions recover about99% of released records in controlled tests. Approximately50 IMDb profiles yield two strong links without an independent identity oracle. The scoring method can abstain; Figure5 tests absent targets. Tuning, membership and auxiliary-data assumptions constrain transfer. This is neither99% verified real identities nor validation of annual financial-aggregate linkage.

Mechanical credibility 3; assessed independently from the research agent. Historical primary material is accepted for the bounded mechanism, not current prevalence. No underlying personal dataset was obtained.
