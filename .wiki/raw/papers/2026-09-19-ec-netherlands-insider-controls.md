# SWD(2025) 919 final, 8 July 2025

Selected extraction, printed pages 8–9. The report describes a police programme detecting unauthorised database access. Footnote 69 notes systems unable to trace the identity of unauthorised accessors. It reports 15 information-leakage and 20 public-authority corruption investigations in 2024, citing the prosecution's report in footnotes 70–71.

The same section describes effective corruption investigation and prosecution and additional protection measures. These are historical institutional observations, not evidence of current Austrian defects, final convictions in those investigations, or resulting attacks.
