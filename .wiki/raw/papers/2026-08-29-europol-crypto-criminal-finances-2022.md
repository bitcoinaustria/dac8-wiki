# Europol crypto criminal-finance spotlight 2022

Europol documents use of crypto across multiple offence types and its growing operational importance. It nevertheless describes crypto as a limited share of criminal finances compared with cash and other methods and stresses measurement difficulty.

The report provides an official qualitative assessment and case typologies, not a current denominator-based estimate of the EU criminal economy.
