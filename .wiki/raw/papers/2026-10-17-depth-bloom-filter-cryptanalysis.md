# Basic Bloom-filter cryptanalysis: encoding is not confidentiality

Niedermeyer et al., Journal of Privacy and Confidentiality6(2),2014, pp.59–79. Locators §§4–5, printedpp.64–70; limits §§6–7.

10,000 sampled surnames produce7,580 distinct basic Bloom-filter encodings. Analysts decipher934 recurrent distinct filters using known construction, surname statistics and substantial manual work. This is not observed recovery of all records. Surname recovery is not unique-person attribution; extension to modern PPRL or cryptography generally is unsupported. Attack is passive on possessed encodings, not evidence of acquisition or Austrian query access.

Mechanical credibility 2; assessed independently from the research agent. Historical primary material is accepted for the bounded mechanism, not current prevalence. No underlying personal dataset was obtained.
