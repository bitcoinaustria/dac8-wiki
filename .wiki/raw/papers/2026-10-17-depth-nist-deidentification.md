# NIST188: lifecycle and sharing-model de-identification

SP800-188 final14September2023. Official full PDF text, especially §§3.3–3.4, printedpp.31–34, and §4.

Evaluate retained originals, identifying maps, auxiliary datasets and lifecycle placement. Security, privacy and disassociability are distinct. Sharing models include public release, agreements, synthetic data/validation and protected query enclaves. Even vetted outputs can leak when combined; periodic releases require cumulative assessment. This is government data-release guidance, not a guarantee of anonymity, an Austrian legal rule or proof of equivalent tax utility.

Mechanical credibility 1; assessed independently from the research agent. Historical primary material is accepted for the bounded mechanism, not current prevalence. No underlying personal dataset was obtained.
