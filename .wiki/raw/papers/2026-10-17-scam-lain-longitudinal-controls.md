# Lain2022: repeated phishing exposure and randomized controls

Lain, Kostiainen and Čapkun, IEEE S&P2022; accessible author manuscript v1. Locators §§III-C–III-G,V-B,VI.

14,733 employees, eight campaigns over15months, randomized warning/training/feedback configurations. Clicks:6,680/117,864 messages versus4,729/14,733 people ever clicking. Dangerous actions:4,885 messages versus3,747 people;896/3,747 ever-action people repeated one.

Warnings reduced engagement; embedded post-failure training did not improve subsequent detection in this implementation. One employer and simulations limit transportability; no breach attribution, individually researched spear campaign or actual losses. Training is not universally disproved. Mechanical credibility3: peer-reviewed+2, institution+1, older than3years.
