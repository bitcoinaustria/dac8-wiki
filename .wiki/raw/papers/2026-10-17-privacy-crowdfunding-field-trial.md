# Burtch, Ghose and Wattal: Crowdfunder Privacy Preferences

Management Science 61(5):949–962 (2015), DOI 10.1287/mnsc.2014.2069; full author preprint dated 19 August 2014. Fourteen days: 128,701 contribution-flow visitors, 37,328 contributions. Treatment delayed controls for hiding identity/amount from the public until after payment; it did not remove them or change platform/organiser visibility. Section 2 and Tables 3–5: adjusted completion +4.9 percentage points, conditional contribution −USD 5.81, unconditional +USD 3.55 per visitor. Conditional estimates compare selected contributor pools, not the same individuals. Timing bundles privacy salience and friction. This is not proof confidentiality always raises participation or political donations. Mechanical credibility 2: peer-reviewed +2, recognised academics +1, historical non-landmark empirical snapshot −1.
