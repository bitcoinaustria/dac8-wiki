# Austria NRA 2025 and FATF evaluation 2026

Austria's 2025 NRA identifies sector-specific money-laundering, terrorist-financing, and proliferation-financing risks and repeatedly anticipates AMLR application from 10 July 2027. The FATF evaluation, adopted in February and published in April 2026, found strong beneficial-ownership infrastructure and improved data-driven FMA supervision of financial institutions and virtual-asset providers.

The same report found only moderate effectiveness in financial intelligence, money-laundering prosecutions, and asset recovery; insufficient FIU, law-enforcement, and prosecution resources; few complex cross-border cases; and low confiscation effectiveness. It therefore supports a serious public interest while undercutting any claim that broader data acquisition automatically produces effective enforcement.
