# Crypto-wallet breach aftermath

Source digest of the publisher's abstract only, not a full-paper extraction. Svetlana Abramova and Rainer Böhme, Universität Innsbruck, USENIX Security 2023, pp. 715–732.

The authors surveyed 104 customers affected by a hardware-wallet customer-data breach. They report spam, scams, phishing, possible further attack vectors, some self-reported crypto losses and heightened safety concerns.

This is peer-reviewed victim-experience evidence. The abstract does not establish representative incidence, causal attribution of every reported event, completed physical attacks or Austrian/DAC8 risk estimates. No numerical finding beyond the sample size is extracted here.
