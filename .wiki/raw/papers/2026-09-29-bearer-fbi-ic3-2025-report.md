# FBI IC3 2025 annual report

Source digest. Federal Bureau of Investigation, Internet Crime Complaint Center, *2025 IC3 Annual Report* (published 2026), 66 pp. Locators are printed page numbers: p. 7 (overview); pp. 17–18 ("IC3 Recovery Asset Team – Financial Fraud Kill Chain"); pp. 52–55 ("Complaints Involving Cryptocurrency", "Crime Types with Cryptocurrency Nexus"); Appendix B definitions; Appendix C data caveats.

**Headline figures.** 1,008,597 complaints and $20.877bn in reported losses. Complaints with a crypto nexus: 181,565, with $11.366bn in losses (up 22% on 2024) and an average loss of $62,604; 18,589 complainants lost more than $100,000. Crypto losses among those aged 60+ were $4.432bn. Crypto investment fraud: 61,559 complaints, $7.228bn. Crypto ATM/kiosk use: 13,460 complaints, $389m. Recovery scams: 10,516 complaints, $1.4bn.

**Crime types with a crypto nexus.** Extortion 23,797 complaints; personal data breach 13,486; SIM swap 121; threats of violence 210.

**Freezing contrast.** Through the Financial Fraud Kill Chain, the IC3 Recovery Asset Team handled 3,900 incidents with $1.164bn in attempted theft and froze $679m (a 58% success rate). The process works through financial institutions: victims are told to "request a recall of the funds". Only 64 of these incidents involving victims aged 60+ were investment/crypto frauds.

Evidence class: official statistics, but based on complaints and self-reported. Appendix C warns that classification may change, that duplicates exist and that age is optional. The report is US-only and undercounts, because not all victims report. "Cryptocurrency" means only that the complaint refers to virtual currency. Most losses come from fraud in which the victim authorised the transfer, not from theft of keys or physical coercion.

Supports: crypto-denominated fraud losses are large and concentrated among older victims. Bank-channel freezing recovers a material share of attempted thefts through intermediaries. The report does not state a comparable freeze rate for self-custodied crypto. Does not support: any European or DAC8-specific incidence, or a measured effect of leaked data on losses.
