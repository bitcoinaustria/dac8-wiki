# Blockchain analysis of the Bitcoin market

The working paper estimated that illegal, scam and gambling activity together represented less than three percent of Bitcoin volume in 2020. Exchange and service activity dominated its transaction-volume denominator.

The difference from earlier high illicit-share estimates illustrates denominator, classification and period sensitivity. It is a working paper using attribution methods, not a definitive current all-crypto crime rate.
