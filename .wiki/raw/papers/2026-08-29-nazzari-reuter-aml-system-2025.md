# How Well Does the Money Laundering Control System Work?

Nazzari and Reuter review evidence on reporting, investigation, confiscation, costs, errors and exclusion. They distinguish visible outputs such as reports and cases from the ultimate outcome of reduced laundering and predicate crime.

The article is a critical narrative synthesis rather than a causal meta-analysis. It supports caution about official success metrics while preserving evidence that AML information assists particular investigations.
