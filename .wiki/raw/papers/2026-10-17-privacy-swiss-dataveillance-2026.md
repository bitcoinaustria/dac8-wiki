# Festic et al.: Dataveillance Inhibits Legitimate Communication

Festic, Büchi, Latzer and Odermatt, Journal of Communication 76(4):277–289, 5 May 2026, DOI 10.1093/joc/jqag004. Preregistered six-week study: 827 German-speaking Swiss online participants, randomisation at 19 prompts. Measures: comfortable search plus submitted link; hypothetical opinion-sharing comfort; answering/refusing a personal question. Within-person results: opinion comfort −0.17/5; search OR 0.87; disclosure OR 0.77. Odds ratios are not percentage-point changes. Two topics with failed manipulation were excluded; secondary between-person opinion/disclosure associations were null and noncausal. Methods/Measures, manipulation checks and within-/between-person Results control interpretation. Task prompting, outcome construction and transfer to durable conduct remain limitations.
