# Europol criminal asset recovery 2016

For 2010–2014, Europol estimated about EUR 2.4 billion per year frozen or seized and EUR 1.2 billion finally confiscated, corresponding to approximately 2.2 and 1.1 percent of estimated criminal proceeds.

National responses were incomplete and heterogeneous and the proceeds denominator was modelled. The result identifies an asset-recovery gap; it does not directly measure deterrence, prevention or the independent effect of KYC.
