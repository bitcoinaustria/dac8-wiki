# FATF gray-listing and capital flows

Using quarterly data for 89 countries from 2000–2017 and a double-selection method, the paper estimated a decline in capital inflows of about 7.6 percent of GDP following gray-listing.

List placement is not random and relatively few observations were treated. The result is evidence of market response to listing, not a direct measurement of AML effectiveness or a FATF-imposed legal sanction.
