# FMA annual report 2025: AML and CASP data

FMA Jahresbericht 2025, presented 2 June 2026. Printed page numbers are used.

**CASP market data (pp. 36–37).** These are the first MiCAR reporting data.
- Eight CASPs were licensed by the FMA by 31 December 2025, four of them operational. 66 EEA CASPs were passported into Austria.
- 6.7 million users were registered with Austrian-licensed CASPs. Of these, 3.0 million (45 percent) "hatten den KYC-Prozess vollständig abgeschlossen" (had fully completed KYC). About 1.0 million were active (traded in the year): 32 percent of fully KYC-identified users, or 14 percent of all registrations.
- Fully KYC-identified users by country: 25 percent Germany, 18 percent Austria, 17 percent France. The FMA cautions that no reliable total of Austrian users can be derived, because of possible double counting and use of foreign CASPs.
- Client crypto-assets held in custody were over EUR 4.4 billion (Bitcoin 47 percent). Exchange volume was over EUR 17 billion.
- The FMA calls these early data points from a young market.

**AML supervision (pp. 74–75).**
- 20 AML on-site inspections: 12 at banks, 3 at virtual-currency providers, 2 at insurers, 2 at investment firms, 1 at a payment institution.
- 15 on-site reviews, 2 of them at virtual-currency providers, and ten management meetings.
- Main themes: risk classification, ongoing monitoring and updating of customer data.
- 117 AML proceedings: 69 investigation proceedings (2024: 78), 7 remedial proceedings (12) and 41 penalty proceedings (44). The FMA attributes the fall to stricter risk-based handling, earlier document review in licensing, and reorganisation.
- Supervision of financial sanctions moved from the OeNB and the Interior Ministry to the FMA under SanktG 2024 (from 1 January 2026).
- The FMA cites FATF's April 2026 "substantial" rating for the financial sector (press release, 2 June 2026).

**Sanctions overall (p. 87).** Across all supervisory areas, the FMA issued 47 penalty decisions in 2025 (2024: 61), with fines totalling EUR 2,674,900. The highest single fine was EUR 460,000. There is no AML-only total in the passage read. The press release adds that the BVwG and the Supreme Administrative Court (VwGH) did not once rule against FMA decisions on the merits in 2025. It also says firms are piloting AI in fraud prevention, identification processes and AML.

Evidence class: official supervisory statistics. The 45 percent KYC-completion ratio is a data point on account-state heterogeneity. More than half of registered CASP accounts had not completed KYC. The data do not say whether such accounts can transact, nor whether "fully identified" accounts belong to the real person. For DAC8, this matters because reporting covers identified crypto-asset users. The population of registered but unidentified accounts, and the accuracy of the identified population, are distinct unknowns. That is an inference, not an FMA statement. No figure on remote-identification fraud is given.
