# Baselgia: Compliance effects of automatic exchange in Switzerland

The 2026 *Journal of Public Economics* study uses Swiss administrative tax data and difference-in-differences designs around the 2017 multilateral AEOI introduction and the permanent tax-amnesty mechanism.

The paper attributes approximately 107,000 amnesty entries, about 2 percent of taxpayers, and CHF 37.6 billion of disclosed hidden wealth, more than 5.5 percent of GDP, to AEOI. Participants increased reported wealth by about 50 percent on average, with persistence in the medium run.

The outcome combines AEOI with Switzerland's amnesty and enforcement setting. It does not identify the incremental value of any DAC8 field, cover crypto-asset data, or balance revenue against privacy and security burdens.
