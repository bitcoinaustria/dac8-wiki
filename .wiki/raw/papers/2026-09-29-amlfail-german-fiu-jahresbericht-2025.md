# German FIU Jahresbericht 2025

Financial Intelligence Unit (Generalzolldirektion), annual report 2025, published 21 July 2026. Locators: "FIU in Zahlen 2025" (Abb. 1), chapter 1 subsections "Gesamtmeldeaufkommen" and "Staatsanwaltschaftliche Rückmeldungen", and Annex A.1.3–A.1.4 (Tabelle 2).

Volume: 374,693 STRs in 2025, about 41 percent more than 2024 (265,708) and containing about 3.2 million transactions; credit institutions filed 319,857. Digital financial service providers ("Neobanken") rose from about 13 percent of reports in 2022 to 29 percent (about 108,000) in 2025; the FIU links this partly to digital onboarding and fraud exposure and names crypto-related anomalies as a secondary reporting reason for that segment. The FIU disseminated 67,024 analysis reports and ordered 106 immediate measures (Sofortmaßnahmen) securing about EUR 751.8 million, of which EUR 585 million came from one case.

Outcome feedback (§ 42(1) GwG): prosecutors sent 41,743 feedback notices (2024: 41,821). Of these, 1,669 concerned judgments, penalty orders, court decisions or indictments (Tabelle 2: 247 Urteile, 841 Strafbefehle, 38 Beschlüsse, 543 Anklageschriften; totals 1,354 in 2023 and 1,625 in 2024). Discontinuations made up about 96 percent. The FIU states that this "does not mean that no results were achieved", because its information is regularly used to pursue the predicate offence.

Evidence class: official administrative statistics. Feedback notices are not a cohort of 2025 STRs, one case may generate several notices, reporting by prosecutors is incomplete, and an indictment is not a conviction. The ratio therefore cannot be read as an STR precision or false-positive rate. German figures are a neighbouring-system comparator, not Austrian evidence; the Austrian A-FIU does not publish comparable prosecutorial-feedback data. The immediate-measure total is adverse to a claim that STRs produce no asset-denial effect.
