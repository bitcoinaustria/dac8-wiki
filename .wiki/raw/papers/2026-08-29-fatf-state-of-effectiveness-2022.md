# FATF state of effectiveness 2022

Across 120 fourth-round mutual evaluations published by December 2021, 28 percent of jurisdictions received high or substantial effectiveness for use of financial intelligence (IO6), 9 percent for money-laundering investigation and prosecution (IO7), and 19 percent for confiscation (IO8). Unlike technical-compliance figures, these effectiveness ratings were not updated through later follow-up ratings.

These percentages apply to separate assessor-rated Immediate Outcomes. They are not a causal estimate of crime reduction and the 19-percent IO8 figure must not be paraphrased as the share of AML systems that work.
