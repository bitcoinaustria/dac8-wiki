# Bank of Italy inspections and STRs

Using 736 banks and 112,674 reports from 2012–2013, the study associated inspections with approximately 18 percent more suspicious-transaction reports and a higher reporting probability, without an average deterioration in the FIU risk assessment used as a quality proxy.

Inspection selection may remain confounded. A report volume or FIU assessment is a compliance or intelligence output, not proof of an offence, conviction or aggregate prevention effect.
