# EBA opinion on de-risking 2022

The EBA identified de-risking affecting payment and e-money institutions, VASPs, non-profit organisations and other customer groups. It warned that excluding entire categories without individual risk assessment may be unwarranted.

The report could not quantify prevalence from comprehensive statistics. De-risking can also reflect profitability, sanctions, reputation and operational complexity, so its AML-attributable share remains uncertain.
