# Brå: Möjliggörare för kriminella nätverk

Selected digest of report 2024:2, published 1 March 2024. Methods on printed pages 21–25 combine interviews with case material and decrypted communications. The interview component covers 93 people in 66 interviews. Page 37 discusses unauthorised database queries; page 39 includes protected-address disclosure, without establishing a subsequent attack.

Printed pages 60–61 expressly disclaim a population prevalence estimate and report that such insiders generally appear uncommon in Swedish workplaces. Page 61 describes a housing/support service forced to stop operating after information about gang defectors leaked to criminal networks. “Boendestöd” is not automatically a protected shelter. This is qualitative, anonymised institutional research, not an identifiable conviction or a crypto/tax-data case. It supports the possibility of disrupted protection before physical injury, not a measured attack probability.
