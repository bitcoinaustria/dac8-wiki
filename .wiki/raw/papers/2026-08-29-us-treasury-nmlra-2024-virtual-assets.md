# US Treasury NMLRA 2024

The assessment states that virtual-asset use for money laundering remains far below fiat currency and conventional methods, with cash retaining major advantages for anonymity, stability and acceptance. It also identifies foreign VASPs, mixers, DeFi, cross-chain bridges and compliance gaps as material risks.

The assessment is US-specific and does not supply a globally comparable percentage. In identified investment-scam cases, it records scammers directing victims toward wires or money mules after VASPs stopped victim transfers; this is not a general causal finding about all laundering displacement.
