# Austrian Court of Audit report on BMF IT-security management, Bund 2024/16

The Austrian Court of Audit examined selected security-management aspects at the Federal Ministry of Finance and two other ministries. The principal review period was 2018 to 2022, with a June 2023 snapshot for some matters. It did not examine security management at service providers such as Bundesrechenzentrum GmbH or at subordinate offices.

For the BMF, the report records an ISO 27001 and ISO 27701 certified management system, standardised regular and incident reporting, a security strategy covering subordinate bodies, a Security Board with BRZ, installed IDS/IPS and SIEM, 255 security reviews from 2018 through 2022, and monthly BRZ vulnerability scans. The Court of Audit concluded that the certifications and reviews allowed the BMF to detect, analyse and reduce IT-security risks to a high degree.

The report also records boundaries rather than established defects in a future DAC8 system. The BMF performed no social-engineering audits in the reviewed period because it said operational security management captured current social-engineering risks. A dedicated BRZ security operations centre was still in preparation in June 2023, while the BRZ computer emergency response team performed the function. No uniform security standards for federal-administration IT existed at the June 2023 snapshot. These facts do not establish an inadequate BMF control environment.

The audit predates the Krypto-MPfG and does not identify the production system, field map, recipients, access model, exports, logs, retention implementation or security testing for DAC8 data. It is therefore strong counterevidence to a generic insecurity claim but not proof of the safeguards or residual risk of the later system.
