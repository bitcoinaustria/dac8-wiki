# UN Terrorist Financing Convention 1999

The convention requires parties to criminalise the provision or collection of funds for specified terrorist purposes and to enable identification, detection, freezing, seizure and confiscation. It entered into force in 2002.

It is a binding public-international-law root of CFT. It does not itself formulate the full private-sector CDD and transfer-information rules later developed by FATF and enacted by the EU.
