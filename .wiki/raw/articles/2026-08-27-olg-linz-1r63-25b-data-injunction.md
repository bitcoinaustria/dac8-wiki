# OLG Linz 1 R 63/25b: Austrian civil interim relief for threatened data disclosure

In 1 R 63/25b, decision of 12 June 2025, the OLG Linz addressed an interim injunction against threatened disclosure of personal information. The court considered privacy injury and risk of repetition capable of supporting urgent civil protection.

The decision demonstrates that Austrian civil interim relief is not merely imaginary. It is not a direct DAC8 analogue: the alleged disclosure was not compelled by a specific reporting statute, the decision predates CJEU C-655/23, and the proper defendant and legal basis differ. Those distinctions must be resolved before treating a civil injunction as reliable protection against Krypto-MPfG processing or transmission.
