# FATF monitored jurisdictions

FATF may place a jurisdiction under increased monitoring with an action plan or call for action against high-risk jurisdictions. Listing is a possible consequence of strategic deficiencies, not a necessary step by which every FATF standard becomes law.

FATF states that increased monitoring does not call for blanket enhanced due diligence or de-risking against an entire jurisdiction. Market and supervisory reactions may nevertheless create substantial practical pressure.
