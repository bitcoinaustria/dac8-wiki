# BVwG W137 2314413-1

Decision of 9 December 2025.

Reasons 3.5–3.7 require concrete organisational findings before an authority may rely on an abstract risk to its tasks as a ground for refusing access. The authority must also consider partial access.

The judgment supports an as-applied challenge to an insufficiently reasoned DAC8 access refusal. It does not make EU-AHG section 19 facially invalid; that section itself conditions restrictions on suitability, necessity and proportionality.
