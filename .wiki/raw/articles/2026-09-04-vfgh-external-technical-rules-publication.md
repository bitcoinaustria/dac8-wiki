# VfGH: external technical rules, normative content and publication

[VfGH V 69/2018](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/VfghEntscheidung.wxe?Abfrage=Vfgh&Dokumentnummer=JFT_20181128_18V00069_00&IncludeSelf=True) treated an imperative and generally applicable guideline as a regulation and held that publication only on a website did not satisfy the legally required form. [VfGH V 23/06, VfSlg 17.944](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Vfgh/JFT_09938997_06V00023_00/JFT_09938997_06V00023_00.pdf) likewise rejected substitute publication methods where the prescribed publication organ had not been used.

[VfGH G 49/03 and related cases](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/VfghEntscheidung.wxe?Abfrage=Vfgh&Dokumentnummer=JFT_09968997_03G00049_00&IncludeSelf=True) connects publication, accessibility, determinacy and intelligibility of referenced rules to legal certainty. This does not make every external technical document a regulation.

[VfGH G 174/06](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/VfghEntscheidung.wxe?Abfrage=Vfgh&Dokumentnummer=JFT_09929691_06G00174_00&IncludeSelf=True) accepted a statutory connection to generally recognised technical standards where those standards were not themselves made binding normative content. [VfGH V 30/2013 and related cases](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Vfgh/JFT_20131002_13V00030_00/JFT_20131002_13V00030_00.html) nevertheless requires the norm-setter to review and maintain technical references.

The decisive fact for an XML, XSD or WSDL challenge is therefore its content and legal operation. Encoding, transport, endpoint and authentication rules are more likely technical implementation. Additional mandatory data fields, substantive validation criteria, new deadlines, retention duties or rejection consequences not determined by the published law are more likely normative requirements requiring a separate publication and authority analysis.

The Krypto-MPfG-DV itself was published in BGBl II. The above cases do not establish any defect in that publication and do not decide the status of the still-unobtained operative technical specifications.
