# OGH 6 Ob 159/23k: preventive GDPR injunction issue suspended for C-655/23

In 6 Ob 159/23k, order of 27 August 2024, the OGH suspended proceedings concerning an injunction against renewed unlawful disclosure pending the CJEU's answer in C-655/23. The requested relief did not depend on erasure, so the existence of a free-standing preventive claim was decision-relevant.

The CJEU later held in C-655/23 that the GDPR does not itself require a special preventive injunction independent of erasure, while national law may provide one. This Austrian order is therefore evidence of uncertainty, not proof that a civil DAC8 injunction is unavailable. As of 27 August 2026, no post-C-655/23 OGH merits decision resolving this Austrian claim basis was located in the official database.
