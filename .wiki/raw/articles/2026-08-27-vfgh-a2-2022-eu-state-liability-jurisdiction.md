# VfGH A 2/2022: jurisdiction for EU-law state liability

The VfGH held that ordinary Amtshaftung claims, including claims based on Convention-violating execution, belong to the AHG courts because Article 137 B-VG is subsidiary. For EU-law state liability, the VfGH is competent only where the alleged breach is directly attributable to the legislature or to a supreme court.

Even in that category there is no strict liability. The claimant must substantiate a sufficiently serious and rights-infringing breach of EU law and the other liability conditions. If the alleged loss is attributed to administrative or lower-court execution, ordinary courts remain competent even where legislation predetermined that execution.
