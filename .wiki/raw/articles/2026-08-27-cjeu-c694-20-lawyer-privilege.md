# Orde van Vlaamse Balies

Case C-694/20 held Article 8ab(5) of Directive 2011/16 invalid under Charter Article 7 insofar as it required a lawyer protected by professional privilege to notify another intermediary who was not the lawyer's client. That notification revealed the existence of the consultation and could cause the lawyer's identity and involvement to reach the tax authority (paragraphs 27 to 31 and 59 to 66).

The judgment is narrow. It does not invalidate DAC reporting generally and does not create a general privilege for ordinary customer, CASP, or transaction records. Its transferable rule is that even an indirect disclosure route must be strictly necessary where it exposes protected communications.
