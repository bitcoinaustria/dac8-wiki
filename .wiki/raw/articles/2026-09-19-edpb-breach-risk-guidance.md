# Guidelines 9/2022, version 2.0, 28 March 2023

Selected extraction, printed pages 23–25. Paragraph 104 distinguishes prospective DPIA analysis of processing and hypothetical breaches from assessment after an actual breach. Paragraphs 107–111 address context, data combinations and identifiability. Paragraphs 113–115 address physical harm, malicious recipients and lasting consequences.

Paragraphs 103 and 105 require both likelihood and severity, assessed in context. This is institutional guidance, not an incident finding or a ruling that every financial-data system poses high risk. It does not independently decide whether a particular statutory processing operation requires its own DPIA.
