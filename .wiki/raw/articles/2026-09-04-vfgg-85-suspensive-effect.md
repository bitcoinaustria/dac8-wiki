# VfGG section 85: suspensive effect of a constitutional complaint

Section 85(1) states that the complaint has no suspensive effect. Under paragraph 2, the Court grants it on the complainant's request where compelling public interests do not oppose it and the balance of relevant interests shows disproportionate disadvantage from enforcement of the challenged decision or exercise of a right it grants. Paragraph 3 governs the resulting suspension of enforcement.

The provision belongs to the constitutional complaint procedure. It is not a statutory suspension mechanism for a direct individual norm-review application.
