# CJEU Foto-Frost, C-314/85

National courts may examine validity objections and reject them where unfounded. They may not definitively declare an EU act invalid; where a decision depends on a serious validity objection, the question belongs to the CJEU through Article 267 TFEU.

For DAC8, the VfGH can repeal Austrian law but cannot invalidate Directive (EU) 2023/2226.
