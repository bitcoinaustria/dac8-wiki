# VfGH G 216/2024: direct application where only a penalty route existed

On 6 October 2025 the VfGH accepted a direct application by companies that were themselves addressees of statutory, administratively penalized transport duties. The applicants had documented concrete transports and current operational effects. The Court held that provoking an administrative penalty proceeding was not a reasonable alternative route. It rejected the constitutional arguments on the merits.

The decision is a useful boundary for a private Krypto-MPfG user. The no-penalty-route exception concerns the regulated provider or another person subject to the penalized duty. It does not by itself remove the data subject's separate DSB, BVwG, or civil routes.
