# WiEReG FAQ, 12 February 2025

Selected official-PDF digest. Locator: section 13, questions 1 and 4–6, pages 41–43; still linked from the BMF guidance page on 19 September 2026.

Question 6 explains that authorities under § 12 WiEReG, credit and financial institutions under § 9(1)(1)–(2), and notaries under § 9(1)(7) still see protected beneficial owners. Other obliged entities cannot see their information in the specified entities' extracts, including historical extracts. An empty owner field indicates that a protected owner exists.

Question 1 requires an application for each beneficial owner identifying all entities for which protection is requested. Questions 4–5 describe provisional protection and five-year grants, or protection until majority for minors. The FAQ warns that a restriction request does not excuse a missed reporting deadline.

This is official administrative guidance, not a judicial holding. It documents recipient-specific suppression, not deletion, cessation of collection, or a general restriction on authority access.
