# Austria GMSG five-year privacy rationale and seven-year compliance records

The 2018 explanatory materials for GMSG section 5 describe five years as an appropriate retention period for data-protection reasons for information transmitted by a reporting financial institution. Current section 5(3) retains that five-year deletion rule.

Current GMSG section 7a separately requires seven-year preservation of evidence, records and documents used to meet reporting and due-diligence duties. The compliance-record category and the transmitted-information copy therefore have different periods under GMSG.

Krypto-MPfG section 20(4) and section 39 each use ten years. The Krypto-MPfG materials refer to simplification, the section 209(3) BAO absolute limitation period and DAC8's five-to-ten-year corridor, but do not discuss why the GMSG 5/7 design is inadequate for the corresponding crypto layers.

This is a strong domestic request-for-justification comparator, not proof that the data, correction needs or audit functions are identical. The State may rely on crypto-specific valuation, reconstruction or limitation needs and on the fact that ten years lies within the DAC8 corridor for compliance records.
