# SEC2016: reporting applications exceeded intended entitlements

SEC8June2016, Release34-78021, File3-17280. Locators §§1–3,5–8,10–15,17–19, printedpp.2–6.

About730,000 accounts linked to330,000 households were misappropriated. Fields included contacts, accounts, balances and holdings. Two reporting applications failed intended group restrictions; a role change failed to reach another entitlement store. Testing/monitoring was deficient; Internet discovery followed transfer and likely later third-party compromise. USD1million is a penalty, not customer theft. Consent-settlement findings without admission/denial, not a contested judgment or quantified customer-loss census.

Mechanical credibility 0; independently assessed. Historical and vendor-primary records are accepted for their unique bounded incident/implementation evidence, not population rates. No private dataset or victim record was collected.
