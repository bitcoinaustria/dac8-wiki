# CJEU C-129/21 Proximus on recipient and erasure propagation

In C-129/21, judgment of 27 October 2022, the CJEU held that GDPR Articles 5(2) and 24, read with Article 19, can require a controller to implement appropriate measures to notify recipients of a legally effective change. Article 17(2) can require reasonable steps to inform additional controllers of a valid erasure request (paragraphs 79 to 99 and the operative part).

The case concerned a consent-dependent public telephone-directory chain. It supports a traceable correction or erasure propagation inquiry but does not establish automatic deletion by every DAC8 recipient. A recipient tax authority may have its own statutory basis, and Article 19 contains its own impossibility or disproportionate-effort limit.
