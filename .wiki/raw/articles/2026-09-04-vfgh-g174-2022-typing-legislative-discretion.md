# VfGH G 174/2022 on typing and legislative discretion

In G 174/2022, judgment of 13 December 2022, the VfGH applied its settled deferential equality review. The legislature has broad policy discretion and may use simple, administrable and typified rules, orient itself to the usual case and accept isolated hardship. The Court does not review whether the chosen solution is the most expedient or optimal.

For a DAC8 or Krypto-MPfG equality claim, this is material adverse authority. A claimant should not rely on a merely preferable alternative or an edge case. The case must identify a concrete comparator, a material mismatch between facts and consequence, and why the stated administrative or enforcement reason does not rationally account for that mismatch.
