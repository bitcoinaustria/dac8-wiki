# CNIL annual report 2025: detailed breach extraction

Pages 53–55 report 6,167 breach notifications in 2025, an increase of 9.5 percent. Public administration was the largest sector at 19 percent and finance and insurance represented 12 percent. The CNIL describes roughly forty incidents affecting several million persons in 2025 and says that approximately 80 percent of the large 2024 incidents involved impersonation of an account protected only by a password.

These are notification statistics and institutional observations, not findings about DAC8 or Austrian systems. They do not measure unique people, crypto breaches, successful misuse or physical attacks.
