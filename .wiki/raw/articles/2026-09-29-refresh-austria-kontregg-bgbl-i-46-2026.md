# BGBl. I Nr. 46/2026: KontRegG amendment

BGBl. I Nr. 46/2026 was issued on 1 July 2026 (NR: GP XXVIII AB 522; BR: AB 11829). Article 2 amends the Kontenregister- und Konteneinschaugesetz, and § 15(11) puts the changes into force on the day after publication. The RIS consolidated version dated 29 September 2026 lists it as the latest KontRegG amendment. The Krypto-MPfG consolidated version of the same date still shows only BGBl. I Nr. 96/2025.

Changes:
- § 1(1) adds "der Sozialbetrugsbekämpfung" to the register's purposes.
- § 2(6) allows processing of the tax number when an Abgabenbehörde or the Amt für Betrugsbekämpfung queries the register.
- New § 4(1) Z 4 grants register access to the Amt für Betrugsbekämpfung "für Zwecke der Sozialbetrugsbekämpfung im Verfahren zur Feststellung von Scheinunternehmen gemäß § 8 SBBG".
- New § 8(1a) permits information requests to credit institutions in such proceedings where, after notice of the suspicion, there are reasoned doubts or no answer, the information is expected to clarify the suspicion, and the interference is not disproportionate.
- §§ 8(2), 9(2) and 9(5) extend the written-form, Bundesfinanzgericht authorisation and evidence-exclusion rules to the new route.
- § 10 extends the Rechtsschutzbeauftragter's inspection rights, including access to register query logs (§ 4(3)).
