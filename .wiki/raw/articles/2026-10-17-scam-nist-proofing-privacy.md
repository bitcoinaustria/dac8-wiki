# NIST63A-4: static knowledge, insider risk and antifraud privacy

Final31July2025; SP800-63A-4, §2.5.1; §§3.2.1–3.2.3,3.3,3.5,3.6.

Knowledge-based verification/authentication must not be used for identity verification; fraud-management knowledge checks are separately scoped. Guidance addresses insider collusion, privacy assessment, effectiveness monitoring and redress.

Authentic identity attributes do not establish that their presenter is the person. Additional signals may create profiling/exclusion risks. This is a technical benchmark, not a finding that Austrian helpdesks rely on static data or that all knowledge checks are prohibited for every purpose.
