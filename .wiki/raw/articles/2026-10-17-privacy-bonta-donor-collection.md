# US Supreme Court: Americans for Prosperity Foundation v. Bonta (2021)

1 July 2021, Nos. 19–251/19–255. Six–three judgment; Roberts II-B-1 was a plurality, not a six-justice holding. Majority Parts II-B-2 and III require narrow tailoring, not necessarily least-restrictive means, and reject blanket upfront donor-schedule collection. Trial-record disclosures and threats supported the analysis. Page 18 expressly reserves revenue collection and tax-exempt status questions. The dissent distinguishes confidential submission from publication, disputes proven deterrence after safeguards improved and stresses fraud oversight. This is comparative US association authority, not Austrian law, a survey of deterred donors or a ban on all reporting. Mechanical credibility 1: primary court authority +1; no recent/empirical bonus.
