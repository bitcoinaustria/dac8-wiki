# Austria Amtshaftungsgesetz: monetary remedy and subsidiarity

Section 1 AHG makes the public legal entity liable under civil-law rules for culpably unlawful conduct by its organs in execution of the laws, but expressly limits the remedy to money. Section 2(2) excludes compensation where the injured person could have avoided the damage through available remedies, including an administrative-court complaint or VwGH revision.

Section 6 contains the limitation rules, including a three-year knowledge period and special outer and post-decision rules. Under the current section 8 wording, the claimant should first ask the legal entity in writing to state within three months whether it recognises the claim. An omitted or insufficient request, or a claim filed before the three-month period ends, can trigger the special cost consequence in section 8(2); the current text must not be described as an absolute filing bar. Section 9 assigns first-instance jurisdiction to the competent Landesgericht. The exact limitation calculation, defendant, request recipient and cost consequence are fact-specific and must be checked before filing.
