# Vienna November 2025 homicide linked to a crypto-wallet drain

Source digest. The canonical source is ORF Wien, "Toter in ausgebranntem Auto: Zwei Festnahmen", 2 December 2025, updated 13:28. It reports a press briefing by Gerhard Winkler, deputy head of the Landeskriminalamt Wien. Supplementary media, all read on 29 September 2026:

- [Der Standard, 2 December 2025](https://www.derstandard.at/story/3000000298860/krypto-vermoegen-soll-motiv-fuer-mord-an-21-jaehrigem-ukrainer-sein);
- [meinbezirk.at, 2 December 2025](https://www.meinbezirk.at/wien/c-lokales/opfer-aus-brennendem-auto-wegen-geld-getoetet_a7863752);
- [oe24, 10 January 2026](https://www.oe24.at/oesterreich/chronik/ukrainer-21-gefoltert-und-angezuendet-das-sind-die-brutalen-krypto-killer/664441512), a tabloid;
- [Focus.ua, 12 March 2026](https://focus.ua/uk/ukraine/746992-vbivstvo-sina-zastupnika-mera-harkova-u-vidni-mozhlivim-motivom-stala-osobista-pomsta-zmi), citing law-enforcement sources via Strana.ua;
- [Antikor, 11 June 2026](https://antikor.info/articles/843783-sud_ostavil_pod_strahej_bez_zaloga_podozrevaemyh_v_ubijstve_syna_zammera_harjkova).

No LPD Wien press release or Staatsanwaltschaft statement was retrieved.

**Police account (ORF, quoting Winkler).** The case facts are:

- Shortly after midnight on 26 November 2025, the body of a 21-year-old Ukrainian was found on the back seat of his family's burning Mercedes in Vienna-Donaustadt. The autopsy found about 80% burns and "massiver stumpfer Gewalt" (massive blunt force) to the head and mouth, including damaged teeth.
- The evening before, there had been an altercation in the car park of a Leopoldstadt hotel. Video shows the victim with a 19-year-old Ukrainian "der an derselben Universität studierte" (who studied at the same university). The same suspect was recorded buying two fuel canisters.
- "Von einem Krypto-Wallet des Opfers war ein beträchtlicher Geldbetrag abgebucht worden" (a considerable sum had been debited from the victim's crypto wallet). Police gave the motive as "finanzielle Hintergründe" (financial) and excluded a political one.
- A Staatsanwaltschaft Wien arrest order was sent via Europol. Ukrainian police arrested the 19-year-old and a 45-year-old on 29 November, seizing "sehr beträchtliche Dollar-Beträge" (very considerable dollar sums).
- Ukraine would not extradite them. Instead Austria was asked to cede the proceedings: "es wird also in der Ukraine das Verfahren gegen die von uns ausgeforschten Tatverdächtigen durchgeführt" (the proceedings against the suspects we identified will be conducted in Ukraine).

**Target selection (media only).** oe24 writes that the fellow student lured the victim to the car park, and that the victim had been "zu geschwätzig" (too talkative) in his company and revealed "über durchaus beträchtliche Rücklagen auf mehreren Krypto-Konten zu verfügen" (that he held considerable reserves in several crypto accounts). It adds, "Berichten zufolge" (according to reports), that he spoke openly with friends and fellow students about his digital assets. Focus.ua reports, from unnamed law-enforcement sources, that the motive may have been "одночасно помста та спроба заволодіти грошима" (both revenge and an attempt to seize money), rooted in personal animosity between acquaintances.

The Lopp list summarises the case as the victim being "betrayed by fellow student" and killed "after revealing cryptocurrency wallets". On all accounts, selection came from personal acquaintance and the victim's own disclosure, and locating came from the social relationship and a lure. Coercion was beating or torture to obtain wallet access, and the transfer went from the victim's wallets. No source mentions a data leak, register or tax data.

**Procedural status.** Evidence class: police statements reported by the media, and media-reported Ukrainian court steps. oe24 (10 January 2026) says the two are held in Ukrainian pre-trial detention on allegations including conspiracy, murder, robbery and torture. It reports a defence claim that the seized cash was a family loan. Antikor (11 June 2026) reports that a Ukrainian court again extended detention without bail and still calls the two "підозрюваних" (suspects). No indictment, trial judgment or final verdict was found as of 29 September 2026. The two are suspects; guilt is not established, and oe24 itself notes "Es gilt die Unschuldsvermutung" (the presumption of innocence applies).

**DAC8 relevance.** This is the most serious Austrian crypto-motivated violent case found. It is adverse to any data-causation theory: the reported pathway is self-disclosure to an acquaintance, not leaked provider or tax data. It supports the general severity point that crypto holdings can motivate lethal coercion in Austria. It also supports advice on personal discretion.
