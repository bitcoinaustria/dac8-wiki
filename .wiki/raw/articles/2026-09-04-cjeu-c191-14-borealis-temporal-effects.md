# CJEU C-191/14 and others Borealis on delayed invalidity effects

In the joined Borealis cases, judgment of 28 April 2016, the CJEU declared provisions invalid but delayed the effect for ten months and protected measures adopted during that period. It relied on legal certainty and the risk that an immediate legal vacuum would impair the wider regulatory system (paragraphs 101 to 111).

Paragraph 108 confirms that the CJEU decides whether the originating party is excepted from a temporal limitation. A DAC8 invalidity ruling can therefore be legally significant without immediately stopping the first reporting cycle or undoing every earlier measure.
