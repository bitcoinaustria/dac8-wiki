# Regulation (EU) 2024/1620

AMLA has applied generally since 1 July 2025, with staged institutional provisions. Articles 1, 5, and 6 establish its supervisory, convergence, risk-analysis, and FIU-coordination functions. Article 5(1)(d) requires a central AML/CFT database of supervisory information; sharing is confined to need-to-know and confidential use.

Direct supervision concerns selected high-risk cross-border financial entities rather than every CASP automatically. National supervisors remain responsible for non-selected entities. Articles 40 and 93 support joint FIU analysis and controlled cross-border information-sharing partnerships. Articles 66 and 67 permit direct sanctions against selected obliged entities.
