# Directive (EU) 2024/1654: account-register access and transaction-record formats

The Directive gives designated competent authorities direct cross-border BARIS access where necessary for serious-crime tasks, subject to case-by-case access, designated staff, logs, security, data protection, and purpose limitation.

It also enables technical standardisation of transaction records supplied by financial institutions and CASPs when competent authorities request them under national law in a criminal investigation. It does not place every transaction record in BARIS or create a universal central transaction-history database.
