# VfGH B 1187/2013

Decision of 10 December 2014.

The VfGH upheld continued automated retention because the data remained necessary and did not include sexual-life information. Its effective cessation and destruction holding concerned separate paper records.

The case supports an effective decision and an as-applied destruction claim after the relevant purpose ends, but only by analogy for routine electronic DAC8 data. It does not establish that a legislatively prescribed tax-retention period is facially invalid.
