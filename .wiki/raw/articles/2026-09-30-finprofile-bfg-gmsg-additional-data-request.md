# Additional GMSG compliance-audit data

BFG, 20 June 2024, RV/5100216/2024, PDF pp. 2–3, 10–11 and 21–24.

The tax authority requested electronically evaluable customer, account and depot lists for year-end 2020 and 2021, including non-reportable relationships and extra identification information, to check GMSG compliance.

The court upheld the demand and coercive fine, accepting its legal basis and proportionality for that audit purpose. This is adverse evidence against a blanket objection to broader collection. It proves the demand, not full delivery, subsequent central profiling or general permission for other purposes. Ordinary revision was admitted; the current VwGH outcome was not verified.

Scoring: recent +1, recognised institution +1. A bank's objections in the decision must not be substituted for the court's holding.
