# CJEU La Quadrature du Net on bulk retention by data category

In Joined Cases C-511/18, C-512/18 and C-520/18, judgment of 6 October 2020, the Grand Chamber rejected general traffic and location-data retention for ordinary crime control while allowing time-limited general retention in response to a genuine and present or foreseeable serious threat to national security. It also recognised targeted retention and differentiated IP-address and civil-identity data (paragraphs 115 to 168).

Particularly adverse to broad analogy, general retention of civil-identity data can be permissible because that category alone does not disclose the same communications profile (paragraphs 157 to 159). The decision therefore supports field-by-field analysis based on actual inference capacity.

For DAC8, identity data, annual values, units, counts and transfer aggregates cannot be treated as one undifferentiated category. Any claim of severe profiling must identify the exact fields, legally and technically available join data, and the resulting inference. The ePrivacy setting prevents direct transplantation of the judgment's categorical rule.
