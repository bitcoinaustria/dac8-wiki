# HUDOC searches: no merits decision on FATCA, CRS, DAC2, DAC8 or CARF automatic exchange

Official HUDOC full-text API searches run on 27 August 2026 produced these result counts:

- `FATCA`: zero;
- `"Common Reporting Standard"`: zero;
- `DAC2`: zero;
- `CARF`: zero;
- `DAC8`: one, *Ferrieri and Bonassisa v Italy*; and
- `"automatic exchange" AND tax`: two, *Ferrieri and Bonassisa* and *Halet v Luxembourg*.

The `DAC8` occurrence in *Ferrieri and Bonassisa* is in the description of the EU legal framework. That case decided targeted access to banking data for an Italian tax audit, not periodic automatic DAC8 exchange. *Halet* concerns whistle-blower freedom of expression, not a data subject's challenge to automatic exchange.

The searches therefore found no ECtHR merits decision directly deciding FATCA, CRS, DAC2, DAC8 or CARF periodic automatic exchange. This is a dated search result, not proof against unpublished, differently worded or not-yet-indexed cases.
