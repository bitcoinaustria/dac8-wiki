# French DGFiP tax and cadastral data breach, August 2026

The Ministry's 14 August 2026 release reports illicit access during June and July 2026 using impersonated credentials of a DGFiP employee and an authorised third party. Later investigation established consultation and extraction of data concerning 678,000 individuals and businesses. The release identifies reference tax income, family quotient, withholding rate, company name or SIREN, and cadastral address and property-surface data. It says user tax accounts and user passwords were not compromised.

Later DGFiP FAQs refine the private-person and business field lists. They also explain that initial access termination did not reveal the extraction. The CNIL confirmed receipt of notifications and opened verification, but did not independently establish the full scope, security fault or downstream misuse.

The incident proves a credential-based attack path against authority-held financial and address data. It does not involve DAC8, crypto records, wallet addresses or a finding that Austrian safeguards are inadequate.
