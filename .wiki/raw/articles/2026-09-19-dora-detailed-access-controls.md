# DORA detailed controls

Selected digest of Title II. Article 12 specifies logging policies, event selection and detail, protection against tampering or unauthorised access, detection of logging failures and time synchronisation. Article 20 requires unique identities and lifecycle management. Article 21 requires need-to-know, least privilege, separation of duties, accountability, controlled privileged access and prompt revocation. Access-right reviews occur at least annually for other ICT systems and every six months for systems supporting critical or important functions. Article 23 concerns collecting and analysing information, including logs and internal and external scenarios, to detect anomalies.

Article 1 requires proportionality. This is binding technical regulation within DORA's scope, not a universal rule for every DAC8 reporting operator or for tax authorities. The ordinary Title II and simplified Title III frameworks must not be conflated. A legal duty is neither proof of implementation nor proof of failure.
