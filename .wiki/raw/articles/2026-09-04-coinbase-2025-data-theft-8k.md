# Coinbase 2025 data-theft incident

Coinbase's Form 8-K states that a threat actor paid multiple contractors or employees in support roles outside the United States to collect information from internal systems. The affected fields included name, postal address, phone, email, government-ID images, masked bank and social-security identifiers, balance snapshots and transaction history. Passwords and private keys were not compromised, and the personnel could not access customer funds.

The filing is a regulated-company disclosure, not a supervisory finding. It establishes a concrete insider and support-access path to an identity-plus-account-history dataset. It does not state that wallet addresses or blockchain transaction identifiers were exposed, and it does not establish physical harm.
