# CJEU La Quadrature du Net, C-470/21

- Court: CJEU Grand Chamber
- Judgment: 30 April 2024
- ECLI: `EU:C:2024:370`

The judgment rejects a purely categorical approach to bulk retention. Interference intensity depends on the data retained, whether profiling links can be made, the purpose and access conditions, and safeguards.

For DAC8 this is important adverse nuance: annual aggregated financial reporting is not automatically equivalent to detailed communications metadata. A challenger must show what personal profile the exact statutory fields and retained provider records permit.
