# Gendarmerie: Waltio leak and crypto-attack target selection

Source digest, not a full-text copy. Céline Morin, Gendinfo, 13 September 2026. Locator: section “Une nouvelle forme de criminalité”, statements attributed to Colonel Patrick Pegeot, UNPJ operations head.

Pegeot links part of the growth in crypto-related violent crime to the Waltio leak. He says criminals use leaked customer email addresses to identify targets and sometimes misjudge actual holdings.

This is official operational attribution, not a published case-by-case causal judgment or quantitative estimate. Waltio is a private crypto-tax preparation service, not the tax administration or DAC8 exchange system. The article's earlier discussion of January 2025 attacks cannot establish causation by a later leak.

The source also describes recovery/interdiction work. Its simplified technical statements about instantaneous transfers and a bank at home are not adopted as universal properties of crypto custody.
