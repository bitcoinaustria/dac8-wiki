# Commission proposal COM(2015) 135 for DAC3

- Proposal for a Council Directive amending Directive 2011/16/EU as regards mandatory automatic exchange of information in the field of taxation, COM(2015) 135 final, 2015/0068 (CNS), dated 18 March 2015; CELEX `52015PC0135`

The explanatory memorandum grounds the proposal in aggressive tax planning and profit shifting towards favourable regimes, the 2012 review of tax-ruling practice by the Code of Conduct Group (Business Taxation), the Commission's 2012 Action Plan against tax fraud and evasion, the European Council's call of December 2014 and OECD BEPS work. A text search of the English Cellar version on 2026-09-30 found no reference to "LuxLeaks".

Timeline consequence: the November 2014 LuxLeaks disclosures are widely described as the political trigger for DAC3, but that link rests on secondary sources and on timing, not on the recitals or the proposal. Any public timeline should present it as context, not as the documented legislative reason.
