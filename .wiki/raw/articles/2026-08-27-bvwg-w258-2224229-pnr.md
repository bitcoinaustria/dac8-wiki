# BVwG W258 2224229-1: Austrian PNR data-processing litigation

The PNR litigation is a positive procedural analogue for the ordinary data-protection route. Affected individuals complained to the DSB, obtained BVwG review, and relied on the CJEU's PNR judgment to establish unlawful processing domestically.

It supports building a concrete DAC8 record through DSB and BVwG rather than depending on the exceptional admissibility of a direct norm challenge.
