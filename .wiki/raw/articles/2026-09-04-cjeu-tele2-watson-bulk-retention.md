# CJEU Tele2 Sverige and Watson on general retention and access safeguards

In Joined Cases C-203/15 and C-698/15, judgment of 21 December 2016, the Grand Chamber held that EU law precludes general and indiscriminate retention of all traffic and location data for fighting crime. Targeted retention can be permitted when based on objective criteria and limited to what is strictly necessary (paragraphs 95 to 112).

In that communications-confidentiality setting, authority access also required objective limits, independent prior review as a rule, later notification where it would no longer jeopardise the investigation, security measures and independent supervision (paragraphs 115 to 125).

The transfer to DAC8 is limited. The judgment concerns an exception to sector-specific communications confidentiality and highly revealing traffic and location data. It does not establish a general requirement of individual suspicion or judicial pre-authorisation for annual tax reports. It remains useful for separating collection, retention, authority access and later use, and for testing each stage against its own purpose and safeguards.
