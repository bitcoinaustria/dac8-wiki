# Rodl & Partner

Case C-562/20 explains that AML risk assessment operates at Union, Member-State, and obliged-entity levels and must be evidence-based (paragraphs 35 to 38). National enhanced measures remain subject to legality, legal certainty, proportionality, and non-discrimination (paragraphs 49 to 53). CDD documentation must be current and available, and the judgment recognises the statutory five-year retention framework (paragraphs 67 to 74).

The case is strong counterauthority to an absolute anti-AML theory. It is also protective: nationality, foreign ownership, or association alone cannot replace an individualised risk assessment, and a published sanction must accurately reproduce the decision.
