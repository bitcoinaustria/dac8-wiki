# CJEU Schrems II on clear safeguards and effective remedy

In C-311/18, judgment of 16 July 2020, the Grand Chamber assessed whether foreign surveillance law provided clear limits, minimum safeguards and effective independent redress equivalent in substance to EU protection (paragraphs 176 to 185 and 191 to 203).

The case is not authority that intra-EU tax exchange requires the same third-country adequacy inquiry. It is relevant only to the quality and practical operation of access limits, oversight and binding relief, particularly if DAC8 information is later transferred outside the EU or made available under another international regime.
