# Directive (EU) 2024/1260

Article 3 treats crypto-assets as property. Articles 4 to 6 require tracing capacity and at least one Asset Recovery Office per Member State, including access to central account information and information on crypto-asset accounts and transfers. Article 10 specifies ordinary and urgent response deadlines; Articles 11 to 16 govern freezing and several confiscation routes.

The directive is a criminal-asset-recovery pillar, not a CASP customer-due-diligence or universal wealth-register act. Its general transposition deadline is 23 November 2026; Ireland and Denmark are not bound.
