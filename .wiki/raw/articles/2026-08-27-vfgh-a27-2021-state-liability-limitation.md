# VfGH A 27/2021: three-year EU state-liability limitation

In its decision of 26 September 2022, the VfGH reiterated that EU-law state-liability claims generally apply section 6 AHG and therefore expire three years after knowledge of the damage, apart from exceptional cases not relevant there. On the facts, the claim filed on 31 December 2021 was timely because the alleged loss could not have arisen before the impugned statutory provision entered into force on 1 January 2019.

The authority confirms the basic period, not the start date for a hypothetical DAC8 loss. Damage, knowledge, any final-decision rule and the effect of an advance demand remain claim-specific.
