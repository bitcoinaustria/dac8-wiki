# Current WiEReG

WiEReG covers Austrian entities, trusts and similar arrangements, and specified foreign entities with Austrian land. Section 5 records beneficial-owner identity, residence, nationality, and control information; the 2024 amendment expanded nominee and nominator data. Section 9 permits access by defined obliged entities, including CASPs. Section 11 prevents exclusive reliance on the register, and section 10a allows restrictions for overriding legitimate interests.

Entity due-diligence material is generally retained at least five years after beneficial ownership ends. Section 15 creates administrative penalties. WiEReG identifies ownership and control; it is not a crypto-transfer or wallet-address register.
