# VfGH Article 18 B-VG boundary

VfGH B 104/61 states that Article 18 B-VG does not itself grant a subjective public right to lawful administration. It can nevertheless support an objection to the constitutionality of a statute.

The later VfGH formulation is that all interpretive methods must first be exhausted. A statute fails the Article 18 determinacy requirement only if it still cannot be determined what the law authorises; the required precision depends on the regulated subject and consequences. See [VfGH G 3502/2023](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/VfghEntscheidung.wxe?Abfrage=Vfgh&Dokumentnummer=JFT_20240305_23G03502_00&IncludeSelf=True).

For Krypto-MPfG section 20(1), Article 18 is therefore a supporting standard for a demonstrated ambiguity about processing, access or deletion, not the private user's primary right and not a substitute for section 1 DSG, Article 8 ECHR or Charter Articles 7 and 8.
