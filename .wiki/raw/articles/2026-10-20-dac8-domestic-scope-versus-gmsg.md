# DAC8 domestic scope versus GMSG

Retrieved 20 October 2026. DAC8 text fetched from the EU Publications Office (Cellar, German and English OJ versions of Directive (EU) 2023/2226, OJ L 24.10.2023); GMSG from the RIS consolidated version "Fassung vom 20.10.2026"; Krypto-MPfG from the local gazette copy `legal-docs/core/at-bgbl-i-96-2025-krypto-mpfg.pdf`. Supplements the English paraphrase in [the DAC8 timeline record](2026-09-30-eu-dac8-legislative-timeline-and-non-crypto-parts.md).

## DAC8 recital 13 (exact text)

DE: „Die Meldepflicht sollte sich sowohl auf grenzüberschreitende als auch auf innerstaatliche Transaktionen erstrecken, um die Wirksamkeit der Meldevorschriften, das reibungslose Funktionieren des Binnenmarkts sowie gleiche Wettbewerbsbedingungen sicherzustellen und den Grundsatz der Nichtdiskriminierung zu wahren.“

EN: "The reporting obligation should cover both cross-border and domestic transactions in order to ensure the effectiveness of the reporting rules, the proper functioning of the internal market, a level playing field and respect of the principle of non-discrimination."

The recital speaks of transactions, not of customers. It is an interpretive statement of purpose, not the operative rule.

## Operative provisions

- DAC8 Annex VI, Section IV(D)(8): "Member State Person" means a person "resident in any Member State under the tax laws of that Member State". "Reportable User" is a crypto-asset user that is a reportable person resident in a Member State (IV(D)(1)). A provider's own-state residents are therefore reportable.
- Krypto-MPfG § 14 Abs. 1 Z 1: „teilnehmender Staat“ means „einen Mitgliedstaat der Europäischen Union“, which includes Austria.
- GMSG § 91 Z 1 (CRS/DAC2 implementation): „teilnehmender Staat“ means „einen anderen Mitgliedstaat der Europäischen Union“, or under Z 2 another state with an exchange agreement. §§ 89–90 define the reportable person as a person resident in a participating state. § 3 Abs. 1 lists the reported data, including „ausländische Steueridentifikationsnummer(n)“.

## Result

Under the GMSG, an account holder resident only in Austria is not a reportable person, so Austrian banks do not report that person's accounts under CRS/DAC2. Under the Krypto-MPfG, the same person is a reportable user of a crypto-asset service provider.

## Limits

- This contrast concerns automatic exchange reporting only. Austrian bank customers remain subject to other domestic layers: the Kontenregister (account existence, not balances or flows), KESt withholding on bank and securities income, and AML records. From March 2015 to December 2022 banks also reported capital outflows of at least EUR 50,000, including cash withdrawals; see [the Kapitalabfluss-Meldegesetz record](2026-10-20-austria-kapitalabfluss-meldegesetz-expiry.md).
- A person resident in Austria and in another participating state can be reportable under the GMSG for the other state.
- The domestic crypto scope is mandated by DAC8 itself. It is not Austrian gold-plating, and Austria cannot remove it without changing EU law. Recital 13 states the EU legislature's non-discrimination and level-playing-field reasons, which the State will rely on.
