# CJEU C-121/14 on partial invalidity and severability

In C-121/14, judgment of 12 November 2015, the CJEU restated that partial annulment of an EU act is possible only if the challenged elements are severable from the rest. Severability depends on their scope and whether removal would alter the spirit or substance of the act (paragraphs 20 to 21).

The case arose under Article 263 TFEU, not a DAC8 preliminary reference. The severability principle remains important when framing a validity question about one DAC8 field, category or functional provision. A narrow challenge should explain how the reporting system remains workable without the targeted element.
