# Germany: Kryptowerte-Steuertransparenzgesetz

- Short title: `KStTG`
- Gazette: `BGBl. 2025 I Nr. 352`
- Enacted: 22 December 2025
- Effective: 24 December 2025

The statute implements DAC8 reporting in Germany. Provider reporting is due by 31 July and authority exchange by 30 September. Section 20 assigns disputes concerning tax-authority measures under the act to the financial courts. No DAC8-specific German judgment was located in the research pass.
