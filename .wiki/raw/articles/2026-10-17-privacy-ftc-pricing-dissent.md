# Ferguson and Holyoak: Dissent on Surveillance Pricing Research Summaries

FTC Matter P246202, 17 January 2025, two pages. Ferguson, joined by Holyoak, supports the unanimously launched study but objects to publication of early impressions and hypothetical use cases before comprehensive fact gathering. Page 2 stresses that definitive price and market-participant effects remain unresolved and raises fairness/cooperation concerns for respondents. This is an institutional disagreement about evidence and publication, not an empirical demonstration that personal-data targeting never occurs. It qualifies the accompanying staff perspective and prevents treating agency publication as unanimous, final or a finding of illegality. Mechanical credibility 2: identified institutional authors +1, recent +1; partisan assertions about motive are not adopted as facts.
