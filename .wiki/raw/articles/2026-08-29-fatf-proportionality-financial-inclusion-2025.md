# FATF proportionality changes 2025

FATF amended Recommendation 1 and related interpretive notes to use proportionate measures, require countries to allow and encourage simplified measures in lower-risk situations and direct supervisors to consider whether institutions over-comply.

The change acknowledges exclusion and over-compliance as implementation risks. It does not quantify their prevalence or determine the legality of a specific EU or Austrian measure.
