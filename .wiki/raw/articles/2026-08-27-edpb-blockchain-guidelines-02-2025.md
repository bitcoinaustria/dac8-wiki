# EDPB Guidelines 02/2025 on blockchain personal-data processing

The guidance explains that public keys and other blockchain data may be personal data when linkable to an individual. It stresses purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, integrity, confidentiality, and data-protection-by-design.

The guidance supports a threat model in which identity-to-ledger correlation can reveal a persistent graph. It does not establish that DAC8 reports wallet addresses or complete histories. It was issued for consultation and is non-binding.
