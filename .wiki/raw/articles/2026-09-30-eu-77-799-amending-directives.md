# Amendments to Directive 77/799/EEC, 1979–2006

All data verified on 2026-09-30 from the official texts and Cellar metadata of each act.

| Act | CELEX / OJ | Date | Effect on 77/799/EEC | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Council Directive 79/1070/EEC | `31979L1070`; OJ L 331, 27.12.1979, p. 8 | 6 Dec 1979 | Extended mutual assistance to value added tax; title amended | Comply by 1 Jan 1981 |
| Council Directive 92/12/EEC (excise general arrangements) | `31992L0012`; OJ L 76, 23.3.1992, p. 1 | 25 Feb 1992 | Extended 77/799/EEC to excise duties (recital and amending article) | Transposition 1 Jan 1993 (metadata) |
| Council Directive 2003/93/EC | `32003L0093`; OJ L 264, 15.10.2003, p. 23 | 7 Oct 2003 | Extended scope to taxation of insurance premiums; references to 77/799/EEC for VAT to be read as references to Regulation (EC) No 1798/2003; permitted use of information for other levies under Directive 76/308/EEC | Comply on 31 Dec 2003 |
| Council Directive 2004/56/EC | `32004L0056`; OJ L 127, 29.4.2004, p. 70 | 21 Apr 2004 | Requested authority acts as if on its own account; disclosure in public hearings no longer needs permission; new Article 8a (notification of instruments) and Article 8b (simultaneous controls) | Before 1 Jan 2005 |
| Council Directive 2004/106/EC | `32004L0106`; OJ L 359, 4.12.2004, p. 30 | 16 Nov 2004 | Removed excise duties (moved to Regulation (EC) No 2073/2004); title became "direct taxation and taxation of insurance premiums" | Before 30 June 2005; apply from 1 July 2005 |
| Council Directive 2006/98/EC | `32006L0098`; OJ L 363, 20.12.2006, p. 129 | 20 Nov 2006 | Accession adaptation for Bulgaria and Romania (also amended Directive 2003/48/EC) | By accession date (1 Jan 2007) |

None of these amendments introduced mandatory automatic exchange or private-sector reporting. The indirect-tax branches were moved into separate regulations, leaving 77/799/EEC for direct taxes and insurance premiums until Directive 2011/16/EU replaced it.
