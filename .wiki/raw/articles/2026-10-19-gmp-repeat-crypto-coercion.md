# GMP: repeat crypto coercion and delayed payment

Greater Manchester Police, 30 January 2025. Full original retrieved; locators: successive 2023 incidents, October detention, December rescue and sentencing.

The account describes repeated abductions and extortion. During an October incident, inability to pay immediately preceded overnight restraint, assault and death threats; payment followed. Police rescued the victim on 2 December 2023. Defendants pleaded guilty or were convicted of offences including attempted robbery, kidnapping and false imprisonment; sentences were imposed on 30 January 2025.

The victim reportedly held crypto worth hundreds of thousands of pounds and had already paid over GBP 100,000. This is not evidence of negligible holdings. No leak origin is identified; judgments and appeal finality were not independently checked.
