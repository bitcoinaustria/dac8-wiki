# Commonwealth Bank: Technology-Facilitated Payment-Message Abuse

Commonwealth Bank statement, June 2020; precise publication day not exposed. Over three months, more than 8,000 customers received multiple low-value deposits with potentially abusive descriptions. The observed spectrum included profane jokes, serious threats and domestic-violence references. This is not 8,000 confirmed abuse survivors, distinct perpetrators or a population rate. Policy responses permitting refusal/suspension are announced measures, not measured long-term effectiveness. No data breach was required for the reported messaging mechanism. False positives and unintended consequences remain relevant. Mechanical credibility 0: known institution +1, first-party promotional framing −1; unique first-hand mechanism retained.
