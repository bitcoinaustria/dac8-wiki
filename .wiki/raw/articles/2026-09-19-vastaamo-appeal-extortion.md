# Helsinki Court of Appeal R 24/1302

Selected digest of the court release, 26 February 2026. The appeal court confirmed convictions concerning an aggravated data breach, privacy disclosure, aggravated extortions and attempted extortions, increasing imprisonment to six years and eleven months.

The case concerns psychotherapy records. It supports downstream extortion after a data breach, not physical assault, crypto-owner targeting or a DAC8 incident. The release's then-open appeal position must be read with the Supreme Court's later refusal of leave.
