# VfGH U 466/11 ua

The VfGH held that sufficiently rights-like Charter guarantees may be invoked as constitutionally guaranteed rights and used as a standard in Austrian general norm review where Article 51(1) of the Charter applies. The VfGH may obtain an Article 267 ruling where necessary and annul conflicting national law under Articles 139 or 140 B-VG.

The holding does not make every Charter provision a review standard and does not create standing or override subsidiarity. It also preserves every national court's ability to refer EU-law interpretation or validity questions under Article 267 TFEU.
