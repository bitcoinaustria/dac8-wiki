# Council Directive (EU) 2016/881 (DAC4, country-by-country reports)

## Source identity

- Council Directive (EU) 2016/881 of 25 May 2016 amending Directive 2011/16/EU as regards mandatory automatic exchange of information in the field of taxation; CELEX `32016L0881`; OJ L 146, 3.6.2016, p. 8
- Entry into force 3 June 2016 (Cellar metadata)
- Transposition by 4 June 2017; application from 5 June 2017 (Article 2(1))
- Commission proposal COM(2016) 25 of 28 January 2016

## Content

The recitals cite the OECD BEPS Action Plan of 19 July 2013 and the outcome of Action 13 (master file, local file and country-by-country report), and direct that the 2015 Final Report on Action 13 be used for illustration and interpretation. The proposal's explanatory memorandum notes the OECD final BEPS reports of 5 October 2015 and their G20 endorsement on 15–16 November 2015.

New Article 8aa requires the Ultimate Parent Entity of an MNE Group resident in a Member State, or another Reporting Entity under Annex III, to file a country-by-country report within 12 months after the end of the Reporting Fiscal Year. Groups with consolidated revenue below EUR 750 million in the preceding year are excluded (Annex III definition of "Excluded MNE Group"). The receiving authority exchanges the report with other Member States in which group entities are resident or have a permanent establishment, within 15 months after fiscal year end; the first report covers fiscal years starting on or after 1 January 2016 and is exchanged within 18 months. Member States could defer secondary (constituent-entity) filing by one year.

Reporters are large multinational groups, not individuals or financial intermediaries.

## Relevance and limits for DAC8

DAC4 is the first DAC amendment that places a filing obligation directly on private companies about themselves, taken over from an OECD standard. It says nothing about individual privacy and is contextual history for DAC8.
