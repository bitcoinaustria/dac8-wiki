# RAC confiscation: orders versus money actually recovered

Dated 29 May 2026; opening decision summary and individual order paragraphs.

ICO reports combined confiscation orders of GBP118,852.32. One GBP33,125 order was paid; the separate GBP85,727.32 amount was ordered, not confirmed recovered.

This is a follow-up to the 2024 RAC convictions, not another breach. Confiscation concerns proceeds and cannot be treated as a measured downstream victim-loss total or as full compensation. The public regulator account, not the underlying order, was retrieved. Mechanical credibility 2: recent institutional primary.
