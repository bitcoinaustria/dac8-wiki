# VwGH Ro 2023/04/0028: the DSG knowledge-based complaint limit applies

In Ro 2023/04/0028, judgment of 17 December 2025, the VwGH upheld the one-year subjective time limit in section 24(4) DSG. The period begins from knowledge of the event giving rise to the complaint and was held compatible with GDPR Articles 58 and 77 because it does not make exercise of the EU remedy impossible or excessively difficult.

The decision is a practical barrier, not a reason to bypass the DSB. A selected DAC8 claimant should document when the relevant processing, notice, response or refusal became known and file early rather than treating the 2027 reporting deadline as the only clock.
