# Government bill 622 d.B.: WiEReG 2027 and FM-GwG amendment

## Procedural status (parliament record, read 29 September 2026)

- Title: Bundesgesetz, mit dem das Wirtschaftliche Eigentümer Registergesetz, das Gebührengesetz 1957 sowie das Finanzmarkt-Geldwäschegesetz geändert werden und ein Wirtschaftliche Eigentümer Registergesetz 2027 erlassen wird (622 d.B., XXVIII. GP). Proposing minister: Dr. Markus Marterbauer (BMF).
- 22.09.2026: received by the Nationalrat (96th sitting, notice of receipt). 23.09.2026: assigned to the Finanzausschuss (97th sitting). 29.09.2026: on the agenda of the committee's 14th sitting.
- The page header shows a "Status Beschlossen" label, but the procedural record shows no committee report, plenary vote, Bundesrat step or BGBl. publication. As at 29 September 2026 this is a government bill, not enacted law. The meaning of the header label is unclear.
- Predecessor: ministerial draft 93/ME (submitted 27.03.2026; consultation to 27.04.2026).

## Content relevant to the AML article

- Article 1 states that Article 2 implements Articles 11 to 14 of Directive (EU) 2024/1640.
- Article 2 amends the current WiEReG. Most changes take effect on 5 November 2026. They include nominee and change-reporting duties, sanctions-related content and legitimate-interest access procedures. The explanatory notes describe a changed retention rule for beneficial-owner data after an entity ends (Erläuterungen zu Z 11, § 7 Abs. 3).
- Article 3 enacts WiEReG 2027: "§ 26. Dieses Bundesgesetz tritt mit 10. Juli 2027 in Kraft". Under § 27 the WiEReG (BGBl. I Nr. 136/2017) then lapses.
- The explanatory notes (Allgemeiner Teil) retain the gold-plating statement: FATF Recommendations 24 and 25 "sollen vollinhaltlich umgesetzt werden, auch wenn dies bedeutet, dass über die Mindeststandards der 6. Geldwäscherichtlinie und Verordnung hinausgehende Vorschriften beibehalten oder erlassen werden müssen."
- Article 5 amends the FM-GwG. These changes enter into force on the day after promulgation.
  - CASPs move into the Finanzinstitut definition (§ 2 Z 2 new lit. i). Domestic branches of foreign CASPs are expressly covered (lit. h). The separate CASP listing in § 1(1) and § 25(1) Z 4 is deleted. The explanatory notes say this cures unequal treatment in § 13 (performance by third parties).
  - § 32b is deleted because it refers to the already repealed § 32a (VASP registration).
  - The fine cap in the closing part of § 34(2) falls from EUR 5,000,000 to EUR 150,000. The notes describe this as correcting a 2024 restructuring error, so that base offences no longer carry the same maximum as qualified offences.
  - § 34(2) Z 5 is reworded for TFR (Regulation 2023/1113) breaches by intermediary payment service providers and CASPs.
