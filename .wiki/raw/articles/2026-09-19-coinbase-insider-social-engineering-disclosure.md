# Coinbase insider theft and downstream fraud

Source digest. Coinbase, 15 May 2025. Locators: “What happened”, “What they got”, “What they didn't get” and reimbursement criteria.

Coinbase reports bribed support personnel copying customer data for impersonation fraud. Exposed records included identity/contact data, ID images, masked identifiers, balance snapshots and transaction history. It excludes stolen login credentials, private keys and direct access to funds, yet acknowledges customers deceived into sending funds and offers reimbursement subject to review.

This is a first-party company disclosure, not a judicial finding. It supports a specific information-theft-to-fraud mechanism, not physical harm. The field list does not justify a categorical assertion that no public wallet address could appear in exposed transaction history.
