# Krypto-MPfG section 39 retention timing correction

Section 39 requires retention of the documented compliance steps for ten years after the end of the relevant `Meldezeitraum`. It does not say ten years after the end of the later calendar year in which the report is due.

For the 2026 reporting period, the official explanatory materials use 31 December 2036 as the section 20(4) deletion date. This correction supersedes the timing sentence in the immutable source record `2026-08-30-austria-krypto-mpfg-section-39-records.md`; the remaining scope and interpretation questions in that record stay open.
