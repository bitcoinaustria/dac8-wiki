# VfGG section 87: annulment and binding direction in the case

Section 87(1) governs the finding of the specified constitutional or norm-application violation and annulment of the challenged judgment. Paragraph 2 requires administrative courts and administrative authorities, in the same matter and using their available legal means, promptly to establish the legal state corresponding to the Constitutional Court's legal view.

This case-bound obligation is distinct from statutory annulment and is not itself a direct data-erasure order addressed to a private provider.
