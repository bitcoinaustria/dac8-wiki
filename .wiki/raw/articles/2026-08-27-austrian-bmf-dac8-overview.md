# Austrian Ministry of Finance overview of DAC8 implementation

The BMF identifies Directive (EU) 2023/2226 as DAC8 and states that Austria implements both DAC8 and the OECD Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework through the Krypto-MPfG. Unlike DAC2 balance reporting, the BMF describes DAC8 implementation as transaction-based.

The BMF lists the statute's main parts: general provisions, definitions, reporting, registration, due diligence for natural-person and entity users, general due-diligence provisions, penalties, and final provisions.

The page is administrative guidance, not a substitute for the statute.
