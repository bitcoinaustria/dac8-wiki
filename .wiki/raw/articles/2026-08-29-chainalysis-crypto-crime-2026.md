# Chainalysis crypto-crime estimate 2026

Chainalysis estimated that vendor-attributed illicit or sanctioned addresses and entities received at least USD 154 billion in 2025 while remaining below one percent of its attributed on-chain transaction-volume denominator. The amount is received value, not automatically money-laundering volume, criminal proceeds or victim harm; sanctioned-entity activity was a material component.

The vendor measure uses incoming transfers to known services across tracked assets, with internal service transfers removed from the denominator. The introduction does not fully disclose chain coverage. Unknown illicit addresses and substantial off-chain or unsupported activity are excluded, and estimates are revised as new labels are added. It is a commercial attribution series, not an official statistic or a measure of all crime involving crypto.
