# European Commission feasibility study for a European asset registry

The March 2024 final study is a feasibility assessment, not an enacted register, legislative proposal, or Commission commitment. It examines additional national registers, interconnection of existing national registers, and a central EU database covering existing and newly captured asset categories.

Its central-register analysis states that high asset coverage and aggregation would greatly increase the seriousness of privacy and data-protection interference, could expose habits, daily life, and social relationships, and could be manifestly disproportionate. It also records severe cyberattack consequences and uncertainty whether central-register benefits would outweigh fundamental-rights disadvantages.

The study is unusually strong institutional evidence for the risk of future asset-data aggregation. It does not prove that DAC8 alone is the central register it studied.
