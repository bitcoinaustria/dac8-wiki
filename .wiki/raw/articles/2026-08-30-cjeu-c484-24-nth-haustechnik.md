# CJEU C-484/24 NTH Haustechnik

Judgment of 18 June 2026, ECLI:EU:C:2026:496.

Paragraphs 70–73 require a legal basis implementing EU data-protection law to define the scope, circumstances and conditions of processing with clear and precise rules, safeguards and a limitation to what is strictly necessary. Paragraphs 91–93 restate suitability, less onerous equally effective means and proportionality in the strict sense.

Paragraphs 94–103 distinguish GDPR Article 5(1)(c) data minimisation from the complete three-part Charter balance. The principle gives expression to proportionality but does not require a court to repeat a full Article 52 balance for every processing operation where Article 5(1)(c) is satisfied. Paragraph 116 confirms that a legislative basis under Article 6(3) must itself permit only suitable, strictly necessary and balanced processing.

For DAC8, the case directs the main facial argument to the field choices and safeguards in the legislative basis. It is adverse to a theory that every later CASP or authority operation independently requires a new complete constitutional balance.
