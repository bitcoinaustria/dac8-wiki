# Meldegesetz 1991, verified 19 September 2026

Selected statutory digest. The RIS page expressly displayed the consolidated version of 19 September 2026. Locators: § 18(2), (4)–(5) and § 20(3).

Under § 18(2), a registered person can obtain an Auskunftssperre by making a protected interest credible. An obvious interest permits an ex officio restriction or extension. A restriction or extension lasts at most five years and remains effective after deregistration during that period.

Section 18(4) requires revocation if used to evade legal obligations or if its basis disappears. Under § 18(5), an applicant proving entitlement to assert a legal obligation against the person can nevertheless obtain information; the person must first be notified and allowed to comment.

Section 20(3) separately permits provision of registration data to organs of territorial authorities where essential for statutory tasks. Linked queries must additionally be proportionate to their occasion and intended result.

These provisions protect a particular disclosure channel. They do not erase registration data or create an exception to tax reporting.
