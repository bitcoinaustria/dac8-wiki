# NIST63B-4: phishing resistance and separate recovery controls

Final31July2025; SP800-63B-4, §§3.2.5,4.1.2.1,4.2.1–4.2.3,4.6.

Phishing resistance cryptographically binds authentication to the verifier/session; manually entered OTPs are not phishing-resistant. Recovery and new-authenticator binding have separate requirements and notifications. AAL2 recovery specifies combinations or repeated proofing.

This is normative guidance within its scope, not Austrian law or measured effectiveness in a CASP. Login protection does not itself prevent a genuine user following deceptive transfer instructions or an insider exporting records. Mechanical credibility2: recent institutional primary; no peer-review score for a standard.
