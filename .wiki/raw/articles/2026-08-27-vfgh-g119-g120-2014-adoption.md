# VfGH G 119-120/2014: joint adoption

The applicants described their existing family, completed stepchild adoption, and concrete intention to adopt jointly. They explained why the ordinary placement process did not produce a reviewable refusal and why finding a child outside that system was not a reasonable detour.

The judgment is useful for linking a precise life situation and proposed legal act to equality and family-life objections rather than pleading only a generalized discriminatory rule.
