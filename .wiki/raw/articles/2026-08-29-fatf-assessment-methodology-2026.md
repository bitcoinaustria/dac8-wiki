# FATF assessment methodology 2026

The methodology separates technical compliance with the Forty Recommendations from effectiveness ratings across eleven Immediate Outcomes. Mutual evaluations and follow-up are peer-assessment mechanisms.

The ratings are institutional assessments, not fines, licence decisions or causal estimates of crime reduction. Technical adoption cannot be treated as proof of effectiveness.
