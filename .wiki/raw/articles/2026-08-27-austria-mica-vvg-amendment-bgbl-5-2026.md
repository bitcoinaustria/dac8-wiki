# Austrian MiCA-VVG amendment, BGBl. I No. 5/2026

The amendment adjusts Austrian MiCA supervision, including FMA data-controller and European Single Access Point functions. It does not displace the analytical distinction between MiCA supervisory records, TFR AML/CFT records, and Krypto-MPfG tax reporting.
