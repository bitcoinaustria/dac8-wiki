# German BFH IX R 36/21: CRS processing and deletion challenge

- Decision: 23 January 2024
- ECLI: ECLI:DE:BFH:2024:U.230124.IXR36.21.0

Taxpayers with a Swiss bank account and securities depot sought non-processing and deletion of CRS data received by Germany's BZSt. The BFH accepted that automatic exchange interferes with informational self-determination but held the interference justified by a foreseeable legal basis, equal taxation, and cross-border enforcement needs.

The court rejected abstract hacking-risk, no-suspicion, unequal-treatment, deletion, and objection arguments. It is non-binding in Austria but strong comparative adverse authority.
