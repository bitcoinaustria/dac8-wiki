# CJEU C-561/19 Consorzio: last-instance reference duty

## Case identity

- Court: CJEU Grand Chamber
- Case: `C-561/19`
- Judgment: 6 October 2021
- ECLI: `EU:C:2021:799`

## Rule captured

A last-instance national court may decline to refer where the EU-law question is irrelevant to resolving the dispute, the CJEU has already interpreted the provision, or the correct interpretation is so obvious that no reasonable doubt remains after considering EU law's multilingual and autonomous characteristics and divergence risk.

If the court declines, its reasoning must show which exception applies. The duty does not mean a party can compel referral merely by asking; the question still must matter to the case and be properly raised.
