# Prosecution account, 6 May 2026

Selected digest of the pre-judgment sentencing submission announcement. It describes population-register extracts concerning six family members found with another suspect and an address query preceding a shooting at that address.

Crucial limit: the prosecution expressly did not charge complicity in the attacks because it could not sufficiently prove that the employee knew the data would be used for them. Her own extortion is a separate allegation, subsequently addressed in the court's 27 May release. An investigation's linkage observations are not a judicial finding of attack complicity.
