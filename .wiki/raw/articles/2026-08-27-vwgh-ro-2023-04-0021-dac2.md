# VwGH Ro 2023/04/0021: final Austrian DAC2 revision

- Decision: 10 December 2025
- File: Ro 2023/04/0021-12
- Outcome: revision rejected as inadmissible; no Article 267 reference

The VwGH held that neither the BVwG's general statement about missing case law nor the revision's formulation identified a concrete, outcome-determinative question of fundamental importance. It also found the bank-secrecy and domestic-account-comparator issues clear or abstract.

The VwGH joined the VfGH's Charter assessment and found no basis to refer the proposed validity questions. It stressed that a large number of affected people does not itself create a legal question of fundamental importance.

This decision is procedurally important: a reference request needs an exact, decision-relevant EU-law question, not a list of abstract objections.
