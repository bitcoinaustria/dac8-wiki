# ECtHR G.S.B. v Switzerland on tax information exchange

- Court: European Court of Human Rights, Third Section
- Application: `28601/11`
- Judgment: 22 December 2015

The Court accepted tax compliance and international cooperation as legitimate aims and found no violation where bank information was transmitted within a legal framework with procedural safeguards and review.

This is serious counterauthority to treating all automatic tax-information exchange as inherently disproportionate. DAC8 must be distinguished, if at all, by population breadth, data scope, aggregation, retention, onward exchange, physical-security implications, or weaker effective remedies.
