# Austria IFG: existing security records, exceptions and partial access

Selected-source digest, not a full-text reproduction. The official bilingual compilation identifies its edition as 1 December 2025. Relevant provisions were checked against separately indexed RIS texts on 19 September 2026; no later amendment to the selected mechanisms was identified. This is not evidence that any DAC8 document exists.

## Enacted mechanisms

- Sections 2(1) and 3(2): existing, available records serving official or business purposes; competence follows the organ's sphere of activity.
- Section 7(2): identify the information as precisely as possible. Section 8: access or secrecy refusal within four weeks; a reasoned extension of four weeks is possible.
- Section 6(1): necessary, proportionate secrecy after balancing competing interests. Relevant grounds include national/public security, unaffected decision preparation or proceedings, personal data and business secrets.
- Sections 6(2) and 9(2): disclose separable unprotected parts where possible without disproportionate effort. Section 9(3) also limits abusive or unreasonably disruptive demands.
- Section 10: hear affected third parties where possible, subject to its rights-based exception.
- Section 11(1): following non-disclosure, a written request for a decision triggers a two-month decision period. Section 16 preserves special statutory access regimes.

## Corroboration and limits

The [BKA circular of 10 January 2025, section on the information concept](https://ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Erlaesse/ERL_BKA_20250110_2025_0_015_115/ERL_BKA_20250110_2025_0_015_115.html) distinguishes existing records from new investigations, research, special preparation or explanations needed to create information. It is administrative guidance, not a judicial holding. Neither the IFG nor that circular proves entitlement to an unredacted security report.
