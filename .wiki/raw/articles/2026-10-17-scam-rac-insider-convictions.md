# RAC insider convictions: extraction, resale and detection

Published 9 October 2024; opening substantive account and investigation paragraphs.

Two customer-service specialists copied and sold over 29,500 lines of accident-related information. New monitoring detected access/copying; subsequent phone evidence showed onward sharing and payment. Both pleaded guilty and received suspended six-month sentences and unpaid work.

Lines are not unique people. This source proves extraction/resale and a working detection/prosecution response, not downstream phishing losses or assault. The later confiscation record concerns the same event. Mechanical credibility 2: recent institutional primary.
