# CJEU C-127/07 Arcelor on equality, evidence and stepwise regulation

In C-127/07, judgment of 16 December 2008, the Grand Chamber held that comparability must be assessed in light of all characteristics of the situations and, in particular, the subject matter and purpose of the act. Different treatment must rest on an objective and reasonable criterion related to a lawful aim and be proportionate. For an EU legislative act, the legislature must identify the objective criteria and supply the Court with information permitting their verification (paragraphs 23 to 26 and 47 to 48).

The Court also recognised broad discretion where political, economic and social choices or complex evaluations are required. A new and complex system may be introduced step by step in light of implementation experience. Review asks whether the choice is manifestly inappropriate, not whether another measure would be preferable (paragraphs 57 to 63 and 74).

This cuts both ways for DAC8. It supports a demand for a concrete, evidence-linked reason for a field or comparator distinction. It also gives the EU legislature substantial room to single out crypto-assets, use administrable categories and phase a complex reporting system.
