# Regulation (EU) 2024/1624

The Regulation is in force but generally applies from 10 July 2027. Article 19 requires CDD for a business relationship and specified occasional transactions; CASPs must perform full CDD for an occasional transaction of at least EUR 1,000 and at least identity verification below that threshold. Article 20 covers identification, beneficial ownership, purpose, business profile, sanctions screening, and ongoing monitoring.

Articles 37 to 40 address correspondent relationships, shell institutions, and risk controls for transfers involving self-hosted addresses. Article 79 prohibits anonymous crypto-asset accounts or accounts enabling anonymisation or obfuscation at obliged entities; it is not a ban on personal self-custody.

Articles 69, 71, 73, and 75 govern suspicious-transaction reports, FIU instructions, confidentiality, and controlled information-sharing partnerships. Articles 76 and 77 regulate sensitive-data processing and require five-year retention, with only a case-specific further period of up to five years. Article 90 fixes general application at 10 July 2027.
