# CJEU C-57/23 Policejní prezidium

Judgment of 20 November 2025, ECLI:EU:C:2025:905.

Paragraphs 108–110 hold, in the context of Directive (EU) 2016/680 and biometric and genetic police data, that legislation need not invariably prescribe a fixed maximum storage period if appropriate review periods ensure that every continuation remains strictly necessary.

The holding is adverse to a categorical claim that the absence of a shorter statutory maximum is invalid by itself. It also makes the reality and quality of periodic necessity review material. The case concerns the Law Enforcement Directive, not the GDPR or tax cooperation, so its transfer to DAC8 is analogous rather than automatic.
