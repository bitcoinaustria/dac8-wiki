# VwGH 2025 average duration

The VwGH's official facts and figures report an average duration of 5.2 months for proceedings completed in 2025.

This is an institution-wide historical average, not a statutory deadline or a forecast for a particular revision. A case involving a preliminary reference or unusual procedural complexity may take materially longer.
