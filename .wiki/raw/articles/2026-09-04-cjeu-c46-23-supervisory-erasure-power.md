# CJEU C-46/23 on supervisory-authority erasure powers

In C-46/23, judgment of 14 March 2024, the CJEU held that GDPR Article 58(2)(d) and (g) empower a national supervisory authority to order the controller or processor to erase unlawfully processed personal data even without a prior Article 17 request by the data subject. The power applies to data collected from the person and data obtained elsewhere (paragraphs 35 to 53 and the operative part).

The power is not confined to the complainant's own requested wording and can address unlawful processing affecting other persons. It does not make erasure automatic. The DSB must establish unlawfulness and select an appropriate corrective measure. A continuing valid statutory duty remains relevant, including the Article 17(3)(b) exception.
