# Article 140 B-VG: Austrian statutory review

Article 140(1)(1)(c) authorises an application by a person who claims direct rights injury from an unconstitutional statute where the statute has become effective for that person without a judicial decision or administrative act. Other paragraphs govern the Court's review perimeter and repeal or declaration powers.

The constitutional text is only the entry point; the VfGH's strict case law supplies the current, direct-effect and subsidiarity requirements.
