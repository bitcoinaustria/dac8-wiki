# Villach-Land 2023: in-person Bitcoin extortion by a fake police officer

Source digest. ORF Kärnten, "Falscher deutscher Polizist festgenommen", 30 June 2023. Read on 29 September 2026. The article reports police information.

**Facts as reported.** On 15 June 2023 a 55-year-old German citizen rang at a house in the Villach-Land district. He presented a forged ID as an officer of the German Bundeskriminalamt. He told the 28-year-old homeowner, with his wife and daughter present, that the BKA had intercepted an email in which a foreign client had ordered the family's murder. The stated reason was "ein Kryptowährungsgeschäft, bei welchem der Mann den arabischen Auftraggeber betrogen hätte" (a cryptocurrency deal in which the man had allegedly cheated the client). The murder order "könne durch eine Zahlung von 60 Bitcoins (Gegenwert von ca. 1,5 Millionen Euro) verhindert werden" (could be averted by paying 60 bitcoins, about €1.5 million).

Police identified the man. On 28 June 2023 he was arrested in a hotel in Velden under a court-ordered warrant, with Cobra special forces involved, and taken to the Justizanstalt Klagenfurt. He was "teilgeständig" (partly confessed). Investigators believe he acted alone and were looking into possible further offences elsewhere in Europe. According to police, he had appeared in similar cases in Germany without being arrested.
