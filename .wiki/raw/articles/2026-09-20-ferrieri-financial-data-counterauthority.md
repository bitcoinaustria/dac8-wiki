# Ferrieri and Bonassisa v Italy: adverse sensitivity finding

Judgment 8 January 2026; final 11 May 2026. [Official full PDF](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/app/conversion/docx/pdf?library=ECHR&id=001-247632&filename=CASE%20OF%20FERRIERI%20AND%20BONASSISA%20v.%20ITALY.pdf), inspected with paragraph locators.

Paragraph 58 treats purely financial bank information as not warranting enhanced protection and preserves a broad state margin. Paragraphs 105–107 locate the violation in deficient legal constraints and safeguards and leave the remaining Article 8(2) requirements undecided.

This supplements, without replacing, the 4 September source digest. Any claim of greater sensitivity through ledger linkage requires separate facts; the judgment does not establish that financial profiling generally equals communications surveillance.
