# Article 267 TFEU: preliminary rulings

Article 267 gives the CJEU jurisdiction to rule on treaty interpretation and on the validity and interpretation of acts of EU institutions, bodies, offices, or agencies.

A Member-State court may refer a question when it considers an answer necessary to give judgment. A court against whose decisions there is no judicial remedy under national law must refer a necessary EU-law question, subject to the limited exceptions developed by the CJEU.

## Research use

The party proposes questions, but the national court decides whether they are necessary and how to frame them. Article 267 is not a direct appeal by the citizen to the CJEU.
