# ECtHR Rule 39: exceptional interim measures

Rule 39 measures are exceptional. The applicant must show an imminent risk of irreparable harm and submit a complete, documented request in time for the Court to act. The Court's guidance identifies removal and comparable risks to life or ill-treatment as the typical cases; other rights can qualify only exceptionally.

No primary authority was located establishing that an ordinary DAC8 data transmission meets this threshold. Rule 39 must therefore not be presented as a realistic routine reporting stay.
