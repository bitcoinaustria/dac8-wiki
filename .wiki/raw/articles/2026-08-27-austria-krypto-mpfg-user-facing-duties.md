# Austria Krypto-MPfG sections 20, 36 and 38: user-facing operation

## Section 20

Reporting providers must determine, collect, store, and process reportable information for all customers, transactions, and crypto-assets to identify reportable accounts and information. Before the first report, the provider must inform a user where personal data are covered by the reporting duty.

## Section 36

A natural-person user's self-certification is valid only if signed or otherwise positively confirmed, dated, and containing name, address, state or states of tax residence, tax identification number for each relevant state, and birth date.

## Section 38

If required section 18 information is missing, the provider must request it after the reporting period and issue two written reminders if it is not supplied. After the statutory waiting and reminder periods, the provider must prevent the user from conducting reportable transactions until the information is supplied.

## Research use

These provisions are more directly relevant to an individual applicant than provider-only reporting and penalty rules. A real admissibility analysis must document which step has already occurred for the applicant and test whether the interference is current, direct, and unavoidable without an intervening decision.
