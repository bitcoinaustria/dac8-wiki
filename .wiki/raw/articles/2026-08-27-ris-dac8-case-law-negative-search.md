# RIS exact-term DAC8 search: no indexed case-law result

The official RIS cross-database search for `DAC8`, run on 27 August 2026, returned four records: three authenticated federal-gazette records and one consultation-draft record. It contained no result classified as VfGH, VwGH, BVwG, DSB or ordinary-court case law.

This negative search is limited to the official index, exact term and stated date. It does not exclude unpublished, differently worded or not-yet-indexed proceedings.
