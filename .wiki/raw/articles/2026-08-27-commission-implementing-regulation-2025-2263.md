# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2263

- Issuer: European Commission
- Adopted: 12 November 2025
- Published: 26 November 2025

The regulation implements technical registration, identification, assessment, statistical, and computerised-format elements of the amended DAC framework. It matters if a practical field or data-flow challenge turns on the technical schema rather than the directive or Austrian statute.
