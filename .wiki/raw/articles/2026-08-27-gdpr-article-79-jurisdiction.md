# GDPR Article 79: judicial remedy and forum

Article 79(1) grants the data subject an effective judicial remedy against a controller or processor without prejudice to available administrative or non-judicial remedies, including the Article 77 supervisory-authority complaint.

Under Article 79(2), proceedings may be brought before the courts of the Member State where the controller or processor has an establishment. Alternatively, they may generally be brought in the Member State of the data subject's habitual residence. That alternative does not apply where the defendant is a Member State public authority acting in the exercise of public powers. The provision selects the Member State forum; Austrian internal jurisdiction and competence still depend on the pleaded claim and national procedural law.
