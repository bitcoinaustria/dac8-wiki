# GAO2007: selected breaches and limits of identity-theft attribution

GAO-07-737, dated4June2007, publicly released5July2007. Results in Brief and selected-breach investigation discussion.

This is not a representative4/24 harm rate or proof of18 harmless breaches. It concerns identity theft, not every possible privacy or targeting consequence, and is too old for current prevalence.
