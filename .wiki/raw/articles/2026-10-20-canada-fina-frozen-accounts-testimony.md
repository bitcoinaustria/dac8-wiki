# FINA Evidence No. 21, 22 February 2022

House of Commons Standing Committee on Finance, 44th Parliament, 1st Session, Evidence number 021, Tuesday 22 February 2022; 32-page PDF via Government of Canada Publications, retrieved 20 October 2026 (the archive serves an interstitial page before the PDF).

## Testimony (Isabelle Jacques, Assistant Deputy Minister, Financial Sector Policy Branch, Department of Finance)

Printed p. 9: "to my knowledge, I would say it's over 206 accounts. For the total value, the latest number I have is approximately $7.8 million." She added that the numbers "must be lower today" because banks were unfreezing accounts. Elsewhere in the same meeting: "I think it's over 206 or maybe 210." She said it would be "unlikely … but not impossible" that a small donor had been captured by a freeze, and that the government gave institutions no written guidelines.

## Limits

Interim, oral estimates on the day after the measures began to be lifted; superseded by the later committee figures in [DEDC Report 3](../papers/2026-10-20-canada-dedc-report-financial-measures.md) (about 280 financial products, CAD 8 million).
