# Court release, 27 May 2026

Selected digest. Rechtbank Overijssel, [ECLI:NL:RBOVE:2026:2805](https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/details?id=ECLI:NL:RBOVE:2026:2805), official court summary rather than full-judgment extraction.

An employee used her own court-system credentials to obtain and sell confidential information. She also used information about a man and his family in her own extortion, including threats of violence against relatives. He paid more than EUR 92,000. The court imposed four years' imprisonment, one suspended.

The release separately mentions indications linking sold information to attempted murder, a shooting and explosions. It does not establish her complicity in those attacks. Finality is unverified. No crypto or tax-reporting dataset is identified.
