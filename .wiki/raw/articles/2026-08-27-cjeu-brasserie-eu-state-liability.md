# CJEU C-46/93 and C-48/93 Brasserie: EU state-liability conditions

The joined judgment confirms that Member State liability can extend to legislative breaches of EU law. Compensation requires that the infringed EU rule be intended to confer rights on individuals, that the breach be sufficiently serious, and that there be a direct causal link between the breach and the loss sustained.

National liability rules govern the action subject to equivalence and effectiveness. The remedy is compensation for proved loss; it is not an abstract action to invalidate legislation and does not itself suspend reporting or transmission.
