# Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 on crypto-asset transfer information

Articles 14–18 require defined originator and beneficiary information, including names, relevant account or DLT identifiers, and additional identification fields. For a transfer exceeding EUR 1,000 to or from a self-hosted address, the CASP must assess whether the address is effectively owned or controlled by the customer.

Article 25 subjects the processing to EU data-protection law and confines it to anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing purposes; incompatible further processing and commercial use are prohibited. Article 26 provides a five-year retention period, with a national extension of up to a further five years only after a necessity and proportionality assessment for the AML/CFT purpose.

The TFR therefore explains why CASPs already hold significant transfer data, but does not make that dataset freely reusable for tax profiling.
