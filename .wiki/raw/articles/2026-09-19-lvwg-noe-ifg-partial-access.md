# LVwG Niederösterreich: document-specific balancing and IFG redaction

Decision of 23 February 2026, LVwG-AV-55/001-2026, ECLI:AT:LVWGNI:2026:LVwG.AV.55.001.2026. Selected holding digest verified in the official HTML; no binary downloaded.

## Holding and posture

The dispute concerned a rescue-services contract and annexes. In reasons 2.3, the court found that generic assertions about personal data, business secrets and internal organisational details did not constitute a concrete, document-specific balancing exercise.

Sections 6(2) and 9(2) IFG require examination of partial disclosure. Confidential parts cannot alone justify withholding the entire document. Reasons 2.3 expressly distinguish older environmental-information case law: under the IFG, active redaction of existing information is contemplated where possible and proportionate. The authority had not explained why the contract's sections and annexes were inseparable or redaction disproportionately burdensome.

The court set aside and remitted for missing fundamental investigative steps. It did not order release of the complete contract. Reasons 2.4 rejected abuse on the facts; 2.5 excluded ordinary revision. Further extraordinary-review history was not verified.

## Transfer boundary

This is a method for examining secrecy and partial access, not a holding that a DAC8 DPIA or security audit must be disclosed. Security-sensitive passages and third-party interests still require individual assessment.
