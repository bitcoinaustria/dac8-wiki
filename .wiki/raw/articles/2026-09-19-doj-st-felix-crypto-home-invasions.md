# St Felix crypto home-invasion convictions

Source digest. DOJ press release 24-1151, 13 September 2024, updated 6 February 2025. Locators: sentencing opening and paragraphs on Homestead, Little Elm and Durham.

The DOJ reports twelve sentences and a 47-year sentence for Remy Ra St Felix. Its account, citing trial evidence and court documents, describes violence against crypto holders and relatives. In Durham, unauthorized access to a couple's email preceded an April 2023 home invasion and forced access to an exchange account, with over USD 150,000 stolen in crypto.

This supports digitally assisted physical coercion and harm extending to relatives. It does not identify a tax dataset or show that a bulk leak caused victim selection. The overall USD 3.5 million figure combines SIM swapping and home invasions and is not a physical-attack-only loss total. No original judgment was inspected in this ingestion.
