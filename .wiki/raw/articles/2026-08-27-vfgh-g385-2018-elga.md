# VfGH G 385/2018: ELGA Individualantrag

The ELGA application failed on admissibility. The applicant's pleaded processing was not yet technically operative for the capacities asserted, some relevant participant-right provisions were not attacked, and the DSB route was an available way to bring the statutory objections through the BVwG to the VfGH.

For DAC8, the claimant must prove operative processing rather than future exposure and map the exact inseparable provisions.
