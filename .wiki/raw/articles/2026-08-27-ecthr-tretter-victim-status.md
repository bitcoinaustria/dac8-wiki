# ECtHR Tretter and Others: potential victim status and available remedies

The Court reiterated that the Convention does not allow abstract review of legislation. In secret-measures cases a potential victim may qualify in specific circumstances, but the availability of effective remedies and the applicant's personal risk are central.

The applicants had not used the available access, data-protection and judicial mechanisms and had shown no particular personal or professional risk. The Court therefore declared the complaint incompatible ratione personae for lack of victim status. It expressly did not decide the separate non-exhaustion objection.

Relevant paragraphs: 60-70 and 80-82.
