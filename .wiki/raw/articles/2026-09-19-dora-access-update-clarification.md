# DORA access-right updates

Independent source check corrects the terminology of the [initial digest](2026-09-19-dora-detailed-access-controls.md), retained as ingestion history. Article 21(e)(iv) specifies updates when changes are necessary and at least annually for other ICT systems, or every six months for systems supporting critical or important functions. Article 21(e)(i) separately assigns responsibility for granting, reviewing and revoking rights.

Calling the specified periodic duty merely a review understates the wording. The source still requires scoped interpretation; these are not deadlines for notifying data subjects or deleting retained tax records. The initial digest's other control and scope descriptions are unaffected.
