# Council Decision 2004/587/EC on the application date of the Savings Directive

- Council Decision of 19 July 2004 on the date of application of Directive 2003/48/EC (2004/587/EC); OJ L 257, 4.8.2004, p. 7; CELEX `32004D0587`
- Adopted under Article 17(3) of Directive 2003/48/EC after the Council concluded that the Article 17(2) conditions (equivalent measures in Switzerland, Liechtenstein, San Marino, Monaco, Andorra and the dependent or associated territories) would not be met by 1 January 2005.
- Article 1 replaces "1 January 2005" with "1 July 2005" in Article 17(2); the new date remained subject to the same conditions.

Directive 2014/48/EU (recital 1) and Directive (EU) 2015/2060 (recital 1) both confirm that the Savings Directive has applied in the Member States since 1 July 2005. For the timeline, 1 July 2005 is the verified start of Savings Directive reporting and withholding.
