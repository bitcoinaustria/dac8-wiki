# Epsilon trial and sentencing: data-selected fraud and repeated losses

DOJ District of Colorado release, 30 September 2024. Locators: opening, trial-evidence and compensation paragraphs.

Two employees received 120- and 48-month sentences following jury convictions. Prosecutors describe transaction-derived targeting models and nearly one hundred lists supplied to one fraud client: over 218,000 victims, over USD 23.7 million lost, including over 12,000 victims defrauded more than twenty times each. These are trial-evidence figures, not complaint totals.

The release separately reports USD 122 million returned to over 200,000 victims through corporate compensation. Do not subtract or equate overlapping populations. This was postal fraud, not crypto phishing or a hack. Judgment text and appeal finality were not independently checked. Mechanical score 2: recent institutional prosecution account.
