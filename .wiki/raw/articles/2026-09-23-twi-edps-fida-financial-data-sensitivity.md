# EDPS Opinion 38/2023 on the Proposal for a Regulation on a framework for Financial Data Access

European Data Protection Supervisor, 22 August 2023, on the Commission proposal COM(2023) 360 (FIDA). Status: advisory opinion under Article 42(1) of Regulation (EU) 2018/1725; not binding on the co-legislators. The full PDF was downloaded and its text inspected.

Paragraph 12 states that personal financial data processed by payment service providers, insurers, pension providers and other financial institutions are inherently sensitive, and welcomes the exclusion from FIDA of payment-account data, health/life insurance data and consumer creditworthiness data. Footnote 41 supports this by quoting paragraph 52 of EDPB Guidelines 06/2020 (financial transactions can reveal sensitive information, including special categories). Paragraph 13 adds that in-scope customer data (pensions, mortgage credit) may still be highly sensitive, and that combining mortgage data with insurance and payment-account data could lead to unfair discrimination.

Paragraph 14 states that access to such data interferes with the rights to privacy and data protection and may create risks of financial exclusion through price discrimination or refusal of products. Paragraphs 15–16 call, in line with data minimisation, for clearly circumscribed data categories and for explicit exclusion from "customer data" of data a holder derives or infers through profiling. Paragraph 32 notes (with recital 18 FIDA) that combining traditional and new data sources can enable more comprehensive analysis of vulnerable segments such as low-income persons; paragraphs 33–34 ask for limits on combining FIDA data with other sources.

This establishes that the EU institutional data protection supervisor treats personal financial data as sensitive by nature and views derived financial profiles as a distinct risk category warranting exclusion. It does NOT establish that DAC8 annual aggregates are equally sensitive: the opinion concerns detailed customer data shared with private data users for commercial purposes, not reporting to tax authorities under a legal obligation, and it does not assess aggregated per-asset totals.
