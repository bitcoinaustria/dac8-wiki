# Austrian BAO section 245: one-month tax appeal period

In the current official RIS consolidation, section 245(1) BAO provides that the complaint period is one month. If the decision announces that reasons will follow, the period does not begin before the missing reasons are notified or the authority states that the announcement is obsolete. Section 245(3) permits the tax authority to extend the period on application for relevant reasons and regulates suspension of the running period.

This is the specific starting rule for a later tax-assessment route to the BFG. It must not be replaced by the ordinary four-week VwGVG period used for a DSB Bescheid complaint to the BVwG.
