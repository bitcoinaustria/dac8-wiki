# C-184/20, OT v Vyriausioji tarnybinės etikos komisija

Judgment of 1 August 2022, ECLI:EU:C:2022:601. Official full text inspected.

Paragraphs 119–128 establish that comparison or deduction can reveal protected sensitive information even where the input is not inherently sensitive. The operative ruling concerns online publication of declaration data capable of indirectly revealing sexual orientation.

This supports examining demonstrated sensitive inferences from linked payment data. It does not establish that all financial records fall within Article 9, that a payment proves a person's beliefs or health, or that confidential provider storage is equivalent to public disclosure. Application to Bitcoin requires a separate factual inference chain and assessment of any applicable Article 9(2) exception.
