# FCA: The Hidden Cost of Domestic Financial Abuse

Joanna Legg, FCA, 17 May 2024; sections on product manipulation, joint working and consumer outcomes. Describes fraudulent loans in a victim's name, account takeover and threats involving joint insurance; discusses safe spaces, specialist help, emergency funds and blocking abusive payment messages. This is institutional commentary, not a new representative survey or controlled evaluation. The article's externally sourced prevalence figures are not FCA sample estimates. Banks can need properly bounded information to detect abuse and restore control: indiscriminate deletion is not the proposed remedy. Mechanical credibility 2: recognised regulator +1, recent +1.
