# FCA recovery-room warning: victim details sold or reused

First published 4 December 2019; updated 19 January 2026. Locator: how recovery-room scams work.

The FCA describes previous investment victims receiving recovery offers from original operators under another identity, or from others who bought their details. Approaches use authority impersonation, urgency and advance charges labelled tax, legal or administrative fees. Clone firms borrow genuine firm identifiers.

This is regulator-described practice, not a named breach investigation or measured prevalence. Prior knowledge does not establish access to a genuine regulator's records. The 2026 edit removed obsolete campaign references; it is not the date the mechanism was discovered.
