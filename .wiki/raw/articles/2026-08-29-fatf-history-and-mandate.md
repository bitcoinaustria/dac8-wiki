# FATF history and mandate

The G7 established FATF in 1989 to examine and develop measures against money laundering. FATF issued its first Forty Recommendations in 1990, added terrorist financing to its mandate in 2001, consolidated AML/CFT and proliferation-financing standards in 2012 and extended its standards to virtual assets and VASPs from 2018–2019.

The institutional history establishes chronology. FATF recommendations remain international standards rather than a treaty or a directly enforceable source of duties for private persons.
