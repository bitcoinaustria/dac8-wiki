# VfGH G 147/08 ua: failed direct challenge to SPG data powers

The applicants challenged security-police data powers before any concrete processing was established. The VfGH rejected the applications: the provisions enabled later measures rather than themselves creating a current retention duty, and data-access, deletion, complaint, and system-review routes were available.

The decision is the adverse baseline distinguished by the successful 2014 retention judgment and later reviewed in Tretter and Others v Austria.
