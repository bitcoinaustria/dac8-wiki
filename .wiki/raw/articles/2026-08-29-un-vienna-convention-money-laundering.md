# UN Vienna Convention 1988

Articles 3 and 5 require parties to criminalise conversion, transfer and concealment of drug proceeds and to provide confiscation measures. The treaty is an origin point for international money-laundering law, focused on drug proceeds rather than the later all-crimes preventive architecture.

It does not itself prescribe the modern package of customer due diligence, beneficial-owner identification, ongoing monitoring and suspicious-transaction reporting.
