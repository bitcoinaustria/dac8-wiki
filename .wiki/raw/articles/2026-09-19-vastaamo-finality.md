# Finnish Supreme Court R1/2026/921

Selected digest of the official Swedish-language release, 13 July 2026. Leave to appeal the Helsinki Court of Appeal's Vastaamo judgment was refused. The appellate outcome therefore stands. This updates procedural finality, not the evidential scope of the underlying breach and extortion findings, and is not a new Supreme Court merits judgment.
