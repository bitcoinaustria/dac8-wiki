# KBM admissions: cross-client response modelling and governance override

Filed 14 June 2021, case 21-cr-00198-PAB, document 3-1. Official PDF: agreement §2; Attachment B §§2,5,8,12(b), printed B-1–B-7 (PDF pp.42–48).

KBM admitted combining client interaction data to identify likely responders, supplying known fraudulent clients from legitimate and fraudulent businesses, and acquiring fraud-client data that enhanced modelling for both. Sales compensation and data acquisition were admitted incentives. Management obtained approval after finance initially rejected a client on fraud warnings.

This is an admitted deliberate-use mechanism, not a hack or a trial conviction. Over 100 million households describes the cooperative database, not proven loss victims. Original collection's GDPR lawfulness is not established. Mechanical credibility 1: older institutional primary, accepted for unique admitted feedback and governance facts. Status is separately sourced.
