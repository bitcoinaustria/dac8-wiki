# OGH 6 Ob 37/24w: preventive GDPR injunction issue suspended for C-655/23

In its order of 27 August 2024, the OGH suspended proceedings seeking to prevent renewed unlawful disclosure of personal data without requesting erasure. It held that the pending CJEU questions in C-655/23 were prejudicial because an answer excluding both an EU-law claim and recourse to national law could remove the legal basis for the claim.

The order records a genuine Austrian civil route but no merits resolution. C-655/23 later held that the GDPR does not itself require the asserted special preventive injunction, while leaving Member States free to provide one. As of 27 August 2026, no later OGH merits decision resolving this proceeding was located in the official database.
