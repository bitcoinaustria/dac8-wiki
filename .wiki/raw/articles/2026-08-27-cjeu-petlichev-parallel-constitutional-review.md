# CJEU Petlichev: ordinary courts retain Article 267 powers despite constitutional review

In Case C-56/25, *Petlichev*, judgment of 12 February 2026, the CJEU held that an ordinary national court does not lose its power or, where applicable, duty to make an Article 267 reference because constitutional review is available or pending. The ordinary court must assess EU compatibility and, if necessary, disapply conflicting national law without awaiting constitutional annulment. Constitutional courts are also subject to Article 267, while only the CJEU may declare an EU act invalid.

Relevant passages: paragraphs 33-49 and 55-60.

The judgment is strong structural counterauthority to any claim that the VfGH must be reached first or exclusively. It does not give a party power to compel a reference and does not cure an inadmissible underlying DSB/BVwG or civil case.
