# Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 on markets in crypto-assets (MiCA)

MiCA Article 59 requires Union CASPs to be authorised under Article 63 or entitled through the regulated-entity notification route in Article 60. Article 65 routes cross-border service notification through the home-state authority; it does not create a new host-state authorisation.

Article 68(9) requires records of all services, activities, orders, and transactions for supervisory, client-protection, and market-integrity purposes. The default period is five years and may reach seven years following a competent-authority request. That existing recordkeeping is a serious State counterargument, but it does not itself supply a tax-reporting purpose for universal Krypto-MPfG processing.

Article 73 keeps an outsourcing CASP responsible and requires Union data-protection compliance. Article 143 permitted Member States to shorten the transitional period otherwise available until 1 July 2026.
