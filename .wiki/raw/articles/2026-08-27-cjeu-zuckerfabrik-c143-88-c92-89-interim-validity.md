# CJEU Zuckerfabrik: strict interim suspension conditions where EU validity is challenged

In Joined Cases C-143/88 and C-92/89, *Zuckerfabrik Süderdithmarschen and Zuckerfabrik Soest*, judgment of 21 February 1991, ECLI:EU:C:1991:65, the CJEU held that a national court may suspend enforcement of a national administrative measure based on an EU regulation while the regulation's validity is challenged.

The court must have serious doubts about validity, refer the validity question to the CJEU unless it is already pending, find urgency and a threat of serious and irreparable damage, and give due regard to the Union interest. The CJEU later applied these conditions to positive interim orders in *Atlanta*.

Relevant passages: paragraphs 20-33 and the operative part.

The judgment confirms that interim protection against implementation of EU law is possible, but not as an independent action and not on an abstract claim of privacy risk.
