# Cloudflare2022: key deployment, migration and recovery exceptions

Published29September2022. Locators “The beginning: selective security key enforcement”, “The end of optional”, “What we’ve learned”.

Rollout began2018; selective enforcement July2020, other methods disabled February2021. More than600 internal sites reportedly followed the model. Application/workflow adaptation was necessary, and an offline alternate recovery process remained for lockouts/lost keys. Multiple keys reduced its need. No monetary cost or recovery false-acceptance rate is supplied. Do not repeat universal phishing-proof marketing claims. First-party implementation, not independent adequacy proof.

Mechanical credibility 0; independently assessed. Historical and vendor-primary records are accepted for their unique bounded incident/implementation evidence, not population rates. No private dataset or victim record was collected.
