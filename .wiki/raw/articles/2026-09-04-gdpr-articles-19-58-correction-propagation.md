# GDPR Articles 19 and 58 on correction propagation and corrective powers

GDPR Article 19 requires a controller to communicate a correction, erasure or restriction carried out under Articles 16, 17(1) or 18 to each recipient, unless impossible or disproportionate, and to inform the data subject about recipients on request.

Article 58(2) authorises the supervisory authority to order compliance with data-subject rights, correction, erasure or restriction and notification of recipients under Article 19, and to impose temporary or definitive processing limitations including a ban. These are corrective powers for established infringements, not free-standing entitlements to erase records that remain subject to a valid statutory duty.

The DAC8 XML correction and deletion message types make a propagation request technically concrete. They do not remove the need to establish the underlying right at each controller and data-copy layer.
