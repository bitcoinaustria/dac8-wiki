# OECD Global Forum CARF commitments (14 September 2026)

## Content (fetched 30 September 2026)

- **First exchanges by 2027 (46):** includes 26 EU Member States, every one except Cyprus: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovak Republic, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden. Also Brazil, the Cayman Islands, Chile, Japan, Korea, Liechtenstein, Norway, the United Kingdom and others.
- **By 2028 (27):** includes Cyprus, Australia, Canada, Hong Kong (China), Singapore, Switzerland, Türkiye and the United Arab Emirates.
- **By 2029 (4):** Argentina, Azerbaijan, Mexico, United States.
- **Relevant but not yet committed (4):** El Salvador, Georgia, India, Viet Nam.

## Changes compared with earlier lists

The archived list of 17 June 2025 showed 52 jurisdictions for 2027 and 17 for 2028. Cyprus, Switzerland, Canada, Mexico and Azerbaijan were then in the 2027 group, and the United States in the 2028 group; Hungary did not appear. The 2025 CARF Monitoring and Implementation Update (data as of 28 November 2025) counted 75 committed jurisdictions. Secondary sources (for example KoinX, gov.je) report a 23 June 2026 version with 76 jurisdictions; that version was not retrieved.

## Relevance and limits

The list records political commitments under the Global Forum process, not legal obligations. Among EU Member States, DAC8 (not this list) sets the binding first exchange by 30 September 2027. Cyprus's 2028 timing on the OECD list therefore concerns exchanges with non-EU partners under the CARF MCAA and is not a DAC8 deferral. The reason for the change is not stated. The Commission opened DAC8 infringement procedures against Cyprus and 11 other Member States (`2026-09-29-refreshat-commission-dac8-infringement-status.md`).
