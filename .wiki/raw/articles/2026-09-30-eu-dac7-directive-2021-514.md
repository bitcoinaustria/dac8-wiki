# Council Directive (EU) 2021/514 (DAC7, digital platforms)

## Source identity

- Council Directive (EU) 2021/514 of 22 March 2021 amending Directive 2011/16/EU on administrative cooperation in the field of taxation; CELEX `32021L0514`; OJ L 104, 25.3.2021, p. 1
- Entry into force 14 April 2021 (Cellar metadata)
- Transposition by 31 December 2022; application from 1 January 2023 (Article 2(1)). The joint-audit definition (Article 3(26)) and Section IIa (joint audits) are to be transposed by 31 December 2023 and applied from 1 January 2024 at the latest (Article 2(2)).
- Commission proposal COM(2020) 314 of 15 July 2020

## Content

The recitals point to the growth of the digitalised economy, the difficulty for tax administrations of detecting income earned through platforms, particularly platforms established elsewhere, and the OECD Model Rules for Reporting by Platform Operators with respect to Sellers in the Sharing and Gig Economy.

New Article 8ac and Annex V require Reporting Platform Operators, including non-EU operators facilitating activity in the Union, to perform due diligence on Sellers and report on Reportable Sellers by 31 January of the following year, with the information also given to the Seller. Relevant Activities are rental of immovable property, personal services, sale of goods and rental of any mode of transport. Sellers of goods with fewer than 30 sales and no more than EUR 2 000 consideration per period are excluded. Reported data include identity, TIN, VAT number, date of birth for individuals, the Financial Account Identifier where available, and quarterly consideration and number of activities. The competent authority exchanges the information within two months after the end of the Reportable Period; first exchange for Reportable Periods from 1 January 2023.

Other changes: a definition and minimum content of requests including requests for groups of taxpayers not identified individually, based on foreseeable relevance; royalties added to the Article 8(1) categories; TIN inclusion for taxable periods from 1 January 2024; a data breach definition and procedure allowing suspension of exchanges (Article 25); and a legal framework for joint audits (Article 12a, from 2024).

Reporters are private platform operators; the data concern individual and entity sellers.

## Relevance and limits for DAC8

DAC7 is the direct template for DAC8: it follows an OECD model rule, imposes due diligence and reporting on private operators about their users, and adds the data breach suspension mechanism that DAC8 security arguments can build on. Its group-request clarification loosens the foreseeable-relevance baseline set by DAC1 and Berlioz.
