# C-609/26, Luxembourg Business Registers II

Official Journal C/2026/4484, 31 August 2026; reference lodged 4 June 2026 by Tribunal d'arrondissement de Luxembourg. Selected digest of questions under Article 15 of Directive 2024/1640.

Question 1(c) concerns whether exceptional, individual, significant, real and present risk is necessary. Question 2 concerns data already available elsewhere. Question 3(b) contrasts specific individual proof with belonging to a particularly vulnerable category.

The checked record is a pending reference, not a judgment answering these questions. It does not concern DAC8 or establish that crypto ownership meets the exemption threshold.
