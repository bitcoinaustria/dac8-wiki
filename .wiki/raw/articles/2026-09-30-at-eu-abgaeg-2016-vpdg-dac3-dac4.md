# Austria: EU-Abgabenänderungsgesetz 2016 (EU-AbgÄG 2016)

## Source identity

- Gazette: `BGBl. I Nr. 77/2016`, ausgegeben am 1. August 2016 (NR: GP XXV RV 1190 AB 1243 S. 136; BR: 9613 AB 9621; CELEX incl. 32015L2060, 32015L2376, 32016L0881)
- Art. 1: Verrechnungspreisdokumentationsgesetz – VPDG; Art. 7: EU-AHG amendment; Art. 12: Aufhebung des EU-Quellensteuergesetzes; further articles amend EStG, FinStrG, KontRegG, Kapitalabfluss-Meldegesetz, GMSG, ZollR-DG, BewG, KStG and BAO.

## Content

- **VPDG (DAC4).** § 1: implements Directive (EU) 2016/881 and the multilateral competent-authority agreement on the exchange of country-by-country reports. It defines the "Länderbezogener Bericht (Country-by-Country Report)", Master File and Local File, and regulates transmission, penalties and exchange with foreign authorities. Temporal scope: documentation relates to financial years from 1 January 2016. Where a secondary filing obligation under § 5(2) applies, information may relate to financial years from 1 January 2017. The extracted gazette body numbers this section § 15, while the table of contents lists 17 sections; the discrepancy was not resolved.
- **EU-AHG § 7a (DAC3).** Inserted "Umfang und Voraussetzungen des automatischen Informationsaustauschs über grenzüberschreitende Vorbescheide und Vorabverständigungen über die Verrechnungspreisgestaltung". The central liaison office sends information to all Member States and the Commission on rulings and APAs issued, amended or renewed after 31 December 2016 (current text). § 1(1) EU-AHG was updated to cite Directive (EU) 2016/881.
- **EU-QuStG.** Expires at the end of 31 December 2016; see `2026-09-30-at-eu-qustg-bgbl-i-33-2004.md`.

## Entry into force

Neither the VPDG nor the EU-AHG amendments carry a general date in the extracted text, and § 22 EU-AHG lists no BGBl. I Nr. 77/2016 paragraph. They therefore took effect after publication (1 August 2016) under Art. 49 B-VG; this is an inference. Application is governed by the substantive dates above.

## Relevance and limits

One act covers the corporate-transparency steps DAC3 and DAC4. Neither concerns natural persons' account data. The GMSG and KontRegG amendments in the same act were not analysed.
