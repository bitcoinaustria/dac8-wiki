# ErlRV 306 BlgNR XXVIII. GP on § 20 Krypto-MPfG

## Fact captured

The materials state that § 20 "soll der Umsetzung von Art. 25 der Richtlinie dienen". Article 25 DAC is the data-protection article.

Within § 20 the materials cite a directive source paragraph by paragraph, except for Abs. 1:

- Abs. 1: the commentary restates the norm — "Diese Pflicht soll unabhängig davon gelten, ob es sich beim Kunden um eine meldepflichtige Person iS dieses Gesetzes handelt" — and cites no article, annex or recital.
- Abs. 3: "in Umsetzung von Art. 25 Abs. 4 lit. b der Richtlinie".
- Abs. 4: "Anhang VI Abschnitt V Unterabschnitt B", with the range recorded as "mindestens 5 und höchstens zehn Jahren".

The materials map Annex VI Section V completely and elsewhere: Unterabschnitt A to § 38, Unterabschnitt B to § 39 and § 20 Abs. 4, Unterabschnitt F to §§ 22 to 27. No subsection is mapped to § 20 Abs. 1.

Austria took the maximum of the directive range for a stated reason of convenience: "Aus Vereinfachungsgründen sollen die entsprechenden Dokumente und Informationen – analog zur absoluten Verjährungsfrist gemäß § 209 Abs. 3 BAO – zehn Jahre" be retained and then deleted.

No necessity or proportionality assessment of the all-customers duty appears anywhere in the materials. Proportionality language does appear in the same document in an unrelated passage on cross-border rulings, which shows the drafters used the category where they considered it engaged.

## Research use

This is the strongest single document for the argument that § 20 Abs. 1 is a national addition rather than an implementation of Union law. That distinction is decisive for the procedural route: a provision that is not unionsrechtlich determiniert can be attacked by an Article 140 application from the BVwG or the VfGH without a detour through Article 267, and cannot rely on Article 6(1)(c) and (3) GDPR cover derived from implementing Union law.

The mis-citation of Article 25 is independently useful. A data-protection safeguard article cannot found an affirmative duty to process the data of persons the directive does not reach.
