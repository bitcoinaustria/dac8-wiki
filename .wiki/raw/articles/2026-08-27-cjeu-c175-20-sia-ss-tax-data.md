# SIA SS

Case C-175/20, paragraphs 54 to 80, treats a tax-authority demand for platform-user information as personal-data processing that requires a clear legal basis, a specific purpose, objective selection criteria, strict necessity, and a time period bounded by the investigation. A request must not become general and indiscriminate collection merely because tax enforcement is legitimate.

The case is a useful claimant-side tax analogy. It does not decide DAC8's validity or the Austrian Krypto-MPfG perimeter.
