# Netherlands: Act implementing EU crypto-asset information exchange

- Official title: `Wet implementatie EU-richtlijn gegevensuitwisseling cryptoactiva`
- Gazette: `Staatsblad 2026, 79`
- Signed: 1 April 2026
- Published: 10 April 2026
- Effect under Article V: 1 January 2026

The delayed publication and retroactive operation may raise domestic notice or reliance questions, but do not themselves invalidate DAC8. No Dutch DAC8 litigation was located.
