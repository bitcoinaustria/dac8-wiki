# Lam information: monthly large-balance reports and count-one losses

Filed 27 May 2026, case 2:26-cr-00184-ES, document 3; count one §4, printed pp.2–3. Official seven-page information; paired plea confirmation is separately sourced.

The filing describes employee credentials and requests for monthly large-balance reports. Count one specifies approximately USD146,000 bribes, USD6.536 million attempted theft and USD3.387 million successful theft. A roughly USD417,300 customer loss was reimbursed by the bank.

A charging document's detailed allegations must not be silently equated with an independently read plea stipulation. The confirmed plea strengthens the mechanism, but is not a DAC8 incident or a finding of net uncompensated customer loss equal to all theft. Mechanical credibility 2: recent institutional primary.
