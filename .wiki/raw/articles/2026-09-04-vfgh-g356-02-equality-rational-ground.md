# VfGH G 356/02 on equality and a rational ground

In G 356/02, judgment of 23 June 2003, the VfGH restated that the equality principle binds the legislature. A differentiation must rest on objective factual distinctions. Equal facts generally require equal legal consequences, while material factual differences may require differentiated treatment.

The Court also formulated the broader requirement of substantive rationality: the relation between the regulated facts and the legal consequence must rest on a rational ground. A rule made without such a ground violates the equality principle.

The judgment supplies the abstract Austrian test. It does not establish which crypto, financial-account or transaction cases are comparable. That comparison must be made against the purpose and effects of each challenged provision.
