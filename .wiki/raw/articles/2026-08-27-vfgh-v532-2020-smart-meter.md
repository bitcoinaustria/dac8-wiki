# VfGH V 532/2020: smart-meter alternative remedies

The VfGH rejected the direct regulation challenge because an ordinary dispute route existed and noted that a DSB complaint might provide an additional route. It found none of the extraordinary circumstances present in the 2014 data-retention case.

This supports treating the retention precedent as a fact-specific exception rather than a general rule for provider-side data processing.
