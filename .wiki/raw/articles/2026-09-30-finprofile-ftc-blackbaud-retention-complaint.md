# Blackbaud retention allegations

FTC complaint, published 2024, pp. 2–5, paragraphs 6–10, 16, 19 and 23.

The FTC alleges theft of identity, address, bank and wealth information, including estimated wealth and identified assets. It describes unencrypted backups of former customers and unnecessary retention as aggravating exposure, together with inadequate access separation; follow-on fraud attempts are described.

These are official allegations resolved by consent, not causes proved in a contested trial. The US cross-sector case does not establish a DAC8 breach or harm caused by an Austrian statutory retention mandate. Read alongside, but distinguish from, the final binding order.
