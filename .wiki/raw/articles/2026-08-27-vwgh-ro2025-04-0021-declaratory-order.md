# VwGH Ro 2025/04/0021: requirements for an administrative declaratory order

In its legal proposition of 25 November 2025, the VwGH held that a party has a legal interest in an administrative declaratory order only where it is suitable to eliminate a present or future legal risk. The requested determination must concretely clarify a right or legal relationship for the future and thereby remove a threat to the applicant's subjective right.

The general case law treats this form as subsidiary where an equivalent statutorily prescribed administrative or judicial procedure can decide the legal question. The proposition does not create a Krypto-MPfG-specific declaratory procedure or establish jurisdiction for an ordinary crypto user.
