# BMF WiEReG protection statistics

Selected official-source digest. Locator: section “Einschränkung der Einsicht gemäß § 10a WiEReG”, tables headed “Stand per Ende” and “Begründungen für Gewährungen”.

For end-2025 the BMF table reports 609 Gewährungen and 57 Verfügungen. The reasons table includes 310 entries for kidnapping/extortion risk, 101 for offences against life or bodily integrity, 55 for fraud, 11 for coercion/threats/stalking, and 268 for protection of minors.

The footnote dates the reasons to end-2025 and expressly allows multiple reasons for one grant. The page does not adequately define the statistical unit or whether figures represent active grants, persons, decisions, or annual new grants. Preserve its end-of-period wording; do not sum the grounds, present these as attacks, or derive a crime rate or annual grant rate.

The figures establish reported administrative use of individual protection. They do not establish register-caused attacks, effectiveness of the restrictions, or a DAC8 exemption.
