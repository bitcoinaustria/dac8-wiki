# CJEU C-33/22: Austrian DSB competence within GDPR scope

- Judgment: 16 January 2024
- ECLI: `ECLI:EU:C:2024:46`

Paragraphs 61–72 hold that, where the GDPR applies, Articles 55 and 77 confer competence on the supervisory authority and national competence rules cannot remove the processing from that review.

The judgment supports DSB jurisdiction over GDPR complaints but does not answer which Member State's supervisory authority is lead in a cross-border provider case or confer a general constitutional-annulment power on the DSB.
