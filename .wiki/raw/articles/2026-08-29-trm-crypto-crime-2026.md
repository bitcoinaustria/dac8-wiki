# TRM Labs crypto-crime estimate 2026

TRM estimated approximately USD 158 billion incoming to attributed illicit wallets or entities for 2025, including sanctioned and state-aligned activity. It reported 1.2 percent of attributed on-chain volume and 2.7 percent of incoming VASP flows. These are not automatically money-laundering volume, criminal proceeds or victim harm.

The denominators use known services, wallets and service-like clusters while excluding specified internal or technical movements; personal-wallet and P2P activity can be absent. The introduction does not fully disclose chain coverage. TRM substantially revised earlier annual estimates as attribution improved. The series is commercial, dynamic and incomplete and is not directly comparable with Chainalysis.
