# Decree 2025-840: RCS home-address masking

Source digest. Décret n° 2025-840 du 22 août 2025, JORF n°0196 of 24 August 2025, NOR JUSC2517565D. Locators: notice ("Publics concernés", "Objet", "Entrée en vigueur"); Code de commerce Article R. 123-3 5° (inserted) and new Articles R. 123-54-1 and R. 123-54-2.

Directors who are natural persons, and partners with unlimited liability, may ask through the single electronic portal (guichet unique) for confidentiality of their personal home address in the RCS. The court registry (greffe) must act within five working days ("cinq jours francs ouvrables"). Access continues for the authorities listed in Code de commerce L. 123-53 and R. 123-318, craft-chamber presidents, agricultural social-security funds and URSSAF. It also continues for the company's legal representatives, partners and creditors with claims arising from the requester's management. The decree entered into force the day after publication.

This is enacted law. The text contains no security rationale; the purpose appears in government answers in the Journal officiel (see `2026-09-29-bearer-france-an-crypto-violence-answers.md`) and in media coverage linking it to the 2025 crypto abductions. The masking is opt-in, applies only from the request onward, and does not reach sole traders whose seat is their home. It also does not remove copies already held by commercial resellers of register data, which the source is silent on.

DAC8 relevance: this is a comparator, not DAC8 evidence. A Member State reduced public address exposure while keeping authority and creditor access. That supports the general point that controlled access and address minimisation are available measures. DAC8 data are not public, so the decree does not show that confidential tax data carry the same risk as a public register.
