# VfGH G 47/2012 ua: full German data-retention judgment

The official 91-page German judgment is the complete authority. The previously ingested English PDF is only a 29-page extract.

Sections III.1.5.5–1.5.7 hold that provider-facing wording did not prevent direct effect: the statutory purpose and operation made users substantive norm addressees. The exceptional facts defeating other remedies were a distributed system involving users' and communication partners' providers, an impracticable number of controllers, continuous new retention, and records that could be erased before constitutional review became effective.

Those facts must not be generalized to DAC8 without proof. A known CASP, a long retention period, and a functioning DSB route materially weaken the analogy.
