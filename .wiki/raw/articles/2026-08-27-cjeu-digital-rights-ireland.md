# CJEU Digital Rights Ireland, C-293/12 and C-594/12

- Court: CJEU Grand Chamber
- Judgment: 8 April 2014
- ECLI: `EU:C:2014:238`

The Court invalidated the Data Retention Directive under Charter Articles 7, 8, and 52(1). It emphasised the general coverage of people and communications, lack of objective differentiation and limits, and insufficient access, security, and retention safeguards.

The decision is structurally relevant but not dispositive for DAC8. Tax reporting uses different data, purposes, aggregation, timing, and safeguards. Those distinctions must be analysed rather than collapsed into the communications-retention rule.
