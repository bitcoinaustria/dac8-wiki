# Mishcon description of the Austrian CRS claim

Mishcon de Reya states that it acted for an Austrian client with approximately EUR 40 in a German bank account. It describes complaints against Austrian and German authorities, including an Austrian BVwG appeal filed on 15 June 2020.

The stated arguments were absence of tax-evasion indicia, disproportionate bulk reporting, GDPR data minimisation, insufficient non-EEA safeguards, and data-security risk.

## Source boundary

This is counsel and campaign material, not a court record. The Austrian court decisions are anonymised. The page should not be used to identify the claimant beyond what the official judgments establish.
