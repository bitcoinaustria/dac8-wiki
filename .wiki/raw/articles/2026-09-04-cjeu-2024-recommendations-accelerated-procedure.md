# CJEU 2024 Recommendations on accelerated preliminary-ruling procedure

Paragraphs 40 to 42 of the 2024 Recommendations address accelerated and urgent preliminary-ruling procedures.

Under Article 105(1) of the Rules of Procedure, the referring court may submit a separate, reasoned request for accelerated treatment. Exceptionally, the President of the Court may decide on accelerated treatment ex officio after hearing the Judge-Rapporteur and the Advocate General.

The number of persons or legal relationships potentially affected, the importance or sensitivity of an issue and the need for the referring court to decide rapidly do not by themselves establish exceptional urgency.

This procedure is controlled by the referring court and the Court of Justice. A party may argue for urgency but cannot compel either the reference or accelerated treatment.
