# BMF-AV 136/2025, 30 September 2025

Selected digest from official PDF text extraction, not a full transcription. Locators: section 8.3, pages 111–112; section 8.4, pages 112–113. The BMF legal-guidance page linked this as its current Erlass on 19 September 2026.

Section 8.3 explains § 10a: provisional restriction within 14 days unless the application is manifestly unfounded; a pre-report application can request a temporary hold implemented by the end of the second following working day. The hold initially lasts at most 14 days; registration starts the provisional-restriction period and correspondingly extends the hold. Authorities and § 9(1)(1), (2) and (7) obliged entities retain access.

The merits decision is due within twelve months. If restricting residential-address data sufficiently protects the interest, only those data are restricted. Grants last five years, or until majority for minors.

Section 8.4 explains that a MeldeG Auskunftssperre replaces residential-address details in extracts with an indication of Austrian residence and the restriction.

The Erlass states on page 1 that it expresses the Ministry's legal view and creates no additional rights or duties. Direct current WiEReG RIS retrieval failed during this check; these details are attributed to the official guidance, not an independently completed consolidation check.
