# Commission complaint handling rules under Notice 2017/C 18/02

Any person or body may submit a complaint free of charge and need not demonstrate a formal interest. A registrable complaint must identify the complainant and the alleged Member State measure or practice. The Commission aims to acknowledge receipt within 15 working days and, as a rule, decide within one year whether to issue a formal notice or close the file. Before an intended closure it gives the complainant four weeks to comment.

These are administrative handling targets, not judicial filing or decision deadlines. The complainant bears their own participation expenses, obtains no private interim order, and cannot compel an Article 258 proceeding.
