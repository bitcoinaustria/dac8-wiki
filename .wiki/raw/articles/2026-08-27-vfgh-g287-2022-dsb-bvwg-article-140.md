# VfGH G 287/2022 ua: DSB complaint reaches successful Article 140 review

Individuals complained to the DSB, appealed the DSB decisions to the BVwG, raised constitutional and EU-law objections, and requested Article 140 and Article 267 referrals. The BVwG then filed applications under Article 140(1)(1)(a) B-VG.

The VfGH admitted the court applications and annulled section 9(1) DSG. The case is direct proof that the DSB does not itself need power to invalidate a statute: the DSB proceeding can create the concrete controversy through which the BVwG brings a prejudicial national provision to the VfGH.

Relevant passages: section III.1-2 on the DSB and BVwG path; section IV.1 on admissibility and the resulting review.
