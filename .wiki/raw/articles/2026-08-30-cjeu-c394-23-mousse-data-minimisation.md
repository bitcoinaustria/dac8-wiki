# CJEU C-394/23 Mousse

Judgment of 9 January 2025, ECLI:EU:C:2025:2.

Paragraphs 22–29 explain that GDPR Article 5(1)(c) gives expression to proportionality and requires data to be adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary. Paragraphs 36–43 require objective indispensability for the contractual purpose and reject a merely useful or conventional data field. Paragraphs 51–56 test whether a neutral and less intrusive communication method can achieve the stated purpose.

The case concerned mandatory collection of a rail customer's title and gender identity under GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f), not statutory tax reporting. It is therefore an analogy for field-level necessity and alternatives, not authority that DAC8 must use the same result or burden allocation.
