# Tyurin sentencing: stolen financial-customer contacts used in securities fraud

DOJ SDNY release, 7 January 2021. Locators: guilty-plea narrative and financial-sector intrusions paragraphs.

After a guilty plea, Tyurin received 144 months. DOJ describes 2012–2015 theft of over one hundred million customer records across financial and other businesses, including over eighty million JPMorgan customers. Stolen contacts were used to promote stocks deceptively and inflate prices. Persistent intrusions refreshed stolen records.

These are prosecutors' accounts of admitted conduct, not independently inspected judgments or proof every exposed customer lost money. The campaign's proceeds are not a per-record risk estimate.
