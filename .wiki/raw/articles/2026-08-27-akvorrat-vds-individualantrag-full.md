# AKVorrat consolidated data-retention Individualantrag

This is the complete 51-page consolidated application filed in the Austrian data-retention proceedings. It pleads concrete subscriber relationships and explains why provider-side retention was said to affect users directly and currently.

The application is party material, not a court-authenticated statement of fact or law. Its procedural claims must be checked against the later VfGH decisions.

## Completeness correction

The separate file `Individualantrag_VDS_Verfahrensdokumente_HS_.pdf` is not the full application. It is a nine-page fragment containing a cover, pages 1–2 of the 51-page application, and a six-page supplementary filing. The local corpus retains both and labels the fragment accordingly.
