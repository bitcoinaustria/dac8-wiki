# Tax-assistance convention: Article 22 confidentiality and uses

Article 22(1) requires secrecy and applicable supplying-state safeguards. Paragraph 2 confines ordinary disclosure and use to persons or authorities engaged in the listed tax functions, including their courts and oversight bodies. Paragraph 4 permits other-purpose use only where the supplying state's law allows it and its competent authority authorises it. Transfer to a third party likewise requires prior authorisation by the original supplying authority.

Pinpoint: Article 22, official PDF pages 11 and 12. These are treaty conditions, not evidence that any particular transfer or permission exists.
