# CJEU C-60/22: accountability and the limit of documentation defects

- Judgment: 4 May 2023
- ECLI: `ECLI:EU:C:2023:373`

Paragraph 53 applies the Article 5(2) accountability burden to the controller. Paragraphs 61–69 distinguish compliance-documentation defects from the lawfulness of the underlying processing: infringement of Articles 26 or 30 does not by itself establish unlawful processing and does not automatically trigger erasure or restriction.

For DAC8, missing or deficient records, controller arrangements or DPIA material can justify investigation and corrective measures. They should not be pleaded as automatic invalidity of the Krypto-MPfG or automatic deletion of otherwise lawfully processed data.
