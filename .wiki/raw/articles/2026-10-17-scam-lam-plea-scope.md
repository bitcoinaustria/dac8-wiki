# Lam guilty plea: high-balance selection and two-scheme totals

Published 28 May 2026. Locators: plea paragraph and factual summary following “According to documents filed”.

DOJ confirms a 27 May guilty plea. In the first scheme, Lam identified high-balance accounts and supplied confidential information to outsiders; a separate later scheme concerned false bank entries. The USD3,433,989.07 loss and USD155,000 bribe totals combine them.

Do not allocate that whole total to the disclosure. Sentencing was scheduled for 15 October 2026, now past: a later outcome was not verified, so “awaiting October sentencing” would be misleading.
