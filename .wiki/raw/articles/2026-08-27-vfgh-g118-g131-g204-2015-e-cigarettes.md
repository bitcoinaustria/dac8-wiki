# VfGH G 118/2015 ua: e-cigarette monopoly

The applicants documented their products, online sales, revenue and profit shares, preparations, and expected business loss. The court accepted present effect before formal commencement because immediate commercial dispositions were required.

The decision also warns that an economic disadvantage is not enough by itself: the challenged law must intervene in the applicant's protected legal sphere, and the challenge perimeter must remove the alleged defect.
