# Austria: AbgÄG 2025 and DAC9

## Source identity

- Gazette: `BGBl. I Nr. 97/2025`, Abgabenänderungsgesetz 2025 – AbgÄG 2025, ausgegeben am 23. Dezember 2025 (NR: GP XXVIII RV 294 AB 331 S. 55; BR: 11720 AB 11741; CELEX 32024L1233, 32025L0872). Published the same day as the Krypto-MPfG act, BGBl. I Nr. 96/2025.
- Art. 3 amends the Mindestbesteuerungsgesetz – MinBestG (`BGBl. I Nr. 187/2023`); a later article amends the EU-AHG.

## Content

- MinBestG § 1(1) now also cites Directive (EU) 2025/872 on automatic exchange of information from the Mindeststeuerbericht (top-up tax information return). New § 1(1a) extends the Act to the multilateral competent-authority agreement with non-EU states.
- New § 73a "Informationsaustausch": the Austrian competent authority transmits the relevant parts of a Mindeststeuerbericht filed by a domestic ultimate parent entity or designated filing entity to implementing states, under the distribution approach, electronically. New § 73b covers corrections and notifications.
- § 84(5) MinBestG: § 1(1) and § 73 as amended, and §§ 1(1a), 73a and 73b, take effect on 1 January 2026 and apply to financial years beginning on or after 31 December 2023.
- New § 7(9) EU-AHG: the DAC9 exchange "richtet sich nach den Bestimmungen des Mindestbesteuerungsgesetzes – MinBestG" (in force 1 January 2026, § 22(7) EU-AHG).

## Relevance and limits

DAC9 is transposed. It concerns large multinational groups, not natural persons. The EU-AHG text cites the OJ date as "14.04.2025" in § 7(9) and "6.5.2025" in § 1(1). This internal inconsistency is recorded as found, not corrected.
