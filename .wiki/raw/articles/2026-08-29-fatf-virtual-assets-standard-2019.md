# FATF virtual-assets standard 2019

FATF amended Recommendation 15 in October 2018 and adopted its Interpretive Note in June 2019. FATF calls on jurisdictions to subject covered VASPs to risk assessment, licensing or registration, supervision, sanctions, CDD, recordkeeping, suspicious reporting and transfer-information duties; it does not directly regulate a VASP.

The linked statement records adoption. The normative text is preserved in the current FATF Recommendations and Interpretive Note to Recommendation 15. The Travel Rule was not invented for crypto in 2019: the change extended the existing Recommendation 16 transfer-information architecture to virtual assets. EU TFR 2023/1113 supplies the directly applicable EU rule.
