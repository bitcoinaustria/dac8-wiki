# French FICOBA account-register breach, February 2026

The DGFiP states that an attacker used the credentials of an official authorised for inter-ministerial information exchange to consult and extract part of FICOBA between 28 January and 13 February 2026. Approximately 1.2 million account records, not 1.2 million people, were affected. The fields included IBAN or RIB, holder identity and postal address.

The official FAQ states that transactions, balances, tax identifiers, passwords and access credentials were not in the affected data. It identifies phishing, fake-bank-adviser fraud and identity misuse as possible risks, not established consequences.

The incident is a close authority-copy and access-control comparator. It does not involve crypto data or prove a legal defect in DAC8.
