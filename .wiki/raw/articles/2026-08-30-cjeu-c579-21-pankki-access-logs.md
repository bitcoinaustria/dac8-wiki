# CJEU C-579/21 Pankki S

Judgment of 22 June 2023, ECLI:EU:C:2023:501.

Paragraphs 57–62 and 79–83 treat dates and purposes of consultations of personal data as information available through GDPR Article 15 where needed to verify lawfulness and exercise rights. The identities of employees who performed the consultations are not automatically personal data of the requester and are disclosed only in narrower circumstances.

The judgment supports a request for DAC8 consultation timing and purpose. It does not establish a general right to the identity of every individual official.
