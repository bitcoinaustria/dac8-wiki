# ECLI:NL:RBOVE:2022:2703

Selected digest of the official full judgment, Rechtbank Overijssel, 27 September 2022. [Reader view](https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/details?id=ECLI:NL:RBOVE:2022:2703).

Sections 4.4–4.5 establish a tax official's repeated unauthorised queries and paid disclosures of vehicle-owner information, including names, addresses and vehicle makes. The proven period is 18 January 2020 to 24 March 2021, with 216 disclosures. Section 7.3 describes the possible serious danger to affected people.

This is a conviction for secrecy breaches, computer intrusion and corruption, not a finding of a subsequent physical attack. The records were vehicle-owner data accessible through tax-administration systems, not tax returns or crypto holdings. Finality and any appeal were not verified.
