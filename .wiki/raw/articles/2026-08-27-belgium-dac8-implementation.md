# Belgium: official CARF and DAC8 implementation overview

The Belgian finance administration identifies the implementing law dated 16 March 2026 and published 1 April 2026. It describes provider due diligence, reporting, registration of relevant non-MiCA operators, and automatic exchange. No DAC8-specific Belgian court proceeding was located.
