# FM-GwG section 11a version correction

The Austrian self-hosted-address provision began applying on 30 December 2024. The currently consolidated amended wording applies from 1 January 2026. The rule requires CASP risk assessment and suitable mitigation for covered self-hosted-address transfers; it is not a ban on self-custody.

This record corrects the date shorthand in the earlier raw FM-GwG source note without altering that immutable source record.
