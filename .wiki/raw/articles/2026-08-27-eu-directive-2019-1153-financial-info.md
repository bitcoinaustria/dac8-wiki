# Directive (EU) 2019/1153

Articles 3 and 4 require Member States to designate competent criminal authorities and allow direct, immediate, case-specific access to central bank-account information for serious-crime work. Article 7 enables requests for FIU-held financial information or analysis. Logging, purpose limits, and data-protection rules apply.

Directive 2024/1654 adds cross-border BARIS access for designated authorities and a structured electronic format for transaction records, including crypto-transfer records held by CASPs under national law. It does not create unrestricted public, tax-authority, or private-party access.
