# Twilio2022: employee compromise and authenticator enrolment

Final incident update27October2022; also24August update. Locators “Investigation Conclusion”, “Smishing incident findings”, “Eradication and remediation”.

Stolen employee credentials enabled internal-tool access. Final counts:209 customers out of over270,000, and93 Authy end users out of about75million. Unauthorized devices were added to affected Authy accounts and removed. No evidence of customer-console credentials/tokens/API-key access was reported. Remediation included resets, session revocation and tool restrictions/FIDO2 distribution. No aggregate financial-loss figure or independent future-efficacy test;209 customers is not209 individual people.

Mechanical credibility 0; independently assessed. Historical and vendor-primary records are accepted for their unique bounded incident/implementation evidence, not population rates. No private dataset or victim record was collected.
