# VfGH current procedure: counsel, fee, duration and suspensive effect

The VfGH states that applications must be in writing and generally prepared and filed by a lawyer. The current filing fee under section 17a VfGG is EUR 340, including for Article 137 claims and Article 139 or 140 applications. Legal aid may be available.

The institutional average is described as roughly four months, not as a deadline or a forecast for a norm-review case. A complaint against an administrative-court decision does not itself suspend that decision. On application, suspensive effect may be granted if no compelling public interest opposes it and enforcement would cause a disproportionate disadvantage after balancing the affected interests.

The published lump-sum cost award for a successful application or complaint is EUR 3,484 including VAT and the filing fee. This is not a forecast of the applicant's actual fee agreement or downside exposure.
