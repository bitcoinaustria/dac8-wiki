# WiEReG 2027 Ministerialentwurf

The draft would implement Directive 2024/1640 Articles 10 to 15 and enable AMLR Articles 24 and 51 to 68. It proposes expanded sanctions screening, nominee and source-of-funds data, legitimate-interest access procedures, inspection powers, and a replacement WiEReG from 10 July 2027.

The explanatory material expressly says FATF Recommendations 24 and 25 should be implemented even where rules above the minimum standards of Directive 2024/1640 and AMLR must be retained or enacted. This is a strong but narrow gold-plating lead. It remains a ministerial consultation draft; no enacted full-package Austrian implementation was located by 27 August 2026.
