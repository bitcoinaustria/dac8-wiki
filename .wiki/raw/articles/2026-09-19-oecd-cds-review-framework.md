# Global Forum confidentiality and data safeguards

Selected digest of the section “Confidentiality and Data Safeguards (CDS) assessments”. The framework covers CRS and CARF. Reviews involve information-security experts from member jurisdictions and the Secretariat. It describes pre-exchange, post-exchange and third-round reviews, with annual monitoring from 2026. Serious deficiencies can suspend clearance to receive information; a breach can trigger suspension and a targeted reassessment.

Detailed review methodology and individual results are confidential. Non-publication therefore does not establish absent controls or defective assessments. The page describes the review framework, not a favourable or adverse assessment of Austrian systems, a guarantee against insider abuse, or a public right to obtain the underlying reports.
