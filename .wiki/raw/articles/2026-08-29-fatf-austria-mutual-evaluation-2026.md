# FATF Austria Mutual Evaluation 2026

FATF rated Austria `Moderate Effectiveness` for financial-intelligence use (IO6), money-laundering investigation and prosecution (IO7) and confiscation (IO8). The narrative found confiscation implemented only to a limited extent, low victim-return levels, insufficient resources and weaknesses in complex and cross-border cases.

The formal rating is not `Low Effectiveness`. The evaluation is a peer assessment against FATF Immediate Outcomes, not a causal estimate of how much crime Austrian controls prevent.
