# VfGH 09.03.2023, E2097/2021 (VfSlg 20.597/2023)

## Holding captured

The RIS Normen field lists § 5, § 112 and § 113 GMSG, but § 5 appears only because the judgment reproduces it in the Rechtslage section. The ratio concerns §§ 112 and 113 — the BMF's outbound exchange and inbound forwarding — not the institution-side collection duty.

**Competence bar** (Pkt. III.2.2.3): "Damit kommt aber dem Verfassungsgerichtshof … eine Zuständigkeit zur Prüfung der Verfassungsmäßigkeit der §§112 und 113 GMSG nur dann zu, wenn zuvor der Gerichtshof der Europäischen Union die zugrunde liegende, die Umsetzungsbestimmungen vollständig determinierende Bestimmung des Art8 Abs3a der Richtlinie für ungültig erklärt."

The bar rests expressly on Article 8(3a) DAC2 being fully harmonised Union law leaving "keinen inhaltlichen Spielraum für die Mitgliedstaaten" (Pkt. 2.2.2).

**Merits** (Pkt. 2.2.4.5 to 2.2.4.6): proportionality upheld. *Digital Rights Ireland* and *Tele2* were held not transferable because CRS data "betreffen … nicht den Kernbereich des Privatlebens" and do not permit "sehr genaue Schlüsse auf das Privatleben".

## Research use

The competence holding is the important part, and it cuts in favour of the § 20 Abs. 1 case. Neither DAC2 nor DAC8 obliges Member States to make institutions collect and store the data of persons who are not reportable. That is national discretion. The Foto-Frost-derived bar therefore does not shield § 5 Abs. 1 GMSG or § 20 Abs. 1 Krypto-MPfG, and an Article 140 application from the BVwG or the VfGH does not require a prior CJEU invalidation.

The merits reasoning is adverse and must be confronted. The distinguishing hook is the reference object: a year-end account balance supports the Court's finding that no precise conclusions about private life are possible, whereas the transaction-level dataset required by § 18 in conjunction with § 20 Abs. 1 — per crypto-asset, per transaction type, with unit counts, transaction counts and transfer categories including transfers to unhosted addresses — does support such inferences.
