# Austria: Krypto-Meldepflichtgesetz, BGBl. I Nr. 96/2025

## Source identity

- Gazette: `BGBl. I Nr. 96/2025`
- Published: 23 December 2025
- Act: Betrugsbekämpfungsgesetz 2025 Teil Daten
- Core statute: Krypto-Meldepflichtgesetz (`Krypto-MPfG`)
- CELEX references: `32011L0016`, `32023L2226`
- In force: 1 January 2026

## Relevant content captured

The act implements mandatory automatic exchange concerning reportable crypto-assets. It creates reporting, registration, due-diligence, record-processing, user-information, and penalty rules and amends related Austrian tax-information legislation.

Section 6 defines reportable transactions to include fiat-to-crypto and crypto-to-crypto exchanges and transfers of reportable crypto-assets. Section 15 identifies providers subject to Austrian reporting and due-diligence duties. Section 18 specifies identity and aggregated transaction reporting. Section 20 requires providers to determine, record, store, and process reportable information for all customers and transactions to identify reportable users and information. The statute also contains enforcement consequences for missing self-certification and provider non-compliance.

## Research use

This is the principal national norm for an Austrian challenge. Standing and the proper challenge perimeter depend on which provisions operate directly on the individual, which operate only through providers, and whether another reasonable legal route exists.
