# CJEU C-623/22 Belgian Association of Tax Lawyers

The Belgian Constitutional Court referred validity questions arising from annulment proceedings against DAC6 implementation. The CJEU largely upheld the reporting framework against equality, legal-certainty, legality, and privacy objections, while preserving the earlier invalidity of the rule requiring privileged lawyers to notify other intermediaries.

The case is not about DAC8. It is useful both procedurally, because it shows a constitutional-court-to-CJEU route, and adversely, because a precisely defined tax-reporting regime can survive Charter scrutiny while a discrete component is severed.
