# EU Charter rights and review framework

Articles 7 and 8 protect private life and personal data. Article 8 separately requires fair processing for specified purposes on a legitimate legal basis, access and rectification, and independent control. Article 47 guarantees an effective remedy before a tribunal for violations of EU-law rights.

Under Article 51, the Charter binds Member States when they implement EU law. Article 52(1) requires any limitation to be provided by law, preserve the essence of the right, be necessary, genuinely meet a recognised general-interest objective or protect others' rights, and satisfy proportionality.

These provisions supply the legal test; they do not predetermine DAC8 invalidity or require a freestanding pre-processing action where effective incidental review exists.
