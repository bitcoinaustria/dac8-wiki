# UN Palermo Convention 2000

Article 6 addresses laundering of proceeds from serious crime. Article 7 requires a comprehensive domestic regulatory and supervisory regime for banks, non-bank financial institutions and other susceptible bodies, emphasising customer identification, recordkeeping and reporting of suspicious transactions, together with cooperation and consideration of a financial intelligence unit.

The convention binds a state once it is in force for that state. Domestic applicability and private duties depend on constitutional arrangements and implementation; it is not a directly detailed customer rulebook.
