# CJEU C-687/21, MediaMarktSaturn

Judgment of 25 January 2024, ECLI:EU:C:2024:72. Official EUR-Lex full judgment inspected; this is a source digest, not a full-text reproduction.

## Holdings

- Paragraphs 36–45: employee disclosure can indicate organisational failure, but does not alone prove inappropriate technical and organisational measures. Concrete risks and the controller's evidence must be considered. The controller's stated burden of proving appropriate security concerns an Article 82 action.
- Paragraphs 62–69: the referring court established that a mistakenly delivered document was recovered within half an hour and the recipient had not read its data. Fear of possible later misuse under those circumstances did not establish compensable non-material damage. A purely hypothetical risk is insufficient.

## Use and limits

Supports examining actual organisational controls while preserving adverse evidence. The compensation holding must not be treated as a prerequisite for preventive Articles 5, 24 and 32 duties. No ruling on mandatory tax-reporting validity or Austrian procedural remedies.
