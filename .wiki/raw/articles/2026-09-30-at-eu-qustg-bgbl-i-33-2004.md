# Austria: EU-Quellensteuergesetz (EU-QuStG)

## Source identity

- Gazette: `BGBl. I Nr. 33/2004`, ausgegeben am 27. April 2004 (NR: GP XXII RV 350 AB 429; CELEX 32003L0093, 32003L0048)
- Act: Bundesgesetz, mit dem das EG-Amtshilfegesetz geändert und ein EU-Quellensteuergesetz (EU-QuStG) erlassen wird
- Entry into force (§ 14, original): at the moment from which Art. 17(2) with (3) of Directive 2003/48/EC requires binding application; the RIS consolidated note states "Es ist ab 1.7.2005 anzuwenden" (source: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20003304&FassungVom=2005-06-09).
- End: Art. 12 EU-Abgabenänderungsgesetz 2016, `BGBl. I Nr. 77/2016` (ausgegeben 1. August 2016), added § 14(3)–(6): the Act "tritt mit Ablauf des 31. Dezember 2016 außer Kraft", with residual application to listed cases until 30 June 2017.

## Content

Where the beneficial owner of interest is resident in another EU Member State, Austrian paying agents deduct "EU-Quellensteuer" at 15 % for the first three calendar years after entry into force, 20 % for the following three and 35 % thereafter. § 10 allows the owner to avoid the deduction by presenting a certificate issued by the residence-state tax office. This is the Austrian use of the transitional withholding option (instead of automatic exchange) under the Savings Directive, preserving domestic bank secrecy.

From 1 October 2016, new § 14(4) excluded interest paid after 30 September 2016 on a "Neukonto" within the meaning of § 82 GMSG where the account falls under GMSG reporting; this is the hand-over from withholding to CRS exchange.

## Relevance and limits

Marks Austria's pre-CRS model: anonymised withholding rather than exchange. The residual 2017 provisions and the Austria–Switzerland/Liechtenstein withholding-tax agreements were not examined here. The 1 July 2005 date is taken from the RIS consolidation note and the Act's reference to Directive 2003/48/EC, not from the Council decision itself.
