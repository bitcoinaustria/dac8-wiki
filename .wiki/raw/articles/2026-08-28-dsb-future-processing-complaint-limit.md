# DSB 2023-0.632.875: section 24 DSG complaint requires existing processing

In decision 2023-0.632.875 of 19 September 2023, the DSB relied on the wording of section 24 DSG, under which a data subject complains that processing of their personal data violates the GDPR or section 1 DSG. It did not treat section 24 as a freestanding actio for a merely possible future processing event.

For a DAC8 claimant, the complaint should therefore identify processing that has already occurred or is continuing: tax-residence classification, creation of transaction fields or aggregates, storage, a refusal of access or correction, or another concrete step. The statutory possibility of a first report in 2027 is not by itself the strongest section 24 case.

This does not eliminate preventive powers under section 22(4) DSG where their separate urgency and danger conditions are proved.
