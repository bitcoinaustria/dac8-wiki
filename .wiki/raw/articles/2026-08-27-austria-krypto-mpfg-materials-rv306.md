# Austria Krypto-MPfG explanatory materials, RV 306 XXVIII GP

The official explanatory materials state that section 20 implements Article 25 of the amended Directive and requires reportable information to be determined, collected, stored, and processed for every customer, transaction, and crypto-asset regardless of whether the customer is reportable.

The same materials describe section 20(4) as applying to reported or transmitted information and justify ten years by administrative simplicity and the absolute limitation period in section 209(3) BAO. They therefore do not support a claim that section 20(4) itself mandates ten-year storage of the full raw profile of a never-reportable user.

For section 39 the materials use the broader phrase "Dokumente und Informationen" created in connection with reporting and due-diligence duties, while the enacted provision refers to retaining "diese Dokumente." That tension creates an interpretation and legal-certainty issue for never-transmitted section 20(1) data.

The materials also describe Article 60/63 MiCA providers as covered when authorised or permitted in Austria, and reserve the special registration regime for operators outside that MiCA route. Read with MiCA's home-state notification structure, this supports a conforming home-state interpretation rather than automatic Austrian host-state duplication.
