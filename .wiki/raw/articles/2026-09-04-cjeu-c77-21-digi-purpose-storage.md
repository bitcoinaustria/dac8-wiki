# CJEU C-77/21 Digi: purpose compatibility and storage limitation

- Judgment: 20 October 2022
- ECLI: `ECLI:EU:C:2022:805`

Paragraphs 29–31 treat recording or storing previously collected data in another database as further processing. Paragraphs 34–38 explain that, where the later purpose differs and is not based on consent or a Union or Member-State law, compatibility must be assessed under Article 6(4) GDPR. The assessment covers the relationship between the purposes, collection context and reasonable expectations, data nature, consequences and safeguards. It requires a specific, logical and sufficiently close link.

The holding is not a categorical bar on reuse. Paragraphs 44–45 upheld the possibility that the test database was compatible with the original contractual purpose, subject to the national court's assessment.

Paragraphs 47 and 52–54 state that the GDPR principles apply cumulatively, the controller must demonstrate that identified data are kept only as long as necessary, and even initially lawful processing can become unlawful when the purpose no longer requires the data.

For DAC8, the judgment supports a concrete test of each later join or copy. It does not overcome a specific Union or national legal basis by itself and does not show that a tax-to-AML use expressly allowed by Directive 2011/16 Article 16 is incompatible.
