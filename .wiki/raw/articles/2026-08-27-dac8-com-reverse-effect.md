# DAC8.com: reverse-effect and MiCA argument

The page argues that reporting makes regulated CASPs less attractive and may shift users toward peer-to-peer, non-custodial, mining, decentralised, or offshore routes. It presents that migration as reducing tax visibility and undermining MiCA's regulated-market objective.

The legal scope of DAC8 and MiCA is verifiable. This behavioural and causal effect was not independently demonstrated in the research pass and should be treated as a testable hypothesis, not an established outcome.
