# Council Directive 2014/48/EU amending the Savings Directive

- Council Directive 2014/48/EU of 24 March 2014; CELEX `32014L0048`; OJ L 111, 15.4.2014, p. 50; entry into force on publication, 15 April 2014 (Article 3)
- Transposition: adopt and publish by 1 January 2016; apply from the first day of the third calendar year after entry into force, i.e. 1 January 2017 (Article 2(1))

The recitals rely on the Commission's first application report of 15 September 2008 and identify circumvention routes: interposed entities and legal arrangements, channelling through non-EU operators, financial instruments equivalent to debt claims, certain life insurance contracts and non-UCITS funds. The amendment introduced definitions of "economic operator", a look-through to beneficial owners using anti-money-laundering data, and date and place of birth and tax identification numbers for identification.

Recital 7 of Directive (EU) 2015/2060 states that, with the repeal of Directive 2003/48/EC, Directive 2014/48/EU no longer had to be transposed. The amendment therefore never took effect; its look-through logic was carried forward in the CRS due-diligence rules of Directive 2014/107/EU.
