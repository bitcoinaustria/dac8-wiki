# VfGH G 62/2025

The VfGH found former section 10(1)(1) WiEReG unconstitutional because the permitted data set was too narrow for persons, including journalists, with a legitimate AML/CFT or sanctions-related information interest. Historical, data-quality, and expanded-extract fields could be necessary for public-interest scrutiny.

The decision supports differentiated access rather than either extreme. Obliged entities receive confidential CDD access; qualifying journalists and civil society may receive defined information; unrestricted anonymous public access remains impermissible.
