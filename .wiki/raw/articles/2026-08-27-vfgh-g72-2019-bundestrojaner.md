# VfGH G 72-74/2019 ua: Bundestrojaner and blanket vehicle-data processing

The VfGH invalidated covert message surveillance and broad vehicle-data processing as disproportionate under privacy and data-protection guarantees. The case helps on merits methodology, especially measures affecting many uninvolved people without a concrete occasion.

It does not solve DAC8 standing: the proceeding was an abstract application by legislators, not an Individualantrag by a data subject.
