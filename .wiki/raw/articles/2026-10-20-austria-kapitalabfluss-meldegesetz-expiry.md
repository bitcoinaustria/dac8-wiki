# Kapitalabfluss-Meldegesetz: scope and expiry

RIS consolidated text retrieved 20 October 2026. Enacted as Article 3 of BGBl. I Nr. 116/2015 (see [the 2015 omnibus record](2026-09-30-at-bgbl-i-116-2015-gmsg-kontregg-bwg.md), which did not analyse this act).

## Provisions

- § 1 Z 3 lit. a: „Kapitalabfluss“ includes „die Auszahlung und Überweisung von Sicht-, Termin- und Spareinlagen“.
- § 2: credit institutions, payment institutions and the ÖBFA must report large outflows to the Finance Minister „zur Überprüfung der Einhaltung der abgabenrechtlichen Vorschriften“.
- § 3 Abs. 1: reportable are outflows of at least EUR 50,000 from accounts or custody accounts of natural persons; business accounts and lawyers', notaries' and tax advisers' trust accounts are excluded. Abs. 2 covers obviously connected multiple transactions. Abs. 3: the report contains the vbPK SA (or name, date of birth, address and state of residence), the account number and the amount.
- § 4: monthly reporting; first period 1 March to 31 December 2015 (constitutional provision).
- § 16: „Meldungen nach diesem Bundesgesetz sind letztmalig für Kapitalabflüsse im Dezember 2022 zu erstatten.“

## Relevance and limits

This qualifies any claim that Austrian banks have never reported domestic customers' cash withdrawals to the tax administration. For about eight years they reported single or connected withdrawals and transfers of EUR 50,000 or more. The duty has ended. It was threshold- and event-based, not an annual total of all withdrawals. Whether reports were actually filed in every case, and how the BMF used them, was not examined.
