# VfGH G 37-38/2024: civil action and party application defeat direct review

The VfGH held that bringing an ordinary civil action is generally a reasonable route for raising constitutional objections to a prejudicial statute. The claimant can ask the trial court to apply to the VfGH and, after a first-instance judgment, can combine an appeal with a party application under Article 140(1)(1)(d) B-VG.

Poor prospects, ordinary litigation risk and cost do not by themselves make that route unreasonable. The decision supports treating a legally available Article 79 GDPR civil proceeding as a potential subsidiarity barrier to a direct Article 140(1)(1)(c) application, without implying that the civil claim will win on its merits.

Relevant passage: paragraph 21.
