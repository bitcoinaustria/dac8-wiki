# VfGH G 34/2022: no applicant right under section 20a VfGG

The applicant combined an Article 140(1)(1)(c) challenge with a request for interim protection under section 20a VfGG. The VfGH rejected the statutory-review application on pleading grounds and clarified that section 20a, at least on its wording, creates no applicant right. Interim protection may be ordered ex officio if the EU-law conditions are met.

The case therefore does not make a direct Individualantrag an automatically suspensive or uniquely controllable route. It also does not exclude ex officio interim protection in an admissible pending case.

Relevant passage: paragraph 6.
