# Desjardins findings: restricted warehouse, accessible shared exports

Canadian OPC, PIPEDA Findings2020-005,14December2020. Locators §§1,7,28–37,68–82,87–96,106,114–131.

An insider without confidential warehouse rights accessed colleagues' exports through shared folders. Identity/contact identifiers and transactions were involved.9.7million people were affected by access and/or exfiltration, not all confirmed exported. Information included3.9million inactive files, some decades old. Police detected the breach. Remediation restricted analytics/shared folders and replaced size-only thresholds with sensitivity-based approval. Offered victim protection was found adequate. Resale allegations partly depended on media; no comprehensive fraud-loss total or current Austrian finding.

Mechanical credibility 1; independently assessed. Historical and vendor-primary records are accepted for their unique bounded incident/implementation evidence, not population rates. No private dataset or victim record was collected.
