# VfGH G 252/2016 and G 188/2021 on challenge scope and remaining text

In [G 252/2016](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/VfghEntscheidung.wxe?Abfrage=Vfgh&Dokumentnummer=JFT_20161125_16G00252_00&IncludeSelf=True&ShowPrintPreview=True), the VfGH held that a statutory challenge must not be framed too narrowly to permit the Court to make the least intrusive legally coherent removal. A broader request does not necessarily make the whole application inadmissible; where the relevant provisions are prejudicial, partial rejection can accompany a narrower annulment.

In [G 188/2021](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/VfghEntscheidung.wxe?Abfrage=Vfgh&Dokumentnummer=JFT_20211005_21G00188_00&IncludeSelf=True), the VfGH restated three failure modes: the requested removal leaves an unintelligible and unusable remainder, does not eliminate the alleged unconstitutionality, or transforms the provision into a meaning no longer attributable to the legislature.

For Krypto-MPfG, the requested provisions and legal consequence must be tested as a functional unit. Isolated removal of a deletion sentence or technical reporting rule can worsen or leave the disputed data processing unchanged.
