# Directive (EU) 2024/1640

The 2024 recast accompanies AMLR and generally requires transposition by 10 July 2027. Articles 11 to 15 govern access to beneficial-ownership registers after the CJEU invalidated unconditional public access. Article 16 requires national central mechanisms for payment, bank, securities, crypto-asset accounts, and safe-deposit relationships held by financial institutions; minimum BARIS interconnection is due by 10 July 2029.

Article 21 gives FIUs immediate and direct access to account mechanisms and defined administrative, tax, transfer, law-enforcement, and obliged-entity information. Articles 55 to 57 require sanction and corrective-measure systems for serious, repeated, or systematic AMLR and TFR breaches. Article 77 repeals Directive 2015/849 from 10 July 2027.
