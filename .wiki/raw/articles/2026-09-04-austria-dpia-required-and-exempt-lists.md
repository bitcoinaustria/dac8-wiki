# Austria: DPIA required and exempt processing lists

The DSB's DPIA-required regulation, BGBl II 278/2018, includes specified automated assessments with possible adverse legal or financial effects and specified uses of new technologies or data combinations.

The [DPIA exemption regulation, BGBl II 108/2018](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/BgblAuth/BGBLA_2018_II_108/BGBLA_2018_II_108.html), lists categories that include customer administration, accounting and bookkeeping (`DSFA-A01`), registers established by law (`DSFA-A06`) and public tax administration (`DSFA-A17`). The exemption applies only within the described category and conditions.

A Krypto-MPfG DPIA inquiry must therefore determine the actual processing rather than assume the result. A provider or authority may rely on an exemption; the complainant can test whether generation, linking, transmission and long-term retention of the relevant crypto-tax profile remain within that exemption or constitute materially different high-risk processing.

The existence or absence of a DPIA is principally an evidence and compliance issue. CJEU C-61/22 precludes treating a legislative DPIA defect as automatic invalidity of the underlying statute.
