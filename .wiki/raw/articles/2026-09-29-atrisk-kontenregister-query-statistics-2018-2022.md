# Kontenregister query statistics 2018-2022

**Sources.** A series of written answers to NEOS questions (Nikolaus Scherak et al.). Each year there is one BMF answer covering criminal-tax authorities, the BFG and tax authorities, and one BMJ answer covering prosecutors and criminal courts. All were read as PDF from parlament.gv.at on 2026-09-29:

- 2018: 2770/AB and 2774/AB (XXVI. GP), 4 April 2019.
- First half of 2019: 3780/AB and 3784/AB (XXVI. GP), 23 August 2019.
- Second half of 2019: 727/AB and 697/AB (XXVII. GP), March 2020.
- 2020: 5075/AB and 5068/AB (XXVII. GP), 19 March 2021.
- 2021: 9451/AB and 9460/AB (XXVII. GP), 4 April 2022.
- 2022: 13991/AB (canonical URL above) and 13994/AB (XXVII. GP), 3 May 2023.

**Information given from the register, by legal basis in § 4 KontRegG:**

| Year | Prosecutors, § 4(1)(1) | Criminal courts, § 4(1)(1) | Criminal-tax authorities, § 4(1)(2) | BFG, § 4(1)(2) and (3) | Tax authorities, § 4(1)(3) | Assessment cases, § 4(5) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2018 | 1,242 | 4 | 335 | 0 | 5,169 | 7 | 6,757 |
| 2019 | 1,680 | 4 | 260 | 3 | 5,498 | 9 | 7,454 |
| 2020 | 1,802 | 5 | 245 | 1 | 3,414 | 2 | 5,469 |
| 2021 | 1,993 | 11 | 213 | 2 | 3,841 | 1 | 6,061 |
| 2022 | 1,877 | 8 | 225 | 2 | 5,025 | 3 | 7,140 |

The totals and the 2019 half-year aggregation are this digest's arithmetic. The 2018 and 2019 totals match figures reported in the media (6,757 and 7,454).

**Account inspection (§§ 8–9 KontRegG, BFG approval).** Tax-authority applications and approvals were: 2018, 6 applications and 2 approvals; 2019, 4 applications and 1 approval; 2020, 3 and 2; 2021, 6 and 2; 2022, 8 and 2. Each year there were no applications in income, corporate or VAT assessment proceedings under § 8(3). The § 4(5) opportunity to comment was used as often as § 4(5) queries occurred.

**Missing facts and negative findings.**

- No answers covering 2023–2025 were found. A search of the parliament's question list for the XXVII. and XXVIII. GP on 2026-09-29 returned no Kontenregister questions after March 2023.
- The BMF publishes no annual Kontenregister statistics on its site.
- A RIS OGD full-text search on 2026-09-29 found no VfGH decisions for "Kontenregister" or "Konteneinschau". The same search found hits at the VwGH (4 and 6). This supports only "no VfGH decision located", not "none exists".

**Evidence class and transfer.** These are official ministerial statistics. For proportionality they show that a central register covering essentially all Austrian account relationships is queried a few thousand times a year, against about 5–7 million assessments. Access to transactional detail is judicially filtered and very rare. This supports the argument that universal collection outruns actual use. It also supports the State's comparator argument that relationship metadata held centrally, with case-by-case logged access and judicial gating of substance, is a minimising design. The Kontenregister figures are not DAC8 figures. DAC8 data are transactional and are received in bulk for all reportable users, not queried case by case, so frequency comparisons need care.
