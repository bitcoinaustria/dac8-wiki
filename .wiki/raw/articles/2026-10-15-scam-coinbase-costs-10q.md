# Coinbase incident costs and recoveries in the June 2026 Form 10-Q

Quarter ended 30 June 2026; signed 30 July. Locators: printed pp.35–38, incident expense definition and Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation.

The incident-specific reconciliation reports USD 306.654 million net losses for Q2 and H1 2025, followed by net recoveries of USD 33.854 million in Q2 2026 and USD 25.244 million in H1 2026. Tables are in thousands; quarterly and half-year amounts overlap.

The measure includes voluntary customer reimbursements, direct legal costs and qualifying rewards if any, net of recoveries. It is not a total of customer funds stolen or unreimbursed losses. Broader platform-incident expenses have different totals. No new victim-count or field-level evidence is supplied.

Mechanical score 1: recent known issuer, vendor discount; accepted as primary accounting disclosure, not an adjudication.
