# Trezor Brevo incident: exported emails and observed phishing

First-party report dated 10 September 2026, updated 17 September. Locators: update, campaign narrative and exposed-data FAQ.

- Brevo's 9 September compromise affected 120 customer accounts. Trezor confirms export of 347,149 newsletter addresses.
- Approximately 347,000 recipients received a message linking to a wallet-backup-seeking app; about 2,500 clicked before DNS takedown within twenty minutes.
- The report excludes passwords and wallet data from the exposed newsletter system. It gives no seed-disclosure or monetary-loss count.

These are different denominators, not interchangeable victim totals. Provider confirmation supports compromise-to-campaign evidence; it is not independent forensics or proof that clicking alone exposed funds. Mechanical credibility score 1: recent, known institution, vendor discount; accepted for unique first-party incident observations.
