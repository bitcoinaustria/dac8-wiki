# Austrian police: detailed prior-fraud knowledge in recovery scams

Undated official guidance, checked 15 October 2026. Locator: Recovery Scam subsection.

Police describe earlier investment-fraud victims approached again by purported officials, lawyers or financial institutions. Scammers claim that lost money is secured on a frozen account and demand advance fees or taxes. Detailed knowledge of the original fraud helps persuade victims to pay again.
