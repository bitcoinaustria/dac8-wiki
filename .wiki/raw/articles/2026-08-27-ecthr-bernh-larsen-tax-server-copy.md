# ECtHR Bernh Larsen: broad tax server copy with safeguards

In application 24117/08, judgment of 14 March 2013, Norwegian tax authorities required a copy of a shared company server. The Court accepted that the measure was broad but relied on concrete safeguards, including an immediate complaint, sealing while the dispute was resolved, logging of searches, filtering and deletion or return of irrelevant material, and judicial review.

The case concerned a corporate tax audit and not automatic international exchange. It is adverse to categorical claims that broad financial-data access is always disproportionate, while illustrating the kind of timely and concrete safeguards that matter to Article 8 proportionality.
