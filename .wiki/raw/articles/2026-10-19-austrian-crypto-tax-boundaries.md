# Austrian crypto tax: realization and transitional dates

Official BMF overview and linked statutory provisions retrieved 19 October 2026. Locators: BMF sections on realized gains and commencement; [EStG §27b(3)](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=27b) and [§124b Z384(a)–(b)](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=124b).

The new regime commenced on 1 March 2022 for acquisitions after 28 February 2021. Older holdings generally retain the prior treatment; newly generated coins require separate classification. Section 27b(3) addresses disposal/exchange gains, not mere holding.

A same-owner wallet movement is not itself disposal to another owner (statutory inference from §27b(3), not an express BMF quotation). This does not resolve associated fees, income events or every asset's classification. Reporting scope and tax liability remain separate questions; this is not an individual tax assessment.
