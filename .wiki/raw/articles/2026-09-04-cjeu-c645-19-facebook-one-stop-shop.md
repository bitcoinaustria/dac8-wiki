# CJEU C-645/19 Facebook Ireland: one-stop-shop and urgent exceptions

- Judgment: 15 June 2021
- ECLI: `ECLI:EU:C:2021:483`

Paragraphs 50–63 and the operative ruling confirm that the lead-supervisory-authority mechanism generally governs cross-border processing. A supervisory authority that is not the lead may exercise binding powers only in the situations provided by the GDPR. Paragraph 59 identifies Article 66 as an urgent provisional-measures route.

An Austrian complaint involving a foreign provider must therefore identify the controller's main establishment and anticipate possible Article 56 and 60 coordination. Article 66 is an exceptional, temporary route, not a general means to avoid the lead-authority system.
