# DAC8.com: unproven-effectiveness argument

The page argues that Commission evaluations could not quantify all benefits, that the European Court of Auditors found quality and use defects, and that CRS evidence shows displacement as well as effects. It proposes that an intrusive system should require stronger necessity and cost-benefit evidence.

## Source boundary

The cited institutional measurement gaps are real leads, but the page overstates them when it says effectiveness "has never been demonstrated." The Commission's 2025 evaluation also concludes that the DAC framework effectively and efficiently strengthened Member States' capacity, while acknowledging limited benefit statistics. Both propositions must be presented.

AML cost and recovery figures are not direct evidence of DAC8's tax effectiveness. Behavioural-exit arguments remain hypotheses unless supported by DAC8-specific evidence.
