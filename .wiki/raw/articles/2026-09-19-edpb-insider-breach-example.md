# EDPB insider breach example

Selected digest of version 2.0, adopted 14 December 2021, case 08, pages 20–21, paragraphs 72–77. A departing employee copies basic client contact data for a new business. Legitimate access makes prevention difficult; access policies, export restrictions and continuing controls can help. The employee's claimed benign intentions do not remove the need for a risk assessment.

Paragraph 77 finds notification to the supervisory authority appropriate but no high risk requiring notification to data subjects in this specific case. The example therefore does not support a claim that every insider leak is high-risk. It is institutional guidance, not an identified adjudicated incident, a crypto case or evidence of a physical attack.
