# VfGH G 151/1999 ua: WEB proceeding appeal deadline

The applicants challenged an inflexible four-week period for preparing a nullity appeal. The court accepted direct effect in light of the imminent judgment and preparations already required, and found no reasonable detour.

The application was fact-heavy: it quantified the exceptional volume of files, expert material, seized documents, trial minutes, and defence work. The case models how concrete scale and evidence can convert an abstract burden into an individual constitutional injury.
