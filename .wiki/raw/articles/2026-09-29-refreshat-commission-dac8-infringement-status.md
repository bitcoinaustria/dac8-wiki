# DAC8 non-transposition infringements, 2026

## Official record (INF/26/115, "January infringements package: key decisions", Brussels, 30 January 2026, Taxation section)

The Commission "decided to open infringement procedures by sending a letter of formal notice to 12 Member States – Belgium, Bulgaria, Czechia, Estonia, Greece, Spain, Cyprus, Luxembourg, Malta, the Netherlands, Poland and Portugal for failing to fully transpose Directive (EU) 2023/2226". They had two months to respond, complete transposition and notify. "In the absence of a satisfactory response, the Commission may decide to issue a reasoned opinion." Individual INFR numbers are not given in the passage.

The same package sent letters of formal notice to BE, BG, CZ, EL, CY, MT, NL, PT, RO and SE for Directive (EU) 2025/872 (DAC9).

Austria does not appear in the DAC8 list. This is consistent with the wiki claim that the Krypto-MPfG (BGBl. I Nr. 96/2025, in force 1 January 2026) met the transposition deadline. That claim appears in `wiki/topics/austrian-dac8-implementation.md`.

## Later packages checked

- 11 March 2026 package (official PDF INF/26/431): no reference to Directive 2023/2226.
- 4 June 2026 package (EUbusiness summary): no DAC8 item. It includes a DAC7 letter of formal notice to Poland, INFR(2026)2070.
- 8 July 2026 package (EUbusiness summary): no DAC8 item. Reasoned opinions went to BE, BG and CY for DAC9 (Directive 2025/872).

Limits: the April, May and September 2026 packages, and the Commission's infringement-decisions database, were not checked. EUR-Lex national-transposition (NIM) pages for 32023L2226 could not be retrieved on 29 September 2026. Several of the 12 States, including NL, BE and LU, have since enacted measures according to `wiki/references/member-state-implementation-map.md`. Whether their files were closed is unknown.

## Research use

Infringement proceedings for non-communication are enforcement between the Commission and a State. They create no individual remedy and say nothing about whether DAC8 is valid. They are relevant to the Member-State playbook's national-delta step (commencement and version dates) and to the claim that delayed transposition does not itself invalidate DAC8.
