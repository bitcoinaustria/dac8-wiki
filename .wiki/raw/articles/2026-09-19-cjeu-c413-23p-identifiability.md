# EDPS v SRB, 4 September 2025

Selected judgment digest, ECLI:EU:C:2025:645. Paragraphs 73–87 reject both universal anonymity and universal personal-data status for every recipient of pseudonymised information. Effective separation and safeguards matter alongside reasonably likely identification means, including cross-checking and access to other information. Onward disclosure requires its own assessment.

Paragraphs 110–115 assess the controller's information obligation from its perspective when collecting the data, not by a later recipient's ability to identify. The Court set aside the General Court judgment; paragraphs 120–122 dispose of the first first-instance plea and remit the unexamined second plea. The whole dispute was not finally resolved.

The case applies Regulation 2018/1725, with homogeneous-interpretation context for equivalent GDPR principles. It is not a DAC8 ruling. Already identity-linked reports need no hypothetical re-identification to qualify as personal data; additional ledger inference is a different question.
