# ECtHR admissibility: victim status, exhaustion and four-month period

An applicant must be a victim of the alleged Convention violation; the Convention does not permit an abstract actio popularis against legislation. The applicant must use available and effective domestic remedies capable of addressing the Convention complaint and must raise the substance of that complaint domestically.

The complete application must ordinarily be lodged within four months of the final decision in the effective domestic chain. International or discretionary public-enforcement petitions do not replace exhaustion. The exact final decision and start date are route- and fact-specific and must be docketed conservatively.
