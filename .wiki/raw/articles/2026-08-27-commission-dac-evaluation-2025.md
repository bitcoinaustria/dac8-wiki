# European Commission 2025 DAC evaluation

The Commission states that assessment of benefits remained extremely limited because statistical information was unavailable. It also concludes that the DAC framework is robust and that its mechanisms effectively and efficiently contributed to Member States' ability to fight tax fraud, evasion, and avoidance, while requiring substantial public and private investment.

This mixed conclusion is important context for necessity arguments: measurement uncertainty is established, but institutional evidence does not support the categorical claim that DAC has no demonstrated effect.
