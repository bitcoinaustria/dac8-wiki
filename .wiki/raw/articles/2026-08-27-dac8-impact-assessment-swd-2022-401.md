# European Commission DAC8 impact assessment SWD(2022) 401

The Commission presents the preferred harmonised option as addressing crypto tax-compliance gaps, avoiding national fragmentation, limiting collection to a minimum dataset, and complying with data-protection law.

For review purposes, this is evidence both of the legislative rationale and of factual assumptions that may be tested. It is not independent proof that every adopted field, retention period, or national implementation choice is strictly necessary.
