# CJEU 2025 judicial statistics: preliminary-ruling duration

The Court of Justice's official 2025 statistics report an average duration of 16.9 months for preliminary-ruling proceedings. The urgent preliminary-ruling procedure averaged 3.4 months, but that procedure is confined to the Area of Freedom, Security and Justice and is not a general acceleration mechanism for DAC8 tax-reporting litigation.

The figures are institutional averages, not a statutory time limit or a forecast for a particular reference. Any national proceedings suspended for a reference add their own duration.
