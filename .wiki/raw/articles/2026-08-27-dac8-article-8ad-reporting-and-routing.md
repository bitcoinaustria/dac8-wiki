# DAC8 Article 8ad and Annex VI: reporting fields and routing

Article 8ad requires the competent authority in the reporting Member State to communicate the specified information to the competent authorities of the Member States concerned.

The reported transaction information is annual and aggregated by crypto-asset and transaction category. It includes amounts or fair-market values, units, and transaction counts. For transfers to distributed-ledger addresses not known to be associated with a virtual-asset service provider or financial institution, the directive requires aggregate fair-market value and aggregate units.

The reporting list does not require the distributed-ledger address itself to be communicated. Nor does it require every user's data to be copied to all 27 Member States. These limits are material to any blockchain-correlation or attack-surface claim.
