# VfGH G 2/2013

Decision of 1 October 2013.

Reasons 2.3.2–2.4 and 2.7.2–2.7.3 reject a design under which data lawfully obtained for one purpose become generally available in other proceedings. Secondary use requires sufficiently precise legal authority, a substantive connection to the new purpose and a proportionate boundary.

The decision supports scrutiny of concrete DAC8 linkage or reuse beyond the purposes authorised in DAC Article 16 and EU-AHG section 15. It does not invalidate the purposes expressly authorised by those provisions.
