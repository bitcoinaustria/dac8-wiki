# ECtHR Centrum for rattvisa on destruction, foreign transfer and binding review

In *Centrum for rattvisa v Sweden*, application 35252/08, Grand Chamber judgment of 25 May 2021, the Court accepted that bulk interception is not inherently prohibited and found much of Sweden's detailed system adequately constrained (paragraphs 261 to 273 and 365 to 368).

The Article 8 violation rested cumulatively on three concrete weaknesses: unclear destruction of some material, insufficient safeguards in transmission to foreign partners, and ex-post control that did not produce sufficiently reasoned and binding decisions (paragraphs 359 to 374).

For DAC8 the case supports questions about recipient-state safeguards, onward use, actual deletion and correction, and whether the chosen route produces a binding remedy. It does not turn tax exchange into secret intelligence surveillance or demand an identical authorisation structure.
