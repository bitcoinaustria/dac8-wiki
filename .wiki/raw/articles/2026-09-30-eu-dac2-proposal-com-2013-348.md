# Commission proposal COM(2013) 348 for DAC2

- Proposal for a Council Directive amending Directive 2011/16/EU as regards mandatory automatic exchange of information in the field of taxation, COM(2013) 348 final, 2013/0188 (CNS), dated 12 June 2013; CELEX `52013PC0348`

The explanatory memorandum presents the proposal as accelerating the extension already foreseen in Article 8(5) of Directive 2011/16/EU. It states that the agreements Member States concluded or would conclude with the United States on FATCA gave further impetus to automatic exchange, and records that on 9 April 2013 France, Germany, the United Kingdom, Italy and Spain announced a pilot for automatic exchange using the FATCA model. It argues that information on the new items would necessarily be available because financial intermediaries would have to report it under the FATCA agreements, and that uncoordinated bilateral arrangements under Article 19 of the directive (most-favoured-nation clause) should be avoided.

The adopted text, Council Directive 2014/107/EU of 9 December 2014, is recorded at [2026-08-27-dac2-directive-2014-107.md](2026-08-27-dac2-directive-2014-107.md). Verified adopted-text details (local PDF `legal-docs/core/eu-dac2-directive-2014-107.pdf`): OJ L 359, 16.12.2014, p. 1; recitals 2–4 cite G20/G8, FATCA and the OECD Common Reporting Standard (main elements February 2014, full standard July 2014, G20 endorsement September 2014); transposition by 31 December 2015 and application from 1 January 2016 (Article 2(1)); Austria applies it from 1 January 2017 for taxable periods from that date (Article 2(2)); recital 19 allows Austria to exchange for the first time by 30 September 2018 instead of 30 September 2017; Annex I Section X reads "2016"/"2017" as "2017"/"2018" and "31 December 2015" as "31 December 2016" for Austrian Reporting Financial Institutions. Entry into force 5 January 2015 (20th day after publication).
