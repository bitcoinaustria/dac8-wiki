# Current KontRegG

Sections 1 and 2 create a central BMF register for bank, payment, securities, depot, and safe-deposit relationships and define identity, institution, account type, and opening/closing data. It does not contain public-chain addresses, self-hosted wallets, or every CASP transaction.

Section 4 limits access to enumerated authorities and purposes, logs the responsible official and transmission, and retains logs for ten years. Persons can inspect their own register data; tax queries trigger FinanzOnline notice. Section 5 retains relationship data for ten years after the closing year. Section 9 requires BFG single-judge authorisation for tax inspection of underlying account information and supplies a statutory evidence-use safeguard.
