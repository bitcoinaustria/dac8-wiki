# FMA-published AML/CFT sanctions, 2025–2026

Source: the FMA "Sanktion" news category (listing pages 1–8, back to October 2024) and the individual notices listed below, all read on 29 September 2026. The FMA publishes under § 37 FM-GwG. "Beschleunigt beendet" means the case was closed under § 22 Abs. 2b FMABG: the party waived appeal before the decision, and the waiver counts as a full admission in exchange for a lower fine (FMA annual report 2025, p. 87).

FM-GwG sanctions found (publication date; entity; fine; subject; status):
- **13 May 2025:** Grazer Wechselseitige Versicherung AG; EUR 40,000; inadequate procedures for verifying source of funds; accelerated, final.
- **23 May 2025:** Raiffeisen-Landesbank Steiermark AG; EUR 40,000; ongoing monitoring procedures not applied continuously; accelerated, final.
- **13 June 2025:** Raiffeisenlandesbank Niederösterreich-Wien AG; EUR 60,000; updating of CDD data and ongoing monitoring not applied continuously, plus inadequate enhanced-due-diligence procedures; accelerated, final.
- **6 October 2025:** Coinfinity GmbH (Austrian crypto broker); EUR 105,000; business-wide risk assessment, staff training, and CDD procedures for occasional transactions; accelerated, final.
- **6 October 2025:** Kurant GmbH (crypto-ATM operator); EUR 70,000; ongoing-monitoring procedures not set out in writing; accelerated, final.
- **23 October 2025:** Volksbank Niederösterreich AG; EUR 52,000; no adequate procedures for obtaining and verifying source of funds for cash deposits; accelerated, final.
- **19 December 2025:** bank99 AG; EUR 60,000; customer risk classification, identification and verification of senders of cash transfers, and source of funds for cash transfers; accelerated, final.
- **17 March 2026:** NOTARTREUHANDBANK AG; EUR 127,500; inadequate ongoing-monitoring procedures; not final, appealed to the BVwG (update of 13 April 2026).
- **5 May 2026:** Sparkasse Oberösterreich Bank AG; EUR 60,000; procedures for understanding ownership and control structure and for regularly updating CDD data; accelerated, final.
- **18 August 2026:** Kurant GmbH; additional fine of EUR 45,000; CDD procedures for occasional transactions and for suspected terrorist-organisation membership or laundering involvement; accelerated, final.

Also relevant:
- **Schelhammer Capital Bank AG:** additional fine of EUR 476,000 (published 20 December 2024) for failing to understand the ownership structure of a high-risk client. The BVwG confirmed guilt on 2 March 2026 and reduced the fine to EUR 356,000.
- **Bitpanda GmbH:** fined EUR 70,000 on 14 August 2026 under MiCAR (white-paper notification and marketing communications), not the FM-GwG.

The characterisation of Coinfinity and Kurant as crypto providers is general background, not stated in the notices.

Evidence class: official enforcement notices. Primary source for the fact, amount and subject of each sanction. The notices are short and give no customer counts or harm. Adverse evidence: the FMA does enforce CDD duties against both banks and crypto providers. Two Austrian crypto providers were fined in 2025–2026 for CDD-procedure failures. Limits: every notice concerns procedures, monitoring, source of funds or ownership structure. **None** concerns forged-ID onboarding or failures of the § 6 Abs. 4 remote-identification route, so these notices cannot be cited as enforcement of the A-FIU remote-identification finding. Fines are small compared with the § 34 caps (EUR 150,000 standard, EUR 5 million for serious breaches), and most were reduced through the accelerated procedure. The list covers only published sanctions; unpublished measures, and sanctions before October 2024, were not reviewed.
