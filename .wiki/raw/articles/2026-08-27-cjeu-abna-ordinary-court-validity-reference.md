# CJEU ABNA: ordinary national proceedings can carry a directive-validity reference

Joined Cases C-453/03, C-11/04, C-12/04 and C-194/04 arose from concrete proceedings before ordinary national courts in several Member States. Those courts referred questions on the validity and interpretation of an EU directive. The CJEU held one mandatory declaration requirement invalid because the measure was disproportionate.

The case is a structural counterexample to procedural exclusivity. A properly constituted DSB/BVwG or civil dispute may also become an Article 267 validity vehicle; the claimant does not need direct access to the VfGH merely to place an EU-validity question before the CJEU. The referring court must still consider the question necessary, and the claimant cannot compel a reference.
