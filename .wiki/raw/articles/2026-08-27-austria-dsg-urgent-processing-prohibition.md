# Austria DSG sections 22(4) and 25(1): urgent processing prohibition

Under section 25(1) DSG, a complainant who credibly shows a substantial impairment of protected confidentiality interests through the processing can trigger consideration under section 22(4).

Section 22(4) permits the DSB, where operation of processing creates a substantial and immediate danger to protected confidentiality interests and there is danger in delay, to prohibit all or part of the processing by a mandate decision under section 57(1) AVG. The power is conditional and discretionary; it is not an automatic stay and does not establish that the DAC8 facts satisfy the threshold.
