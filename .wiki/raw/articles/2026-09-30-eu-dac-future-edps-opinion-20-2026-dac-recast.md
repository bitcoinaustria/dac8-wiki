# EDPS Opinion 20/2026 on the DAC recast proposal

## Source identity

EDPS Opinion 20/2026 on the Proposal for a Council Directive on administrative cooperation in the field of taxation (recast), COM(2026) 308. It was issued under Article 42(1) of Regulation (EU) 2018/1725 and signed in Brussels on 17 August 2026 by Wojciech Wiewiórowski. It was circulated to Council delegations as ST 12521/26 on 24 August 2026 (interinstitutional file 2026/0168(CNS)). The Council copy is image-only; the executive summary, contents and conclusions were read from rendered pages.

## Content

The EDPS supports consolidation and recommends that the recast:

- keep "foreseeable relevance" as the overarching interpretative principle of the DAC;
- clarify how the receiving Member State is identified under tax-residence rules;
- extend the feedback mechanism in Article 28 to individual inaccuracies and duplications;
- ensure that TIN or verified-identifier reporting matches the correct taxpayer;
- set out purpose limitation and access conditions for tax-authority access to AML registers (Article 38) in the enacting terms;
- limit access to pension registers (Article 39) to secure, query-based access for specified purposes and data categories;
- require aggregation for statistical use (Article 30, recital 107);
- specify restrictions of data-subject rights in the Directive itself (Article 40) rather than leaving them to Member States;
- make the five-year retention period in Article 40(6) apply only while retention remains necessary and proportionate, with periodic review and erasure or anonymisation.

## Relevance and limits

This is an advisory opinion. It does not bind the Council and does not assess the validity of DAC8. Its retention recommendation concerns information exchanged between tax administrations, not the RCASP record-keeping range of five to 10 years in Annex VI Section V. It is the most recent EU institutional data-protection text on the DAC framework as a whole. It sits alongside EDPS Opinion 9/2023 on DAC8 (`2026-08-27-edps-opinion-9-2023-dac8.md`).
