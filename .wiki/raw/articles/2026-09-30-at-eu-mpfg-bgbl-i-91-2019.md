# Austria: EU-Meldepflichtgesetz (EU-MPfG)

## Source identity

- Gazette: `BGBl. I Nr. 91/2019`, Abgabenänderungsgesetz 2020 – AbgÄG 2020, ausgegeben am 22. Oktober 2019 (NR: GP XXVI IA 983/A AB 686 S. 88; BR: AB 10251; CELEX 32011L0016, 32018L0822)
- Art. 1 Digitalsteuergesetz 2020; Art. 2 EU-Meldepflichtgesetz; further articles amend EStG, UStG, FinStrG, BAO, Werbeabgabegesetz, GMSG, FAG 2017 and EU-AHG.
- The bill was an Initiativantrag (983/A), not a government bill.
- Entry into force: § 27 EU-MPfG, "Dieses Bundesgesetz tritt mit 1. Juli 2020 in Kraft."

## Content

§ 1 implements Directive (EU) 2018/822 on mandatory exchange concerning reportable cross-border arrangements. Reporting covers arrangements whose first step was implemented between 25 June 2018 and 30 June 2020, arrangements whose first step is implemented from 1 July 2020, and arrangements designed, marketed, organised, made available or managed from 1 July 2020. § 5 lists unconditionally reportable hallmarks, including arrangements that undermine the CRS reporting obligation or obscure beneficial ownership.

## Relevance and limits

DAC6 targets intermediaries and arrangements, not ordinary account holders. Later amendments (the current § 27 also covers BGBl. I Nr. 96/2025) and any Austrian handling of the optional COVID-19 deferral (Directive (EU) 2020/876) were not examined.
