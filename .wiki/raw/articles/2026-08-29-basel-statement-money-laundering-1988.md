# Basel Committee 1988 statement

The statement asked bank management to identify customers, discourage business associated with criminal funds or purposes and cooperate with law-enforcement authorities subject to local confidentiality rules. Refusal or termination was contemplated where the bank had reasonable grounds to suspect criminal origin or purpose. It expressly described itself as a statement of principles rather than a legal document.

It is an early prudential and operational predecessor of KYC, not binding legislation and not a complete FATF-style AML framework.
