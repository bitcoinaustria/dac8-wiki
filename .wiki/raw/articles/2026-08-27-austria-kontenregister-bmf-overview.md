# Austrian Kontenregister and Konteneinschau

Austria's account register has operated since 2016. It records who holds which covered account, depot, or safe-deposit relationship with which institution, but the BMF says it does not contain balances or account movements. Those details require a separate account-inspection procedure, including stated statutory conditions and BFG approval for tax inspection.

The register supplies an important comparator: limited relationship discovery can be separated from access to transactional substance. It also shows the State's answer that a central query can be more privacy-preserving than broadcasting requests to hundreds of institutions, provided access, purpose, logging, notification, and review are genuinely bounded.
