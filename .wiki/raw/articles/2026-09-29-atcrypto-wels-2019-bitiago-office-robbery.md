# Wels July 2019 armed office robbery at a crypto firm

Source digest built from two media reports read on 29 September 2026:

- ORF Oberösterreich, "Mann im Büro überfallen" (canonical URL). The story names no company.
- [Trending Topics, "Bitiago: Welser Krypto-Firma musste nach Raubüberfall Konkurs anmelden"](https://www.trendingtopics.eu/bitiago-konkurs/). It identifies the firm and quotes the police.

This is the Lopp list's "Man raided in office" row, dated 26 July 2019.

**Facts as reported.** At about 21:00 on 25 July 2019, two masked men armed with a pistol and a rubber truncheon rang at an office in Wels. They pushed back the 30-year-old who opened the door and struck him. They demanded cash and forced him to transfer cryptocurrency to an address they specified. They left him bound with cable ties and tape; he freed himself and called the police.

According to Trending Topics, the victim was a co-founder of Crypto Consulting GmbH (brand Bitiago). The losses were a mid-five-figure cash sum and crypto reportedly worth over €100,000. The founder put the total damage at under €200,000 and said the business could not continue "wegen des Raubüberfalls" (because of the robbery). The company filed for insolvency about three weeks later. The same article says the firm had raised €140,000 from 115 crowd-investors in 2018. ORF reports that the Landeskriminalamt was investigating and that the crypto transfers could provide leads.

**Status and limits.** Evidence class: media reports at the investigation stage. No arrest, charge or judgment was found in searches on 29 September 2026. How the perpetrators chose the office is not reported. The victim was a business openly operating in crypto, which differs from a private holder identified through data.
