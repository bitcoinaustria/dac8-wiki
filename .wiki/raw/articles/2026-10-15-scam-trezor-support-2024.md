# Trezor support incident: 41 seed-seeking emails without disclosed seeds

Displayed publication 19 January 2024; body includes 20 January update. Locators: incident, immediate response and FAQs.

After detecting third-party portal intrusion on 17 January, Trezor identified 41 users directly emailed for recovery seeds. It states none disclosed seeds and no funds were compromised through this incident.

Up to 66,000 support contacts since December 2021 were potentially accessible, not confirmed extracted. Fields were name/nickname and email, excluding phone/postal address. Eight trial-forum participants were separately potentially exposed; overlap is unspecified.

This documents attempted exploitation and an adverse successful-intervention outcome, not 41 thefts or 66,000 proven leaks. Mechanical score 1; recent first-party, with vendor discount; accepted for uniquely reported contacts and limiting findings.
