# CARF MCAA: bilateral activation

Section 1(1)(e) defines an agreement in effect between two competent authorities by their notifications, including each listing the other's jurisdiction. Section 7(1) specifies notifications about domestic law and dates, reciprocity, safeguards and intended partners. Section 7(2) makes the relationship effective when the second authority supplies the required notification. Signature alone does not demonstrate an active bilateral relationship.
