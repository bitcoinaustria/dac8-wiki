# CJEU C-203/22 Dun & Bradstreet Austria: intelligible explanation and confidential evidence

- Judgment: 27 February 2025
- ECLI: `ECLI:EU:C:2025:117`

Paragraphs 40–66 require meaningful information about the logic involved in an Article 22 decision to explain the procedure and principles actually applied so the person can understand how their data were used. Complete source code or every mathematical step is not required.

Paragraphs 67–76 reject a blanket reliance on trade secrets against the supervisory authority or court. Protected information can be submitted to those bodies for the required balancing, even where it is not disclosed unfiltered to the data subject.

The ruling matters only if the concrete system produces a solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect. Automated aggregation, validation, transmission or risk preparation is not automatically an Article 22 decision.
