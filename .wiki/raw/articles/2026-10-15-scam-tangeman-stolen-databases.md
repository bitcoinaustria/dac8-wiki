# Tangeman plea announcement: stolen databases used for crypto victim selection

DOJ release 8 December 2025, case 24cr417. Locators: enterprise-role and stolen-database paragraphs.

Tangeman pleaded guilty to RICO conspiracy. DOJ says court documents describe using stolen databases to select crypto-theft targets. The enterprise included database hackers, target identifiers, callers and residential burglars seeking hardware wallets.

The announcement also describes superseding-indictment allegations. It does not name an originating provider/database, record fields or the database used for each burglary. Its explicit targeting sentence is prosecution attribution, not a separately inspected causal judgment. The scanned indictment was not independently readable.
