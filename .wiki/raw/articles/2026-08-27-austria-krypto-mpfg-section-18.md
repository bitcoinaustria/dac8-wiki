# Austria Krypto-MPfG section 18: general reporting duties

## Rule captured

For each reportable person for whom a provider conducted reportable transactions during the reporting period, section 18 requires reporting to the competent tax office. Identifying information includes name, address, residence state or states, tax identification number or numbers, and for natural persons the birth date and, where otherwise required, birth place.

The transaction side is aggregated by reportable crypto-asset and transaction category. It covers values, units, and transaction counts for acquisitions, disposals, exchanges, reportable retail-payment transactions, and other transfers to or from the user, subject to the statute's detailed classifications.

## Research use

Section 18 anchors the scope of data collection. A proportionality argument must use the exact categories and aggregation rules, not a broader description of an identity-to-wallet database unless another provision actually requires wallet-address transmission.
