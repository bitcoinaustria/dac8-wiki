# Blackbaud final controls

Final order dated 17 May 2024, consent package PDF pp. 10–14, order sections II, III and IV.E. FTC public confirmation dated 20 May 2024.

The order requires deletion of unnecessary backups but expressly preserves legally required retention and evidence holds, with documented grounds and deletion after the obligation ends. It mandates defined schedules rather than indefinite retention, phishing-resistant employee MFA, least privilege, access monitoring and protection against one customer's credentials opening other customer databases.

This establishes binding regulator-imposed controls that distinguish retention from operational exposure. It does not measure implemented risk reduction, show equivalent tax usefulness or transplant US law into Austrian processing. The package includes the complaint; do not confuse allegations with the operative order.
