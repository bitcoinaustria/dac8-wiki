# VwGH Ro 2020/04/0015

Decision of 3 August 2023.

The VwGH recognised that access under GDPR Article 15 encompasses concrete recipients and the content of transmitted personal data within the conditions of that provision.

For DAC8, the case supports a request for the actual report and actual recipients. Any EU-AHG section 19 restriction remains subject to its own suitability, necessity and proportionality conditions.
