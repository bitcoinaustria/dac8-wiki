# CJEU État luxembourgeois: effective remedies in tax-information proceedings

Joined Cases C-245/19 and C-246/19 concerned information orders issued for international tax cooperation.

Article 47 of the Charter required the information holder, who faced a sanction for non-compliance, to have direct access to a court. A taxpayer under investigation and concerned third parties did not necessarily require the same direct action where an effective later or incidental route enabled review of the information request and use of the evidence.

The judgment therefore rejects a universal equation between effective protection and a pre-transfer direct action. Its factual model is not identical to automatic DAC8 reporting: a DAC8 user may never receive a later assessment in which the data collection and exchange can be reviewed. That distinction must be proved rather than assumed.
