# Austria Krypto-MPfG section 39

Section 39 requires each reporting crypto-asset service provider to document the steps taken to perform the duties in Chapters 3 to 7 and to retain those documents for ten years following the end of the calendar year in which the provider was required to report the information under section 18.

Annex VI Section V.B.1 DAC8 covers records of steps and information relied upon for both Section II reporting and Section III due diligence. Its retention-period sentence ends with **if the information is reportable pursuant to Section II**. Section 39 does not reproduce that phrase, but the phrase is ambiguous as to whether it limits record scope or only identifies the period from which retention is calculated.

The textual difference is unresolved rather than an established national excess. Its practical significance for a non-reportable user depends on the correct interpretation of Annex VI, what the provider treats as a section 39 compliance document and whether such records exist for that person. An ordinary Austrian tax resident is normally reportable and cannot rely on this point without a separate status fact.
