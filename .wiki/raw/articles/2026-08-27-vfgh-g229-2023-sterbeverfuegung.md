# VfGH G 229-230/2023 ua: death directive rules

The applicants challenged criminal-law and Death Directive Act provisions. Some words were annulled, while other requests failed because they were too narrow, too broad, or not connected to a specific constitutional objection.

The judgment is a current drafting authority: each attacked provision requires its own direct-effect facts and constitutional concerns, and main and eventual requests must be structured so that the successful perimeter removes the alleged defect without unnecessarily deleting unrelated law.
