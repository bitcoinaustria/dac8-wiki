# Luxembourg Business Registers and Sovim

Joined Cases C-37/20 and C-601/20 held that access in all cases by any member of the general public was a serious interference with Charter Articles 7 and 8 (paragraphs 37 to 44). AML transparency was legitimate and public access was suitable, but it was not strictly necessary and the balance was disproportionate (paragraphs 65 to 88).

The judgment does not invalidate controlled access by competent authorities or obliged entities; paragraphs 83 and 84 expressly distinguish those channels. Its transferable rule is access-tiering, strict necessity, field minimisation, safeguards, and protection against bulk search and reuse—not total secrecy.
