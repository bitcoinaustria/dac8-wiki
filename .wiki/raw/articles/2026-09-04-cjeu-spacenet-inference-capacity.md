# CJEU SpaceNet on inference capacity despite short retention

In Joined Cases C-793/19 and C-794/19, judgment of 20 September 2022, the Grand Chamber held that short retention periods did not cure general retention of traffic and location data because the categories themselves permitted precise conclusions about private lives (paragraphs 67 to 101). Strong security likewise did not remove the interference.

The Court distinguished the ECtHR's bulk-interception judgments because they concerned a different legal subject and regime (paragraphs 123 to 125).

The usable point for DAC8 is narrow: duration is not a sufficient proxy for interference; actual inference capacity matters. The analogy requires a reproducible demonstration using the fields actually present in the DAC8 report and only those additional data to which the relevant actor has a lawful and practical join path. Annual aggregates are not communications traffic or location data.
