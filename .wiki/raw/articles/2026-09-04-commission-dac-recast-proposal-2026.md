# Commission 2026 proposal to recast the DAC

COM(2026) 308 is a Commission proposal, not enacted law. It consolidates the DAC amendments and includes targeted simplifications while seeking to preserve the level of protection against tax fraud, evasion and avoidance.

For DAC8, the proposal retains the annual asset-and-category transaction fields. It also develops a common validated-TIN and identification-service approach under which less identification information can be reported in qualifying cases. The existing DAC8 rule already permits a narrower identity tuple when a qualifying Member-State or Union identification service directly confirms identity and residence.

The proposal is therefore not evidence that the Commission intends to remove the contested transfer, unit or count fields. It is evidence that identification minimisation can be operationalised when an equally reliable identifier exists. Its pending status requires separate treatment from current law.
