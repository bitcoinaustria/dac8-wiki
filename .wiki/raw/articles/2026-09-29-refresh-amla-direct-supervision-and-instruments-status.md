# AMLA status September 2026

**Direct supervision timing (enacted law).** Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 (AMLAR), Article 13(4), requires the Authority to "commence the first selection process by 1 July 2027", conclude it within six months, publish the list, and "commence direct supervision of the selected obliged entities six months after publication of the list". Later selections follow every three years. A transitional derogation caps the first selection at 40 obliged entities or groups (Article 106(2) in the signed PE-CONS 35/24 text; [local corpus](../../../legal-docs/supporting/eu-amla-2024-1620-full.pdf)). Eligibility requires credit or financial institutions (a category that includes CASPs under AMLR) operating in at least six Member States. Selection is therefore not automatic for every CASP, and no entity comes under AMLA's direct supervision before 2028.

**Preparatory steps (AMLA press release, 12 May 2026, `https://www.amla.europa.eu/amla-takes-next-step-toward-2027-selection-entities-direct-supervision_en`).** AMLA published a reporting package for identifying "provisionally eligible obliged entities". It said it would collect data from national supervisors by 15 August 2026 and expected to finalise the provisional list of eligible entities by end-September 2026. On 29 September 2026 no published list was found; it is a provisional eligibility list, not the Article 13 selection.

**Regulatory-instrument tracker (AMLA "Regulatory Instruments" page, "Last update: 28 September 2026").** The RTS under AMLAR Article 12(7) (risk assessment for selection) and AMLD Article 40(2) (financial-sector risk profiles), and several ITS (AMLAR 15(3), AMLR 81(1), AMLAR 41(2), AMLD 31(2)) plus the AMLD 53(10) sanctions RTS, show "Final report published". The CDD RTS under AMLR Article 28(1) shows "Consultation closed". Its consultation ran from 9 February to 8 May 2026 and its page says "Results will follow". Also at "Consultation closed": the RTS on AMLR Article 19(9) thresholds and linked transactions, the ITS on the STR/transaction-record format (AMLR 69(3)), the guidelines on ongoing monitoring (AMLR 26(5)) and business-wide risk assessment (AMLR 10(4)), group-wide requirements (AMLR 16(4)/17(3)), home-host cooperation (AMLD 46(4)) and non-financial-sector risk profiles (AMLD 40(2)). The RTS on cross-border FIU information exchange (AMLD 31(3)) is open until 6 October 2026. Pending AMLA instruments, the EBA guidelines listed on the page, including the Travel Rule Guidelines under TFR Article 36, continue to apply (AMLAR Article 54(5)). The tracker records no Commission delegated or implementing regulation adopting an AMLA-developed standard. EUR-Lex could not be queried automatically, so non-adoption is unverified beyond the tracker.
