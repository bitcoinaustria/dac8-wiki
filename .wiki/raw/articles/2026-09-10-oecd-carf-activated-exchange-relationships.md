# OECD CARF activated exchange relationships

## Fact captured

The OECD publishes an activation table for the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework alongside the CRS table. As at 10 September 2026 it records 1,066 directional relationships across 41 jurisdictions: 676 on the basis of the EU Directive, 388 under the CARF MCAA, and 2 under a bilateral agreement (Denmark and the Faroe Islands, signed 20 June 2025).

Austria has 26 outbound and 25 inbound EU-Directive relationships. Bulgaria appears as a receiver but not as a sender; no explanation was located.

Austria's activated third-country relationships under the CARF MCAA are eleven outbound and twelve inbound:

Guernsey, Iceland, Indonesia, Japan, Jersey, Korea, Liechtenstein, New Zealand, Norway, South Africa, United Kingdom. The Cayman Islands send to Austria without a reciprocal Austrian relationship, consistent with a non-reciprocity notification under Section 7(1)(b) CARF MCAA.

Signature is not activation. Fifteen of the 56 CARF MCAA signatories have no activated relationship at all, including Switzerland, the United Arab Emirates, Singapore, Canada, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Israel, Panama, Mauritius, the Bahamas, Barbados, Gibraltar and the Isle of Man. The United States is not a signatory.

The table carries only three columns and no activation or effective date, and exposes no last-updated stamp.

## Research use

This is the operative evidence for the § 14 Abs. 1 Z 2 Krypto-MPfG question in the absence of an Austrian Verordnung. It converts claimant selection from a cohort forecast into a checkable fact: a person tax-resident only outside these eleven states is not a meldepflichtiger Nutzer for Austrian purposes.

The absence of an activation date is a live limitation. The table proves activation as at the date of retrieval, not activation during the 2026 reporting period, and the § 14 assessment date is 31 December of the reporting period.
