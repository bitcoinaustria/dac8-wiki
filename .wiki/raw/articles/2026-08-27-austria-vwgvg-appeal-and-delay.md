# Austria VwGVG: BVwG appeal and delay rules

Section 7(4) VwGVG gives a party four weeks from service to challenge an administrative decision. Section 9 requires the complaint to identify the challenged decision and authority, the grounds of unlawfulness, the requested result, and facts showing timeliness.

Section 8 permits a complaint for failure to decide once the authority has not decided within six months, unless a different statutory period applies. The period does not include time during which a preliminary issue is stayed or proceedings are pending before the VwGH, VfGH, or CJEU.

For a DAC8 data-subject case, the ordinary merits path is therefore a timely complaint against the DSB decision. Delay is addressed separately and should not be confused with the three-month status-information right under section 24(7) and (8) DSG.
