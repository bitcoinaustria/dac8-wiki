# FATF Recommendations current to June 2026

Recommendation 1 provides the risk-based framework and Recommendation 10 sets the core CDD elements. Recommendation 15 and its Interpretive Note cover virtual assets and VASPs; Recommendation 16 governs payment and value-transfer information.

The recommendations are international standards addressed to jurisdictions. They do not themselves impose directly enforceable private duties in Austria. The June 2025 Recommendation 16 revision has a later implementation path and must not be substituted for the currently applicable EU TFR text.
