# United States v. Storm: docket status

Source: CourtListener docket 67720380, *United States v. Storm*, 1:23-cr-00430 (KPF), S.D.N.Y., Judge Katherine Polk Failla, read on 29 September 2026. The last filing shown is 11 September 2026. The entries come from PACER and RECAP, and CourtListener warns they "may not be up to date". The underlying documents were not read, only the docket text.

**Docket entries.**

- 24 July 2025 (minute entry): at the close of the government's case, the defence moved for acquittal under Rule 29, and the court reserved decision.
- 6 August 2025 (minute entry): "Jury reached a partial verdict". Dkt. 224, entered 7 August 2025: "JURY VERDICT as to Roman Storm (1) Guilty on Count 2s."
- Dkt. 229–230 (30 September 2025): motion for acquittal and supporting memorandum.
- 9 April 2026 (minute entry): oral argument; "The Court defers ruling on Defendant's Rule 29 motion".
- Dkt. 288 (24 April 2026): retrial set for 26 October 2026, contingent on the Rule 29 ruling.
- Dkt. 300 (25 August 2026): "In light of Mr. Storm's pending motion for acquittal (Dkt. #229)" and his request, the court "ADJOURNS the retrial to April 26, 2027". It sets a pretrial schedule and excludes Speedy Trial Act time to that date.
- Dkt. 302 (4 September 2026): defence letter motion to dismiss Counts One and Three with prejudice under 18 U.S.C. § 3162(a)(2).
- Dkt. 303 (10 September 2026): the court "GRANTS the Government's request to clarify and DENIES Mr. Storm's motion to dismiss".
- Dkt. 304 (11 September 2026): government letter on the Speedy Trial Act.

**Not shown by the docket text.** The docket text identifies the verdict only as "Count 2s". That this is the unlicensed money-transmitting conspiracy comes from the SDNY release already in the wiki.
