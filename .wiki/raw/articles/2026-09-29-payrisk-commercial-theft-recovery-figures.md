# Commercial theft and recovery figures

Sources, both commercial blockchain-analytics or security vendors:

1. Chainalysis, "2025 Crypto Theft Reaches $3.4 Billion", blog, 18 December 2025.
2. Immunefi, "Crypto Losses Q1 2025 Report", <https://immunefi.com/blog/research/immunefi-crypto-losses-q1-2025-report/>.

Both were read through web-fetch summaries, and the methods are proprietary.

**Chainalysis.**

- More than USD 3.4bn was stolen in 2025. The February Bybit compromise accounted for USD 1.5bn.
- DPRK-attributed actors took USD 2.02bn, 76% of service compromises.
- Personal-wallet compromises numbered 158,000 incidents with at least 80,000 unique victims and USD 713m stolen, 20% of losses, against 44% (USD 1.5bn) in 2024.
- The post gives no figure for frozen, recovered or returned funds and does not discuss physical coercion.
- It acknowledges blind spots: private-key transfers and OTC sales may be invisible on-chain.

**Immunefi.**

- Q1 2025 losses were USD 1,635,933,800 across 40 incidents, all from hacks.
- USD 6.5m was recovered in two cases (1inch USD 5m; Moby Trade USD 1.5m of USD 2.5m), 0.4% of losses, against 21.2% in Q1 2024.
- The quarter is dominated by Bybit, where nothing was recovered.

**Evidence class.** These are commercial analytics with proprietary attribution. They are not audited, definitions differ between vendors, and the vendors have a commercial interest in the salience of the problem.

**Use.** Recovery of stolen crypto is episodic and small relative to losses, varies strongly by incident, and depends on negotiation, freezing or seizure. It is not a claim the victim can enforce. Personal-wallet (self-custody) theft is a large victim population with no reported recovery figure. None of this measures recovery for coerced self-custody transfers. A search for peer-reviewed studies of recovery rates found none; the Oxford *Journal of Cybersecurity* DeFi-crime dataset (Carpentier-Desjardins et al., 2025) reports losses, not recoveries.
