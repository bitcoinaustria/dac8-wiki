# DAC8 corrigendum: distributed-ledger address references TFR, not MiCA

## Source identity

- Official Journal: `OJ L 2025/90947`, 26 November 2025
- CELEX: `32023L2226R(01)`
- ELI: `http://data.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/2226/corrigendum/2025-11-26/oj`
- Corrected act: Council Directive (EU) 2023/2226

## Corrections captured

The corrigendum replaces the reference to Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 with a reference to Article 3(18) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 for:

1. Article 3(30) of Directive 2011/16/EU, the definition of distributed-ledger address;
2. Article 8ad(3)(c)(ix), the aggregate outward-transfer field; and
3. Annex VI Section II B(3)(i), the corresponding provider reporting field.

Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 expressly defines a distributed-ledger address. The Austrian Krypto-MPfG, promulgated on 23 December 2025, still refers in sections 4(7) and 18(1)(3)(i) to Regulation (EU) 2023/1114.

## Research use

The mismatch is a concrete transposition and legal-certainty issue. Its legal consequence is not established. A court may consider the Austrian reference an obvious error capable of conforming interpretation; limits on contra-legem interpretation and legality remain relevant. It should be pleaded as a precise issue for an applicant actually affected by the outward-transfer field, not as proof that the whole regime is invalid.
