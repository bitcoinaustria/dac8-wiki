# Austrian DSG section 21 legislative and regulatory consultation duty

Section 21(1) DSG requires the DSB to be heard before federal statutes and federal implementing regulations that directly concern data-protection questions. This implements a distinct consultation function related to GDPR Article 36(4); it is not the operational prior consultation triggered by residual high risk under Article 36(1).

For a federal statute, breach of section 21 is breach of ordinary federal law and is not by itself a constitutional standard under B-VG Article 140. CJEU C-61/22 also forecloses treating an alleged lawmaking DPIA defect as automatically invalidating the EU or national legal act.

For the Krypto-MPfG implementing regulation, an omitted legally mandatory consultation may be relevant to legality under Article 139 because the challenged act is subordinate legislation. Before using that theory, the file must establish whether consultation actually occurred, whether Austrian Constitutional Court doctrine treats the identified procedural breach as material, and whether the claimant can properly challenge the provider-facing regulation. The Parliament record shows invitation of the DSB and Datenschutzrat during the statute process; it does not answer the separate regulation process.
