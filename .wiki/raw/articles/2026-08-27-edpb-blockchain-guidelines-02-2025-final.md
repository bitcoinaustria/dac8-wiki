# EDPB Guidelines 02/2025 on blockchain personal-data processing, final v2.0

The final guidance explains that public keys and other on-chain data may be personal data where an individual can be singled out or linked using reasonably likely means. It treats data-protection roles, purpose specification, minimisation, storage, security, DPIAs, rights, and data-protection by design as case-specific requirements rather than assuming that technical pseudonymity removes GDPR protection.

For DAC8, it supports an evidence-based identity-to-ledger threat model. It does not establish that a DAC8 report contains wallet addresses, transaction IDs, or a complete ledger history; those facts would have to come from another retained dataset and an actual linkage path.
