# French cyber-assistance annual report: breach exploitation and physical harm

Rapport d'activité 2025, published March 2026. Original PDF retrieved; relevant text extracted with pdftotext; printed p.49 visually inspected. Locators: pp.48–49; methodology p.31.

The report describes personalised Prime Family phishing with identity/address/IBAN fields; origin in particular earlier leaks is only possible. It reports theft attempts after the shooting federation's breach and crypto-diversion attempts following sector leaks, sometimes escalating into threats or assaults against victims or relatives.

This is aggregated official operational attribution, not a named crypto provider's causal judgment. No exposed-population denominator is supplied. Assistance pathways changed after 17Cyber; counts are not incidence rates.
