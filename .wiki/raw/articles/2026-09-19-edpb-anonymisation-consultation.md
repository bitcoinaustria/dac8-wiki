# EDPB Guidelines 02/2026, version 1.0

Selected digest. Adopted for consultation on 7 July 2026, not final guidance. The [official consultation page](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/public-consultations/guidelines-022026-on-anonymisation_en) shows feedback open from 8 July to 30 October 2026, checked 19 September.

Paragraph 27 excludes already-identified information from anonymity. Paragraphs 29–31 assess actual data access, auxiliary information, resources and technology; relevant actors can include rogue employees and criminals, depending on context. Paragraphs 51–52 distinguish clear cases and the need for further assessment after a technical criterion fails. Paragraphs 88–90 address changed capabilities following leaks, safety margins and effective security restrictions, without treating contractual or technical measures alone as conclusive.

This is proposed interpretive guidance, not a binding rule or empirical result. It does not test DAC8 annual aggregates or establish inevitable future linkage. Its actor-specific method requires evidence of access and capabilities, not every imaginable attacker.
