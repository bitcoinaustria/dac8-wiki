# MiCA and DORA documents as provider-side evidence sources

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/305 Article 9 requires a MiCA authorisation dossier to describe ICT and DLT architecture, human and technical resources, the DORA risk framework, confidentiality, integrity, availability and authenticity controls, critical ICT services and third parties, locations, contracts, security and incident arrangements.

[Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/299](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2025/299/oj/eng) requires continuity arrangements protecting and re-establishing client-data confidentiality, integrity and availability, including scenarios, backups and recovery. [DORA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32022R2554) additionally requires documented ICT governance, asset and dependency inventories, access and encryption controls, incident records and root-cause analysis, third-party registers and contracts, testing and review reports for authorised CASPs.

These files are concrete evidence sources for provider architecture and control effectiveness. They do not establish compliance with the distinct DAC8 pipeline, are not automatically accessible to a private complainant, and do not apply to every possible DAC8 reporting operator. The DSB may obtain controller records under GDPR Article 58; some MiCA or DORA material may instead be held by the FMA and require supervisory coordination.
