# Safe Interenvios

Case C-235/14 held that the AML framework permits standard CDD where suspicion exists and enhanced, risk-sensitive CDD for inherently higher-risk situations, including fund transfers (notably paragraphs 60, 86 to 87, and 93). It is adverse authority against claiming that enhanced AML controls are inherently unlawful.

The judgment does not authorise blanket access, indefinite retention, automatic account closure, or uncontrolled data sharing. Each measure remains subject to proportionality and data-protection law.
