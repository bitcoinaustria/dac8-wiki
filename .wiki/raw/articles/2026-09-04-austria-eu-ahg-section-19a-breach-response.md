# Austria EU-AHG section 19a breach response

Section 19a EU-AHG requires the competent Austrian authority to notify an Austrian data breach and remedial measures to the Commission without delay. If it cannot contain the breach immediately and appropriately, it must request suspension of its CCN access. It must notify remediation to the Commission.

For a breach in another Member State, the Austrian authority must suspend exchange with the affected Member State or States, notify the Commission and affected States, and the suspension takes immediate effect.

The provision is an enacted response safeguard. It operates after a breach and does not itself establish preventive controls, deletion of copies already received, individual notification or compensation.
