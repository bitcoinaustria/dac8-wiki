# Commission proposal COM(2017) 335 for DAC6

- Proposal for a Council Directive amending Directive 2011/16/EU as regards mandatory automatic exchange of information in the field of taxation in relation to reportable cross-border arrangements, COM(2017) 335 final, 2017/0138 (CNS), dated 21 June 2017

The explanatory memorandum states that recent leaks, including the Panama Papers, highlighted how certain intermediaries appeared to have actively helped clients use aggressive tax planning arrangements and conceal money offshore. It records that Member States at the informal ECOFIN of April 2016 invited the Commission to consider mandatory disclosure rules inspired by OECD/G20 BEPS Action 12, and that ECOFIN conclusions of May 2016 repeated that invitation. It cites the Commission's Communication COM(2016) 451 of 5 July 2016 as the "Panama Communication" and presents intermediary reporting as capturing information that may escape CRS reporting under Directive 2014/107/EU.

This is the clearest primary-source statement in the DAC series naming a specific leak as a legislative motive.
