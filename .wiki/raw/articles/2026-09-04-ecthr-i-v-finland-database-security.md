# ECtHR I v Finland

The judgment of 17 July 2008 concerned access controls for highly sensitive medical records. Paragraphs 36–38 frame a positive obligation to secure respect for private life. Paragraphs 44 and 46–49 find a violation on the basis of acknowledged system deficiencies and failure to meet domestic legal requirements. Paragraph 47 states that an ex post compensation possibility did not provide practical and effective protection against unauthorised access.

The case does not hold that every database or every residual breach risk violates Article 8 ECHR. Its medical-data context, the demonstrated access-control defect and the breach of domestic safeguards materially limit the analogy to tax reporting.
