# Austrian BAO section 243: tax complaints require a Bescheid

## Enacted rule

In the official RIS consolidation verified on 28 August 2026, section 243 BAO permits complaints to the administrative courts against **Bescheide issued by tax authorities**, unless tax legislation provides otherwise.

## Krypto-MPfG relevance and limit

Section 2(2) Krypto-MPfG makes the BAO applicable mutatis mutandis unless the Krypto-MPfG provides otherwise. Section 19(2) treats the reporting provider's annual report as an `Abgabenerklärung`.

Those provisions do not by themselves identify a Bescheid addressed to the private user before the provider's report. They therefore do not establish an ordinary pre-report BFG route for that user. A later tax assessment may create a separate tax appeal route, but its availability and capacity to review or prevent earlier provider processing and reporting depend on the concrete act, party status, timing, and relief.

This is a negative route boundary, not a holding that no tax remedy can ever arise.
