# Austria GMSG section 5 processing and five-year deletion rule

Section 5(1) GMSG requires reporting financial institutions to determine, collect, store and process the reportable information for all account holders. The existence of an all-customer processing rule is therefore not unique to section 20(1) Krypto-MPfG.

Section 5(3) requires deletion of the transmitted information five years after the end of the calendar year in which the reporting financial institution transmitted it. Section 20(4) Krypto-MPfG instead uses ten years for the corresponding provider deletion duty.

The comparison identifies a concrete national difference between two automatic tax-reporting regimes. It does not establish that the underlying records, tax limitation issues or operational needs are identical, or that five years is legally required for crypto records.
