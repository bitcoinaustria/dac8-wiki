# Council Directive (EU) 2020/876 (COVID-19 deferral of DAC time limits)

- Council Directive (EU) 2020/876 of 24 June 2020 amending Directive 2011/16/EU to address the urgent need to defer certain time limits for the filing and exchange of information in the field of taxation because of the COVID-19 pandemic; CELEX `32020L0876`; OJ L 204, 26.6.2020, p. 46
- Entry into force 27 June 2020 (Cellar metadata); no transposition deadline recorded

New Article 27a lets Member States (it is an option, not an automatic postponement) allow:

- filing of the look-back arrangements (first step 25 June 2018 to 30 June 2020) by 28 February 2021 instead of 31 August 2020;
- first DAC6 exchange by 30 April 2021 instead of 31 October 2020;
- the 30-day filing period to start by 1 January 2021 for arrangements or advice between 1 July 2020 and 31 December 2020;
- the first periodic report on marketable arrangements by 30 April 2021;
- DAC2 exchange for calendar year 2019 within 12 months instead of nine after the end of the year.

Article 27b allowed the Council, acting unanimously on a Commission proposal, to extend the deferral by a further three months if the pandemic conditions persisted. Whether that extension was used is not verified here.

Timeline point: DAC6 itself continued to "apply from 1 July 2020"; what moved, in Member States that used the option, were the filing and exchange dates. A timeline should not describe 2020/876 as postponing DAC6's application date.
