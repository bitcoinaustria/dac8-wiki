# VfGH E 2189/2025

The VfGH found no rights violation in a challenge involving collection of birthplace and residential address for unique identification, multi-source AML/CFT data, and classification of specified foundation persons as beneficial owners. The case supports internal register integrity and controlled-access data collection.

It does not revive the invalidated open-public-register model. Its strongest use is as State counterauthority when a challenge attacks all beneficial-ownership data rather than excessive fields, access, retention, or insufficient remedies.
