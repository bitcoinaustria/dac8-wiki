# Tether: cumulative freeze figures (September 2026)

Source: Tether press release, "Tether Has Supported Nearly $550 Million in Iran-Linked USD₮ Freezes as U.S. Expands Sanctions Campaign", 28 September 2026. It was read through a web-fetch summary, so exact wording may differ slightly.

**Figures stated by the issuer.**

- More than 340 law-enforcement agencies in 67 countries.
- More than 2,800 investigations supported, over 1,500 of them involving US agencies.
- More than USD 4.9bn in assets frozen, including more than USD 2.4bn connected to US authorities.
- About USD 550m of Iran-linked USDT frozen in 2026: more than USD 344m in April and more than USD 130m in July.
- The release mentions "reissuance" or transfer in some cases. It gives no figure for funds returned to victims and no split between theft, scam and sanctions freezes.

**Evidence class.** This is an issuer self-disclosure by an interested party. It is unaudited and cumulative since an unstated start date. It complements the issuer terms in `2026-09-29-acctmodel-stablecoin-issuer-freeze-terms.md` and the independent lower-bound freeze counts in Wu et al. (preprint).

**Use.** The release shows that freezing at issuer level is used at scale for USDT held in self-hosted addresses, so the "irreversible" label is weaker for issuer-controlled stablecoins than for native assets such as BTC or ETH. It does not show a recovery rate for stolen funds, restitution to individual victims, or any EU or Austrian case. Freezing depends on timely detection and on the issuer's discretion.
