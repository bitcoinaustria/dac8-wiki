# Austria: direct individual application and Anlassfall effect

[VwGH Ro 2015/16/0024](https://ris.bka.gv.at/JudikaturEntscheidung.wxe?Abfrage=Vwgh&Dokumentnummer=JWR_2015160024_20171019J01&ShowPrintPreview=True) states that an individual application does not trigger Anlassfall effect, irrespective of whether the person concerned was a party to the individual-application proceeding. A successful direct application under Article 140(1)(1)(c) B-VG therefore does not automatically disapply the annulled provision to facts realised before the general effective date for that applicant.

The Constitutional Court can extend temporal effect by an express pronouncement under Article 140(7) B-VG or, for regulations, Article 139(6). [VfGH V 436/2020](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Vfgh/JFT_20201210_20V00436_00/JFT_20201210_20V00436_00.html) is an example of a direct regulation application accompanied by a pronouncement that the provisions were no longer to be applied. [VfGH G 74/2014 and related cases](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/VfghEntscheidung.wxe?Abfrage=Vfgh&Dokumentnummer=JFT_20141202_14G00074_00&IncludeSelf=True) expressly used Article 140(7)'s power.

The result differs where a court applies for norm review in its pending case, or where the VfGH starts norm review incidentally from an Article 144 complaint. That originating proceeding is the technical Anlassfall. [VfGH E 846/2021, paragraphs 2 to 4](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/VfghEntscheidung.wxe?Abfrage=Vfgh&Dokumentnummer=JFT_20221214_21E00846_00&IncludeSelf=True) explains the effect for the originating and qualifying already-pending cases.

[VfGH G 287/2022 and related cases](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Vfgh/JFT_20221214_22G00287_00/JFT_20221214_22G00287_00.html) illustrates the distinction in a DSB/BVwG setting. The BVwG filed the Article 140 court applications; the general expiry of the annulled rule was delayed, while the originating proceedings received the privileged case effect. The later DSB merits process also shows that successful norm review can open the route to a decision without guaranteeing the requested erasure or substantive outcome.

A DSB decision is therefore an appealable concrete case, not yet an Anlassfall of constitutional norm review. It becomes such an Anlassfall only if the BVwG applies for review or the VfGH opens incidental review from the later Article 144 complaint.
