# B-VG Article 139: regulation review and temporal effects

Article 139(1) specifies routes to review of regulations. Paragraph 5 governs publication and the possibility of a later effective date of annulment. Paragraph 6 binds courts and administrative authorities to the ruling, while generally preserving application to earlier facts except the Anlassfall and subject to the Court's ruling. It also addresses facts realised during a deferred-expiry period.

These paragraphs do not alone establish standing or an Anlassfall for a particular applicant.
