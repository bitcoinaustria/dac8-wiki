# Directive 91/308/EEC

The directive's recitals refer to the Vienna Convention and FATF work. It required Member States to provide customer identification, recordkeeping and cooperation through suspicious-transaction reporting in national law.

This is the clearest early transition from international treaty and standard-setting into an EU legal regime. Later directives expanded predicate offences, obliged entities, beneficial-ownership rules and the risk-based approach.
