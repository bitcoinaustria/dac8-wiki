# Saavedra plea agreement

Selected digest, not a transcript. United States v Eric Chase Saavedra, CR 2:25-cr-00035-DSF, agreement filed 16 January 2025. Court document reproduced by Courthouse News, not retrieved from the court docket. The DOJ announcement separately confirms the filing.

## Pages 12 and 22–24

Paragraph 19 incorporates Exhibit A as the admitted factual basis. Exhibit A paragraphs 2 and 12 describe police databases and unauthorised personal-information queries for a private client, including the client's adversaries and relatives.

## Pages 27–29, Exhibit A paragraphs 21–27

Saavedra admits procuring phone-location access through a knowingly false warrant affidavit, sharing GPS results and locating the targeted person. The resulting address was passed onward for surveillance. The claimed cryptocurrency wealth was an attributed belief, not a verified balance.

These paragraphs establish location abuse, not the later armed attack. That event is separately described in the DOJ's 17 January announcement. This is neither a DAC8 leak nor proof that a static database query supplied this victim's address.
