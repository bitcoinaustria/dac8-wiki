# Parliamentary answer 2592/AB (XXVIII. GP)

**What it is.** Written answer by Finance Minister Markus Marterbauer dated 17 September 2025 (GZ 2025-0.572.542). It answers question 3057/J of 17 July 2025 (Peter Wurm et al.). Locators are the answers to questions 4–5 and 6 on pages 3–4 of 4.

**Key statements.** On question 6, the minister says: "Künstliche Intelligenz im Sinne von Machine Learning wird bereits seit 2016 zur Aufdeckung von Steuerverkürzung und -betrug eingesetzt". In English: AI in the sense of machine learning has been used since 2016 to detect tax evasion and fraud. PACC is named as the dedicated department for the ML risk models. The listed areas of use are customs audit, family allowance, employee assessment, business assessment, business start-ups, tax securing ("Abgabensicherung") "u.v.m." (and many more). The listed methods are logistic regression, decision trees, random forest, gradient boosting, quantile regression, neural-network autoencoders, NLP and social network analysis. They are "mit den eigenen historischen Daten trainiert" (trained on the BMF's own historical data). On data protection the answer says: "Datenquellen werden nur insoweit verwendet, als es hierfür eine eindeutige gesetzliche Grundlage gibt. Des Weiteren wird in Zweifelsfällen das Einvernehmen mit dem Datenschutzbeauftragten des BMF hergestellt." In English: data sources are used only where there is a clear legal basis, and in doubtful cases agreement is sought with the BMF data-protection officer. On questions 4–5, the planned fraud package includes "effektive Nutzung neuer (EU weiter) Datenquellen wie automatischer Informationsaustausch über Kryptokonten". The package's expected additional revenue is +EUR 270m in 2026, rising to +EUR 450m in 2029. On question 2, the answer says the effects and actual additional revenue of recent fraud-fighting legislation have "noch nicht evaluiert" (not yet been evaluated). The BBKG 2024 is due for evaluation in 2027. On questions 1 and 3, the BMF has no estimate of the tax gap.

**Evidence class.** Official ministerial statement to Parliament. It is high-confidence evidence of what the BMF asserts. It does not independently verify how the models work.

**Transfer to the DAC8 proceeding.** This is the clearest official statement that ML scoring of identified persons is routine across tax procedures. It also links DAC8-type crypto AEOI data explicitly to the fraud-fighting revenue programme. It does **not** say that crypto AEOI or CRS data are, or will be, inputs to PACC models. The data-source rule ("eindeutige gesetzliche Grundlage") points to § 114(4) and § 48b BAO as the claimed basis. It is a self-assessment, not a published data-source list, DPIA or model register. No legal basis is cited for Art. 22(2)(b) GDPR, which is consistent with the BMF's position that officials, not models, decide.
