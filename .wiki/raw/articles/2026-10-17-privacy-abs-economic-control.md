# ABS: Partner Economic Abuse in the 2021–22 Personal Safety Survey

Released 22 November 2023; economic-abuse section, prevalence table and linked methodology. Estimated experience since age 15 among adults aged 18+: 16.3% of women and 7.8% of men reported economic abuse by a cohabiting partner. Examples include preventing independent accounts and controlling assets or income. Completed sample 11,905, response rate 52.2%; fieldwork March 2021–May 2022. Private-dwelling scope, remote/institutional exclusions, self-report, nonresponse and pandemic constraints limit transfer. These are weighted Australian estimates, not Austrian prevalence or evidence that disclosure caused abuse. Confidential access and retained evidence may help victims. Mechanical credibility 2: national statistical institution +1, publication within three years +1.
