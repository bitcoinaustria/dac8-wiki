# Parliamentary answer 2541/AB (XXVIII. GP)

**What it is.** Written answer by Finance Minister Markus Marterbauer dated 16 September 2025 (GZ 2025-0.569.966). It answers question 3030/J (Peter Wurm et al.) on "Steuerbetrug" 2019–2024. The answer notes that "Steuerbetrug" is not a legal term and covers Abgabenhinterziehung and Abgabenbetrug. Data for 2019–2021 are partly unavailable because of the FABE-ST IT changeover, citing Rechnungshof Bund 2023/26 items 8.1 and 15.1 (p. 2).

**Figures (pp. 2–9):**

| Measure | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Referrals received at ABB, including voluntary disclosures | – | – | 6,686 | 8,134 | 9,566 | 10,629 |
| Administrative criminal-tax cases closed with a penalty | – | – | 1,680 | 1,557 | 1,456 | 1,702 |
| Court criminal-tax cases closed with a conviction | – | – | 95 | 94 | 62 | 59 |
| House searches | 168 | 199 | 168 | 292 | 169 | 158 |
| Account openings ("Kontoöffnungen"; question 11) | 83 | 74 | 75 | 394 | 276 | 260 |

Account openings and house searches require "das Vorliegen eines strafrechtlichen Anfangsverdachtes" (an initial criminal suspicion). Since 2020, 55 money-laundering suspicion reports were passed directly to the criminal-tax authority. Other reports go to the assessing offices and cannot be traced further (question 12).

**AI statement (question 16, p. 10).** "Zur Aufbereitung umfangreicher IT-Datenvolumina wird eine Datenanalysesoftware eingesetzt. Es erfolgt keine KI-gestützte Mustererkennung." In English: data-analysis software is used to process large IT data volumes, and there is no AI-supported pattern recognition. The question asked about tools used "bei der Aufdeckung von Steuerbetrug" (in detecting tax fraud). The whole answer is framed around the ABB and criminal-tax proceedings.

**Evidence class and use.** Official ministerial statement. The AI statement conflicts on its face with 2592/AB, which was issued one day later and says ML has been used since 2016 to detect "Steuerverkürzung und -betrug". The likely reconciliation is scope: 2541/AB covers ABB and criminal investigation, while PACC scoring feeds the assessment and audit side. That reconciliation is inference and is not stated in either answer. A litigant should not rely on either answer alone. The account-opening counts are low compared with millions of scored cases. They support the argument that intrusive, suspicion-based measures are rare. They also support the State's likely reply that intrusive access is already filtered by suspicion thresholds. The figures differ from the ABB annual reports (238 openings in 2024 and 417 in 2025 in the activity tables), so the counting unit is unclear.
