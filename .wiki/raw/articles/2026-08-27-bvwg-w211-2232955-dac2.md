# BVwG W211 2232955-1/7E: Austrian DAC2 challenge

## Proceeding

- Decision: 12 April 2021
- Court: Bundesverwaltungsgericht
- File: W211 2232955-1/7E
- Outcome: complaint dismissed; ordinary revision allowed

An Austrian taxpayer held approximately EUR 40 in a German bank account. German authorities transmitted his identifying, account, balance, and interest information to the Austrian BMF under CRS/DAC2, and the BMF forwarded it to the competent tax authority.

The applicant challenged the processing as suspicionless, disproportionate, unnecessary for such a small balance, inconsistent with GDPR data minimisation, and insecure. He proposed CJEU questions.

## Holding captured

The BVwG held that the data were personal data but not GDPR special-category data; the reporting purpose was legitimate; the specified fields were suitable and necessary for equal cross-border tax enforcement; no de minimis threshold or individualized suspicion was required; the data-retention authorities were not directly comparable; and the processing was lawful under GDPR Article 6(1)(c) and (3).

The court declined an Article 267 reference because it found no unresolved outcome-relevant question. It nevertheless allowed revision because Austrian supreme administrative case law on GDPR Articles 5(1)(c) and 6(1)(c) was lacking.
