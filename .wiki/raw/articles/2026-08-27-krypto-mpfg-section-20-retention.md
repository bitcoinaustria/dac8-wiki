# Austria Krypto-MPfG section 20: processing, notice and retention

Section 20 requires providers to determine, collect, store, and process reportable information for all customers concerning transactions and crypto-assets, whether or not the customer ultimately qualifies as reportable. Where personal data are reportable, the provider must inform the user before the first report and provide relevant data-subject information in time to exercise rights.

The provision sets a ten-year deletion point after the end of the reporting period for transmitted information. The wider act separately requires documentation of due-diligence steps to be retained for ten years.

The text is central to the possible present interference and to the question whether Austria chose the maximum permitted retention period.
