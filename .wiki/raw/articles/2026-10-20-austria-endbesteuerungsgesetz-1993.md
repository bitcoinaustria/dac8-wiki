# Austrian Endbesteuerungsgesetz 1993

Primary text retrieved from RIS on 20 October 2026 (consolidated § 1, PDF of [NOR40172776](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Bundesnormen/NOR40172776/NOR40172776.pdf)). Kurztitel: Endbesteuerungsgesetz. Kundmachungsorgan: BGBl. Nr. 11/1993, last amended by BGBl. I Nr. 103/2015; this version in force from 14 August 2015. Secondary sources describe it as a Bundesverfassungsgesetz (federal constitutional act); the consolidated § 1 retrieved here does not itself show the title line.

## Text

Abschnitt I is headed „Steuerabgeltung bei bestimmten Einkünften aus Kapitalvermögen und sonstigem Vermögen durch Abzug von Kapitalertragsteuer“.

§ 1 Abs. 1 requires federal law to provide that for income from capital (§ 27 EStG 1988), including „Kapitalerträgen aus Geldeinlagen bei Banken“ (Z 1 lit. a), realised gains (lit. g) and derivatives (lit. h), and for the underlying assets and their acquisition on death, the taxes „mit dem Kapitalertragsteuerabzug abgegolten sind“. Federal law may provide exceptions where the values underlying the deduction do not match actual circumstances.

## Related authority

- VfGH G 278/01 (7 March 2002) read the act's reference to the 1993 ordinary-law position as a narrow, static reference and struck down part of § 97 Abs. 1 EStG 1988 (search excerpt; judgment not read for this record).
- Crypto income since 2024 follows the same withholding mechanism through domestic providers: [crypto KESt withholding](2026-10-20-austria-crypto-kest-withholding-scope.md).

## Limits

- The act settles tax by withholding. It does not itself say that the tax office receives no names, and this record does not establish the reporting content of the KESt return (§ 96 EStG not retrieved).
- The absence of an annual domestic report on bank accounts follows from the GMSG's scope, not from this act: see [DAC8 domestic scope versus GMSG](2026-10-20-dac8-domestic-scope-versus-gmsg.md).
- Whether the constitutional character of the act affects crypto-asset reporting is not examined; no court has linked the two.
