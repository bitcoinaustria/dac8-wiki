# 404 Media: OnlyFake

Joseph Cox, 404 Media, 5 February 2024. The report describes an underground site, OnlyFake, selling images of fake driver's licences and passports for USD 15, which it claimed were generated with "neural networks". 404 Media generated a California driver's licence and states it "used another fake ID generated by this site to successfully step through the identity verification process on OKX", a crypto exchange. The operator, "John Wick", claimed hundreds of documents could be generated at once from spreadsheet data.

Only an excerpt was readable; expert commentary and exchange responses reported by secondary outlets were not verified in the source. Secondary coverage attributes to a named academic the view that the service may have used templates rather than generative AI; that attribution is unverified here.

Evidence class: single journalistic test of one exchange's document-based verification at one point in time, plus unverified operator claims about other platforms. It shows feasibility, not the prevalence of bypass, and not whether any later monitoring or liveness checks would have caught the account.

Transfer limits: illustrates the FinCEN 2024 and A-FIU 2024 typologies of document-level KYC defeat. It must not be cited as evidence that EU-regulated CASPs routinely accept fake IDs or that other named exchanges were bypassed.
