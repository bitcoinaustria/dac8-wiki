# Commission Work Programme 2026 and the tax omnibus COM(2026) 560

## Verified facts

- **21 October 2025:** Commission Work Programme 2026, "Europe's Independence Moment". It announced an omnibus on taxation within a burden-reduction target (25 % overall, 35 % for SMEs) and put the proposal in Q2 2026. The Programme also announced the withdrawal of four tax proposals, including UNSHELL (2021/0434(CNS), which would have amended Directive 2011/16/EU). Source: ECOFIN report on tax issues, ST 10073/26, 12 June 2026, points 30–32, and the COM(2026) 308 explanatory memorandum.
- **24 June 2026:** COM(2026) 560 final, procedure 2026/0163(CNS). This is a proposal amending Directives 2003/49/EC, 2009/133/EC, 2011/96/EU, (EU) 2016/1164, (EU) 2017/1852 and (EU) 2025/50 to simplify the Union direct-tax framework. It does not amend Directive 2011/16/EU. Its memorandum says it "should also be considered alongside the DAC Recast proposal".
- The DAC recast memorandum records that the Council's conclusions of 11 March 2025 set a "tax decluttering and simplification agenda".
- **24 April 2026:** the EP–Council–Commission "One Europe, One Market" roadmap sets a target to agree the tax omnibus by Q4 2027 (ST 10073/26, point 31).

## Relevance and limits

The label "tax omnibus" refers to COM(2026) 560, not to the DAC recast. DAC reporting duties, including DAC8, change only through COM(2026) 308 (`2026-09-30-eu-dac-future-com-2026-308-dac-recast.md`). Both are pending Council unanimity files. The UNSHELL withdrawal removes a pending DAC amendment; the Commission instead said it would look at integrating UNSHELL concepts into a simplified DAC (COM(2025) 695).
