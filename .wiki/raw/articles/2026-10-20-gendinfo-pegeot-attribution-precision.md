# Gendinfo: precise Waltio attribution

Supplement to the [September source digest](2026-09-19-gendarmerie-waltio-target-selection.md), which paraphrases the statement. Article: "La stratégie de la gendarmerie face aux cryptorapts (partie 2/2)", Gendinfo, 13 September 2026.

## Attribution as supplied with the campaign's source notes

- Speaker: Colonel Patrick Pegeot, head of the Division des opérations of the Unité nationale de police judiciaire (UNPJ).
- The Waltio leak is named „notamment“ (notably, among other factors) as a reason for the rise in crypto-related kidnappings and extortion.
- Quoted wording on the leaked data: „que les malfaiteurs utilisent pour identifier leurs cibles, même si des erreurs surviennent parfois sur les montants réels détenus“.

## Limits

"Notamment" makes the leak one contributing reason, not the sole or proven cause of any specific attack. The statement is an operational police assessment, not a case-by-case judgment or quantitative estimate. Waltio is a private tax-preparation service; its leak is not a DAC8 or tax-authority incident. The earlier digest's limits continue to apply.
