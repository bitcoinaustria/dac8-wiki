# Final Report: Guidelines on loan origination and monitoring

European Banking Authority, EBA/GL/2020/06, 29 May 2020; applicable from 30 June 2021. Status: guidelines under Article 16 of Regulation (EU) No 1093/2010 (comply-or-explain for competent authorities and institutions). The full Final Report PDF was downloaded and sections 5.1–5.2 and Annex 2 inspected.

Paragraph 85 requires lenders assessing consumer creditworthiness to have evidence on, among others, source of repayment capacity, financial commitments, and regular expenses; paragraph 106 requires accounting for non-discretionary expenditure and living expenses. Paragraph 90 requires a "single customer view" of the borrower's ability to repay, supplemented by information on assets and liabilities held elsewhere. Paragraphs 87–89 permit use of data already held on existing customers and collection from third parties, subject to the GDPR.

Annex 2, section A (Lending to consumers, p. 71), items 6–7, lists "current bank account statements" as evidence of income or other sources of repayment, and savings-account and loan statements as information on financial assets and liabilities.

This establishes that the EU banking supervisor treats account-transaction records as probative of a person's income, expenses and financial position for regulated credit decisions. It does NOT address privacy or profiling risk, and it concerns full account statements (itemised inflows and outflows, balances) voluntarily supplied in a loan context, not DAC8 annual per-asset aggregates, which contain no balances, counterparties or itemised flows.
