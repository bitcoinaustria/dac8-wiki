# Watchlist Internet: FinanzOnline-branded crypto phishing

Source digest. Watchlist Internet is run by the ÖIAT with the Internet Ombudsstelle. Its funders include the social/consumer ministry, BMI–Bundeskriminalamt and the Oesterreichische Nationalbank (per its "Über uns" page). It is a publicly co-funded consumer-protection platform, not an authority. Two items were read on 29 September 2026:

1. "Achtung, Phishing-Falle: FinanzOnline will keine Infos zu Krypto-Beständen einholen!", 21 August 2025 (canonical URL).
2. ["FinanzOnline-Phishing: Krypto- und Unternehmensdaten im Visier"](https://www.watchlist-internet.at/news/finanzonline-phishing-krypto-unternehmensdaten/), 24 February 2026.

**August 2025 email.** The fake "Finanzamt" email cites "neuen Steuervorschriften für Kryptowährungen, die ab 2025 in Kraft treten" (new tax rules for cryptocurrencies taking effect from 2025). It asks for a "verpflichtendes Formular zu Ihren Krypto-Beständen" (mandatory form on your crypto holdings) by 25 August 2025 and threatens a fine. It even invokes the "europäischen Know Your Customer (KYC)-Richtlinie". The fake portal offers two routes: "automatischen Verifizierung des Krypto-Wallets über Wallet-Connect" (automatic wallet verification via WalletConnect) or manual entry for exchange users. Watchlist explains that anyone who connects a wallet "gewährt den Kriminellen den Zugriff – und am Ende ist das Geld weg" (grants criminals access, and in the end the money is gone). An info box headed "Reale Basis für Fake-Geschichte" (real basis for the fake story) explains that new EU tax-transparency rules oblige wallet providers and exchanges to report crypto transactions to tax authorities. Those providers must collect the data from their own customers. "Behörden wie etwa das Finanzamt senden keine entsprechenden Aufforderungen aus!" (Authorities such as the tax office send no such requests.)

**February 2026 SMS.** The fake FinanzOnline renewal portal ("finanz.online-erneuern.cc") adds screening questions: "Verwenden Sie Kryptowährungen oder haben Sie ein Kryptokonto/Devisenkonto?" (Do you use cryptocurrencies or have a crypto or forex account?). It also asks about an Austrian-registered company and a company account, and has a checkbox for "Vermögenswerte wie Gold, Schmuck oder größere Bargeldbeträge" (assets such as gold, jewellery or larger cash amounts). It then asks for online-banking logins. Watchlist says the SMS are sent "wahllos" (indiscriminately) in large numbers. The aim is "so viele Informationen wie möglich … für spätere Betrugsmaschen" (as much information as possible for later scams).

**Evidence class and relevance.** Both are consumer-protection warnings describing observed phishing samples; they are not police statistics or judicial findings. They support two points:

- Criminals in Austria already use the crypto tax-reporting reform, and the tax authority's brand, as a social-engineering pretext.
- Phishing in 2026 combines holding and wealth screening (crypto, gold, cash, business) with address harvesting. That is target-profiling data of the kind relevant to physical risk.

They do not show that any genuine BMF, FinanzOnline or DAC8 dataset leaked or was used. The mass sending indicates the opposite: the criminals lack a list of crypto holders and try to build one by self-disclosure. Whether harvested answers were later used for physical targeting is unknown.
