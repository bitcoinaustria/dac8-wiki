# Austria DSG section 1

Section 1 DSG is a constitutional provision. It gives everyone a right to secrecy of personal data where a legitimate confidentiality interest exists. A public authority may interfere, outside consent or vital-interest cases, only on a statutory basis necessary for a reason recognised by Article 8(2) ECHR. The law must provide adequate safeguards, and any permissible interference must use the least intrusive means capable of achieving the objective.

For Krypto-MPfG section 20(1), this is the central Austrian merits right. It does not itself remove the separate requirements of current personal effect and use of an available DSB and court remedy.
