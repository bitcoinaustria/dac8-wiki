# Council Directive 2014/107/EU (DAC2)

DAC2 amended Directive 2011/16/EU to require annual automatic exchange of specified financial-account information between Member States. Article 8(3a) fixes the identifying and financial fields, including account number, year-end balance or value, and specified gross income or proceeds.

The directive is the controlling EU-law source in the Austrian CRS litigation. The Austrian VfGH later treated the disputed reporting fields as fully harmonised and materially leaving Austria no implementation discretion.
