# Scalable Capital: data theft is not itself identity theft

Judgment20June2024, joinedC-182/22,C-189/22, ECLI:EU:C:2024:531. Locators §§6–8,41–46,52–58 and operativepoint5.

The case concerns trading-app identity/contact documents stolen by unknown third parties. Data theft alone is not identity theft: actual identity misuse is required for that classification. Compensation is nevertheless not limited to subsequent identity theft; infringement, actual damage and causation remain cumulative. A small award is possible if it fully compensates proven damage.

This is not an award to these claimants by the CJEU, proof of actual financial theft, or a DAC8 holding. Mechanical credibility2: recent institutional primary.
