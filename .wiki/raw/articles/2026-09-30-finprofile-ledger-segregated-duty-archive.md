# Ledger segregated archive

15 February 2022, section 2 on data management.

Ledger reports migration of order data older than eighteen months into a segregated environment with strictly limited access. It states a ten-year accounting-retention period. Migration after three months was still a target, not completed implementation.

This is a concrete first-party report of separation between operational access and mandatory retention, not an independently audited architecture, a verified 2026 deployment or a measured reduction in breaches. The underlying business is hardware/e-commerce, not Austrian CASP tax reporting.

Scoring: recognised vendor +1, first-party promotional source -1, no recency point. Retained solely for its unique implementation statement; do not use it to establish effectiveness or the applicable Austrian duty.
