# Kontenregister-Durchführungsverordnung – KontReg-DV

## Source identity

- Gazette: `BGBl. II Nr. 92/2016`, ausgegeben am 26. April 2016
- Issued by the Federal Minister of Finance under § 3(1) and § 6 KontRegG, `BGBl. I Nr. 116/2015`

## Content

Section 6 (data transmissions): "Die Inbetriebnahme des Kontenregisters erfolgt mit 10. August 2016." Initial delivery: from Inbetriebnahme until the end of 30 September 2016, the data set as of 1 March 2015 plus openings and closures since then. The Bundesrechenzentrum had to accept test deliveries from 25 May 2016. Follow-up deliveries cover changes after 31 July 2016. For trust accounts (Anderkonten) opened after Inbetriebnahme, all trustors must be reported.

## Relevance and limits

Supplies the operational date for a timeline entry ("Kontenregister in Betrieb seit 10. August 2016"). A secondary summary gives 5 October 2016 as the date from which queries were possible; that date was not verified from a primary source here. The BMF overview record `2026-08-27-austria-kontenregister-bmf-overview.md` only says "since 2016".
