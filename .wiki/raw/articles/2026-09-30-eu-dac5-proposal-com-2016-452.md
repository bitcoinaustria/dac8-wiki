# Commission proposal COM(2016) 452 for DAC5

- Proposal for a Council Directive amending Directive 2011/16/EU as regards access to anti-money-laundering information by tax authorities, COM(2016) 452 final, 2016/0209 (CNS), dated 5 July 2016; CELEX `52016PC0452`

The explanatory memorandum states that "recent media leaks" exposing large-scale concealment of offshore funds confirmed the importance of the tax transparency agenda and showed that tax authorities need better access to beneficial-ownership and customer due diligence information. It refers to the G20 statement of 18 April 2016 calling on the FATF and the Global Forum to improve beneficial-ownership standards. The proposal text itself does not name the Panama Papers (English Cellar version searched on 2026-09-30); the companion Commission Communication COM(2016) 451 of the same day is cited in the DAC6 proposal as the "Panama Communication".

Timeline consequence: attributing DAC5 to the Panama Papers (April 2016) is supported by the proposal's reference to recent media leaks and its timing, but the name appears only in related Commission documents.
