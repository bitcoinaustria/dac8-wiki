# BVerfG, 15 December 1983, 1 BvR 209/83 (Volkszählungsurteil)

Official court website, retrieved 20 October 2026. ECLI:DE:BVerfG:1983:rs19831215.1bvr020983; official report BVerfGE 65, 1–71. The site's citation suggestion uses Rn. 1–215. The page locator "BVerfGE 65, 1 (43)" for paragraph 146 comes from the campaign's source notes; the official HTML shows paragraph numbers, not report pagination, so the page number was not re-checked here.

## Exact quotations (Rn. 146, re-checked against the official HTML on 20 October 2026)

„Mit dem Recht auf informationelle Selbstbestimmung wären eine Gesellschaftsordnung und eine diese ermöglichende Rechtsordnung nicht vereinbar, in der Bürger nicht mehr wissen können, wer was wann und bei welcher Gelegenheit über sie weiß.“

„Wer unsicher ist, ob abweichende Verhaltensweisen jederzeit notiert und als Information dauerhaft gespeichert, verwendet oder weitergegeben werden, wird versuchen, nicht durch solche Verhaltensweisen aufzufallen.“

The same paragraph continues with registration of attendance at an assembly or citizens' initiative (Art. 8, 9 GG) and states that impaired self-determination harms the common good, because self-determination is an elementary condition of a free democratic polity.

## Limits stated in the judgment itself

- Headnote 1 and Rn. 147 ground the right in Art. 2(1) with Art. 1(1) GG.
- Rn. 148: the right "ist nicht schrankenlos gewährleistet"; the individual has no absolute control over "seine" data. Restrictions are permissible in the overriding public interest, on a clear statutory basis and subject to proportionality and procedural safeguards (headnote 1).
- The judgment concerned the 1983 census statute. Its statistical-purpose holdings do not decide tax reporting.

## Evidence class and transfer

Binding German constitutional authority; persuasive at most in Austria, where § 1 DSG and Art. 8 ECHR / Arts. 7–8 Charter govern. Rn. 146 is constitutional reasoning about the conditions of free self-determination, not an empirical finding that a particular data collection changes behaviour.

Adverse authority on the same right: [BFH IX R 36/21](2026-08-27-germany-bfh-ix-r-36-21-crs.md) (23 January 2024) accepted an interference with informational self-determination by CRS exchange and held it justified. A public text citing the 1983 judgment for the value of privacy must not imply that German courts have found automatic tax-account exchange unconstitutional.
