# CNIL 2025 annual breach-notification figure

The CNIL reports 6,167 personal-data-breach notifications in 2025 and indicates that roughly half involved hacking.

The research did not locate official support for DAC8.com's figures of 8,613 notifications and 12.2 million affected people. Those figures remain unresolved and should not be repeated as verified.
