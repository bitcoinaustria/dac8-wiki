# Austria Krypto-MPfG regulatory impact assessment, RV 306 XXVIII GP

The official WFA treats the Krypto-MPfG as a new information obligation. Based on information from the Austrian Chamber of Commerce financial-services group, it assumes twelve potentially affected providers, approximately EUR 500,000 implementation cost per company in 2025, and EUR 425,000 annual running cost from 2026. The aggregate administrative burden is EUR 11.1 million.

The WFA says providers lacked an existing infrastructure for the Krypto-MPfG duties and records zero percent "Sowieso-Kosten" in its administrative-cost model. That supports material current burden and the credibility of a direct provider application.

It does not isolate the cost of section 20(1)'s all-customer processing from the cost of the DAC8 minimum, MiCA/AML records, annual reporting, or the rest of the Austrian act. It therefore cannot by itself establish disproportionality or quantify gold-plating.
