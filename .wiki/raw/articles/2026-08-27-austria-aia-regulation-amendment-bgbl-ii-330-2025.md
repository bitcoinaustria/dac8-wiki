# BGBl II 330/2025: amendment of Austria's general automatic-exchange regulation

The Federal Minister of Finance issued BGBl II 330/2025 on 30 December 2025 under section 7(3) of the EU Administrative Assistance Act. It amends the general regulation on automatic information exchange, BGBl II 380/2014.

The amendment adds royalties as a category and makes the electronic transmission rule refer to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/2378 in its current version. The royalty amendment applies from 1 January 2026 to periods beginning on that date.

The instrument cites Directive (EU) 2023/2226 among its CELEX references, but its operative text does not create a separate crypto-asset customer-reporting duty. It is therefore not a core Article 139 target for an ordinary Krypto-MPfG user unless a concrete proceeding identifies one of its provisions as prejudicial.
