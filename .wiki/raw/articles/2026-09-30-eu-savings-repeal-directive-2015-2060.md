# Council Directive (EU) 2015/2060 repealing the Savings Directive

## Source identity

- Council Directive (EU) 2015/2060 of 10 November 2015 repealing Directive 2003/48/EC; CELEX `32015L2060`; OJ L 301, 18.11.2015, p. 1; legal basis Article 115 TFEU
- Entry into force 8 December 2015 (20th day after publication)

## Content

Recitals 2 to 6 explain the repeal: the G8/G20/OECD global standard, Directive 2014/107/EU extending automatic exchange under the OECD standard released in July 2014, the broader scope of that directive and its priority in cases of overlap, and the cost of dual reporting standards. Recital 6 records that the Council, when adopting Directive 2014/107/EU, invited the Commission to propose the repeal and to coordinate it with the Austrian derogation.

Article 1(1) repeals Directive 2003/48/EC with effect from 1 January 2016. Run-off obligations continue: paying-agent reporting and Member State exchange obligations until 5 October 2016 or fulfilment; residence-State certificate obligations until 31 December 2016; credit and refund of withholding tax levied in 2016 and earlier until fulfilled (Article 1(2)).

For Austria, the Savings Directive continues to apply until 31 December 2016, and the withholding-tax revenue sharing, Article 4(2) and Article 13 obligations until 30 June 2017 or fulfilment (Article 1(3)). Recital 11 records that Austria announced it would not make full use of its DAC2 derogation and would exchange information by September 2017 on a limited set of accounts. The Savings Directive stopped applying after 1 October 2016 to Austrian accounts already reported under DAC2 within the Article 8(6)(b) deadline.

## Relevance and limits for DAC8

The repeal marks the point at which the EU folded its first private-intermediary reporting regime into the DAC framework and the OECD standard. The Austrian carve-out is the direct link between the end of the Austrian withholding-tax alternative and the Austrian CRS start in 2017.
