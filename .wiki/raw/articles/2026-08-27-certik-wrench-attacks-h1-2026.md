# CertiK Intel3D H1 2026 wrench-attacks report

CertiK records 52 worldwide incidents, including 39 in Europe, 20 home invasions, and 16 kidnappings, and discusses family or proxy targeting and open-source intelligence.

The report establishes a real crypto-specific physical-threat environment. It expressly treats its incident set as indicative and non-exhaustive. It does not establish that DAC8 data caused an attack or validate DAC8.com's family multiplier and exposed-population estimates.
