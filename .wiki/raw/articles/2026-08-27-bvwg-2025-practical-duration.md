# BVwG 2025 practical duration figures

The BVwG's official 2025 activity report announcement states that approximately 54 percent of all proceedings were concluded within the generally applicable six-month period and 78 percent within one year.

These are court-wide figures, not data-protection-specific statistics and not a forecast for a Krypto-MPfG case. A constitutional-review or Article 267 reference may suspend or materially extend the national timetable.
