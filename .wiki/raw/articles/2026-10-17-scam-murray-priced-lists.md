# Murray sentencing: maintained and priced targeting lists

Published 28 May 2026; substantive paragraphs following the sentence announcement.

After a January guilty plea, Murray received 121 months. His 2016–2023 business maintained and sold name/phone/address lists, sometimes age/email. Typical price: USD500 for 100–300 names. DOJ reports at least 22,000 lists concerning over seven million elderly Americans, over USD5.2 million broker receipts and over USD9.5 million victim losses.

The list population is not a proven-loss-victim count. Original acquisition and list-refresh methods are unspecified; no dataset was inspected. This is a sentencing account, not an independently retrieved judgment or appeal-finality check. Mechanical credibility 2: recent institutional primary.
