# Krypto-MPfG-DV: the specific Austrian implementing regulation

The Krypto-MPfG-Durchführungsverordnung, BGBl II 331/2025, was issued under sections 17(4), 19(2) and 22(4) Krypto-MPfG and sections 86a and 97(3) BAO. It governs provider exemption communications, reporting through the designated portal and technical or organisational transmission specifications.

It is a regulation potentially reviewable under Article 139 B-VG only where a claimant identifies a prejudicial or directly applicable provision and an alleged excess of statutory authorisation or other unlawfulness. Its technical provider-facing provisions do not by themselves establish direct and personal effect on an ordinary cryptocurrency user.
