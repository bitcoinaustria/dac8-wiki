# E-commerce data use and its limits

Rechnungshof, Bund 2026/5, February 2026; printed pp. 31–32 and 101–107, TZ 22–23. Examination mainly covers 2021–2024; BMF response October 2025, RH rejoinder February 2026.

PACC made aggregated platform data available for manual selection, but these did not enter its automated selection. BP-2023 combined EU-OSS and tax-return data, not platform/CESOP/package/IOSS data. The BMF reported individual Eurofisc CESOP queries from March 2025, not comprehensive analytical access. Among 37 BP-2023 cases, completed examinations had no additional EU-OSS result; not all were complete.

The RH recommended broader data use. Announced BP-2026 extensions are not observed outcomes. Neither these limits nor this recommendation proves anything about CRS or crypto model inputs.

Scoring: recent +1, recognised independent institution +1. Separate audit findings from BMF replies.
