# OECD Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework

The OECD Committee on Fiscal Affairs approved CARF in August 2022. It defines due-diligence and reporting rules intended to identify reportable crypto users, transactions and tax residences for exchange between jurisdictions.

CARF and DAC8 belong to a tax-transparency lineage. Reuse of AML-derived customer information creates operational overlap but does not turn CARF or DAC8 into AML law.
