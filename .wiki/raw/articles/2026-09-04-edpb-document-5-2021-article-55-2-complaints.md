# EDPB Internal Document 5/2021 on Article 55(2) complaints

The EDPB adopted this internal document on 7 July 2021 and published it after its 14 June 2022 plenary decision. It is supervisory-authority guidance, not a court judgment.

Paragraph 1 expressly covers processing by a public authority or by a private body acting under Article 6(1)(c) or (e) GDPR in another EEA Member State. Paragraph 2 treats these cases as falling within the exclusive competence established by Article 55(2). Article 56's lead-authority mechanism does not apply to that processing.

Paragraphs 4 and 10 to 19 distinguish filing from handling. Article 77 permits a data subject to lodge the complaint with an authority close to the person's centre of interests even where that authority is not competent to decide it. The complaint-receiving authority should identify the competence issue, facilitate or forward the complaint where appropriate and avoid acting as the deciding authority without a basis for doing so.

For provider processing attributed to a statutory reporting duty, the controller, the exact processing operation, its Article 6 basis and the Member State whose duty applies must therefore be identified before selecting the deciding supervisory authority. Processing for separate contractual, MiCA, AML or other purposes requires its own competence analysis.
