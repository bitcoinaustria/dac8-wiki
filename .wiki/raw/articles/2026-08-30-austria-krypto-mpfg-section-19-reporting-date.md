# Austria Krypto-MPfG section 19

Section 19(2) requires a reporting crypto-asset service provider to transmit the information under section 18 electronically by 31 July of the calendar year following the reporting period.

For the 2026 reporting period, the Austrian provider report is therefore due by 31 July 2027. DAC Article 8ad(6) requires the competent-authority exchange within nine months after year-end, by 30 September 2027.
