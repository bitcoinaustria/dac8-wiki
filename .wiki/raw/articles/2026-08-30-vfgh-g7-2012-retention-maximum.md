# VfGH G 7/2012

Decision of 29 June 2012, VfSlg 19.659.

The VfGH upheld a nominal 60-year deletion provision by construing it as a maximum rather than an unconditional minimum. Section 1 DSG required earlier deletion once continued storage was no longer necessary, assessed through an individual balance.

This is adverse to a facial argument that Krypto-MPfG section 20(4)'s ten-year deletion date necessarily commands ten years of active storage. Section 39 is textually different because it affirmatively requires covered records to be retained for ten years.
