# VfGH G 231/2021 ua: no party application from an EO interim-injunction proceeding

In G 231/2021 ua, decision of 29 November 2021, the VfGH applied section 62a(1)(9) VfGG: an interim-injunction proceeding under the Exekutionsordnung cannot itself support a party application under Article 140(1)(1)(d) B-VG.

The exclusion does not eliminate the civil route. It means that urgent interim protection and constitutional review must be procedurally separated: the first-instance merits judgment may support a party application combined with an appeal, while the EO interim proceeding does not.
