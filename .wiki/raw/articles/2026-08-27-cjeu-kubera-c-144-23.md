# CJEU KUBERA, C-144/23

- Court: CJEU
- Judgment: 15 October 2024
- ECLI: `EU:C:2024:881`

A last-instance court must assess whether a party's relevant EU-law question requires referral and, if it declines, give reasons tied to irrelevance, existing CJEU interpretation, or absence of reasonable doubt. The rule does not remove the national court's control over relevance or turn the litigant's proposed wording into a mandatory reference.
