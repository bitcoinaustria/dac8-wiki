# VfGH G 123/2023: climate application and challenge perimeter

The applicants attacked selected words of the Climate Protection Act. The VfGH rejected the application because deleting that perimeter would not eliminate the alleged defect.

For Krypto-MPfG drafting, the case requires testing the legal remainder after every proposed deletion. A rhetorically attractive phrase is not a valid perimeter if the same duty continues elsewhere.
