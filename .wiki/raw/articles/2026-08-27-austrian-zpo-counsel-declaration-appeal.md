# Austrian ZPO: counsel, declaratory action and appeal timing

Section 27 ZPO requires lawyer representation before the Landesgerichte and higher courts and, subject to statutory exceptions, in Bezirksgericht disputes above EUR 5,000. This is why Article 79 counsel cannot be stated without first determining Austrian subject-matter jurisdiction and claim value.

Section 228 permits an action to establish the existence or non-existence of a legal relationship or right where the claimant has a legal interest in prompt determination. It is not an abstract validity action. Section 464 sets a non-extendable four-week period for the appeal against a first-instance judgment. Section 528b coordinates continuation of the civil appeal after a party constitutional-review application.
