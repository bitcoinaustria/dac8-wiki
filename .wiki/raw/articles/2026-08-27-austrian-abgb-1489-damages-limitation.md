# Austria ABGB section 1489: damages limitation

Section 1489 ABGB generally requires a damages action within three years from knowledge of the damage and the responsible person. It provides a thirty-year period where the injured person lacks that knowledge or where the damage arises from one or more judicially punishable intentional acts carrying more than one year of imprisonment.

This rule is relevant to an Article 82 GDPR damages claim. It does not supply a uniform period for every Article 79 compliance, erasure, restriction or injunction claim; those remain claim-specific.
