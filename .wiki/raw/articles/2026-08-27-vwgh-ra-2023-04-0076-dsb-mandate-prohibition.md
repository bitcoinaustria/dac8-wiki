# VwGH Ra 2023/04/0076: DSB mandate prohibition was used in practice

In the proceedings underlying Ra 2023/04/0076, the DSB immediately prohibited specified video processing by mandate decision under section 22(4) DSG and section 57 AVG. After further investigation it lifted that prohibition. The VwGH decision of 30 August 2023 principally concerned the BVwG's impermissible remittal, but its procedural history confirms actual use of the urgent DSB power.

Relevant passages: paragraphs 1-4.

The case disproves the claim that the statutory urgent route is purely theoretical. It does not show that the thresholds of substantial immediate danger and danger in delay are met by every Krypto-MPfG processing operation, nor that a prohibition would remain in force through the first report.
