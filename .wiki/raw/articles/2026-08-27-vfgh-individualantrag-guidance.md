# VfGH guidance: essential elements of an Individualantrag

## Core requirements captured

The application must rely on Article 140(1)(1)(c) B-VG, identify the challenged provisions precisely, request their repeal or the correct alternative declaration for expired law, and state the constitutional objections individually and specifically. Several defects are substantive and cannot be cured after filing.

The applicant must show that the law itself affects a legally protected sphere adversely, directly, currently, and without an intervening judicial decision or administrative act. No other reasonable route may exist. The challenge perimeter must be neither too narrow to remove the alleged defect nor unnecessarily broad.

The guidance records mandatory lawyer representation for this class of proceeding, filing fees, and detailed service/form requirements. Exact current amounts and exceptions must be checked when preparing a real filing.

## Research use

Admissibility is a threshold issue independent from the merits. A useful litigation plan must identify the exact norm, present current direct effect, and explain why provider correspondence, tax proceedings, data-protection remedies, or another judicial route are not reasonable alternatives.
