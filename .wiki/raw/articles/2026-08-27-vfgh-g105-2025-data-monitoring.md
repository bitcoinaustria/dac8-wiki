# VfGH G 105/2025: current CASP-like implementation burden and adverse data-monitoring merits

The corrected full judgment of 18 December 2025 concerns a statutory duty on pharmaceutical wholesalers to transmit detailed inventory and movement data daily through an electronic interface.

Paragraphs 17–22 accepted the main Article 140 application. Material technical, administrative, or other preparations necessary before a fixed compliance date to avoid sanctions are not merely economic reflexes. Current impairment begins early enough to allow review before those expenses become unavoidable. Unrebutted measures in electronic data processing and associated effort established present effect.

The judgment is also a strong merits warning. Paragraphs 47–68 upheld detailed, daily, non-aggregated monitoring across the relevant product range. The VfGH accepted public-interest purposes, broad coverage, recipient-specific access, inferred purpose limitation from the surrounding act, and existing secrecy safeguards. The occupational-freedom and property arguments largely followed the data-protection analysis.

For DAC8 this is the closest current standing analogue for a provider with documented interface and internal-data changes. It does not establish that costs alone prove disproportionality; claimant-specific quantification and a severable Austrian excess remain necessary.
