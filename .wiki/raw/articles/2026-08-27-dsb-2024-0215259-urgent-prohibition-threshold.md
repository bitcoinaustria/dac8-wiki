# DSB 2024-0.215.259: threshold for an urgent processing prohibition

In decision 2024-0.215.259 / DSB-D124.0775/24 of 28 March 2024, section D.1, the DSB accepted that a data subject has a right to a decision on an application under section 22(4) DSG. The applicant must nevertheless substantiate two cumulative conditions from the outset: a rationally founded suspicion of a weighty data-protection violation, and a substantial immediate danger to protected confidentiality interests with danger in delay.

General apprehension, speculation and hypothetical future events were insufficient. The asserted danger must be tied to the applicant and the concrete circumstances. This is adverse authority against presenting section 22(4) DSG as an automatic precautionary stop before a documented DAC8 processing or transfer event.
