# Bobigny: derivative account and limits

Source digest. Protos Staff, 9 January 2026. Locators: opening paragraphs and payment/defence paragraphs, all before the separate “French Women held captive” section. This account cites Le Parisien and is not independent corroboration.

It reports a tax-derived new home address, an attack on a prison officer and his wife, and payments to the employee. EUR 800 refers to alleged payment to the attackers, not a demonstrated price for her data. It also reports searches concerning crypto investors, without connecting those searches to attacks on them. The employee reportedly acknowledged disclosure but disputed knowledge of its intended use.

The separate crypto-attack section is not evidence of her involvement. Original court materials and disposition remain unverified.
