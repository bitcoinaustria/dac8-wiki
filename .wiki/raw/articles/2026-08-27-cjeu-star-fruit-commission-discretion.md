# CJEU 247/87 Star Fruit: Commission discretion under Article 258

In *Star Fruit v Commission*, Case 247/87, the Court held that the Commission is not bound to commence Article 258 proceedings upon a complaint. The Commission has discretion whether to initiate the procedure and whether to refer a Member State to the Court. An individual therefore has no admissible action to compel that enforcement choice.

For the DAC8 route comparison, the decision makes a Commission complaint a supplementary public-enforcement tactic, not an enforceable substitute for Austrian judicial remedies.
