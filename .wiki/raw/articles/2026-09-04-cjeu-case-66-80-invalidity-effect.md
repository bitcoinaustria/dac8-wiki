# CJEU Case 66/80 International Chemical Corporation on invalidity effects

In Case 66/80, judgment of 13 May 1981, the Court held that an Article 267 predecessor judgment declaring an institutional act invalid is directly addressed to the referring court but is sufficient reason for any other national court to regard the act as invalid for the judgment it must give. A later court may refer again where the ground, scope or consequences require clarification (paragraphs 12 to 16).

An invalidity ruling therefore has system-wide practical force beyond the originating case. It does not itself amend the text of an Austrian implementing statute or decide the underlying Austrian dispute.
