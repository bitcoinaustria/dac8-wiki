# European Commission DAC8 proposal COM(2022) 707

The Commission proposal is legislative history rather than the final binding text. Its explanatory memorandum describes reporting as covering relevant transactions carried out by reportable users and separates the identification of users from transaction reporting.

Together with final Annex VI Sections II and III, the proposal supports the textual conclusion that universal self-certification is required to determine status, while transaction reporting is structured around reportable users. It does not establish a maximum-harmonisation rule or independently forbid a Member State from adding domestic processing duties.
