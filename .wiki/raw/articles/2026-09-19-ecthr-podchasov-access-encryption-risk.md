# ECtHR Podchasov v Russia

Application 33696/19, judgment of 13 February 2024, final 13 May 2024. Official [HUDOC full-text conversion](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/app/conversion/docx/html/body?library=ECHR&id=001-230854) retrieved directly after the web-reader conversion failed. Judgment header confirms finality; source digest.

## Holdings

- Paragraphs 51–58: compelled storage itself interfered with Article 8. Proof of subsequent official access was unnecessary; the additional secret-surveillance analysis relied on broad coverage and ineffective remedies.
- Paragraphs 70–75: extensive retention and direct access capable of bypassing authorisation demanded effective safeguards against abuse.
- Paragraphs 76–79: encryption protects against hacking, theft, fraud and disclosure; criminal exploitation of backdoors informed the finding that compelled weakening of end-to-end encryption was disproportionate.
- Paragraphs 80–82: Article 8 violation; no separate Article 13 ruling.

## Use and limits

Supports prospective scrutiny of architecture, but concerned communications content, secret-service access and decryption. It does not equate confidential tax aggregates with communications surveillance. Vulnerable-user examples in paragraph 46 are intervener submissions, not a distinct holding.
