# DSG section 21: correct official locator and consultation wording

The official RIS URL requires `Artikel=2`. The URL in the earlier [consultation record](2026-09-04-austria-dsg-section-21-consultation-duty.md) omitted that parameter. This record supplies the correct locator without altering the original record.

Section 21(1) requires the Datenschutzbehörde to be heard before federal statutes and regulations within federal administration that directly concern data-protection matters. The statute alone does not establish whether a particular consultation occurred. No absence of consultation in the Krypto-MPfG-DV process is established here.
