# DAC history: primary-law scope and deadline corrections

This is an authored verification supplement, not a reproduction of a single act. The controlling provisions and official URLs are identified below. Verification was performed on 30 September 2026 during a fixed-head review of the campaign history page. Legislative sources establish legal rules; they do not establish actual filing or transmission events.

## DAC2: review and reporting are different, neither universally covers every account

- [Directive 2014/107/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/107/oj/eng), Annex I Sections I and VIII, distinguishes reportable accounts and persons from the broader due-diligence population.
- Annex I Section V.A permits existing entity accounts with an aggregate balance/value not exceeding USD250,000 to remain unreviewed, unidentified and unreported unless the institution elects otherwise, until the balance/value exceeds the threshold in a later year. Excluded account types provide a separate limitation.
- [BGBl. I 116/2015](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/BgblAuth/BGBLA_2015_I_116/BGBLA_2015_I_116.html), GMSG §§34–36, reproduces the existing-entity-account threshold and reportability distinction. This does not remove duties arising separately under AML/KYC legislation.

## DAC2: an exchange deadline is not an execution day

[Finance Committee report 749 d.B., XXV. GP](https://www.parlament.gv.at/dokument/XXV/I/749/fnameorig_433777.html), discussion of GMSG, specifies exchange of Q4 2016 new-account information by 30 September 2017 and other information, with exceptions, by 30 September 2018. The wording is a latest deadline in legislative materials, not evidence of transmission on that exact day. The earlier immutable committee-report digest already preserves this distinction.

## DAC3: covered forms and historical qualifications

[Directive 2015/2376](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32015L2376), recital 5, covers qualifying instruments irrespective of binding/non-binding character and material form; this is not a rule covering every informal tax opinion. Article 8a(2) establishes historical exchange for instruments issued, amended or renewed in the five years before 1 January 2017:

- Instruments from 2012–2013 must still have been valid on 1 January 2014.
- Instruments from 2014–2016 are covered irrespective of continued validity.
- Member States may exclude qualifying pre-1 April 2016 rulings for groups below EUR40 million preceding-year annual net turnover, other than groups mainly conducting financial/investment activities.

[BGBl. I 77/2016](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/BgblAuth/BGBLA_2016_I_77/BGBLA_2016_I_77.html), EU-AHG §7a, contains the Austrian historical qualifications. A bare statement of unrestricted five-year coverage or binding-only rulings is incomplete.

## DAC6: holdings are provision- and question-specific

- [C-694/20, operative part published in the Official Journal](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A62020CA0694): Article 8ab(5) is invalid insofar as it obliges a lawyer exempt from reporting because of legal professional privilege to notify another intermediary who is not the lawyer's client. Client notification was not abolished by this holding.
- [C-623/22](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A62022CJ0623), paragraphs 149–150 and operative part: the examined challenges did not establish invalidity; the special lawyer protection remains. This is not an unlimited approval of every provision, implementation or future legal objection.

## DAC7: count activities, not individual items

[Directive 2021/514](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2021/514/oj/eng), Annex V Section I.B.4(d), and [BGBl. I 108/2022](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/BgblAuth/BGBLA_2022_I_108/BGBLA_2022_I_108.html), DPMG §5(4)(4), exclude a seller for whom the platform facilitated fewer than 30 relevant goods-sale activities in the reporting period and whose total paid/credited consideration does not exceed EUR2,000. Both conditions must hold. Counting physical items instead of sale activities changes the stated test; one transaction can contain several items.

## DAC9: central filing is conditional; first filing dates are deadlines

[Directive 2025/872](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32025L0872), recital 4, describes local filing as the general rule and central filing as a derogation involving an ultimate parent or designated filing entity and a qualifying exchange arrangement. DAC9 supplies that arrangement between Member States.

[Directive 2022/2523](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2523/oj), Articles 44 and 51, supplies the filing periods, including an 18-month transitional period. For calendar-year 2024 reporting fiscal years this produces a 30 June 2026 deadline, not proof of an actual universal filing event. [BGBl. I 97/2025](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/BgblAuth/BGBLA_2025_I_97/BGBLA_2025_I_97.html), MinBestG §84(5), confirms Austrian commencement of §§73a–73b on 1 January 2026 for fiscal years beginning from 31 December 2023.

## DAC5: German quotation precision

[COM(2016)452, German text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/DE/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52016PC0452) refers to information leaked to the media, not the purported quotation "jüngste Medienberichte". The English phrase "recent media leaks" is supported. A German paraphrase without quotation marks avoids representing a translation as an exact quotation.

## Verification limits

Several exact EUR-Lex ELI routes returned bot challenges. Legal content was cross-checked using official CELEX/indexed originals, accessible Council texts and live RIS legislation. A challenge is not evidence that the legal link targets the wrong instrument. No primary source in this supplement establishes every actual transmission, successful implementation, authority query or real-world processing configuration.
