# VfGH G 178/2024: DSB route defeats direct eImpfpass challenge

On 28 April 2026 the VfGH rejected a direct challenge to mandatory electronic vaccination-record processing. Section 24 DSG allowed a complaint to the DSB; its decision could be appealed to the BVwG and then challenged under Article 144 B-VG, where the underlying statutory provisions could be attacked.

The Court expressly held that the applicant's immediate prospects before the DSB were irrelevant to whether that route was reasonable. This is the strongest current Austrian authority against making Article 140 the primary DAC8 vehicle.
