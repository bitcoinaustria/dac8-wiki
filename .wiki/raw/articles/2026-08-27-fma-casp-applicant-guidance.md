# FMA information for CASP authorisation applicants

The FMA describes the Article 59 authorisation requirement and the Austrian application process. It is useful for verifying provider status and separating licence work from tax-reporting implementation costs. It does not establish that every MiCA cost is attributable to the Krypto-MPfG.
