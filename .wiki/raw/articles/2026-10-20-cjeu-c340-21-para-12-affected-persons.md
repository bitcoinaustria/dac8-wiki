# C-340/21, paragraphs 11–12

Judgment of 14 December 2023, ECLI:EU:C:2023:986, English text retrieved from the EU Publications Office (Cellar) on 20 October 2026; EUR-Lex served a bot challenge. Supplements the [existing C-340/21 record](2026-09-04-cjeu-c340-21-tax-authority-breach-security.md), which covers the holdings.

## Exact text

Para. 11: "On 15 July 2019, the media revealed that unauthorised access to the NAP IT system had taken place and that, following that cyberattack, personal data contained in that system had been published on the internet."

Para. 12: "More than 6 million natural persons, of Bulgarian and foreign nationality, were affected by those events. Several hundred of them, including the appellant in the main proceedings, brought actions against the NAP for compensation for non-material damage allegedly resulting from the disclosure of their personal data."

## Limits

Paragraphs 11–12 state the facts of the reference; they are not a finding on how many records were exchanged tax data. The holdings remain those in the earlier record: a breach alone does not prove inadequate security. Combine with the [Global Forum statement](2026-10-20-oecd-global-forum-bulgaria-breach-statement.md) for the CRS/DAC content of the leak.
