# Parliamentary answer 4507/AB (XXVIII. GP)

**What it is.** Written answer by Finance Minister Markus Marterbauer dated 24 April 2026 (GZ 2026-0.171.767). It answers question 4944/J of 24 February 2026 (Nina Tomaselli et al.) on revenue from the 2025 anti-fraud package. Locators are the answers to questions 1–5 on pages 1–4 of 8.

**Key figures.** Planned revenue from "BBKG 2025 Teil Steuern" is EUR 430m for 2026–2029. Planned revenue from "BBKG 2025 Teil Daten" is "insgesamt 110 Mio. Euro" over 2026–2029 (question 1). BGBl. I Nr. 96/2025, the "Teil Daten" act, contains the Krypto-MPfG (Article 1) and amendments to the GMSG, EU-AHG, EU-MPfG, DPMG, BWG and KontRegG. That link comes from the gazette text in the local corpus, not from this answer. Administrative measures listed in the government resolution (MRV 31a/1) are to bring EUR 505m. Among these, the answer names "die verbesserte Nutzung von Daten" (better use of data) and targeted steering of control resources, with "Mehreinnahmen im zweistelligen Millionenbereich" (additional revenue in the tens of millions; question 4c). Building crypto expertise at the Amt für Betrugsbekämpfung (blockchain analysis, finding wallets during searches, securing crypto-assets) is expected to bring additional revenue in the single-digit millions net of implementation costs (question 4b). Staff: 30 additional FTE are to be reallocated, 12 to the Finanzamt Österreich, 10 to the Finanzamt für Großbetriebe and 8 to the ABB (question 5).

**Evidence class and use.** These are official budget-planning estimates, not measured results, and no method is given. For proportionality the answer supplies the State's own stated benefit for the whole data package: EUR 110m over four years for all measures in BGBl. I Nr. 96/2025 together. It does not isolate the Krypto-MPfG or DAC8 share. It does not say whether the revenue assumes risk-analytics use of the new data. It does not identify how many persons' data would be processed. Any per-person cost/benefit comparison would therefore be inference.
