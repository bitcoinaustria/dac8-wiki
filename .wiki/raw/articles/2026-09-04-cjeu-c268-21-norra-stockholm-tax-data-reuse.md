# CJEU C-268/21 Norra Stockholm Bygg: tax-record reuse and proportionality

- Judgment: 2 March 2023
- ECLI: `ECLI:EU:C:2023:145`

The dispute concerned a staff register created principally for tax inspection and sought as evidence in civil litigation. Paragraph 28 treats production of the document in judicial proceedings as further processing. Paragraphs 31–41 require a legal basis satisfying Article 6(3) and (4) GDPR for the later use.

The operative ruling holds that Articles 5 and 6 GDPR require the national court to consider the interests of the data subjects and balance them in the circumstances of the case, the type of proceedings and the requirements of proportionality and data minimisation.

The judgment is useful because the initial tax purpose did not make every later legally authorised disclosure automatic or unlimited. Its direct setting was court-ordered production in private litigation, not administrative joining of DAC8, AML, TFR, MiCA or public-ledger data. Any transfer to DAC8 therefore remains analogical and requires identification of the later purpose, legal basis, data subset and safeguards.
