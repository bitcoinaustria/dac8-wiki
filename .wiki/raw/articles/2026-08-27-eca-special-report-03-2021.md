# European Court of Auditors Special Report 03/2021

The ECA found that the legislative framework was transparent and logical but incomplete, and that exchanged data were often of limited quality or under-used. It found no uniform EU performance indicators and called for better data quality, analysis, use, and monitoring.

The report also states that the exchange system can work well when data are accurate, complete, and timely, and that exchanges on request, spontaneous exchanges, and joint controls had proved effective.

Its audit covered five Member States. It supports scrutiny of implementation quality and real-world use, but is not a finding that automatic exchange is inherently ineffective or invalid.
