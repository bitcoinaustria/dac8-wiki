# VfGH G 17/2024: current, direct effect and alternative remedy

## Case identity

- Court: Austrian Constitutional Court (`VfGH`)
- Case: `G 17/2024`
- Decision: 12 June 2024
- Result relevant here: application inadmissible

## Rule captured

The VfGH states that not every person addressed by a norm has standing. The statute itself must determine the interference by type and extent, affect legally protected interests currently rather than merely potentially, and leave no other reasonable way to resist the alleged unlawful interference.

The decision shows that time and cost do not automatically make an otherwise available route unreasonable. Whether provoking unlawful conduct would be required is assessed concretely; the existence of a lawful route to a reviewable decision can defeat the direct application.

## Research use

The intended DAC8 route cannot begin with abstract disagreement. It needs evidence that operative Krypto-MPfG provisions already and directly alter the applicant's legal position, plus a defensible account of why other routes are unavailable or unreasonable.
