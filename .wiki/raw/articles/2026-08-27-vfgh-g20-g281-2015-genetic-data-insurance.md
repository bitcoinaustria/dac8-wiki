# VfGH G 20/2015 and G 281/2015: genetic data and insurance

The actually regulated insurers had standing against restrictions on collecting and using genetic results; the industry association did not establish an equivalent injury in its own legal sphere. The companies did not have to trigger a penal proceeding to obtain review.

For DAC8, the case supports using the obligated CASP rather than a campaign body and separating a direct legal duty from reflective commercial or advocacy interests.
