# GDPR Articles 35 and 36: DPIA and consultation conditions

Article 35(1) requires the controller to conduct a DPIA before processing likely to result in high risk, considering its nature, scope, context and purposes. Article 35(7) requires a systematic description, assessment of necessity and proportionality, risk assessment and planned safeguards. The DPO is consulted under Article 35(2), and Article 35(9) addresses data-subject views where appropriate.

Article 35(10) is conditional, not a general exemption for statutory processing. It applies where Article 6(1)(c) or (e) is the basis, the Union or Member-State law regulates the specific processing operation or set of operations, and a DPIA was already carried out as part of a general impact assessment when that legal basis was adopted. A Member State may still require controllers to conduct an operational DPIA.

Article 36(1) requires controller consultation with the supervisory authority where a DPIA shows residual high risk absent mitigating measures. Article 36(4) separately requires Member States to consult the supervisory authority while preparing legislative or regulatory measures relating to processing. These are different files and triggers.

Articles 35 and 36 do not replace the continuing duties under Articles 5, 24, 25, 30 and 32. CJEU C-61/22 prevents treating adoption of a law as the processing operation for a shortcut invalidity claim.
