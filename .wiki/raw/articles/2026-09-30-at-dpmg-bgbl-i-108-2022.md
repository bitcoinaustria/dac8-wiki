# Austria: Digitale Plattformen-Meldepflichtgesetz (DPMG)

## Source identity

- Gazette: `BGBl. I Nr. 108/2022`, Abgabenänderungsgesetz 2022, ausgegeben am 19. Juli 2022 (NR: GP XXVII RV 1534 AB 1585 S. 168; BR: 11010 AB 11044; CELEX 32021L0514)
- Art. 25: Bundesgesetz über den verpflichtenden automatischen Informationsaustausch betreffend meldende Plattformbetreiber im Bereich der Besteuerung (Digitale Plattformen-Meldepflichtgesetz – DPMG). Art. 24 amends the EU-AHG and Art. 23 the EU-Meldepflichtgesetz.
- Entry into force: § 35 DPMG, "Dieses Bundesgesetz tritt mit 1. Jänner 2023 in Kraft." The EU-AHG changes (§§ 1, 2, 4, 4a, 5, 7, 7a, 10, 11, 12a, 12b, 15, 17, 19, 19a) also took effect on 1 January 2023 (§ 22(5) EU-AHG).

## Content

The EU-AHG now states that exchange of information reported by platform operators under Directive (EU) 2021/514 is governed by the DPMG. The same act added DAC7's general changes to the EU-AHG, including new § 19a on data breaches. It also made reporting financial institutions (GMSG), intermediaries (EU-MPfG), reporting platform operators (DPMG) and the Austrian competent authority controllers under Art. 4(7) GDPR where they determine purposes and means (§ 19(2) EU-AHG).

## Relevance and limits

DAC7 reaches private sellers on platforms and so moves reporting towards natural persons outside the financial sector. DPMG's substantive due-diligence content was not analysed here.
