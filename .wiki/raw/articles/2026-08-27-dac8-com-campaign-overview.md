# DAC8.com campaign overview

The campaign argues that automatic reporting links civil identity and address information to crypto transaction and transfer data, increasing privacy and physical-security risks. It advances proportionality, effectiveness, less-intrusive-alternative, and MiCA policy-coherence arguments.

The page states that the first reporting period is 2026 and the first inter-authority exchange is expected in 2027. It promotes targeted, motivated information requests as an alternative to mass reporting.
