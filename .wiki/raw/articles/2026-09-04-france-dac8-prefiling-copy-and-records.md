# France DAC8: pre-filing user copy, reusable due diligence and ten-year records

Article 1649 AC quinquies I of the General Tax Code requires the provider to use necessary due diligence, including personal-data processing, to identify users who carry out one or more covered transactions and their relevant controlling persons.

Article 1649 AC quinquies IV requires the provider, before filing the statutory report, to give each affected crypto-asset user or controlling person the information transmitted to the tax administration concerning that person. This is enacted proof that a concrete pre-filing view of the reportable dataset is administratively possible. The provision does not give the person a veto and does not automatically suspend filing while an objection is considered.

Decree No. 2025-1276, Article 10, permits reliance on completed Common Reporting Standard due diligence and a self-certification already collected for another tax purpose if the statutory conditions are met. Article 8 sets ten years for retention of the statutory register. France therefore is not a shorter-retention comparator.

The relevant texts are also available in the [official decree](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053157956), especially Articles 8 and 10.
