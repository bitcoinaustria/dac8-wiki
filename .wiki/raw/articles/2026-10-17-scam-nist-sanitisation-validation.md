# NIST88r2: sanitisation validation and cryptographic erase limits

Final26September2025; full PDF https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-88r2.pdf . Locators §3.2, printedpp.11–15; §§4.5–4.6, pp.24–25 onward.

Verification of a sanitisation operation and validation of adequacy are distinct. Cryptographic erase depends on appropriate encryption/key-management history. Prior plaintext, external keys, backups and escrow can defeat an overbroad assurance; cloud abstraction constrains direct control.

Media sanitisation is not row-level application deletion. These conditions motivate copy/key inventories and restoration tests, not a claim that every lawful record can be erased or stolen copies recalled. Mechanical credibility2: recent institutional primary.
