# CJEU C-356/12 Glatzel on Charter equality

In C-356/12, judgment of 22 May 2014, paragraph 43, the CJEU held that the general principle of equal treatment is enshrined in Article 20 of the Charter and that Article 21(1) is a particular expression of it. Comparable situations must not be treated differently and different situations must not be treated alike unless objectively justified by a reasonable criterion tied to a lawful aim and proportionate to that aim.

For a technology- or asset-based DAC8 comparison, Article 20 is the more direct starting point. The judgment does not establish that holding or using a crypto-asset is itself a protected status or a discrimination ground under Article 21.
