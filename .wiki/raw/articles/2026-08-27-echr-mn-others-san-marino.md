# M.N. and Others v San Marino

The ECtHR treated banking documents as personal data protected by Article 8 ECHR even without classifying every item as especially sensitive. The violation arose from the applicants' inability to obtain effective review of a broad seizure and transmission of their banking material.

For the wealth-profile argument, the judgment supports the need for precise access conditions, notice or an effective substitute, independent review, and a usable remedy. It does not hold that controlled international tax or criminal-cooperation access to financial data is categorically unlawful.
