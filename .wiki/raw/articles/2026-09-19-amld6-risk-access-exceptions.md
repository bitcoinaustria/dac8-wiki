# Targeted supplement: Articles 11, 15 and 78

Article 15 requires case-specific exceptions from access under Articles 11(3) and 12(1) where exceptional circumstances expose beneficial owners to disproportionate risks including kidnapping, extortion and violence. Detailed assessment and remedies are required. Certain obliged entities acting as public officials are excluded from the exception.

Authority access under Article 11(1)–(2), expressly including tax authorities, is not covered. Neither collection nor DAC8 reporting is exempted. Article 78(1) sets 10 July 2026 as the transposition deadline for Articles 11, 12, 13 and 15, earlier than the general deadline. Austrian transposition was not verified in this extraction.
