# BVwG current filing fee and administrative appeal procedure

The BVwG's official notice states that, from 1 July 2025, the fee under the VwG-Eingabengebuehrverordnung is EUR 50 for covered complaints, reinstatement and reopening applications, and other proceeding-initiating submissions. A separately filed application for the grant or exclusion of suspensive effect, a legal-aid application or a Vorlageantrag is EUR 25.

This source verifies the current fee only. The relevant DSB-Bescheid appeal period and whether a particular ancillary filing is separately chargeable must be checked against the served decision and the applicable procedural rule.
