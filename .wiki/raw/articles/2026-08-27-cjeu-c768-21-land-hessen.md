# CJEU C-768/21 Land Hessen: supervisory-authority corrective powers

The CJEU held that a supervisory authority does not have to use a corrective power, especially a fine, in every established GDPR infringement. A complainant has no subjective right to demand an administrative fine.

The authority must nevertheless act where one or more Article 58(2) corrective powers are appropriate, necessary, and proportionate to remedy the established shortcoming and fully enforce the GDPR. Those powers include ordering compliance and imposing a temporary or definitive limitation, including a processing ban. An authority may refrain where the violation has already been fully cured and will not recur.

This supports requesting effective protection in a DAC8 DSB complaint, but it does not establish an automatic right to suspend a statutory reporting regime. The exact remedy remains case-specific, and a serious challenge to the validity of an EU act still belongs before a court capable of making an Article 267 reference.
