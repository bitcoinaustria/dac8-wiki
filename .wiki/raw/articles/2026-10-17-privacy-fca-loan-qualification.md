# FCA financial-abuse digest: loan-description qualification

The companion `2026-10-17-privacy-fca-financial-autonomy.md` uses “fraudulent loans”. The FCA's “Why does this matter to the FCA” section says loans taken out in the victim-survivor's name, without establishing that every such loan involved fraud or identity theft. Coerced borrowing need not involve either. The supported wording is **loans taken out in a victim's name in a context of financial abuse**. This qualification controls synthesis; it is not another study, incident or quantified finding. Mechanical credibility 2: institutional source +1, recent +1; not independent corroboration.
