# COM(2025) 695: the announced way forward

## Source identity

Report from the Commission to the European Parliament and the Council on the evaluation of Council Directive 2011/16/EU, COM(2025) 695 final, Brussels, 19 November 2025, with SWD(2025) 365. The local copy is `legal-docs/supporting/eu-com-2025-695-dac-evaluation.pdf`. Article 27 requires the report every five years. This second evaluation covers 2018–2023 and amendments up to and including DAC6; DAC7 is treated only qualitatively. The High-Level Working Party on Tax Questions discussed it on 27 January 2026.

## Way forward (Section 4)

The Commission said it would, "as appropriate, explore or take" these measures:

- consolidate the DAC texts;
- remove duplications and streamline or eliminate unnecessarily burdensome reporting;
- review the DAC6 hallmarks;
- explore integrating UNSHELL concepts;
- publish EU-level guidance on recent amendments;
- engage with Member States on penalty regimes (named for DAC2, DAC4 and DAC6);
- study an EU TIN and a central validation tool, aiming at a 100 % matching rate;
- push more systematic use of data (pre-filling, risk analysis) and more binding KPIs;
- in the long term, assess a centralised single access point for exchange and possibly reporting.

## Relevance and limits

This is the policy basis for the June 2026 recast. Consolidation, DAC6 streamlining and TIN verification went into COM(2026) 308; the central access point did not. The evaluation says nothing on DAC8 effectiveness because DAC8 was outside its scope.
