# KBM case status: completed DPA and dismissal

Official case page updated 22 September 2025, retrieved 17 October 2026; opening case-status paragraphs.

The DPA required USD33.5 million for victim compensation and an USD8.5 million fine. It expired on 11 December 2023 after KBM completed its obligations; the case was then dismissed.

This controls the status description of the separate 2021 admissions. A completed DPA is not a guilty verdict. The compensation amount does not establish a distinct non-overlapping victim population or a loss total to add to Epsilon figures. Mechanical credibility 2: recent institutional primary.
