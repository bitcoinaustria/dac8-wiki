# VfGH G 265/2023

The VfGH held section 10 WiEReG, in the version allowing access by anyone, unconstitutional under section 1 DSG together with Article 8 ECHR. Section 10a's exceptional restriction mechanism survived. The decision translates the CJEU's Luxembourg Business Registers reasoning into Austrian constitutional law.

It is direct authority against open public search, not against access by authorities, obliged entities, or persons satisfying a legitimate-interest gate.
