# The Community: carrier authority as an intermediate capability

DOJ30November2021. Locators SIM-hijacking, later-account access and scheme-total paragraphs.

Six defendants were sentenced after guilty pleas. Bribed carrier employees or customer impersonation enabled number control, followed by email/cloud/exchange-account access. Scheme theft was reported in tens of millions. Individual restitution orders may overlap and are not payments recovered. Original target discovery and specific customer-record exports were not identified. Carrier transfer authority is distinct from leaked financial data, private-key possession or a DAC8 field.
