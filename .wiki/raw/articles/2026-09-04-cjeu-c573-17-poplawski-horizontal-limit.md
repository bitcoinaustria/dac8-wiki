# CJEU C-573/17 Popławski: limit on disapplication based on a directive

- Judgment: 24 June 2019
- ECLI: `ECLI:EU:C:2019:530`

Paragraphs 65–68 restate that a directive cannot of itself impose obligations on an individual and cannot therefore be relied on as such against that individual. Primacy does not allow a national court to set aside national legislation solely because it conflicts with a directive that lacks the necessary direct effect in the dispute.

In a complaint against a private crypto-asset service provider, DAC8 over-transposition can show that Austria's interference was not required by Union law, but the requested disapplication should rest on directly applicable GDPR or primary-law rules rather than DAC8 alone.
