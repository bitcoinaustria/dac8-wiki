# VfGH G 4/2020: primary-school headscarf prohibition

The child and parent applicants described their family circumstances, religious practice, schooling, and the norm's concrete effect. The court assessed current and direct effect, exact identification of the norm, and the absence of another reasonable route before addressing religious freedom and equality.

The judgment is useful for communications because it shows that social exclusion can matter when it is tied to the actual rule and claimant facts—not when presented as an unsupported generalized risk.
