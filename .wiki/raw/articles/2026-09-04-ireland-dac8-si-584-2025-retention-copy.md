# Ireland SI 584/2025: six-year DAC8 records and user copy

Statutory Instrument No. 584 of 2025 inserted section 891M into the Taxes Consolidation Act 1997.

Section 891M(6) requires a reporting crypto-asset service provider, by the specified return date, to make the return and provide each reportable user with a copy of the information in that return relating to the user. The same deadline does not establish that the copy must arrive before filing, and the provision creates no user veto or automatic suspension.

Section 891M(13)(d) requires the provider to retain the reporting and due-diligence records for the longer of an open-enquiry period or six years beginning after the end of the relevant reporting period. Special rules preserve records on winding-up or dissolution.

The six-year rule is enacted evidence that DAC8's permitted five-to-ten-year range does not require every Member State to select ten years. Whether six years is an equally suitable Austrian alternative still requires comparison with the relevant Austrian audit, limitation and enforcement periods.
