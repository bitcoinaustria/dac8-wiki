# Directive (EU) 2018/1673

This instrument is often called the criminal-law AMLD6 and must not be confused with the 2024 recast institutional AMLD6. Article 2 defines property broadly enough to include electronic or digital documents. Articles 3 and 4 cover conversion, transfer, concealment, acquisition, possession, use, aiding, abetting, inciting, and attempt.

Article 5 requires a maximum imprisonment ceiling of at least four years for the core offences. Article 6 requires aggravating treatment for professional commission by an obliged entity and criminal-organisation involvement. Articles 7 and 8 require legal-person liability and sanctions. Directive 2024/1226 later added restrictive-measures offences as predicate offences.
