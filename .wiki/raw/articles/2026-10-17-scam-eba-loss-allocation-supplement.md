# EBA ECB supplement: selection explanation and insurance exclusions

Controlling precision supplement to the same 2025 EBA/ECB report, not a new study or independent population. Locators: executive discussion p.6, transfer-SCA discussion p.26; losses p.34 and footnote13.

The report's explanation of higher SCA credit-transfer fraud by higher-risk/value transaction selection is a likely explanation, not a causally identified effect. The original digest's shorthand about confounding must not be reported as a proved decomposition.

Reported losses are recorded in providers' books and may differ in timing from fraudulent transactions. Footnote13 excludes refunds by insurance agencies. EEA/Austrian allocation percentages therefore do not measure ultimate net household burden after insurance. This is additional context, not proof that insurance normally covers a specified loss.
