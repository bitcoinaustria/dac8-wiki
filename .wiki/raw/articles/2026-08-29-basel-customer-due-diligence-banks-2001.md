# Basel customer due diligence for banks 2001

The Basel paper organised KYC around customer-acceptance policy, identification, ongoing monitoring and risk management. It linked weak KYC to reputational, operational, legal and concentration risks and distinguished the Basel prudential perspective from FATF's AML standard-setting role.

It is supervisory guidance rather than legislation. Its broader KYC concept should not be treated as identical to every statutory CDD requirement.
