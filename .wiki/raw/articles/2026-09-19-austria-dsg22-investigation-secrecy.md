# Austria DSG section 22: investigation powers and revised secrecy rule

Selected digest of Article 2 section 22 DSG, BGBl. I No. 50/2025, effective 1 September 2025, RIS document NOR40271297. The current version identifier was checked on 19 September 2026.

## National rule

Subsection 1 permits necessary explanations and inspection of processing and related documents, with controller/processor support and regard for their and third parties' rights. Subsection 2 provides notified premises access, operation and testing of systems, and strictly necessary data-carrier copies.

Subsection 3 limits information obtained to data-protection supervision. Secrecy now applies insofar and for as long as necessary and proportionate under section 6(1) IFG. It also addresses secrecy toward courts and administrative authorities, including tax authorities, with specified criminal-reporting exceptions. It is not a general publication entitlement.

## Related primary provisions

[GDPR Articles 30(4), 36(3)(e), 57(1)(f) and 58(1)(a), (b), (e), (f)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) respectively support supervisory access to processing records, submission of the DPIA on prior consultation, appropriate complaint investigation, information demands, audits and access to necessary information and premises. Authority access is distinct from complainant access.

[Section 17(1)–(4) AVG](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1991/51/P17/NOR40148215) gives parties access to files concerning their case, subject to protected interests, authority tasks and procedural purpose. Refusal in pending proceedings takes the form of a procedural direction. These provisions do not create private discovery of every document or an unconditional right to a chosen investigative measure.
