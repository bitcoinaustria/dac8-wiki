# Austria EU-AHG sections 15 and 19

Section 15 governs downstream use of information exchanged under the EU administrative-cooperation regime. Subsections 1 and 2 permit use for covered taxes, VAT, other indirect taxes, customs, AML/CFT, recovery claims, compulsory social-security contributions and related penalty proceedings. Subsection 3 permits another purpose with approval of the originating Member State where Austrian law permits that use. Subsection 3a permits use without case-specific approval for a purpose on a list previously communicated by the originating Member State. Subsection 4 permits onward transmission to another Member State subject to the Directive's conditions and the originating State's opportunity to object.

Section 19(1) restricts GDPR Articles 13, 14(1) and 15 for EU-AHG execution only insofar as the restriction is suitable, necessary and proportionate to protect an important objective of general public interest. It is not an unconditional exclusion of access. Section 19(2) allocates controller status for the entities and Austrian authority covered by its current wording.

The Krypto-MPfG applies parts of the EU-AHG mutatis mutandis. The exact authority, controller and restriction invoked in a concrete DAC8 response must therefore be established rather than presumed.
