# CJEU C-61/22: legislative act is not itself processing and DPIA defect is not automatic invalidity

- Judgment: 21 March 2024
- ECLI: `ECLI:EU:C:2024:251`

Paragraphs 64–68 distinguish adoption of a law or regulation from personal-data processing. Paragraphs 66–67 hold that adoption of the statutory measure at issue did not itself constitute processing within Article 4(2), so Article 35(1) did not apply to that act and Article 35(10) could not have been infringed on that basis.

The judgment makes a pure "no DPIA, therefore invalid" challenge to DAC8 or the Krypto-MPfG unsound. A DPIA remains useful evidence for the concrete provider or authority processing and for whether risks, necessity and safeguards were actually evaluated.
