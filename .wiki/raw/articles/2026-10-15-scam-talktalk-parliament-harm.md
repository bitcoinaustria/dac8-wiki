# UK Parliament: supplier-breach scam losses distinguished from TalkTalk cyberattack

Culture, Media and Sport Committee report, 17 June 2016, chapter 2 paragraphs 13–14 and 20.

The committee records evidence from individuals losing money after scam calls following breaches at TalkTalk suppliers. It explicitly received no evidence of financial loss directly attributable to the cyberattack itself. These event classes must not be merged.

It recommends accessible independent verification of purported company contact and planned incident communications; notification can itself provide an impersonation pretext.

This is parliamentary reception of witness evidence, not a court's case-by-case causal finding, representative survey or basis for transferring the October 2015 exposure count to scam losses. Mechanical score 1: historical institutional original, accepted for its unusually explicit evidentiary distinction.
