## Content and dates

DAC9 amends Directive 2011/16/EU to put into operation Article 44 of Directive (EU) 2022/2523 (Pillar Two). A multinational group can file one top-up tax information return (TTIR) centrally through a designated filing entity, instead of filing locally in each Member State. The return is based on the OECD GloBE Information Return. It adds Article 8ae (exchange of TTIRs) and Article 27d (first year and timing), and extends Article 25a on penalties.

Article 27d, as consolidated:

- the first Reporting fiscal year is the first fiscal year beginning from 31 December 2023;
- competent authorities exchange the first-year information no later than six months after the filing deadline;
- the first exchange may not be earlier than 1 December 2026.

For calendar-year groups the first filing deadline under Directive 2022/2523 is **30 June 2026**, and the first exchange is due **by 31 December 2026**. The Commission and Council communications give the same dates. Transposition: adopt and publish by **31 December 2025**. The standard forms are in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1325 of 7 July 2025.

## Later developments

The pending DAC recast (COM(2026) 308) would merge the DAC4 and DAC9 notifications into one group notification. It would also move the TTIR template out of an annex and into implementing acts. This is proposed, not adopted.

## Relevance and limits

DAC9 is company-level Pillar Two information and has no crypto-asset or individual-taxpayer content. On the DAC timeline it is the ninth and most recent adopted amendment (as of 30 September 2026). This record does not verify how many Member States had transposed DAC9 by the deadline. The consolidated text used is `legal-docs/core/eu-directive-2011-16-consolidated-2026.pdf`.
