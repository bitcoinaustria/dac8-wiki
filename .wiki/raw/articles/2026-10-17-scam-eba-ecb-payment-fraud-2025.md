# EBA ECB2025: authentication, manipulation and loss allocation

December2025 report, released15December. Locators pp.6–9,19–20,25–26,34–37; methodology47–48.

For2024, payer manipulation accounts for74% of fraudulent credit-transfer value. Card SCA shows lower fraud; high-risk transaction selection confounds transfer SCA comparisons. Authentication cannot automatically be treated as authorisation. Credit-transfer fraudulent valueEUR2.5bn differs from recorded lossesEUR2.2bn. Users bore about85% of EEA transfer losses, but Austria approximately59%, PSPs40%.

Reporting revisions, exclusions and classification limits matter. No leak-attributable share, crypto-loss estimate or DAC8 effect is provided. Statistics are not an individual legal-liability determination. Mechanical credibility2: recent institutional primary.
