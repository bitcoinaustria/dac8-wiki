# VfGH G 16/2017: alternative route defeats an Individualantrag

The former owner challenged the expropriation statute directly while a land-register proceeding and a party application supplied a route to constitutional review. The VfGH rejected the direct application.

The decision is an important adverse comparator for a DAC8 user whenever a DSB decision, BVwG appeal, and later constitutional complaint can realistically reach the issue.
