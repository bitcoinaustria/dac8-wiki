# Finland: DAC8 implementation with an additional national gains-and-losses report

The Finnish Tax Administration states that Finland's 2026 reporting obtains the information required by DAC8 and CARF and, for Finnish tax residents, additional information from which taxable gains and losses can be calculated. Users remain responsible for reporting gains and losses in their tax returns.

The Finnish Government's [HE 84/2025 proposal page](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/paatokset/paatos?decisionId=4399) and [Act 1043/2025](https://www.finlex.fi/en/legislation/collection/2025/1043) provide the legislative context.

This is adverse comparative evidence against any proposition that Member States may never add crypto-tax reporting fields or purposes beyond the DAC8 minimum. It also supplies a useful distinction: a national extension can be expressly identified and tied to a defined domestic tax-calculation purpose. It does not decide whether Austria's all-customer processing language is necessary, proportionate or sufficiently justified.
