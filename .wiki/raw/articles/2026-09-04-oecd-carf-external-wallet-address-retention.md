# OECD CARF commentary on external-wallet address records

Paragraph 21 explains that tax administrations may use existing exchange-of-information channels to request more detailed wallet-address information if the reported external-wallet aggregate raises compliance concerns.

The commentary to Section II, paragraphs 25 and 26, states that the ordinary report does not contain wallet addresses. It also states that a reporting provider should collect and retain external wallet addresses or equivalent identifiers associated with transfers in the external-wallet category for at least five years so they are available for follow-up requests.

This is an OECD standard and commentary, not by itself a binding Austrian storage rule. DAC8 did not copy the CARF Section III.D.3 address-retention clause verbatim. The binding EU and Austrian record rules therefore require separate interpretation.
