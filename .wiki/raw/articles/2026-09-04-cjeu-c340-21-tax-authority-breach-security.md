# CJEU C-340/21, Natsionalna agentsia za prihodite

The judgment of 14 December 2023, ECLI:EU:C:2023:986, arose from unauthorised access to and publication of personal data held by the Bulgarian revenue authority.

Paragraphs 39 and 42–47 establish that third-party access does not by itself prove that the controller's measures were inappropriate and that courts must examine the nature, content, implementation and practical effects of those measures against the concrete risks of the processing. Paragraph 52 places the burden of demonstrating appropriate security on the controller in an Article 82 action. Paragraphs 71–74 reject automatic exemption merely because the immediate actor was a third party. Paragraphs 77 and 84–86 permit fear of future misuse to constitute non-material damage only where the fear and its negative consequences are proved and the fear is well founded.

This is GDPR security and damages authority, not a ruling on DAC8 validity. It supports fact-specific scrutiny and rejects an abstract inference from the mere possibility or occurrence of hacking.
