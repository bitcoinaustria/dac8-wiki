# NIST SP 800-86 digital-forensics process

NIST SP 800-86, published in August 2006, describes a four-stage digital-forensics process: collection and preservation of potentially relevant data; examination and extraction; analysis; and reporting. It stresses preserving integrity, documenting the process and choosing data sources and techniques appropriate to the incident and legal setting.

For a DAC8-to-ledger test, the useful benchmark is operational: freeze the exact provider export and public-chain snapshot, hash all inputs, retain tool and parameter versions, preserve the transformation from source record to candidates, and make an independent rerun possible.

The document is an IT incident-response guide, not an Austrian evidentiary standard and not a blockchain attribution validation.
