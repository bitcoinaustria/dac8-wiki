# Luxembourg: official crypto-asset reporting framework overview

Luxembourg implemented the framework through the law of 27 March 2026, published as Mémorial A No. 144. The administration states that the first report for the 2026 year is due on 30 June 2027. No DAC8-specific Luxembourg judgment was located.
