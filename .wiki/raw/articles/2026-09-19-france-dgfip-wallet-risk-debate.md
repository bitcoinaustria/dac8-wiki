# Parliamentary record of DGFiP risk warning

Selected digest. Assemblée nationale, first sitting of 26 February 2026, Article 3 quater debate, interventions by Daniel Labaronne and minister David Amiel before the vote on amendments 483 and 995.

Labaronne quotes a DGFiP questionnaire response warning that generalised self-hosted-wallet declarations would centralise sensitive identity and asset-value data and increase fraud risks if attacked. The official record verifies his statement, not the unpublished underlying email independently.

The proposal was additional self-hosted-wallet reporting, not DAC8's provider reporting. Amiel expressly favours DAC8's third-party verification. The deletion amendments were rejected, 12 to 40. This record supports recognition of a data-concentration risk; it establishes neither a DGFiP finding against DAC8 nor a legislative repeal caused by that warning. It mentions fraud risk, not an adjudicated physical attack. Subsequent legislative status is outside this extraction.
