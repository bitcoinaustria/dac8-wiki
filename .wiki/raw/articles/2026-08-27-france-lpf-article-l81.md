# France Tax Procedure Code Article L81

Article L81 gives the tax administration a right to obtain documents and information from specified third parties. The current text permits requests concerning unidentified persons under conditions set by decree after CNIL opinion.

This corrects the narrower DAC8.com description of the power as restricted to an identified taxpayer in an open investigation. The mechanism remains a relevant less-intrusive comparator, but its scope and equivalence to cross-border automatic reporting require separate analysis.
