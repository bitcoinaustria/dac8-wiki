# CJEU Atlanta: strict conditions for interim relief against an EU measure

Case C-465/93, *Atlanta*, applies the *Zuckerfabrik* conditions to national interim relief against a domestic measure implementing an EU act.

A national court must have serious doubts about the EU act's validity and refer that question to the CJEU unless it is already pending. Interim relief also requires urgency and a threat of serious and irreparable harm. The court must take the Union interest into account and respect existing CJEU or General Court rulings on the act.

Relevant passages: paragraphs 30-33 and 41-51.

The route exists, but its conditions are a major obstacle to using an Austrian urgent proceeding as a simple pre-report suspension of DAC8.
