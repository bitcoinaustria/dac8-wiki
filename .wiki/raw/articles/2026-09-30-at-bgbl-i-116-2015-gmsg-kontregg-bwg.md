# Austria: BGBl. I Nr. 116/2015

## Source identity

- Gazette: `BGBl. I Nr. 116/2015`, ausgegeben am 14. August 2015 (NR: GP XXV RV 685 AB 749 S. 83; BR: 9401 AB 9413; CELEX 32014L0107)
- Structure: Art. 1 BWG amendment; Art. 2 Kontenregister- und Konteneinschaugesetz – KontRegG; Art. 3 Kapitalabfluss-Meldegesetz; Art. 4 Gemeinsamer Meldestandard-Gesetz – GMSG; Art. 5 EU-AHG amendment; Art. 6 Amtshilfe-Durchführungsgesetz amendment.
- Companion act of the same day: Steuerreformgesetz 2015/2016, `BGBl. I Nr. 118/2015` (RV 684), which carried the tax-rate reform; it was not examined for this record.

## GMSG entry into force

The gazette text of § 117 GMSG reads "Dieses Bundesgesetz tritt mit xx. xx 20xx in Kraft" — an unfilled placeholder. Art. 8 Abgabenänderungsgesetz 2015, `BGBl. I Nr. 163/2015` (ausgegeben 28. Dezember 2015), replaced it: "Dieses Bundesgesetz tritt mit 1. Jänner 2016 in Kraft." (https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/BgblAuth/BGBLA_2015_I_163/BGBLA_2015_I_163.html). The current consolidated § 117(1) GMSG shows 1 January 2016.

Key dates in the Stammfassung: "bestehendes Konto" = account held on 30 September 2016 (§ 79); "Neukonto" = opened on or after 1 October 2016 (§ 80); review of high-value pre-existing individual accounts by 31 December 2017 and lower-value by 31 December 2018 (§ 27). § 112(1): the BMF transmits within nine months after the calendar year; § 112(2): information relates to periods from 1 January 2017, except that new accounts already capture 1 October–31 December 2016. This follows the Austria-specific deferral in Art. 2(2) and Annex I Section X of Directive 2014/107/EU (first exchange by 30 September 2018 instead of 2017). See `2026-09-30-at-ab-749-xxv-gmsg-first-exchange-dates.md` for the stated exchange dates.

## KontRegG

Creates the central account register at the BMF and the Konteneinschau procedure. The Stammfassung contains no general entry-into-force clause (§ 17 is Vollziehung); under Art. 49 B-VG it therefore took effect after publication (inference, not a stated date). The register's operational start was fixed by regulation: see `2026-09-30-at-kontreg-dv-bgbl-ii-92-2016.md`. Initial data are as of 1 March 2015. Current consolidated text: `2026-08-27-austria-kontregg-current.md`.

## § 38 BWG (Bankgeheimnis)

Art. 1 rewrote § 38(2) Z 1 (criminal and intentional fiscal-offence proceedings; in force 1 January 2016) and added exceptions Z 10 (automatic exchange under the GMSG), Z 11 (information requests by federal tax authorities under § 8 KontRegG), Z 12 (reporting to and information from the Kontenregister under §§ 3, 4 KontRegG) and Z 13 (reporting under §§ 3, 5 Kapitalabfluss-Meldegesetz). § 107(89) BWG, marked "Verfassungsbestimmung", makes Z 11 and 12 applicable to periods from 1 March 2015. No separate date was given for Z 10 and Z 13.

## Relevance and limits

This is the Austrian legal break with the withholding-plus-bank-secrecy model: DAC2 exchange, a domestic account register and tax-authority account inspection arrived in one act. The Kapitalabfluss-Meldegesetz and the Amtshilfe-Durchführungsgesetz changes were not analysed. The placeholder in § 117 is a documented drafting defect cured before the intended start date; it does not by itself show that the GMSG applied late.
