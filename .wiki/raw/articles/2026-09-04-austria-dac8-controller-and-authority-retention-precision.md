# Austrian DAC8 controller designation and authority-copy retention precision

DAC Article 25(3), as amended for DAC8, lists Reporting Crypto-Asset Service Providers and Member-State competent authorities as controllers acting alone or jointly. Current EU-AHG section 19(2) expressly lists reporting financial institutions, EU-MPfG intermediaries, DPMG platform operators and the Austrian competent authority, but not reporting crypto-asset service providers.

The omission is a textual transposition and precision question. It does not remove controller status that follows from GDPR Article 4(7), the provider's actual role or a sufficiently clear Union obligation. It does not by itself invalidate provider processing. Its significance is whether Austrian law identifies controller allocation, joint-control relationships and rights channels with the precision required for the concrete processing.

Krypto-MPfG section 20(4) imposes a ten-year deletion date on each reporting crypto-asset service provider for information transmitted under that Act. It does not impose that date on the competent tax authority's originating copy. DAC Article 25(5) separately requires every controller to retain information no longer than necessary and in accordance with domestic limitation rules.

No conclusion should be drawn that the BMF copy has no lawful retention rule. The correct open question is which Austrian provision governs that copy, whether it provides a maximum or periodic necessity review, and how it interacts with the BAO, EU-AHG, archival rules, pending proceedings and DAC Article 25(5). Section 20(4) cannot answer that question.
