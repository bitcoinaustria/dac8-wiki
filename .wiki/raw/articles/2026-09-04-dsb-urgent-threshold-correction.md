# Correction: DSB interim-protection threshold

This record corrects the description of a "weighty" or "serious" infringement in the immutable August 27 extraction of DSB 2024-0.215.259. The official decision was reread on September 4, 2026.

Section D.1, paragraph beginning "Die Erlassung eines Mandatsbescheides erfordert einerseits", distinguishes two cumulative requirements: substantive unlawfulness of processing, for which a reasoned suspicion supported by factual indications suffices, and substantial immediate danger to protected confidentiality interests, meaning danger in delay. The decision does not add an independent seriousness threshold to the suspected infringement. General apprehension, speculation and hypothetical events remain insufficient to establish the concrete danger.

[Section 25(1) DSG](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10001597&Artikel=2&Paragraf=25) permits the DSB to proceed under section 22(4) if a complainant credibly demonstrates substantial impairment of protected confidentiality interests. Its text does not mandate an immediate prohibition or contain a separate requirement to act without delay.

[Section 22(4) DSG](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10001597&Artikel=2&Paragraf=22) permits all or part of processing to be prohibited by a section 57(1) AVG decision in the stated urgent circumstances. Neither provision creates an automatic stay. The DSB decision recognises a right to a decision on an application, not an automatic entitlement to the prohibition requested.
