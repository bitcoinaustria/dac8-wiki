# CJEU Unibet: effective incidental remedies and interim protection

In Case C-432/05, *Unibet*, the CJEU held that EU law does not generally require a free-standing action whose principal object is review of national law's compatibility with EU law when the claimant has effective incidental routes. The national system is assessed as a whole under equivalence and effectiveness.

Where an admissible domestic action seeks to protect EU-law rights, however, the court must be able to grant interim relief when that is necessary to ensure the full effectiveness of the eventual judgment. National criteria govern subject to EU-law requirements.

Relevant passages: paragraphs 38-44, 54-65 and 72-83.

The decision is directly adverse to the claimed procedural exclusivity of an Article 140 Individualantrag. It does not prove that every Austrian alternative is timely or effective in a concrete DAC8 case.
