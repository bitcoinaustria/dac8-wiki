# CJEU C-378/17: administrative bodies must disapply conflicting national law

- Judgment: 4 December 2018
- ECLI: `ECLI:EU:C:2018:979`

Paragraphs 35–39 and 47–52 apply the primacy principle beyond courts. A national body established to enforce EU law in a particular field must be able, within its competence, to set aside a conflicting national rule. It cannot be required to wait for the legislature or a constitutional court to remove that rule.

For the Austrian DSB route, the judgment supports concrete disapplication of Krypto-MPfG provisions that conflict with a directly effective rule of EU law within the DSB's data-protection competence. It does not empower the DSB to annul the statute generally or declare DAC8 invalid.
