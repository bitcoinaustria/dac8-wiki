# FATCA: HIRE Act, Public Law 111-147

## Verified facts

Public Law 111-147, 124 Stat. 71, dated **18 March 2010**, is the Hiring Incentives to Restore Employment Act. Title V ("Offset provisions"), Subtitle A, is headed "Foreign Account Tax Compliance". It added the FATCA reporting and withholding regime to the Internal Revenue Code. The OECD's CRS second edition states that the CRS "draws extensively on the intergovernmental approach to implementing FATCA".

## Relevance and limits

FATCA is unilateral US law enforced through withholding and intergovernmental agreements, not a multilateral standard. It is the historical starting point of the modern AEOI timeline. The United States has not adopted the CRS. On the Global Forum CARF list it is committed to first CARF exchanges by 2029 (`2026-09-30-oecd-carf-commitments-list-september-2026.md`). A related CJEU reference on FATCA data transfers is in `2026-09-10-cjeu-c804-25-fatca-reference.md`.
