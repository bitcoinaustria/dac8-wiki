# DAC8.com: targeted information requests alternative

The page identifies French Tax Procedure Code Article L.81 and following as an existing power to request third-party information about an identified taxpayer. It argues that this distributed, request-based approach is less intrusive than automatic mass reporting.

## Source boundary

The existence of targeted powers is verifiable. Equivalence is not. Automatic reporting is intended to reveal unknown cross-border assets and select cases for investigation; a targeted request ordinarily presupposes an identified target or lead. The Austrian VfGH accepted that discovery and selection function in its DAC2 judgment.

The alternative is therefore a serious proportionality comparator, not a demonstrated one-for-one substitute.
