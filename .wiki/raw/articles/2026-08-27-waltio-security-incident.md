# Waltio security-incident disclosure

Waltio states that the incident exposed email addresses, gain or loss data, and 31 December 2024 balances. It states that transaction histories, wallet addresses, identity documents, postal addresses, bank data, private keys, and transfer capability were not exposed.

This provider statement confirms that crypto-tax datasets are attractive breach targets but materially narrows DAC8.com's identity-plus-wallet-history analogy. A direct causal link to later kidnappings was not independently established in this research pass.
