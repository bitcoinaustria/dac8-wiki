# European Commission infringement complaints: public enforcement, not individual redress

The Commission may identify an EU-law infringement through a citizen, business or stakeholder complaint. It may send a formal-notice letter, then a reasoned opinion, and may refer the Member State to the CJEU. The response periods at the first two formal stages are usually two months, but those are state-response periods, not a completion deadline for the complainant.

The Commission's linked national-redress guidance states that only national courts can annul a national decision or award individual compensation. A Commission complaint does not suspend national limitation or appeal periods and provides no individual interim order.
