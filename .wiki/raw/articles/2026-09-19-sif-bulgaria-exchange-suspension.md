# Switzerland suspended automatic exchange with Bulgaria

Selected source digest. The Federal Council's announcement of 20 September 2019 links suspension to the Bulgarian tax administration's July security incident. Switzerland withheld the scheduled transmission of financial-account information for 2018 already received from its institutions. Bulgaria confirmed that exchanged information concerning residents of both countries was affected. Resumption depended on corrective measures and confirmation by the Global Forum.

This was CRS exchange under the Swiss–EU agreement, not DAC8. The release establishes neither leaked crypto holdings nor physical attacks. It is evidence both of confidentiality failure and an operational response. It does not establish that all collection or domestic storage stopped. Current exchange status must be checked separately.
