# VfGH G 52/2024: social egg freezing

The applicant supplied a medical finding showing that no statutory medical indication existed and sworn declarations from herself and the treating doctor proving their concrete intention to contract. A criminal case against the doctor and a civil claim to force a forbidden contract were not reasonable detours.

The court heard experts and assessed alternatives under Article 8 ECHR. For a DAC8 user, the transferable method is to prove an actual provider relationship, actual processing, and exact individual consequences—not merely an intention to use crypto someday.
