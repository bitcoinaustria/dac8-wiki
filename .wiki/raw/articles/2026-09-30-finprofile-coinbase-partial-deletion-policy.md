# Coinbase partial deletion policy

Undated first-party FAQ, section Request Deletion, retrieved 30 September 2026.

The policy permits requests for some or all data, but says regulatory-retention records remain until requirements are met. Marketing and communication data can be deleted without losing access to services.

This directly distinguishes optional and mandatory records. It is not independently observed execution, an Austrian field-specific legal determination or evidence that all CASPs follow the same process. A blanket claim that every deletion request must be refused goes beyond the necessary statutory core.
