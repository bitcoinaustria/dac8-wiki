# Krypto-MPfG WFA: revenue estimate

Supplement to the [existing WFA record](2026-08-27-austria-krypto-mpfg-wfa-rv306.md), which covers compliance costs only. Parliamentary PDF, 306 der Beilagen XXVIII. GP, Vorblatt und WFA, 22 pages; retrieved 20 October 2026. SHA-256 `aa62dd3c8f95fd9a6854bfbdf8e06be14d60e412bb9ed53eda05c11963913633`, identical to `legal-docs/supporting/at-krypto-mpfg-wfa-rv-306.pdf`.

## Figures

Page 2 of 22, "Finanzielle Auswirkungen pro Maßnahme" (EUR thousand): "Umsetzung der Meldepflicht von Kryptowerten" 0 (2025), 5,000 (2026), 35,000 (2027, 2028, 2029). The "BBKG 2025 Teil Daten" as a whole is expected to raise about EUR 110 million to 2029.

## Stated basis (page 17 of 22)

The estimate starts from the Commission impact assessment of 8 December 2022 (about EUR 1.7 billion potential EU tax on realised crypto gains in 2020 at an assumed 25 percent rate). It notes that national KESt withholding on crypto already exists since the Ökosoziales Steuerreformgesetz 2022. It then expects additional revenue „aufgrund der zukünftigen Meldungen über ausländische Kryptowerte“: „ab dem Jahr 2027 rund 35 Millionen Euro an zusätzlichem Steueraufkommen“. For 2026 it expects about EUR 5 million because some taxpayers holding crypto abroad for avoidance reasons will move it back to domestic providers, „auch aufgrund des Vorteils des automatischen KESt-Abzugs“. It flags high uncertainty because of price volatility.

## Relevance and limits

The government attributes the expected yield to information about foreign-held crypto-assets and to relocation into domestic withholding, not to reports about Austrian residents at domestic providers that already withhold KESt. That supports, as a strategic inference only, the question why the domestic-user layer is needed for the stated revenue aim.

Limits: the WFA does not say that domestic-user reports are useless. DAC8 requires them (see [domestic scope record](2026-10-20-dac8-domestic-scope-versus-gmsg.md)), so Austria could not drop them without EU-law change. The figure is a forecast, not measured revenue; 1948/AB records EUR 33,839,499.66 crypto KESt withheld in 2024, which is a different quantity (withheld tax, not additional DAC8 yield).
