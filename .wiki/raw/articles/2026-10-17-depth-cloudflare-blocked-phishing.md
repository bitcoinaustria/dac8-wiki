# Cloudflare2022: disclosed credentials without system compromise

Published9August2022; incident20July. Locators “Targeted Text Messages”, “Protected Even If Not Perfect”, “Our Response” and timeline.

At least76 employees were targeted; three entered credentials. Required origin-bound hardware keys blocked subsequent login and Cloudflare reported no system compromise. Employee reporting and response supported containment. Telephone-number acquisition was unknown. Endpoint-protection claims about a stage nobody reached are counterfactual. This is vendor reporting, not a randomized comparison, independently measured loss prevention or proof that leaked data supplied targets.

Mechanical credibility 0; independently assessed. Historical and vendor-primary records are accepted for their unique bounded incident/implementation evidence, not population rates. No private dataset or victim record was collected.
