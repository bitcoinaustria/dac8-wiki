# Krypto-MPfG retention gap for non-reportable users

## Rule captured

§ 20 Abs. 1 requires providers to determine, record, store and process the reportable information for all customers in respect of all transactions and crypto-assets, irrespective of reportable-user status under § 7 Abs. 4.

§ 20 Abs. 4 orders deletion of "die gemäß diesem Bundesgesetz übermittelten Informationen" ten years after the reporting period.

§ 39 requires documentation of "die zur Durchführung der in den Hauptstücken 3 bis 7 festgelegten Meldepflichten und Verfahren zur Erfüllung der Sorgfaltspflichten unternommenen Schritte" and retention of those documents for ten years, followed by deletion.

§ 19 Abs. 3 limits the reporting duty to information concerning participating states under § 14 Abs. 1.

## Consequence captured

For a user tax-resident only outside the participating states nothing is ever transmitted. § 20 Abs. 4 therefore never triggers. § 39 reaches the record of steps taken, not the § 18 dataset generated under § 20 Abs. 1. No provision sets an outer limit for that dataset.

## Research use

This is a self-contained storage-limitation ground under Article 5(1)(e) GDPR that does not depend on winning the wider necessity argument, and it can be raised before the Datenschutzbehörde without any court. It also sharpens the comparison with § 14 Abs. 2 KStTG, which orders deletion of exactly the corresponding German record categories after the retention period.

The ground still requires the factual gate: the complainant must show that the provider in fact generates the dataset for them.
