# CJEU C-817/19 Ligue des droits humains on purpose-bounded databases

In C-817/19, judgment of 21 June 2022, the Grand Chamber interpreted the PNR Directive in light of Charter Articles 7, 8 and 52(1). Paragraphs 232 to 237 hold that the PNR Directive's listed purposes are exhaustive and preclude other purposes. Paragraph 235 states that PNR data may not be held in one database consultable both for the Directive's purposes and for other purposes, because that creates a risk of unauthorised use.

The holding does not establish that every multi-purpose database is unlawful. Its force depends on the source regime's purpose architecture. DAC Article 16 expressly permits several tax, VAT, indirect-tax, customs, AML/CFT, recovery, social-security and tax-penalty uses and conditionally permits further purposes. C-817/19 therefore supports technical and organisational enforcement of the boundary between authorised and unauthorised purposes; it cannot be used to rewrite DAC as tax-only.

The judgment also subjects systematic PNR processing, database comparison and retention to strict necessity and safeguards. PNR concerns a continuous travel-surveillance system for security purposes, not annual tax aggregates. Any analogy must account for the different fields, purposes, access rules, profiling potential and remedies.
