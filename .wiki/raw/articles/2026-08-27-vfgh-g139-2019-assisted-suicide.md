# VfGH G 139/2019: assistance in suicide

The four applicants included seriously ill persons and a doctor. Their application set out medical conditions, mobility, intended conduct, professional consequences, supporting material, and precise criminal provisions.

The court annulled the words criminalising assistance in suicide but rejected the section 77 challenge because its perimeter was too narrow. The case is both a merits model and a warning that the requested deletion must actually remove the alleged defect.
