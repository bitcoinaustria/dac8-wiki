# Article 263 TFEU: individual direct-action standing and time limit

A natural or legal person may challenge an EU act addressed to them, an act of direct and individual concern, or a regulatory act of direct concern that does not entail implementing measures. The action must be instituted within two months of publication, notification or, in the absence of those events, knowledge of the act.

For an Austrian private user on 27 August 2026, the publication periods for Directive (EU) 2023/2226 and the identified 2025 implementing regulation have elapsed. The directive is a legislative act and the user-specific standing conditions are not established. Article 263 does not challenge Austrian implementing law. This leaves Article 267 in a timely national case as the realistic EU-validity mechanism.
