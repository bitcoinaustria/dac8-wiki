# CJEU C-590/22, PS

Judgment of 20 June 2024, ECLI:EU:C:2024:536. Official EUR-Lex full judgment inspected; source digest. The dispute concerned a tax consultancy's erroneous disclosure of a tax return (paragraph 2).

## Holdings

- Paragraphs 22–28: infringement, damage and causation are cumulative. No minimum seriousness threshold applies, but the infringement alone is insufficient.
- Paragraphs 29–36: inability to establish whether third parties learned the data does not preclude compensation for fear of disclosure if that fear and its negative consequences are duly proved. Brief loss of control can qualify; a bare allegation of fear cannot.
- Paragraphs 37–45: compensation is not calculated using administrative-fine criteria or a punitive function.

## Use and limits

Supports preserving claimant-specific evidence of anxiety and consequences where third-party knowledge remains uncertain. Unlike C-687/21's factual premise, non-reading was not established. This is post-infringement compensation authority, not an automatic preventive injunction, reporting exception or prerequisite for security obligations.
