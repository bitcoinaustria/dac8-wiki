# GAO review of BSA reports 2020

More than 72 percent of surveyed employees across six US federal law-enforcement agencies reported using BSA reports during 2015–2018, often to identify new subjects or expand investigations. This supports operational usefulness, not a causal reduction in crime.

GAO also obtained 2018 direct BSA-cost data from eleven banks, ranging from about USD 14,000 to USD 21 million. At each of the three smallest banks direct costs were about two percent of operating expenses; at each of the three largest they were below one percent. CDD and reporting averaged 29 and 28 percent of sampled direct costs. The sample was not nationally representative and must not be extrapolated nationally or globally.
