# DAC security, controller and breach-response architecture

Article 21 of the consolidated Directive allocates CCN-network development and security to the Commission and national-system development and security to Member States. Persons accredited by the Commission Security Accreditation Authority may access exchanged information only as necessary for maintenance and development of the CCN and central directory.

For DAC8 information, Article 21(5) requires a secure central directory by 31 December 2026. A Member State authority may access only information concerning Reportable Users and Reportable Persons resident in that Member State. The Commission's own access is limited by Article 8ad(5) and to statistics.

Article 25(3) treats reporting providers and Member-State competent authorities as controllers acting alone or jointly. The Commission processes personal data on their behalf, subject to a processor contract. Article 25(4) requires pre-reporting information early enough for the person to exercise data-protection rights. Article 25(5) retains data only as long as necessary for the Directive's purposes and in accordance with each controller's domestic limitation rules.

Article 21(2) requires an affected Reportable Person to be notified of a security breach likely adversely to affect personal-data protection or privacy. Article 25(6) requires notification to the Commission, allows immediate suspension of exchanges with an affected Member State, requires investigation, containment and remediation, and allows CCN access to be suspended. Article 25(7) requires practical breach-management and controller-processor arrangements aligned with recognised good practice.

These provisions are material safeguards and adverse to any claim that DAC8 has no security architecture. They do not establish how a particular provider, Austrian authority or recipient authority implements the rules, whether the central directory is operational as designed, or whether field-level access and bulk export are necessary and proportionate.

The text uses both `Reportable Person` and, for DAC8 directory access, `Reportable User`. Whether the Article 21(2) individual-notification wording covers every DAC8 Reportable User and how Austria implements the duty require separate legal verification before pleading a transposition gap.
