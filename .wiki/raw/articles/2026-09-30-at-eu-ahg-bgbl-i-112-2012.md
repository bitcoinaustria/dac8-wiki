# Austria: EU-Amtshilfegesetz (EU-AHG)

## Source identity

- Gazette: `BGBl. I Nr. 112/2012`, Abgabenänderungsgesetz 2012 – AbgÄG 2012, ausgegeben am 14. Dezember 2012 (NR: GP XXIV RV 1960 AB 1977; CELEX incl. 32011L0016)
- The EU-AHG is Article 1 of the omnibus act.
- Entry into force (§ 22(1)): 1 January 2013; "Zugleich tritt das EG-Amtshilfegesetz – EG-AHG, BGBl. Nr. 657/1994, außer Kraft. § 7 ist erstmals ab dem 1. Jänner 2015 anzuwenden." § 22(2) extends the Act to requests made before 1 January 2013 and still pending.

## Relevance and limits

Anchors the DAC1 step and shows the EU-AHG as the hub that the later sector statutes (GMSG, VPDG, EU-MPfG, DPMG, Krypto-MPfG, MinBestG) plug into. The EU-AHG changes by BGBl. I Nr. 77/2016 carry no separate entry into force in § 22; see the EU-AbgÄG 2016 record. DAC5 was not implemented through the EU-AHG (see the WiEReG record).
