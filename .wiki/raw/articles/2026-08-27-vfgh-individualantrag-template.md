# VfGH official Individualantrag form

The form complements the court's recommendations. It separates the applicant and representation data, the exact legal basis and version, current and direct effect, the absence of another reasonable route, the constitutional objections, the precise main and eventual requests, evidence, fees, and costs.

The form is a pleading aid, not a substitute for applicant-specific facts or a complete initiating brief.
