## Non-crypto content of DAC8

- **DAC2 / CRS alignment:** recital 26 adopts the OECD's CRS amendments (Part II of the package the OECD approved on 26 August 2022), including e-money and central bank digital currency provisions. For example, a Depository Institution now includes an entity that holds E-money or CBDC for customers.
- **DAC1:** non-custodial dividends added as an income category (recital 28). Member States had to notify at least five DAC1 categories before 1 January 2026, for taxable periods from 1 January 2026.
- **DAC3:** advance cross-border rulings for individuals, issued, amended or renewed after 1 January 2026. The trigger is a transaction amount above EUR 1,500,000 or a ruling on whether a person is tax-resident in the issuing Member State.
- **TIN:** new Article 27c on reporting and communicating TINs, in stages from 2026 to 2030.
- **Penalties:** Article 25a (covering Articles 8aa to 8ad) requires only "effective, proportionate and dissuasive" penalties. COM(2022) 707 had proposed minimum pecuniary penalties for serious infringements, such as at least EUR 50,000 or EUR 150,000 by turnover and EUR 20,000 for natural persons. The Council did not keep them.
- Also: DAC6 legal-privilege alignment with C-694/20; use of DAC information for restrictive-measures enforcement; the Article 27 evaluation timing.

## Domestic scope (recital 13)

Recital 13: the reporting obligation "should cover both cross-border and domestic transactions" for effectiveness, internal-market functioning, a level playing field and non-discrimination. So RCASPs report on users resident in their own Member State as well. This goes beyond the CRS model, which is limited to non-resident account holders.
