# L.B. v Hungary

The ECtHR Grand Chamber held that systematic online publication of tax debtors' names, home addresses, tax identification numbers, and tax information violated Article 8 ECHR. The Court stressed the lack of a sufficiently tailored proportionality assessment and the Internet's worldwide reach, searchability, reuse, and misuse risks.

The case is powerful against public or broadly searchable wealth and tax registers. DAC8 exchange is confidential, so the holding does not automatically invalidate DAC8 or a restricted authority register.
