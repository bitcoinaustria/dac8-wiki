# CJEU C-655/23: GDPR preventive-injunction limit

In *Quirin Privatbank*, the CJEU held that the GDPR does not itself require Member States to create a special preventive action prohibiting future unlawful repetition where the data subject does not request erasure. National law remains free to provide such a remedy.

The judgment limits any claim that Article 79 automatically guarantees a preventive DAC8 injunction. It does not eliminate the Article 79 judicial route, supervisory relief under Articles 77 and 58, interim judicial protection required by EU effectiveness principles in an admissible case, or remedies supplied by Austrian law.

Relevant passages: paragraphs 41-52.
