# VfGH E 3249/2016

Decision of 12 December 2017.

For an intimate paper file, the VfGH rejected generic reliance on possible future proceedings or the authority's defence of past conduct as a substitute for a present and individualised necessity assessment of continued retention.

The decision is strongest after an actual controller refuses deletion, but its intimate paper-file facts limit the analogy to routine electronic DAC8 data. It does not remove DAC8's minimum periods or decide the Krypto-MPfG facially.
