# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1140 on MiCA record keeping

The Regulation supplements MiCA Article 68(9). Article 7 and Annex Section 3 Table 3 require transaction records that can identify a natural-person buyer or seller by national identifier, name and date of birth. Field 3 may contain the transaction hash or another DLT-generated identifier where relevant.

Annex Section 4 Table 4 specifies on-chain fields including transaction hash, wallet addresses, smart-contract address, block timestamp, quantity, network fee and `To` and `From` identifiers. Field 13 contains the firm's transaction-record number so that the on-chain record can be linked to the off-chain transaction record.

The scope is the MiCA services, activities, orders and transactions covered by the Regulation. It does not establish that every CASP transfer produces every Table 3 or Table 4 field, that the Austrian tax authority routinely receives those records, or that the DAC8 report contains wallet addresses or transaction hashes.
