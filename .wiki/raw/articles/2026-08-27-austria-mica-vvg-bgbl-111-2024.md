# Austrian MiCA-VVG, BGBl. I No. 111/2024

The MiCA-VVG makes the FMA the Austrian competent authority, supplies supervisory and enforcement powers, creates administrative offences for unauthorised activity, and provides an appeal to the Federal Administrative Court.

Section 23 shortened Austria's legacy-provider transition to 31 December 2025 or the earlier authorisation decision. The act implements MiCA supervision; it does not create the separate Krypto-MPfG tax-processing purpose.
