# Trezor ShipMonk incident: historical records despite deletion assurances

Report dated 13 August 2026; controlling update 4 September. Locators: update and FAQs on older orders, fields and totals.

Trezor reports 80,689 affected customers overall. Initially 11,742 had full and 1,947 partial exposure; approximately 67,000 historical US customers were added later. Do not add the overall total to components. Full fields included name, email, phone, shipping address and order number; parcel contents were excluded.

The company says 2019–2021 records survived despite repeated written deletion confirmations. This qualifies the original ninety-day containment assertion. Phishing and physical-security consequences are warnings, not demonstrated losses or assaults. Vendor policy and deletion verification are distinct. Mechanical score 1; unique first-party retention-failure account, not an independent audit.
