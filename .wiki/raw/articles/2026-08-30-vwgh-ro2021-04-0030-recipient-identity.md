# VwGH Ro 2021/04/0030

Decision of 6 March 2024.

The VwGH held that, where possible, GDPR Article 15 permits identification of actual recipients because that information enables the effective exercise of Articles 16–18, 21 and 79 rights.

The decision supports a concrete DAC8 recipient and correction-propagation claim. It does not remove a lawful and proportionate statutory restriction under GDPR Article 23 or EU-AHG section 19.
