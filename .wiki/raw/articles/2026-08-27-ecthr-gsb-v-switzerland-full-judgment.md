# ECtHR G.S.B. v Switzerland: targeted tax-information transmission

In application 28601/11, judgment of 22 December 2015, the Court accepted the economic and tax-cooperation aim, treated the purely financial bank data as less intimate information allowing a wider margin, and relied on the applicable data safeguards and genuine procedural review. It found no Article 8 violation.

The case concerned targeted transmission under the Swiss-US mutual-assistance framework. It did not adjudicate FATCA, CRS, DAC2 or DAC8 periodic automatic exchange. It is adverse on the proposition that financial-data transmission with adequate legal and procedural safeguards is inherently disproportionate, but it cannot be promoted as a holding validating all automatic exchange.

Relevant paragraphs: 83-84, 89-90 and 93-98.
