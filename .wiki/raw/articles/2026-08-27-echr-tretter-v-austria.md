# ECtHR Tretter and Others v Austria

The Court's 29 September 2020 disposition is an inadmissibility decision, not a judgment on the merits. It records that the challenged SPG provisions did not themselves create a general retention duty and treats the Austrian access, deletion, complaint, and review framework as relevant to effective-remedy analysis.

It is an adverse comparator to a claim that a person may bypass available Austrian data-protection procedures merely because statutory data powers exist.
