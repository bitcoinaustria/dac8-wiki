# CJEU C-26/22 and C-64/22 SCHUFA: diligent DPA inquiry and full judicial review

- Judgment: 7 December 2023
- ECLI: `ECLI:EU:C:2023:958`

Paragraphs 52, 55–56 and 67–70 require the supervisory authority to handle a complaint with all due diligence. Judicial review under Article 78 GDPR must be full and effective as to law and fact. Where the authority has discretion in choosing an Article 58 measure, the court reviews whether the authority remained within the limits of that discretion.

The ruling supports precise DSB evidence requests and substantive BVwG review. It does not remove the complainant's need to identify the concrete processing, alleged infringement, timing and requested relief.
