# Austria: EG-Amtshilfegesetz, BGBl. Nr. 657/1994

## Source identity

- Act: Bundesgesetz zur Durchführung der Richtlinie der Europäischen Gemeinschaften über die gegenseitige Amtshilfe im Bereich der direkten Steuern, bestimmter Verbrauchsteuern und der Steuern auf Versicherungsprämien (EG-Amtshilfegesetz – EG-AHG)
- Stammfassung: `BGBl. Nr. 657/1994` (NR: GP XVIII RV 1705 AB 1820)
- Entry into force (§ 6): "gleichzeitig mit dem Inkrafttreten des Vertrages über den Beitritt Österreichs zur Europäischen Union", i.e. 1 January 1995; RIS lists `BGBl. Nr. 50/1995` as the "K über Idat" (announcement of the entry-into-force date).
- Repealed: by § 22(1) EU-AHG with effect from 1 January 2013 (see `2026-09-30-at-eu-ahg-bgbl-i-112-2012.md`).

## Content

§ 1(1) applies the Act to mutual assistance among EU Member States for direct taxes, insurance-premium taxes and certain excise duties "zur Durchführung der EG-Amtshilferichtlinie (Richtlinie Nr. 77/799/EWG ...)", citing the Accession Act of Austria, Finland and Sweden (OJ C 241, 29.8.1994) among the amending instruments. The 2004 version shown in RIS also reflects Directive 2003/93/EC (applicable from 1 January 2004) and the BGBl. I Nr. 33/2004 amendment that accompanied the EU-QuStG.

## Relevance and limits

Confirms that 77/799/EEC became operative for Austria with accession on 1 January 1995 through a dedicated federal statute. The publication (Ausgabe) date of BGBl. Nr. 657/1994 was not retrieved in this pass; only the consolidated RIS version as of 30 December 2004 was read.
