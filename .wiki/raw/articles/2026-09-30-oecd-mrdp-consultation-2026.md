# OECD consultation on the Model Reporting Rules for Digital Platforms (2026)

## Content

The consultation ran from 15 June to 14 August 2026. It proposes targeted amendments to the Model Rules for Reporting by Platform Operators (approved in 2020, with the 2021 optional module for sales of goods and transport rental):

- remove the fewer-than-30-transactions condition for the sale-of-goods seller exclusion and raise the monetary threshold from EUR 2,000 to EUR 3,000;
- clarify "Platform" and "Platform Operator";
- limit transactional reporting where the seller is itself a Reporting Platform Operator;
- introduce a Related Entity concept to exclude certain intra-group arrangements.

## Relevance and limits

This is an OECD consultation, not an adopted standard. DAC7 changes in the EU would only come through the pending DAC recast (COM(2026) 308, Annex V). That proposal already includes the same EUR 3,000 threshold and removes the activity threshold. The consultation does not concern CARF or DAC8.
