# EDPB2024: financial centralisation and distributed alternatives

Statement2/2024, adopted23May2024; §§11–12 printedpp.3–5; §§14–17 pp.5–6.

For the then-proposed PSR antifraud platform, EDPB identifies higher interference and security risk than distributed PSP processing. It calls for demonstrated necessity/proportionality, specified categories/triggers, qualified access, purpose limits, justified retention and narrow sharing. It rejects treating another provider's flag alone as sufficient for automatic blocking.

This is a nonbinding institutional position on proposals, not a DAC8 judgment, verification of current PSR/FIDA status, or a benchmark proving equal utility of decentralisation. Mechanical credibility2: recent institutional primary.
