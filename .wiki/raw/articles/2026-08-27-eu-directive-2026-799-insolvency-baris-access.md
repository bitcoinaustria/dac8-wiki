# Directive (EU) 2026/799

Articles 14 to 17 create a future case-specific mechanism under which designated courts or administrative authorities can access BARIS for insolvency asset tracing following a request by an insolvency practitioner. The route is subject to designation, purpose, staff, security, and logging controls.

This is an enacted future access path. It does not make BARIS public or add wallet addresses, balances, holdings, or transaction histories to BARIS.
