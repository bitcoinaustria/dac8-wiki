# Austrian crypto KESt: withholding scope

Primary texts retrieved from RIS and the BMF on 20 October 2026. Read with [realization and transitional dates](2026-10-19-austrian-crypto-tax-boundaries.md) and the revenue baseline in [1948/AB](2026-09-29-atrisk-ab-1948-crypto-tax-revenue-data.md).

## Who withholds: § 95 Abs. 2 Z 3 EStG 1988

[RIS, § 95 EStG](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=95). For realised gains, lit. b names "der inländische Dienstleister". Eligible domestic service providers are crypto-asset service providers under § 2 Z 22 FM-GwG "mit Sitz, Wohnsitz oder Ort der Geschäftsleitung im Inland" and "die inländische Zweigstelle oder Betriebsstätte von ausländischen Anbietern". For current income, lit. a names the domestic debtor, otherwise the domestic service provider that credits the income.

## Since when: § 124b Z 384 lit. d EStG 1988

[RIS ELI](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P124b/NOR40272477): „Die Verpflichtung zum Kapitalertragsteuerabzug für Einkünfte aus Kryptowährungen gilt erstmals für Kapitalerträge, die nach dem 31. Dezember 2023 anfallen.“ Withholding was voluntary for 2022 and 2023.

## Swaps: § 27b Abs. 3 EStG 1988

[RIS, § 27b EStG](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004570&Paragraf=27b): „Der Tausch einer Kryptowährung gegen eine andere Kryptowährung stellt keine Realisierung dar“. Realisations are sales and exchanges for other assets or services, including legal tender.

## BMF guidance

[BMF, Steuerliche Behandlung von Kryptowährungen](https://www.bmf.gv.at/themen/steuern/sparen-veranlagen/steuerliche-behandlung-von-kryptowaehrungen.html), section "Kapitalertragsteuer": for income accruing after 31 December 2023, domestic debtors and service providers must withhold KESt, which then generally settles income tax (Endbesteuerung). From 2025, withholding agents must provide a tax report on request.

Sub-section "Beschränkte Steuerpflicht": current and realised crypto income under § 27b Abs. 2 and 3 is not subject to limited tax liability. „Sofern dem KESt-Abzugsverpflichteten bekannt ist, dass es sich um keinen unbeschränkt steuerpflichtigen Anleger handelt, kann in diesen Fällen der Kapitalertragsteuerabzug unterbleiben.“ Tax withheld anyway can be refunded under § 240 Abs. 3 BAO.

## Limits

- Withholding applies only through domestic providers and domestic branches. An Austrian resident using a foreign provider without an Austrian establishment is not covered and must declare the income.
- "Not subject to limited tax liability" refers to persons without Austrian Wohnsitz or habitual abode (general rule of § 1 EStG, not separately retrieved for this record). It is not identical to "tax resident abroad" in a treaty sense. The BMF says withholding "kann unterbleiben" where the provider knows the investor is not unlimited taxable; it does not say it never occurs.
- Withholding settles tax on covered income. It does not by itself make DAC8 reporting unnecessary for reconciliation, foreign-provider gaps, loss offset, opt-in regular assessment or holdings acquired before March 2021. Whether reports on Austrian residents at withholding domestic providers are necessary is a proportionality question, not a statutory conclusion.
- Not an individual tax assessment.
