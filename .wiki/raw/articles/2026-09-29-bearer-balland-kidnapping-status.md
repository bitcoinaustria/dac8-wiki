# Balland abduction: status and limits

Source digest. Two media reports:

- [franceinfo, 25 January 2025](https://www.franceinfo.fr/faits-divers/police/sequestration-de-david-balland-sept-personnes-mises-en-examen-pour-l-enlevement-du-cofondateur-d-une-entreprise-de-cryptomonnaies-et-de-son-epouse_7036124.html), relaying statements of the Paris prosecutor's office (JUNALCO, the national organised-crime unit).
- [France 3 Centre-Val de Loire, March 2026](https://france3-regions.franceinfo.fr/centre-val-de-loire/cher/vierzon/enlevement-et-torture-de-david-balland-un-suspect-arrete-en-espagne-3320439.html), relaying the Spanish Guardia Civil.

No prosecutor press release or court document was retrieved.

According to these reports, David Balland and his partner were abducted at their home near Vierzon (Cher) on 21 January 2025. He was freed on 22 January in a gendarmerie/GIGN operation and she on 23 January in Essonne. Seven people were placed under formal investigation ("mis en examen"). The charges include kidnapping or sequestration with torture or acts of barbarity and offences committed as an organised gang. The kidnappers demanded a large ransom in crypto, reported as €10 million. A video of a severed finger was sent to co-founder Éric Larchevêque. A further suspect was arrested in Spain on 20 March 2026. Search results also indicate two further persons charged in April 2025; those reports were not fetched.

Procedural status: judicial investigation (instruction) pending as of the latest source. No verdict; the persons charged are presumed innocent.

None of the fetched reports explains how the victims were selected or located. The link to the 2020 Ledger customer leak is only an association drawn by commentators; the company-founder profile and register data are alternative explanations. This case documents coercion at home and a demand for ransom paid in crypto. It does not establish a data-leak cause and has no DAC8 connection.
