# DOJ: official location access and armed attack

Source digest. US Attorney's Office, Central District of California, 17 January 2025, release 25-012. Locator: paragraphs beginning “For example, in late 2021” through “After securing the illegal warrant”.

The DOJ describes Saavedra obtaining a GPS warrant under false pretences, locating a person believed by Iza to possess valuable cryptocurrency and providing the address to Iza. In March 2022, Iza sent three armed individuals to force entry and steal a laptop. They fled after the victim fired a shot.

The account is attributed to filed court documents and plea agreements. At publication, both defendants had agreed to plead guilty; this release alone does not establish a completed plea or sentencing. The attack detail must not be attributed to the selected Saavedra factual-basis paragraphs, which describe location and surveillance. Neither source identifies tax-reporting data.
