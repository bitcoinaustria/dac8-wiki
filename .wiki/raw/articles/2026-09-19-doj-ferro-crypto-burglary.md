# Ferro sentencing and mixed digital/physical crime

Source digest. DOJ, 6 May 2026, updated 8 June 2026; case 24cr417. Locators: sentencing opening and paragraphs beginning “According to court documents” and “The conspiracy's operatives”.

Marlon Ferro received 78 months after a RICO-conspiracy guilty plea. The DOJ describes a group combining database hacking, victim identification, fraud calls and home burglary. Targets were believed to hold substantial crypto; physical theft supplemented unsuccessful remote methods.

This documents an integrated criminal enterprise, not a specific DAC8 leak-to-attack chain. The source does not establish which database identified each burglary victim. Hardware-wallet theft does not by itself establish universal access to funds. This ingestion relies on the official sentencing release, not independent inspection of the judgment.
