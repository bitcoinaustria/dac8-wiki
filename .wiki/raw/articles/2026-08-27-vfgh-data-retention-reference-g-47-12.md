# VfGH G 47/12 and G 59/12: data-retention preliminary reference

## Procedural facts

The joined Austrian proceedings included a provincial-government application and individual applications under Article 140 B-VG. One individual documented four active telephone or internet subscriber lines; more than eleven thousand others also asserted that provider-side retention directly affected their subscriber data.

On 28 November 2012, the VfGH stayed the proceedings and referred questions under Article 267 TFEU about the validity of the Data Retention Directive under Charter Articles 7, 8, and 11 and about Charter interpretation. The CJEU later invalidated that directive in the joined Digital Rights Ireland and Seitlinger cases.

## Significance and limit

This proves the institutional route can operate in Austrian norm-review proceedings initiated by an individual. It does not relax admissibility: the later VfGH decision accepted only the properly framed part of one individual application and rejected thousands of others or parts on procedural grounds.

The analogy is strongest where an applicant can document an existing covered service and automatic provider-side processing required by statute. Differences in purpose, data categories, safeguards, aggregation, retention, and tax administration must be assessed rather than assumed away.
