# Directive 2011/16/EU consolidated with DAC8 from 1 January 2026

## Source identity

- Base act: Council Directive 2011/16/EU
- Consolidation date: 1 January 2026
- CELEX: `02011L0016-20260101`
- Status: documentation aid; the amending acts and corrigenda remain the authentic legal sources

## Relevant content captured

The consolidated text contains Article 8ad and Annex VI as applicable from 1 January 2026. Annex VI Section IV C defines reportable transactions without a general monetary threshold; the USD 50,000 threshold concerns only the special reportable retail payment category. Section II B limits transaction reporting to reportable users, while Section III requires due diligence to identify user status.

The consolidation incorporates the 26 November 2025 corrigendum and identifies a distributed-ledger address by reference to Article 3(18) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1113.
