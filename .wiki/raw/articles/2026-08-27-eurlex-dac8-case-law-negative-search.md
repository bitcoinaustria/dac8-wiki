# EUR-Lex exact-citation DAC8 search: directive only, no case-law result

The official EUR-Lex quick search for the exact citation `2023/2226`, run on 27 August 2026, returned one result: Council Directive (EU) 2023/2226 itself. It returned no EU case-law document.

This is a dated negative search, not proof against unpublished, differently cited or not-yet-indexed proceedings. It supports using DAC2, DAC6, general data-protection, effective-remedy and interim-relief decisions as analogies rather than representing them as DAC8 holdings.
