# Haute-Garonne crypto extortion investigation

Source digest. Romain Bastet, Gendinfo, 11 May 2026. Locators: opening factual account and concluding procedural-status paragraph.

The Gendarmerie reports that armed offenders detained a person at home on 23 March 2026 and forced a crypto transfer worth approximately EUR 68,000. It places the case within a pattern targeting wallet holders whose personal data had apparently been hacked.

The source identifies neither a particular breached database nor the exposed fields or a forensic data-to-victim chain. It reports judicial investigation and pretrial detention, not convictions. Do not relabel this as a proven Waltio, DAC8 or tax-authority leak case.
