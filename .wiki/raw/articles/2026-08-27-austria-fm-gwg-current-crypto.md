# Current Austrian FM-GwG crypto duties

Section 6 requires risk-based customer and beneficial-owner identification and verification. Section 11a, effective from 1 January 2026, requires CASPs to assess AML/CFT and sanctions risks for transfers to or from self-hosted addresses and use at least one risk mitigation such as verification, additional origin/destination information, enhanced monitoring, or another suitable control.

Section 16 requires prompt FIU reporting of knowledge, suspicion, or reasonable grounds; section 20 prohibits tipping off. Section 21 currently retains CDD and transaction records for ten years, materially longer than the future AMLR five-year baseline plus case-specific extension. Sections 25, 31, and 34 give the FMA supervision, corrective, licence-withdrawal, and fine powers, including for TFR breaches.
