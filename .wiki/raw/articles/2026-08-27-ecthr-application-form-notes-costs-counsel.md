# ECtHR 2026 application notes: four months, counsel and court fees

The Court's 2026/1 notes state that a complete Rule 47-compliant application must be dispatched by post within four months of the final domestic decision. The application can initially be lodged without instructing a lawyer.

If the Court gives notice of the application to the respondent government, legal representation may become required; limited legal aid may then be available. The notes expressly state that the Court charges no court fee. They do not eliminate the applicant's own lawyer, translation, copying or postage costs.
