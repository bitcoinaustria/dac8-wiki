# Actual use of foreign account reports

BFG, 15 September 2022, RV/1300013/2020, PDF pp. 2–3 and 13, section II.1.

The court establishes an undisputed sequence: automatic foreign-account reports for 2017–2018 prompted a November 2019 enquiry about capital income and supporting records. Correction produced a paid EUR 33,356 tax claim for 2017.

This establishes actual person-linked use of multiple report years and tax utility in one case. It does not establish automated cross-provider fusion, PACC inputs, ten-year necessity or general causal effectiveness. Personal characteristics and identifying case details are unnecessary for this use and omitted.

Scoring: recognised institution +1, no recency point. Retained as a unique direct judicial fact record; the low mechanical tier is not a finding that the court's established sequence is unreliable.
