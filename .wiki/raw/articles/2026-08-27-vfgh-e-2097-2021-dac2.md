# VfGH E 2097/2021: DAC2 validity and Article 267 refusal

## Identity and route

- Decision: 9 March 2023
- File: E 2097/2021-13
- ECLI: ECLI:AT:VFGH:2023:E2097.2021
- Collection number: 20.597
- Route: actual data transfer, DSB complaint, BVwG dismissal, Article 144 B-VG complaint to the VfGH

This was not an Article 140 Individualantrag. The official judgment anonymises the claimant.

## Holding captured

The VfGH held that DAC2 Article 8(3a) expressly and fully determines the disputed data fields and that sections 112 and 113 GMSG implement them materially verbatim. It reasoned that invalidity of the underlying fully determinative EU provision would first have to come from the CJEU.

The court nevertheless found no reasoned doubt about DAC2's validity under Charter Articles 7 and 8 and therefore refused an Article 267 reference. It accepted prevention of tax evasion, efficient tax collection, aggressive-tax-planning control, and equal taxation as public-interest objectives. It treated the data as less revealing than communications and location metadata, accepted annual automatic exchange without a de minimis threshold, and relied on GDPR security duties.

The constitutional complaint was dismissed and transferred to the VwGH for any remaining ordinary-law review.

## Research significance

This is the closest Austrian precedent for the proposed DAC8 route. It proves that a concrete individual processing case can reach the VfGH and that the applicant cannot force a preliminary reference. It is controlling adverse context for any argument based only on suspicionless reporting, duplication, a small amount, abstract hacking risk, or analogy to communications-data retention.
