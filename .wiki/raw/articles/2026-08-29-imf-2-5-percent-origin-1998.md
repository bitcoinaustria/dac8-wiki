# IMF 1998 speech and the 2–5 percent range

IMF Managing Director Michel Camdessus described 2–5 percent of global GDP as a possible consensus range for the scale of money laundering while expressly declining to vouch for its accuracy.

The source establishes the origin and uncertainty of the number. It is not an empirical measurement for 1998 or a reusable estimate for later years.
