# OGH 6 Ob 53/25z: GDPR injunction question left open

In its decision of 3 July 2025, the OGH dismissed a revision appeal concerning an interim injunction against announced telephone recordings. The pleaded individual balancing issue did not establish an important legal question.

At paragraph 14 the court expressly left open whether, and under which conditions, a stand-alone injunction without an erasure request can be based on a GDPR infringement. It referred to the then-pending CJEU case C-655/23 and OGH 6 Ob 159/23k. The decision therefore does not establish an Austrian general preventive GDPR injunction and is adverse to treating such relief as automatic.
