# CARF milestones 2022–2026

## Verified dates

- **26 August 2022:** the OECD approves the "Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework and Amendments to the Common Reporting Standard". The date is stated in DAC8 recital 26 and in the recast recitals. The existing record `2026-08-29-oecd-carf-2022.md` gives "August 2022". The public release and delivery to the G20 in October 2022 were not re-verified here.
- **16 November 2022:** the G20 Bali Leaders' Declaration welcomes CARF and the CRS amendments.
- **8 June 2023:** the OECD publishes "International Standards for Automatic Exchange of Information in Tax Matters: Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework and 2023 update to the Common Reporting Standard". The package includes the CARF rules and commentary, the CARF MCAA and its commentary, and the amended CRS.
- **9–10 September 2023:** the G20 New Delhi Declaration calls for swift implementation and notes the aspiration to start exchanges by 2027.
- **10 November 2023:** Joint Statement on the Implementation of CARF by 48 jurisdictions, including Austria and most EU Member States, the United States, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Signatories intend to transpose CARF and activate agreements "in time for exchanges to commence by 2027", and to implement the CRS amendments on the same timeline. Eleven more adhered later (the OECD's 2025 update counts 58 adherents).
- **2024:** the Global Forum CARF Group builds the commitment process. The step-by-step guide was adopted on 9 November 2024.
- **CARF MCAA signatures:** the OECD signatory list (status 3 March 2026) shows the first signatures in October 2024 (21 October 2024 and 31 October 2024). Twenty-seven signatures are dated 26 November 2024, and signing continued into December 2025. Austria signed on 21 November 2024, Cyprus on 19 November 2024.

## Relevance and limits

Correction for timeline copy: a "CARF MCAA of November 2023" conflates the 10 November 2023 joint statement with the MCAA. The MCAA text dates from the June 2023 package, and signatures began in October 2024. Bilateral activation depends on notifications (`2026-09-04-carf-mcaa-bilateral-activation.md`). The political commitments under the Global Forum are recorded separately (`2026-09-30-oecd-carf-commitments-list-september-2026.md`).
