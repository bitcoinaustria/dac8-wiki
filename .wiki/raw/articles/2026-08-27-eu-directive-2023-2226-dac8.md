# Council Directive (EU) 2023/2226 (DAC8)

## Source identity

- CELEX: `32023L2226`
- Adopted: 17 October 2023
- Published: 24 October 2023
- Legal bases recorded by EUR-Lex: Articles 113 and 115 TFEU
- Transposition deadline: 31 December 2025
- Application: 1 January 2026, subject to limited later dates in Article 2

## Relevant content captured

The directive expands the administrative-cooperation framework to reporting crypto-asset service providers and reportable crypto-asset users. It requires due-diligence collection, annual reporting, and automatic exchange between competent tax authorities. The reported categories include identifying and tax-residence data plus aggregated transaction information by crypto-asset and transaction type.

The recitals acknowledge GDPR applicability, Charter rights, data minimisation, proportionality, confidentiality, security, and retention limits. Those safeguards are part of the legal measure and must be assessed alongside the breadth of the reporting obligation.

## Research use

This is the controlling EU instrument. Any challenge must distinguish defects required by DAC8 itself from choices added by the Austrian transposition. A national court cannot declare the directive invalid; a validity question belongs to the CJEU through Article 267 TFEU.
