# Ausschussbericht 749 d.B., XXV. GP

## Source identity

- Bericht des Finanzausschusses über die Regierungsvorlage (685 der Beilagen) — the bill that became `BGBl. I Nr. 116/2015`.

## Content

On the GMSG: reporting duties of financial institutions relate "in Bezug auf Neukonten erstmals auf das vierte Quartal 2016, sonst grundsätzlich auf Besteuerungszeiträume ab 1. Jänner 2017". Onward electronic transmission to partner-state authorities "erfolgt hinsichtlich der im 4. Quartal 2016 eröffneten Neukonten bis spätestens 30. September 2017, sonst (mit Ausnahmen) bis spätestens 30. September 2018."

On the BWG: the amendments are described as widening access to information protected by bank secrecy by adding further exceptions for GMSG exchange, for the federal tax authorities, for KontRegG reporting and queries, and for the Kapitalabfluss-Meldegesetz. On the KontRegG: an account register is to be set up, with rules on its content, continuous data delivery, Konteneinschau by federal tax authorities and special legal protection.

## Relevance and limits

Precise wording for timeline copy: Austria's CRS exchange did not start in full in 2017. A first partial exchange (Q4 2016 new accounts) was planned by 30 September 2017; the general first exchange (calendar year 2017) by 30 September 2018. This is a statement of legislative intent. Whether the September 2017 partial transmission actually took place was not verified from a BMF or Commission source.
