# Epsilon DPA: admitted cross-client data reuse for fraud

Filed 19 January 2021, case 1:21-cr-00006-RM, document 2-1. Extraction: official web PDF text. Locators: agreement §2; Attachment B §§2,10,14(d)–(e),17, file pp.32–39.

Epsilon accepted the statement of facts. Models combined clients' transaction/response records. Fraud clients received information from legitimate and fraudulent businesses. Staff continued monetising one operator's victim list after court-ordered closure, using it for other fraudulent clients. The admitted total exceeded thirty million consumers' data supplied to fraudulent mass-mailing clients.

These are corporate admissions under deferred prosecution, not a corporate trial conviction. The source establishes knowing sale and reuse, not hacking, DAC8 processing or individual attack probability.
