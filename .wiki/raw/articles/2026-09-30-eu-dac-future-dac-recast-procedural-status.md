# DAC recast 2026/0168(CNS): procedural status

## Verified steps

- **24 June 2026:** Commission adopts COM(2026) 308. It is received by the Council the same day and circulated as ST 11060/26 (FISC 235, ECOFIN 895, IA 182) with annexes in ADD 1 and further addenda.
- **1 July 2026:** Working Party on Tax Questions (Direct Taxation). The agenda is the Commission's presentation of the proposal and impact assessment, followed by an exchange of views (Council notice CM 3282/26 of 12 June 2026). The Council meetings calendar lists further working-party meetings on the DAC recast during the Irish Presidency, including in September 2026; their content is not public.
- **17 August 2026:** EDPS Opinion 20/2026 on the proposal, circulated to delegations as ST 12521/26 on 24 August 2026 (see `2026-09-30-eu-dac-future-edps-opinion-20-2026-dac-recast.md`).
- **European Parliament (OEIL, read 30 September 2026):** consultation procedure, recast subtype. Status "Preparatory phase in Parliament". Committee referral "pending final decision". The only key event is the 24 June 2026 proposal. Indicative plenary date: 15 February 2027.
- **Irish Presidency (1 July to 31 December 2026):** the presidency programme (p. 30) says the Presidency will "significantly progress and aim to conclude" the DAC recast, which will streamline the DAC's "nine iterations". It will also progress the expected Tax Simplification Omnibus.

## Context in Council reporting

The ECOFIN report on tax issues approved on 12 June 2026 (ST 10073/26, Cyprus Presidency) records three things. The Commission Work Programme 2026 of 21 October 2025 expected an omnibus on taxation in Q2 2026. On 24 April 2026 the three institutions agreed a "One Europe, One Market" roadmap with a target to agree the tax omnibus by Q4 2027. The High-Level Working Party discussed the DAC evaluation on 27 January 2026. The same report lists the Commission's announced withdrawal of the UNSHELL proposal (2021/0434(CNS), which would have amended Directive 2011/16/EU) and three other tax proposals.

## Not found

As of 30 September 2026 there is no Council general approach, no political agreement, no Parliament opinion and no adopted act. No public presidency compromise text was found.

## Relevance and limits

The recast is a pending proposal that requires unanimity. Presidency targets are aspirations, not procedural facts. Any public statement should date the status ("as of 30 September 2026") and should not describe recast provisions as applicable law.
