# VfGH G 62/2012 ua: rejection of 11,129 additional applicants

On 10 June 2014 the VfGH rejected the applications of the 11,129 persons joined after the first applicant. The pleading described direct/current affectedness only for the first applicant. A generic statement that the campaign had instructed only subscribers of covered providers to sign could not replace applicant-specific facts.

The defect was substantive and not curable through an improvement order. The precedent therefore cuts against a templated DAC8 mass filing: every applicant needs their own provider relationship, processing facts, affected provisions, and current legal injury pleaded.
