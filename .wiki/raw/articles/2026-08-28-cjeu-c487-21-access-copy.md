# CJEU C-487/21: Article 15 copy must reproduce personal data faithfully and intelligibly

In judgment C-487/21, *CRIF*, of 4 May 2023, the CJEU held that the right to a copy under Article 15(3) GDPR requires a faithful and intelligible reproduction of all personal data undergoing processing. Extracts from documents or databases, or entire documents, may be required where they are essential to understand the data.

Relevant passages are paragraphs 21, 28, 32, 36–45 and the operative part. For DAC8 fact development, the user should request the actual classification, transaction fields and aggregates in an intelligible form; an abstract category list may not show the concrete processing needed for standing.
