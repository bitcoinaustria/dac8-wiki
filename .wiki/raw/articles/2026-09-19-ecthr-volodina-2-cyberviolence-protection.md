# ECtHR Volodina v Russia (no. 2)

Application 40419/19, judgment of 14 September 2021, final 14 December 2021. Official [HUDOC full-text conversion](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/app/conversion/docx/html/body?library=ECHR&id=001-211794) retrieved directly after the web-reader conversion failed. Header confirms finality; source digest.

## Holdings

- Paragraphs 47–50: Article 8 protects physical and psychological integrity against private perpetrators; domestic-abuse vulnerability and connected online/offline violence matter. Paragraph 49 retains the real and immediate risk of recurrent violence known or knowable to authorities for operational protective measures.
- Paragraph 51: adequate protection can sometimes consist of civil remedies and injunctions.
- Paragraphs 57–61: this severe case required a criminal-law response and effective protection against recurrence; delayed, discretionary measures were inadequate.
- Paragraphs 62–68: authorities failed to preserve evidence promptly and investigate effectively, violating Article 8.

## Use and limits

Supports examining an identified victim's threat history, data misuse and practical protection. It does not classify crypto owners as vulnerable, eliminate the risk threshold or create a tax-reporting exception. Paragraph 55 expressly limits the inquiry to the law's application in this case.
