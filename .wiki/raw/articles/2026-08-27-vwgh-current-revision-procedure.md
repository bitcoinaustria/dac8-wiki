# VwGH current revision procedure: deadline, counsel and fee

The official VwGH FAQ states that a revision against an administrative-court decision must generally be filed within six weeks, requires representation by a lawyer, tax adviser or auditor as applicable, and currently attracts a EUR 340 filing fee. Legal aid may be available.

A revision has no automatic suspensive effect. Any request for suspensive effect must satisfy the statutory balancing test. These general rules do not establish that suspending a DSB dismissal would itself prohibit a CASP report.
