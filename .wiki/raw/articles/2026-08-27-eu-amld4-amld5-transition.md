# AMLD4 and AMLD5 transition

Directive 2015/849 supplies the current national-law framework until repeal on 10 July 2027. Articles 13, 18, 19a, and 19b govern risk-based CDD, enhanced controls, and transfers involving self-hosted addresses. Articles 30 and 31 govern beneficial-ownership information; Articles 32 to 34 govern FIUs and suspicious reporting; Article 32a requires central account mechanisms.

Article 40 begins with five-year retention and permits a further five years only where necessary and proportionate. Directive 2018/843 added virtual-currency exchange and custody providers, expanded beneficial-ownership transparency, and strengthened FIU and central-account access. The post-TFR current perimeter now follows MiCA CASP categories, generally excluding advice.
