# ECtHR Othymia Investments: tax-information exchange needs no general prior notice

In *Othymia Investments BV v Netherlands*, application 75292/10, decision of 16 June 2015, documents including bank statements, annual accounts and correspondence had been transmitted to Spain.

The Court accepted an Article 8 interference but found a lawful basis and legitimate tax-enforcement aim. Article 8 does not require prior notice to every potentially affected person before a lawful tax investigation or information exchange. The related Article 13 complaint therefore lacked an arguable claim. A two-stage ex-post administrative-court route remained available and was sufficient in the circumstances.

Relevant passages: paragraphs 37, 39-47.

The decision is adverse to any claimed general right to stop DAC8 transmission in advance. It does not decide whether Austria's DSB/BVwG or civil urgent remedies are timely enough for a specific first transfer.
