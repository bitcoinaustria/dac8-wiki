# FMA guidance on the Transfer of Funds Regulation

The FMA explains that control of self-hosted addresses must be assessed risk-sensitively and that customer self-certification alone is insufficient. The page evidences a substantial existing AML/CFT compliance baseline. Any provider claimant must therefore identify only the incremental Krypto-MPfG processing and cost.
