# Epicenter.works account of the Austrian data-retention campaign

AKVorrat, renamed epicenter.works in 2016, organized the public campaign, gathered powers of attorney, and supported the litigation. Christof Tschohl was the successful lead applicant in the collective filing; Ewald Scheucher and Scheucher Rechtsanwalt GmbH acted as counsel and co-drafters.

The association itself is not identified in the official CJEU or final VfGH judgments as the juridical applicant. It should therefore be described as organizer, coordinator, and funder—not as the successful institutional applicant.

Campaign and judicial sources disagree numerically: the campaign page states 11,139 complaints, while the VfGH's operative account is Tschohl plus 11,129 others. Legal analysis should use the judicial figure and label the campaign number as such. The separate 106,067-signature parliamentary petition was political advocacy, not the court-applicant count.
