# CJEU Recommendations to national courts on preliminary rulings

Only a national court may initiate the reference. The referring decision should set out the dispute's facts, applicable national and EU provisions, the reason an answer is needed, and concise questions. Parties may propose questions but cannot compel the reference.

The guidance confirms that only the CJEU can declare an EU act invalid. A reference is therefore relevant only after a national case is admissible and the EU-law answer is necessary to decide it.
