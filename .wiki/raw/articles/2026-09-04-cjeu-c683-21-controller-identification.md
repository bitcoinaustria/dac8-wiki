# CJEU C-683/21: functional identification of controllers

- Judgment: 5 December 2023
- ECLI: `ECLI:EU:C:2023:949`

Paragraphs 30–46 treat controller status functionally. An entity may be a controller without itself performing the processing or having direct access to the data where it actually influences purposes or essential means. Joint responsibility does not require equal responsibility, a formal arrangement or identical access.

For a Krypto-MPfG complaint, the named respondent should be the legal person that actually determines or is legally assigned the relevant processing, not merely the consumer-facing brand. Provider, group company, service provider and financial authority roles must be mapped separately.
