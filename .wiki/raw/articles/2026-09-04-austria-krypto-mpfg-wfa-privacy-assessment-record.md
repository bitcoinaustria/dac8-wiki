# Austria Krypto-MPfG legislative impact and privacy-assessment record

The government proposal's 22-page WFA, last updated 19 November 2025, identifies material financial, administrative-cost and macroeconomic effects. It relies on the Commission impact work and MiCA, describes authority staffing, data-warehouse and IT costs, and records the all-customer processing and ten-year deletion choices. The public text does not visibly provide an Article 35(7)-structured assessment of data-subject risk, field-level necessity, security architecture or physical-abuse and linkage threats.

The [Parliament proceeding](https://www.parlament.gv.at/gegenstand/XXVIII/I/306?selectedStage=100) records that the Finance Committee decided on written consultation on 20 November 2025 and invited the Datenschutzbehörde and Datenschutzrat. Invitation is not proof of completed Article 36(4) consultation. The transmission, receipt, response or non-response, minutes and treatment of any advice remain facts to obtain.

The public record can test a claim that this WFA itself satisfied Article 35(10): the asserted assessment should be mapped against Article 35(7)'s required elements. The absence of visible analysis does not prove that no separate or confidential operational assessment exists, and CJEU C-61/22 forecloses automatic statutory invalidity from this point alone.
