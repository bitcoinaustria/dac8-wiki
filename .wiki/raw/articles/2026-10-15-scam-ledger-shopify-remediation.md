# Ledger Shopify insider exposure, phishing and proposed minimisation

First-party statement 13 January 2021; sections 1–3.

Shopify support insiders exported order records in April/June 2020. Ledger reports approximately 292,000 customers, 93% overlap with earlier exposure and about 20,000 additional records. Fields included contact/address details and ordered products. Do not sum overlapping breach populations.

Ledger acknowledges active customer phishing and 216 site takedowns, without allocating losses between the datasets. It proposes segregating retained order information after three months, reducing identity fields passing through email suppliers, and in-app security messaging. These were plans, not independently verified implementation. Hardware keys were not described as compromised.
