# CJEU C-154/21: Article 15 generally requires actual recipient identity

In judgment C-154/21, *Österreichische Post*, of 12 January 2023, the CJEU held that Article 15(1)(c) GDPR generally gives the data subject the right to the identity of actual recipients to whom personal data have been or will be disclosed. Categories may suffice where actual identification is impossible or the controller demonstrates that the request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

Relevant passages are paragraphs 47–51 and the operative part. For a DAC8 evidence request, this supports asking for concrete known tax-authority or other recipients and known planned recipients rather than accepting generic recipient categories without explanation.
