# Austrian VwGVG section 28 merits substitution

Section 28 VwGVG requires the administrative court to decide the merits where the relevant facts are fixed or merits determination serves speed or cost saving. Outside penalty matters, remittal remains possible under the statutory conditions where the authority omitted necessary fact-finding.

For a Krypto-MPfG data-protection route, a BVwG merits decision can replace the DSB disposition within the legally available remedy. A remittal only returns the matter for another decision. The application should therefore separate the desired merits order, any request for constitutional review and any Article 267 reference; those outcomes are not interchangeable.
