This is an exact-text check, not a comprehensive review of later amendments or the current transfer arrangements of any tax authority.

It is not itself a ruling on CARF exchanges between authorities.

## Repository reconciliation and inferential corrections

- The September 10 activation account itself admits missing effective dates. Its final non-reportability claim therefore exceeds its evidence. Likewise, missing public specifications, notifications, notices or commentary establish only the bounded search result, not universal absence or legal invalidity.
- The [existing national-addition audit](../../wiki/references/austrian-gold-plating-and-claimant-audit.md) distinguishes the absence of an EU mandate from a Member-State basis under GDPR Article 6(3). The September 10 legislative-materials records cannot collapse those questions. Statutory authority, necessity and proportionality require separate examination.
- The [existing retention analysis](../../wiki/theses/bitcoin-payment-profile-retention.md) separates § 20(4), § 39, MiCA and § 21(1) FM-GwG. The latter supplies an independent ten-year rule for the records it covers, with a different starting point. This is inherited repository analysis, not a fresh complete statutory audit. A missing specific tax-deletion trigger proves neither indefinite actual retention nor automatic GDPR infringement.
- German drafting and Austrian Model-2 FATCA history are bounded comparisons, not proof of Austrian illegality or an equally effective voluntary CARF substitute. Distinguish consent, aggregate reporting and the group-request mechanism.
- The September 10 DSB record itself says that the 105 decisions are classified by the controller's non-EEA location. They cannot be counted as merits decisions for third-country-resident complainants. The underlying report and all individual decisions were not independently rechecked in this correction.
- The [existing VwGH record](../articles/2026-08-27-vwgh-ro-2023-04-0021-dac2.md) and [local judgment](../../../legal-docs/core/at-vwgh-ro-2023-04-0021-12.pdf) contradict the September 10 E 2097/2021 record's claim that no subsequent VwGH judgment was published. Preserve the inadmissibility disposition, the refusal to refer and adverse Charter reasoning; do not confuse them with an affirmative ruling on Krypto-MPfG § 20(1).
